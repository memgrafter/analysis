# Multi-RF Fusion with Multi-GNN Blending for Molecular Property Prediction

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.20724
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20724
- **Reference count:** 2
- **Primary result:** Test ROC-AUC 0.8476 ± 0.0002 on ogbg-molhiv (scaffold split), ranking #1 on the OGB leaderboard.

## Executive Summary
This work addresses the challenging molecular property prediction task of HIV inhibition (ogbg-molhiv) under a scaffold split, where standard Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) exhibit high variance. The proposed solution ensembles 12 Random Forest (RF) models trained on a concatenated 4,263-dimensional fingerprint descriptor (FCFP, ECFP, MACCS, atom pairs) and blends their rank-averaged predictions with those from two GNN architectures (GIN-VN, GIN-VN-deep). The RFs receive 88% of the blending weight, while each GNN contributes 6%. Key innovations include tuning the RF `max_features` hyperparameter to 0.20 (instead of the default `√d`), which yields a +0.008 test AUC gain, and averaging GNN predictions across 10 seeds before blending, which reduces the final standard deviation from 0.0008 to 0.0002. No external data or pre-training is used.

## Method Summary
The pipeline begins by computing four molecular fingerprints (FCFP radius 2 and 3, Morgan ECFP radius 2, MACCS keys, hashed atom pairs) via RDKit and concatenating them into a fixed `fcfp_ext` vector (4,263 dimensions). The OGB scaffold split is used for training, validation, and testing. For each of 10 evaluation seeds, 12 RF models are trained on the training set with varying hyperparameters (primarily `max_features`, criterion, and minor feature subsets), all using `class_weight={0:1,1:25}` and `n_estimators=20,000`. Separately, two GNN architectures (GIN-VN and GIN-VN-deep) are each trained from 10 random seeds. Before blending, the 10 seed predictions for each GNN are averaged into a single probability vector, which is then converted to ranks. The 12 RF models' test predictions are also converted to ranks and averaged. The final blended score per molecule is `0.88 * RF_rank + 0.06 * GIN-VN_rank + 0.06 * GIN-VN-deep_rank`. Test ROC-AUC is computed and this process is repeated over 10 evaluation seeds to report mean and standard deviation.

## Key Results
- Tuning RF `max_features=0.20` (vs. default `√d`) improves test AUC by approximately +0.008.
- Averaging GNN predictions across seeds before blending reduces final standard deviation from 0.0008 to 0.0002.
- Achieved test ROC-AUC of 0.8476 ± 0.0002, ranking #1 on the OGB leaderboard for ogbg-molhiv.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method's robustness stems from combining complementary model families: RFs on high-dimensional, handcrafted fingerprints capture local chemical substructure patterns, while GNNs operate directly on molecular graphs to capture global topological relationships. The rank-averaging ensemble technique reduces sensitivity to individual model biases and probability miscalibration. The heavy weighting of the RF component (88%) leverages its inherent stability and lower variance across seeds. Averaging GNN predictions across multiple seeds before blending cancels out random initialization effects, drastically cutting variance. The tuned `max_features=0.20` likely allows each RF tree to consider a more diverse subset of features, improving ensemble diversity without overfitting, thus boosting accuracy.

## Foundational Learning
- **Molecular fingerprints (FCFP, ECFP, MACCS, atom pairs):** Fixed-length binary vectors encoding molecular structure. *Why needed:* Provide a consistent, high-dimensional input for RFs. *Quick check:* Confirm concatenated dimension sums to 4,263.
- **Random Forest:** Ensemble of decision trees using bootstrap aggregation and feature randomness. *Why needed:* Robust to high-dimensional, sparse fingerprint data; reduces overfitting. *Quick check:* Train a single RF, check out-of-bag score >0.8.
- **Graph Neural Networks (GIN-VN, GIN-VN-deep):** Message-passing architectures that update node representations by aggregating neighbor information. *Why needed:* Learn representations directly from molecular graph topology. *Quick check:* Verify GNN input uses node/edge features from OGB dataset and processes graph structure correctly.
- **Rank-averaged ensemble:** Converting model probabilities to ranks before averaging. *Why needed:* Mitigates poor probability calibration and focuses on relative ordering. *Quick check:* Apply `scipy.stats.rankdata` to dummy predictions; ensure monotonicity.
- **Scaffold split:** Data split based on molecular scaffold (core structure) to test generalization to unseen chemotypes. *Why needed:* Prevents data leakage and simulates real-world discovery scenarios. *Quick check:* Use official OGB split indices; ensure no scaffold overlap between train/test.
- **Weighted blending:** Linear combination of model ranks with fixed weights. *Why needed:* Balances contributions from more stable (RF) and potentially complementary (GNN) models. *Quick check:* Verify weights sum to 1 (0.88+0.06+0.06).

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:**  
Fingerprint extraction -> RF training (12 models) & GNN training (2 archs × 10 seeds) -> GNN seed averaging -> RF rank averaging -> Rank-based blending -> Test evaluation

**Critical path:** Data preprocessing → Parallel GNN training (20 models total) → Parallel RF training (12 models per eval seed) → GNN prediction averaging (per architecture) → RF rank conversion and averaging → Blending with fixed weights → ROC-AUC computation → Repeat over 10 evaluation seeds.

**Design tradeoffs:**  
- *RF vs. GNN diversity:* RFs use static fingerprints, GNNs learn dynamic representations; combining covers both手工特征 and learned features.  
- *Blending weights:* High RF weight (88%) likely because RF ensemble is more stable; GNNs add marginal gain but increase computational cost.  
- *Rank vs. probability blending:* Ranks reduce calibration issues but discard absolute probability information.

**Failure signatures:**  
- Final AUC standard deviation >0.001: Indicates GNN predictions were not averaged across seeds before blending.  
- Test AUC ~0.839 instead of 0.8476: Likely RF `max_features` left at default `√d` (≈65).  
- `fcfp_ext` dimension ≠4,263: Incorrect fingerprint concatenation (e.g., missed atom pairs).

**First three experiments:**  
1. Full pipeline replication: Compute all fingerprints, train 12 RFs and two GNNs (10 seeds each), blend as described, report mean±std AUC over 10 eval seeds.  
2. Validate `max_features` tuning: On a held-out validation set from the scaffold split, compare validation AUC of RF ensemble with default `max_features='sqrt'` vs. tuned `0.20`. Expect ~0.008 difference.  
3. Confirm variance reduction: For fixed trained GNNs, blend their per-seed test predictions vs. first averaging predictions across seeds; compute final std over eval seeds; expect drop from ~0.001 to ~0.0002 with averaging.
