# Discovering mathematical concepts through a multi-agent system
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2603.04528
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04528
- **Reference count**: 40
- **Primary result**: A multi-agent AI system autonomously discovers and relates two definitions of the Euler characteristic (χ) from polyhedral data, effectively rediscovering homology concepts like Betti numbers.

## Executive Summary
The paper introduces a multi-agent system to model the interplay of conjecture, proof, and counterexamples in autonomous mathematical discovery. It combines a Conjecturing agent (using symbolic regression) with a Skeptical agent that dynamically adjusts data attention, guided by a provability measure from an automated prover. The benchmark task—recovering the Euler characteristic and Betti numbers from polyhedral incidence matrices—is successfully completed by the full system. Ablation studies demonstrate that both adaptive data attention and provability feedback are essential; removing either causes severe performance degradation, while symbolic regression alone fails entirely. The results suggest that optimizing interactions between local processes can generate mathematically valuable notions aligned with human interestingness.

## Method Summary
The system employs a multi-agent reinforcement learning framework (MADDPG) where a Conjecturing agent (CA) performs decomposed symbolic regression via per-patch "Feature Spotters" and a global "Scaffolder." A Skeptical agent (SA) modulates the visibility of data patches through weights λ_i, steering the CA’s focus. Candidate statements are translated to a formal language (Lean) and evaluated by a prover, yielding a binary provability score ρ(s) that serves as a reward signal. This closed loop enables the CA to iteratively generate and refine conjectures, while the SA adaptively reveals counterexamples to prevent premature convergence. The approach is validated on a dataset of polyhedra with the goal of deriving χ and Betti numbers from raw incidence data and a premise set of linear algebra facts.

## Key Results
- The full system (M0) successfully recovers both the combinatorial (χ = V−E+F) and homological (χ = Σ(−1)^i b_i) definitions of the Euler characteristic and discovers the first Betti number b₁.
- Ablations removing the Skeptical agent’s adaptive λ_i (M1) or provability feedback (M2) degrade performance by ≥5σ on key metrics; symbolic regression alone (OnlyCA) fails to notice χ or b₁ entirely.
- The interplay of dynamic data attention and provability guidance leads to a discovery trajectory mirroring historical mathematics: χ=2 is first found for spheres, then tori appear as counterexamples, prompting generalization to homology.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Dynamic Data Attention via Skeptical Agent
**Claim**: Dynamically adjusting which data points the Conjecturing agent focuses on enables efficient exploration of the hypothesis space, leading to the discovery of non-trivial mathematical concepts like Betti numbers.  
**Mechanism**: The Skeptical agent modifies λ_i weights governing data visibility for each Feature Spotter. By initially masking certain surfaces (e.g., tori) and later revealing counterexamples, it prevents premature convergence to simple patterns (e.g., χ=2 for spheres only) and forces generalization.  
**Core assumption**: Restricting attention to subsets of data can make latent patterns detectable, which can then be reinterpreted globally—a process mirroring historical mathematical practice.  
**Evidence anchors**: [abstract] removal of adaptive λ_i degrades performance; [section 1.11] "χ=2 was first spotted for polyhedra homeomorphic to spheres, with tori only noticed as counterexamples afterwards".  
**Break condition**: If λ_i updates are too infrequent or too random, the system may never form coherent hypotheses; if too aggressive, learning destabilizes.

### Mechanism 2: Provability as a Guiding Signal
**Claim**: Binary provability feedback from an automated prover shapes conjecture formulation toward statements that are both true and non-trivial, aligning with human mathematical interestingness.  
**Mechanism**: Conjectures with ρ(s)=1 terminate an episode with high reward, creating selection pressure for statements provable within the system’s premise set. This encourages the CA to synthesize concepts that connect data to available theorems.  
**Core assumption**: Provability (vs. mere data fit) is a meaningful proxy for "mathematical value" in this constrained domain.  
**Evidence anchors**: [abstract] removal of provability feedback leads to ≥5σ worse performance; [section 2.2] Ablation M1 (CA+SA without provability) underperforms on noticing χ and b₁.  
**Break condition**: If the prover is too weak (proves nothing non-trivial) or too strong (proves all well-formed statements), the signal becomes uninformative; moderate noise degrades but does not eliminate performance.

### Mechanism 3: Decomposed Symbolic Regression with Scaffolding
**Claim**: Breaking symbolic regression into (a) local "Feature Spotters" operating on data patches and (b) a global "Scaffolder" combining atomic formulae reduces search complexity and enables emergence of compound concepts.  
**Mechanism**: Feature Spotters identify atomic expressions (e.g., n(∂₁), w(∂₂)) over weighted data subsets; the Scaffolder combines them into logical statements. This two-stage structure mirrors mathematical definition-building.  
**Core assumption**: Mathematical concepts often arise from combining simpler, locally detectable patterns; decomposing the search space makes tractable the discovery of expressions like b₁ = null(∂₁) − rank(∂₂).  
**Evidence anchors**: [section 1.10] decomposition simplifies search space; [section 2.3] OnlyCA model (monolithic SR+RL) never spots χ or b₁.  
**Break condition**: If patches are poorly initialized or the Scaffolder lacks operator diversity, meaningful combinations may not form.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map**: SA updates λ_i weights → CA uses λ_i to weight data patches for Feature Spotters → Feature Spotters fit atomic expressions → Scaffolder combines them into statement s → s + premises sent to Prover → Prover returns ρ(s) → Both CA and SA update policies via MADDPG (centralized training, decentralized execution). Environment holds dataset D and controls episode termination.

**Critical path**: An episode runs: 1) SA initializes/updates λ_i. 2) CA's Feature Spotters fit atomic formulae on weighted patches. 3) Scaffolder combines features into s. 4) Prover evaluates s, returns ρ(s). 5) If ρ(s)≥threshold, episode ends with high reward; else repeat (max 50 steps). Training loops over episodes, optimizing CA and SA policies against this loop.

**Design tradeoffs**:  
- Prover choice: LLM-based (flexible but hallucinate) vs. deterministic tactic script (reliable but brittle); paper found no qualitative difference.  
- Regressor implementation: PySR is flexible but not math-optimized; custom operators/constraints needed.  
- Premise set P: Must suffice to prove target relations but not make χ/b_i directly expressible; requires domain experimentation.

**Failure signatures**:  
- OnlyCA model never spots χ/b₁ → SR search space too vast without RL-guided attention.  
- M1 (CA+SA no provability) finds concepts but rarely proven → provability signal necessary to combine meaningfully.  
- M0+Noise degrades → system sensitive to provability noise; robust to moderate noise.  
- SA not adapting λ_i (e.g., all λ_i fixed) → similar to OnlyCA failure.

**First 3 experiments**:  
1. Run baseline 'OnlyCA' on D0 with random hyperparameter sweeps to confirm SR alone cannot find χ or b₁.  
2. Test M2 (CA+Provability, fixed λ_i=1) to isolate impact of provability feedback without adaptive attention; expect some improvement but suboptimal.  
3. Run full M0 on D0 to validate system completes Learning Problem 1; then compare M0 vs M1 vs M2 via ablation tables (like Fig 4) to quantify contributions of SA and provability.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
**Major Uncertainties:**
- **Domain Generalization**: The system is validated only on a specific algebraic topology benchmark (Euler characteristic/Betti numbers). Unknown if the discovered interplay (skeptical attention + provability) scales to other mathematical domains (e.g., group theory, combinatorics) or non-mathematical scientific discovery.
- **Prover Dependence**: The provability measure ρ(s) relies on a specific prover (Lean Copilot/tactics) and a fixed premise set P. The sensitivity of results to prover strength, premise selection, and noise in ρ is not fully mapped; the "sweet spot" for useful provability feedback is inferred but not systematically characterized.
- **"Mathematical Interestingness" Proxy**: The alignment of provability + data fit with human-valued concepts is demonstrated post-hoc for this task. It is unknown whether the system would converge on historically "important" but technically provable trivialities, or miss deep but currently unprovable patterns.
- **Component Isolation**: Ablations remove entire agents (SA) or signals (provability). The precise contribution of the *decomposition* (Feature Spotters + Scaffolder) versus other architectural choices (e.g., RL framework, λ_i mechanism) is not disentangled; the OnlyCA baseline combines SR with RL but without decomposition, confounding factors.

## Limitations
- **Domain generalization**: Only validated on Euler characteristic/Betti numbers discovery; unknown scalability to other mathematical or scientific domains.
- **Prover dependence**: Results may be sensitive to prover strength, premise set, and ρ(s) noise; effective range not fully characterized.
- **Interestingness proxy**: Provability + data fit alignment with human-valued mathematics is task-specific; system may converge on trivialities or miss deep unprovable patterns.

## Confidence
- **High**: Dynamic data attention (Skeptical agent’s adaptive λ_i) is necessary for full system success (≥5σ degradation in ablations).
- **Medium**: Provability feedback is sufficiently informative to guide conjectures toward non-trivial, combinable concepts (M1 finds but fails to prove; noise degrades but doesn’t eliminate performance).
- **Medium**: Decomposed symbolic regression reduces search complexity (OnlyCA fails), but its unique contribution is not isolated from RL scaffolding.

## Next Checks
1. **Cross-domain generalization test**: Run full system (M0) and key ablations (M1, M2) on 2–3 distinctly different mathematical discovery tasks (e.g., group homomorphism properties from Cayley tables, combinatorial identities from binomial data) to test if the attention+provability interaction is domain-agnostic.
2. **Prover sensitivity mapping**: Systematically vary prover strength (e.g., Lean with increasing tactic libraries/LLM hints) and premise set completeness (minimal to nearly self-evident). Measure discovery success, convergence speed, and conjecture complexity to identify effective range and failure modes of the provability signal.
3. **Decomposition isolation experiment**: Construct a variant where CA uses identical SR and RL infrastructure but a monolithic conjecture generator (no Feature Spotters/Scaffolder split), while keeping SA and provability unchanged. Compare against OnlyCA and full M0 to isolate the contribution of spatial decomposition.
