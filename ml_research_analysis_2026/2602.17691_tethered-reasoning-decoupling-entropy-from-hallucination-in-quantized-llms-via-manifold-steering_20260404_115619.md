# Tethered Reasoning: Decoupling Entropy from Hallucination in Quantized LLMs via Manifold Steering

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.17691
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.17691
- **Reference count:** 27
- **Primary result:** HELIX enables quantized LLMs to operate at high temperatures (T>2.0) without hallucination by steering hidden-state trajectories to a truthfulness manifold, reducing duplicate ideas from 70–80% to 5–20% while preserving reasoning accuracy.

## Executive Summary
Large language models face a fundamental trade-off: low sampling temperatures produce repetitive outputs, while high temperatures induce hallucination through geometric divergence of hidden-state trajectories. HELIX addresses this by introducing a "truthfulness manifold" derived from low-temperature activations on factual data. Using a Unified Truth Score (UTS) that fuses semantic entropy with Mahalanobis distance from this manifold, the method detects when outputs stray into incoherent regions. A graduated steering mechanism then applies minimal, targeted penalties to logits, redirecting the model's activation path. On quantized models, HELIX maintains near-optimal reasoning accuracy even at T=3.0 and unlocks a "High-Entropy Creative Reservoir" where diverse yet coherent concepts are generated.

## Method Summary
HELIX is an inference-time intervention requiring no model fine-tuning. First, a truthfulness manifold is precomputed: mean activation vector 𝝁_T and precision matrix 𝚺_T⁻¹ are extracted from a factual dataset (e.g., TruthfulQA) using a low-temperature (T=0.1) forward pass on designated Transformer layers (e.g., ℓ=4,12,20 in Granite). During generation, for each token, the system computes (a) normalized Shannon entropy from logits and (b) Mahalanobis distance D_t from the manifold using the current hidden state. These are combined into a UTS_t score using a temperature-adaptive weight β(T) that favors manifold distance at high T. If UTS_t falls below a temperature-dependent threshold τ(T), a graduated penalty δ proportional to the UTS deficit is subtracted from the top logit, biasing the next token. This steering affects only 0.2–2.5% of tokens, preserving natural distribution for the majority. For hybrid SSM-Transformer models (e.g., Granite), steering is applied only to the sparse Transformer residual streams to correct SSM drift.

## Key Results
- On 4-bit Granite 4.0 H Small, HELIX maintains 88.84% GSM8K accuracy at T=3.0 (vs. 2.81 pp drop from T=0.5) and 72.49% MMLU accuracy (1.24 pp degradation) across 14,042 questions.
- On Qwen3-30B-A3B MoE, HELIX increases unique concept generation by 46.7%.
- High-temperature operation (T>2.0) with HELIX reduces idea duplication in creative tasks from 70–80% (conservative settings) to 5–20% while maintaining coherence, revealing a High-Entropy Creative Reservoir.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** High-temperature hallucination is primarily geometric trajectory divergence from a truthfulness manifold, not semantic knowledge collapse.
- **Mechanism:** The Unified Truth Score (UTS) fuses token-level Shannon entropy (distributional uncertainty) with Mahalanobis distance from a pre-computed manifold of truthful activations. This dual signal detects when hidden states escape structurally coherent regions. At high temperatures (T > 2.0), entropy saturates and becomes non-discriminative, but manifold distance remains stable, enabling detection.
- **Core assumption:** Valid reasoning trajectories occupy a lower-dimensional submanifold in activation space; hallucinations are geometric departures.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract states "high-temperature 'hallucination' is primarily trajectory divergence—activations escaping structurally coherent regions—rather than semantic collapse." Section 3.3 notes the β→0 handover at high T where manifold distance drives the score. Neighboring work explores geometric metrics but does not validate this specific hypothesis.
- **Break condition:** If the truthfulness manifold is poorly constructed, Mahalanobis distance loses discriminative power at high T, rendering UTS ineffective.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** Graduated steering vectors can redirect activations toward the manifold with minimal token intervention (<2.5%), decoupling entropy from hallucination.
- **Mechanism:** When UTS_t < τ, a penalty proportional to the UTS deficit via a logistic function is applied to the top logit: z̃_t[k*] = z_t[k*] - δ. This creates a smooth "elastic tether" affecting only tokens likely to cause divergence, preserving high-entropy distribution for others.
- **Core assumption:** Small, targeted perturbations to logits of divergence-prone tokens nudge the entire subsequent hidden-state trajectory back toward the manifold due to autoregressive dynamics.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract reports affecting only 0.2–2.5% of tokens. Section 3.5 details the steering activation condition. Prior work on activation addition shows small interventions can alter behavior, but the graduated, uncertainty-triggered penalty scheme is novel.
- **Break condition:** Threshold τ mis-calibration: too low causes over-steering (>10% tokens, harming creativity/fluency); too high fails to prevent divergence.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** Geometric tethering reveals and enables access to a "High-Entropy Creative Reservoir"—a region of semantically diverse but structurally coherent outputs at T > 2.0.
- **Mechanism:** By preventing trajectory divergence, HELIX allows full high-temperature probability distribution exploration without incoherence, unlocking latent, non-overlapping conceptual spaces inaccessible at low T (due to mode collapse) or high T without steering.
- **Core assumption:** The truthfulness manifold encodes structural coherence (syntax, logic, causality) rather than specific facts, permitting maximum semantic exploration.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract reports duplication reduction to 5–20% and breakthrough concepts. Section 6.2 shows high-T outputs occupy fundamentally different conceptual regions. Neighboring entropy-creativity work describes high-T degeneration, not unlocked coherence.
- **Break condition:** If the manifold overly constrains semantic diversity (e.g., built from narrow factual data), the creative reservoir is not accessed.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept: Manifold Learning & Mahalanobis Distance**
  - *Why needed:* Core to HELIX's "truthfulness manifold" and deviation measurement. Understanding lower-dimensional manifolds in high-dimensional activation spaces and how inverse covariance in Mahalanobis distance accounts for feature correlations is essential.
  - *Quick check:* Why is Euclidean distance insufficient for measuring deviation from a cloud of training activations, and what does the inverse covariance in Mahalanobis distance account for?
- **Concept: Temperature Sampling & Entropy in LLMs**
  - *Why needed:* The problem is framed around the temperature-entropy trade-off. Understanding how T scales logits, affects softmax entropy, and the conventional wisdom about T>1.5 degradation is prerequisite.
  - *Quick check:* How does increasing sampling temperature from 0.7 to 2.0 change the shape of the softmax output distribution, and why is this traditionally linked to "hallucination"?
- **Concept: Activation/Logit Steering (Representation Engineering)**
  - *Why needed:* HELIX is an inference-time intervention. Basics of how adding vectors to hidden states or subtracting from logits changes output are needed to understand the "how".
  - *Quick check:* What is the key difference between supervised steering (e.g., training a router) and the unsupervised, geometry-based steering HELIX proposes?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:**  
  Truthfulness Manifold (𝝁_T, 𝚺_T⁻¹) -> UTS Calculator (computes SE + SD) -> Steering Controller (compares UTS to τ(T), applies graduated penalty) -> Modified Logits -> Sampling -> Output. For hybrid models: Hybrid Architecture Adapter targets only Transformer residual streams.
- **Critical path (inference loop):**  
  `input x → forward pass → at each step t: get z_t, h_t^(4,12,20) → compute UTS_t → if UTS_t < τ(T): modify z_t[k*] → sample y_t from modified distribution → repeat.` Manifold parameters and τ(T) curve loaded at startup.
- **Design tradeoffs:**  
  - **Steering aggressiveness (τ) vs. Coherence:** Lower τ increases safety but reduces diversity; higher τ increases diversity but risks divergence. Requires per-model/task calibration.  
  - **Checkpoint layer selection:** Sparse steering (e.g., 3 Transformer layers) reduces overhead but may miss drift; more layers increase safety at computational cost.  
  - **Manifold construction data:** Generic (TruthfulQA) enables zero-shot but may not capture task-specific coherence; domain-specific data improves relevance but reduces generality.
- **Failure signatures:**  
  - **Over-steering:** Steering rate >5–10% consistently, accompanied by accuracy drop >5pp at T≥2.5 and loss of creative diversity (SAC drops).  
  - **Under-steering:** High UTS variance but no steering activation, followed by abrupt accuracy collapse at T>2.0 and/or high perplexity (PPL>15).  
  - **Manifold mismatch:** Consistently low UTS (high confidence) but low benchmark accuracy, indicating manifold does not represent current task distribution.
- **First 3 experiments:**  
  1. **Manifold Distance Sanity Check:** Run 500 factual prompts at T=0.1 to extract activations; verify Mahalanobis distances are ~N(1,1). Test held-out hallucinated prompts have significantly higher distances (p<0.01).  
  2. **UTS Threshold Calibration Sweep:** On 200 GSM8K problems, sweep base thresholds τ₀ ∈ [0.3, 0.9] and relaxation rates γ. Measure steering rate vs. τ and accuracy at T=1.0 and T=2.5. Identify knee point where T=2.5 accuracy begins to drop as τ increases.  
  3. **Creative Reservoir Discovery Protocol:** For a creative prompt, generate 10 completions per temperature in [0.5,0.7,…,3.0] with HELIX. Compute pairwise semantic similarity (SBERT) within each temperature band. Verify SAC (duplication) decreases monotonically from low-T to high-T, and high-T outputs pass coherence filter (PPL<15).

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Adapting to Expert Routing Uncertainty in MoE:** How can steering handle discrete router decisions in MoE (e.g., Qwen3-A3B) beyond penalizing output logits? The paper suggests directional vectors to guide router selection but provides no method. Resolution requires developing and evaluating a router-steering variant, measuring expert activation changes and task coherence.
- **Exploiting the Creative Reservoir:** What are the precise boundaries and optimal exploitation strategies for the High-Entropy Creative Reservoir? It is an emergent observation based on a single songwriting task; its generality, limits, and best use for diverse creative tasks are unknown. Resolution requires systematic multi-domain evaluation (e.g., scientific hypothesis, product design) across model scales to find temperature "sweet spots" and reservoir saturation.
- **Manifold Encoding: Structure vs. Facts:** Does the truthfulness manifold primarily encode syntactic/logical structure rather than factual knowledge, and is this the mechanism for creative diversity? The hypothesis is untested; the manifold is built from factual prompts. Resolution requires ablation: build manifolds from syntactically manipulated but factually hollow text or formal logic systems, and test if steering preserves coherence and boosts diversity.
- **Optimal Intervention Sparsity in Hybrid Architectures:** Is steering a fixed 10% of layers (Transformer checkpoints) optimal for all hybrid SSM-Transformer architectures? The finding in Granite may not generalize to models with different SSM/Transformer ratios. Resolution requires comparative experiments varying steered layer count/position across a suite of hybrid models with systematically varied proportions, measuring temperature stability and overhead.

## Limitations
- Key hyperparameters (base threshold τ₀, relaxation rate γ, maximum penalty β) are undisclosed, blocking exact replication.
- Steering mechanism description is ambiguous: paper mentions both "logit penalty" and "steering vectors" without defining the latter mathematically; the graduated penalty scheme is novel and unvalidated independently.
- Mamba-2 state correction details are absent; how steering Transformer layers affects SSM parameters is unspecified.

## Confidence
- **Mechanism 1 (trajectory divergence):** Medium — plausible internal logic, but geometric hypothesis lacks external validation.
- **Mechanism 2 (graduated steering):** Low — novel intervention scheme; no independent corroboration of token impact claim.
- **Mechanism 3 (creative reservoir):** Low — emergent observation; unverified beyond hybrid architectures.
- **Foundational concepts (manifolds, temperature, steering):** High.

## Next Checks
1. **UTS discriminative power:** On a balanced hallucination benchmark (TruthfulQA false samples), compare UTS distributions at T=0.1 vs. T=3.0; expect Mahalanobis component stable while entropy saturates.
2. **Threshold relaxation ablation:** Sweep relaxation rate γ ∈ [0.1, 0.5, 1.0] on GSM8K at T=2.5; plot accuracy vs. steering rate to locate optimal knee point.
3. **Cross-architecture test:** Apply HELIX (identical manifold) to a non-hybrid decoder-only model (e.g., Llama-3-8B); measure Semantic Area Coverage (SAC) and Logical Coherence (LC) at T=3.0 to verify creative reservoir generalizability.
