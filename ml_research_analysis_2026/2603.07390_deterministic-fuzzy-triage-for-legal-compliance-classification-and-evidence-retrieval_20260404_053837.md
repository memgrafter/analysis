# Deterministic Fuzzy Triage for Legal Compliance Classification and Evidence Retrieval

## Quick Facts
*   **arXiv ID:** 2603.07390
*   **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07390
*   **Reference Count:** 3
*   **Primary Result:** A deterministic RoBERTa-based dual-encoder with a fuzzy triage head auto-decides 96-98% of legal compliance queries with a constrained ≤2% error rate on held-out decisions.

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes a deterministic, explainable alternative to opaque LLMs for legal compliance triage. The method uses a RoBERTa-base dual encoder trained with a listwise loss, followed by fine-tuning on a binary compliance dataset. A post-hoc "fuzzy triage" head maps cosine similarity scores into three operational regions (auto-compliant, auto-noncompliant, human-review) by tuning thresholds to maximize automated coverage while strictly limiting the error rate on auto-decided examples. The approach is evaluated on the ACORD and CUAD benchmarks, demonstrating high discriminatory performance (AUC 0.98-0.99) and the ability to automatically resolve the vast majority of cases with very low estimated error.

## Method Summary
The pipeline begins with a RoBERTa-base dual encoder pre-trained on the graded ACORD benchmark using a listwise loss to rank relevant documents. This encoder is then fine-tuned on a binary compliance dataset derived from CUAD, with a positivity weighting to handle extreme class imbalance. For inference, the model computes cosine similarity between a query and a document embedding. A deterministic "fuzzy triage" head applies two tuned thresholds to this similarity score, segmenting outcomes into three zones: scores above the upper threshold are auto-classified as compliant; scores below the lower threshold are auto-classified as noncompliant; scores in the middle band are flagged for human review. The thresholds are optimized on a validation set to maximize the proportion of examples falling into the auto-decision zones while ensuring the error rate on these auto-decided examples does not exceed a predefined cap (e.g., ≤2%).

## Key Results
*   On the graded ACORD test set, the model achieves an NDCG@5 of 0.38–0.42 and a 4-star Precision@5 of 0.37.
*   On the highly imbalanced CUAD binary task (positive rate ~0.6%), it achieves an AUC of 0.98–0.99 and an F1 score of 0.22–0.30.
*   The fuzzy triage head automatically decides 96–98% of examples, with auto-only error rates on the validation-tuned decisions between 0% and 3.2%, depending on the positive-class weighting used during fine-tuning.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism relies on the high discriminative power of the fine-tuned dual encoder, which produces a meaningful cosine similarity score that correlates with compliance relevance. The fuzzy triage head operationalizes this continuous score into a actionable, risk-controlled triage process. By explicitly decoupling the binary classification training (on CUAD) from the graded retrieval evaluation (on ACORD), the system leverages the encoder's learned semantic matching. The threshold tuning on validation data directly optimizes for the operational goal: maximizing throughput (auto-decided percentage) under a hard constraint on decision risk (error rate). This creates a deterministic, auditable decision boundary for each zone, contrasting with probabilistic or generative LLM outputs.

## Foundational Learning
*   **Dual Encoder Architecture:** Two identical neural networks (here, RoBERTa-base) that map queries and documents into a shared embedding space. *Why needed:* Enables efficient similarity search and scalable retrieval. *Quick check: Can you compute a single cosine similarity score between a query and a document vector?*
*   **Listwise Loss (e.g., InfoNCE, RankNet):** A loss function that optimizes the ranking order of a list of documents for a given query. *Why needed:* Pre-trains the encoder for graded relevance retrieval, not just binary classification. *Quick check: Does the loss function consider the relative ordering of multiple positive/negative documents per query?*
*   **Fine-tuning for Binary Classification:** Adapting a pre-trained model to a specific downstream task (compliance/non-compliance) using a binary loss (e.g., BCE). *Why needed:* Specializes the general retrieval model to the nuanced, imbalanced legal compliance task. *Quick check: Is the final layer changed from a ranking objective to a classification head?*
*   **Positivity Weighting:** Applying a higher class weight to the minority positive class during training. *Why needed:* Mitigates the impact of extreme class imbalance (e.g., 0.6% positives) to prevent the model from being biased toward the majority negative class. *Quick check: Is the `pos_weight` parameter in the loss function set >1?*
*   **Threshold Tuning / Model Calibration:** Selecting decision thresholds on a validation set to meet specific operational constraints (coverage vs. error). *Why needed:* Transforms a continuous similarity score into discrete, risk-managed operational decisions. *Quick check: Are two separate thresholds (T1, T2) learned where similarity > T1 -> accept, < T2 -> reject?*
*   **Graded vs. Binary Relevance:** The distinction between multi-level relevance scores (ACORD) and binary labels (CUAD). *Why needed:* Highlights a potential task mismatch in the training and evaluation pipeline. *Quick check: Are the evaluation metrics (NDCG) designed for graded or binary relevance?*

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map:** Query Text -> RoBERTa Query Encoder -> Query Embedding   Document Text -> RoBERTa Document Encoder -> Document Embedding -> Cosine Similarity Score -> [Fuzzy Triage Head (Threshold T1, Threshold T2)] -> Triage Decision (Auto-Compliant / Auto-Noncompliant / Human-Review)

**Critical Path:** 1) Pre-training on ACORD with listwise loss establishes shared semantic space for ranking. 2) Fine-tuning on CUAD binary labels adapts the encoder to distinguish compliance/non-compliance. 3) Fuzzy head uses validation set to find (T1, T2) that maximize auto-coverage subject to ≤ error rate constraint on auto-decided items.

**Design Tradeoffs:** Determinism & Auditability vs. Nuanced Reasoning. The dual-encoder + rule-based thresholding offers full reproducibility and explicit control over risk (error rate cap), but likely sacrifices the complex, contextual reasoning an LLM might apply to borderline legal clauses. The system optimizes for operational throughput (high auto-coverage) rather than maximal F1 on a test set.

**Failure Signatures:** 1) A significant drop in auto-coverage on out-of-distribution legal text, indicating the learned similarity space or thresholds do not generalize. 2) A sharp increase in error rate on auto-decided examples when the constraint is violated in a real deployment, suggesting overfitting to the validation threshold tuning. 3) Poor performance on the "human-review" band, where the model is most uncertain, potentially indicating the fuzzy head's thresholds misrepresent model confidence.

**First Three Experiments:** 1) **Replication:** Train the exact pipeline (ACORD pre-train -> CUAD fine-tune) and report NDCG@5 and AUC/F1 on the provided test splits. 2) **Constraint Validation:** On the validation set, sweep the error rate cap (0%, 1%, 2%, 5%) and plot the resulting auto-coverage to confirm the Pareto frontier. 3) **Embedding Space Analysis:** t-SNE/UMAP visualize the query and document embeddings colored by CUAD binary label and ACORD grade to inspect class separation and potential overlap in the "human-review" band.

## Limitations
*   **Dataset Specificity:** Results are confined to ACORD (graded retrieval) and CUAD (binary compliance); generalizability to other legal domains, jurisdictions, or languages is unproven.
*   **Fuzzy Head Dependency:** Thresholds are tuned on a specific validation set; performance is expected to degrade on out-of-distribution legal text without careful recalibration.
*   **Task Mismatch:** The model is optimized on binary CUAD labels but evaluated on graded ACORD; the paper does not analyze how this discrepancy affects performance or the selection of triage thresholds.

## Confidence
*   **High:** The deterministic pipeline (dual-encoder, cosine similarity, rule-based thresholding) is clearly described and reproducible.
*   **Medium:** Reported metrics (ACORD NDCG@5, CUAD AUC/F1) lack confidence intervals or statistical testing across multiple runs.
*   **Low:** The ≤2% auto-decided error rate claim is a validation-set-tuned constraint; real-world error rates on unseen documents are highly uncertain.
*   **Low:** The qualitative contrast with "opaque LLMs" is not supported by a comparative empirical study or expert evaluation.

## Next Checks
1.  **Reproduce core metrics:** Implement and train the model from scratch using the described ACORD pre-training and CUAD fine-tuning pipeline; verify all reported test metrics with exact hyperparameters.
2.  **Out-of-distribution robustness test:** Apply the tuned fuzzy triage head (without adjustment) to a held-out legal compliance dataset from a different regulatory domain (e.g., GDPR contracts); measure the deviation in auto-coverage and the violation rate of the ≤2% error constraint.
3.  **Borderline case audit:** Manually review a random sample of examples forced into the "human-review" zone. Quantify the proportion where human experts agree with the model's uncertainty, identify clear model errors, or flag dataset noise or ambiguity.
