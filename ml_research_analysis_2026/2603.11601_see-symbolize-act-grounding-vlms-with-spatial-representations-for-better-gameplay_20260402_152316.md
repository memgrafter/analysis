# See, Symbolize, Act: Grounding VLMs with Spatial Representations for Better Gameplay

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.11601
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11601
- **Reference Count:** 33
- **Primary Result:** Symbolic grounding improves VLM gameplay performance only when object extraction is accurate; visual context remains essential, and performance is highly sensitive to minor coordinate noise.

## Executive Summary
Vision-Language Models (VLMs) struggle to translate visual input into precise, grounded actions in interactive environments. This study evaluates whether providing object-centric symbolic information (coordinates) alongside visual frames can improve decision-making. Testing four pipelines (frame-only, frame with self-extracted symbols, frame with ground-truth symbols, symbol-only) across three VLMs (Claude-4-Sonnet, GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-Pro) in Atari, VizDoom, and AI2-THOR reveals that symbolic benefits are conditional. Performance gains occur only when symbol extraction is highly accurate (as with Claude), while noisy extraction harms results. Crucially, visual frames are indispensable; perfect symbols alone cannot sustain performance. The central bottleneck is identified as perception quality—the VLM’s ability to extract reliable spatial representations from pixels.

## Method Summary
The study conducts a systematic comparison of four input pipelines for VLM-driven agents in interactive environments: (1) **Frame-only (F)**, (2) **Frame + Self-extracted Symbols (F+S-self)**, (3) **Frame + Ground-Truth Symbols (F+S-GT)**, and (4) **Symbols-only (S-GT)**. Self-extracted symbols are obtained via a zero-shot object detection prompt, while ground-truth symbols are sourced from game state (OCAtari RAM, engine metadata). The decision-maker VLM receives a single frame (and optional symbols) and outputs an action independently (stateless). Evaluation occurs in three games from Atari (Breakout, Pong, Space Invaders), one from VizDoom (defend_the_center), and one from AI2-THOR (kitchen task). Primary metrics are cumulative reward over 600 frames (300 for AI2-THOR) and symbol extraction quality (F1, IoU) against ground truth.

## Key Results
- Symbolic grounding (F+S-self) improves performance only when extraction F1 exceeds ~0.7 (Claude-4-Sonnet); lower extraction (F1 < 0.19) harms performance (GPT-4o, Gemini-2.5-Pro).
- Visual frames are essential: symbol-only pipelines (S-GT) collapse despite perfect ground-truth coordinates (e.g., Claude Breakout score: 0 vs. F+S-GT: 12).
- Minor coordinate noise (σ=0.1 relative to frame dimensions) causes significant reward drops (30–40%), demonstrating extreme sensitivity to spatial precision.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** Symbolic grounding improves VLM gameplay performance only when the VLM can extract accurate object-centric symbols from the visual frame.
- **Mechanism:** Accurate symbols provide precise spatial coordinates (object positions, boundaries) that compensate for the VLM's inherent limitations in fine-grained spatial reasoning from raw pixels alone. The VLM integrates these symbols into its decision loop, enabling more reliable trajectory prediction and action planning.
- **Core assumption:** The VLM's symbolic extraction module (its vision component) can achieve sufficiently high detection precision and localization (IoU) to be trustworthy for action selection.
- **Evidence anchors:** Claude-4-Sonnet (F1: 0.715) shows consistent improvement with self-extracted symbols; GPT-4o (F1: 0.124) and Gemini (F1: 0.189) degrade in complex scenes.
- **Break condition:** Symbol extraction F1 drops below a model-specific threshold (likely ~0.5–0.7), or scene complexity exceeds extraction capacity.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** Visual frame context is an essential scaffold; even perfect symbolic coordinates are insufficient for optimal performance when detached from the visual input.
- **Mechanism:** The visual frame provides holistic scene understanding, texture, object identity confirmation, and dynamic cues (e.g., ball spin, alien formation patterns) that pure coordinates lack. Symbols alone strip this context, forcing the VLM to reason from sparse data, increasing hallucination or misidentification of object roles.
- **Core assumption:** The VLM's policy network requires multimodal fusion of visual and symbolic streams; symbolic-only input cannot activate the same visual grounding pathways.
- **Evidence anchors:** Symbol-only (S-GT) scores collapse (e.g., Claude Breakout: 0 vs. F+S-GT: 12) despite perfect coordinates.
- **Break condition:** Environment is such that all critical decision variables are fully captured by coordinates and object identities.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** VLM-based agents are highly sensitive to small errors in extracted spatial coordinates; minor noise (σ=0.1) leads to significant reward degradation.
- **Mechanism:** Action selection in precision-control games depends on calculating distances, velocities, and intercept points. Noisy coordinates introduce systematic errors in these geometric computations, leading to mistimed or misdirected actions. The VLM lacks robust uncertainty handling, treating noisy symbols as ground truth.
- **Core assumption:** The VLM does not internally model or reason about the confidence/uncertainty of provided symbolic data.
- **Evidence anchors:** σ=0.1 (~16–20px error) reduces Breakout reward by ~30–40% from the no-noise baseline.
- **Break condition:** Game dynamics are highly stochastic or provide frequent random rewards, or VLM is explicitly prompted to handle noisy inputs.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept: Object-Centric State Representation**  
  *Why needed here:* The paper substitutes raw frames with detected objects and coordinates. Understanding this abstraction—scenes as lists of (label, bbox) tuples—is prerequisite to grasping why it helps or harms.  
  *Quick check question:* With ground-truth Breakout coordinates (paddle, ball, bricks), can you write a simple rule for LEFT/RIGHT without looking at the image? What is missing?

- **Concept: Detection Quality Metrics (F1, IoU)**  
  *Why needed here:* The conclusion hinges on how well VLMs extract symbols. F1 balances precision/recall for object presence; IoU measures spatial alignment. Without these, Table 3 and noise ablations are unintelligible.  
  *Quick check question:* If a VLM detects 8 objects but 5 are false positives and misses 3 true objects, what is the approximate F1? If predicted boxes overlap ground truth by 50% on average, what does IoU indicate?

- **Concept: Zero-Shot VLM Decision-Making in Interactive Loops**  
  *Why needed here:* The experimental paradigm treats each frame as an independent decision point with a frozen VLM, isolating reasoning from memory/planning. This explains why performance is low and why symbols help (or hurt) immediate perception.  
  *Quick check question:* In the frame-only pipeline, does the VLM see previous frames or actions? How might remembering the ball's previous position change its prediction?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:**  
  Raw Frame → Symbol Extractor (VLM sub-module) → Symbol JSON → (optional) Concatenate with Frame → Decision Maker (VLM full) → Action → Environment → (repeat). For S-GT: Ground-Truth Symbols → Decision Maker (no Frame).  
- **Critical path:** For F+S-self: Frame → Symbol Extractor → Symbol JSON → Concatenate with Frame → Decision Maker → Action → Environment step → repeat. Bottleneck is Symbol Extractor accuracy.
- **Design tradeoffs:**  
  - Resolution vs. Cost: Higher resolution (1280×720) improves detection (F1 ~0.68 vs 0.31) but increases API cost/latency 10–20×.  
  - Symbol Precision vs. Noise: Even σ=0.1 noise breaks performance; non-perfect extraction risks harming the policy.  
  - Visual Context vs. Symbol-Only: Symbol-only is cheaper but catastrophically underperforms; visual context is non-negotiable.
- **Failure signatures:**  
  - Low extraction F1 (<0.2) → F+S-self performance worse than F.  
  - S-GT score significantly lower than F+S-GT → confirms visual scaffolding necessity.  
  - Noise ablation: σ=0.1 causes >30% reward drop → fragile dependence on coordinate precision.
- **First 3 experiments:**  
  1. **Baseline extraction audit:** Run Symbol Extractor on 100 curated hard frames per game. Compute F1 and IoU against OCAtari/engine GT. Anchor: If average F1 < 0.5, expect F+S-self to underperform F.  
  2. **Pipeline comparison:** Execute all four pipelines (F, F+S-self, F+S-GT, S-GT) for 600 frames × 2 seeds. Normalize each model's scores to its F+S-GT. Anchor: If F+S-self ≈ F+S-GT (>80% of GT), extraction is adequate; if F+S-self < F, extraction is harmful.  
  3. **Coordinate noise injection:** Take F+S-GT pipeline, add Gaussian noise (σ=0.1, 0.2, 0.3) to GT coordinates before feeding to Decision Maker. Anchor: If reward at σ=0.1 drops >25% from σ=0.0, task is noise-sensitive; robust extraction is mandatory.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Open Question 1:** What specific architectural or training modifications can significantly improve the spatial reasoning and object detection accuracy of VLMs like GPT-4o and Gemini-2.5-Pro to match Claude-4-Sonnet's symbol extraction quality?  
  *Basis in paper:* The paper identifies model-specific differences (F1: Claude 0.715 vs. GPT-4o 0.124 vs. Gemini 0.189) and concludes perception is the central bottleneck, suggesting future work on "more robust symbol-extraction methods, such as hybrid detectors or lightweight fine-tuning."  
  *Why unresolved:* The bottleneck is demonstrated but not investigated; the cause of the disparity is not analyzed.  
  *What evidence would resolve it:* A systematic study comparing architectures, training data, or prompting techniques that close the accuracy gap, achieving F1 >0.6 across all VLMs and showing corresponding gameplay improvement.

- **Open Question 2:** To what extent does the lack of temporal context (stateless decision-making) limit the effectiveness of symbolic grounding, and can incorporating frame history or action memory mitigate the sensitivity to detection noise?  
  *Basis in paper:* Limitations explicitly list "Stateless Decision-Making": the setup treats each frame independently with no access to previous frames or action history.  
  *Why unresolved:* The experiments isolate per-frame reasoning, so interaction between memory and noisy symbols is unknown.  
  *What evidence would resolve it:* Extend F+S-self to include N previous frames/symbols/actions. Compare performance on noisy symbol pipelines to see if temporal integration reduces the drop at a given noise level σ.

- **Open Question 3:** How do the findings on symbolic grounding transfer to environments with substantially higher visual complexity, partial observability, or continuous control spaces beyond the discrete action spaces of Atari, VizDoom, and AI2-THOR?  
  *Basis in paper:* Limitations note "Limited Environment Diversity" and call for exploration in "additional environments with different visual and control characteristics."  
  *Why unresolved:* Tested environments are relatively constrained (discrete actions, well-defined objects). Generalizability to real-world robotics or open-world games is not established.  
  *What evidence would resolve it:* Replicate the core pipeline study (F, F+S-self, F+S-GT, S-GT) in 2–3 new domains with fundamentally different challenges (e.g., physics-based manipulation, open-ended sandbox games). The key metric: does the "symbols only help if accurate" pattern hold?

- **Open Question 4:** Can the visual context in the Frame+Symbols pipeline be optimally utilized by VLMs, or does the fixed prompt structure underutilize the synergistic potential between visual and symbolic streams?  
  *Basis in paper:* The paper uses a "generic prompt template" and notes symbol-only underperformance proves "visual frames act as essential scaffolding," but prompt design is not varied, and model fusion is a black box.  
  *Why unresolved:* Unclear if limitations are purely due to extraction accuracy or also suboptimal integration. The prompt may not effectively guide cross-verification or weighting.  
  *What evidence would resolve it:* Ablate prompt variations (e.g., instruct model to explicitly verify symbols against image, assign confidence weights). Measure performance changes for models with different extraction qualities. Improvement for lower-accuracy models under a better prompt would indicate underutilization.

- **Open Question 5:** What is the theoretical lower bound on the detection accuracy (F1/IoU) required for symbolic grounding to provide a net benefit over frame-only reasoning across different task complexities?  
  *Basis in paper:* Noise ablation shows degradation begins at very low noise (σ=0.1, ~16-20px). The study uses synthetic Gaussian noise on GT coordinates; the relationship between model-specific detection errors (false positives/negatives, not just jitter) and task performance is correlational, not a precise threshold.  
  *Why unresolved:* The required accuracy likely depends on task spatial sensitivity and object density; no formula or model exists.  
  *What evidence would resolve it:* Controlled experiment systematically varying type of detection error (false positives, false negatives, localization error) and game complexity (object count, speed). Outcome: a model or formula predicting minimum F1/IoU needed for positive return on symbolic grounding given game parameters.

## Limitations
- Undisclosed environment configurations (VizDoom wad, AI2-THOR scene, Atari ROM version) and VLM inference parameters may impede exact replication.
- Stateless, per-frame decision loop simplifies evaluation but does not capture memory-dependent reasoning, limiting generalizability.
- The claim that perception is the central bottleneck rests on correlation between extraction metrics and performance; alternative confounders are not fully ruled out.

## Confidence
- Symbolic grounding helps only with accurate extraction: **High** – cross-model differences and noise ablations consistently support.
- Visual context is essential: **High** – symbol-only pipelines collapse even with perfect ground-truth coordinates.
- High sensitivity to coordinate noise: **High** – σ=0.1 noise causes >30% reward degradation across tasks.
- Perception is the central bottleneck: **Medium** – evidence strong but constrained to the stateless protocol.

## Next Checks
1. **Cross-environment generalization:** Test the four pipelines on diverse interactive domains (e.g., robotic manipulation, 3D navigation) to verify if findings extend beyond Atari, VizDoom, and AI2-THOR.
2. **Extraction accuracy threshold study:** Systematically vary object detection quality (controlled bbox errors) to identify the minimum F1/IoU required for net benefit over frame-only.
3. **Uncertainty-aware prompting:** Provide decision-makers with confidence scores from the extractor; evaluate whether explicit uncertainty handling mitigates the observed noise fragility.
