# MobileFetalCLIP: Selective Repulsive Knowledge Distillation for Mobile Fetal Ultrasound Analysis

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2603.05421
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05421
- Reference count: 39
- Primary result: MobileFetalCLIP (11.4M) surpasses its 304M-parameter FetalCLIP teacher on zero-shot HC18 validity (88.6% vs. 83.5%) and brain sub-plane F1 (0.784 vs. 0.702), while running at 1.6 ms on an iPhone 16 Pro and retaining 97–98% of teacher linear probing performance.

## Executive Summary
Standard knowledge distillation fails when transferring a large fetal ultrasound foundation model (FetalCLIP, 304M parameters) to a compact mobile architecture (MobileFetalCLIP, 11.4M) due to an extreme ~26× capacity gap. The student wastes capacity mimicking teacher-specific architectural artifacts rather than learning essential features. Selective Repulsive Knowledge Distillation solves this by decomposing contrastive distillation loss into diagonal (preserving matched-pair alignment) and off-diagonal (non-target) components. The off-diagonal weight `β(t)` linearly decays into negative values, actively repelling the student from the teacher’s inter-class confusion patterns. This forces the compact hybrid convolutional-attention student to discover features native to its design. MobileFetalCLIP thus outperforms its teacher on zero-shot fetal biometry tasks while achieving real-time mobile inference.

## Method Summary
The method distills FetalCLIP (ViT-L/14 image encoder plus text encoder) into MobileFetalCLIP (FastViT image encoder plus lightweight text transformer). Training minimizes a combined loss: the standard CLIP contrastive loss (`L_CLIP`) plus a decoupled knowledge distillation loss. The KD loss splits the row-wise cross-entropy between teacher and student similarity matrices into a diagonal term (`L_diag`, for matched image-text pairs) and an off-diagonal term (`L_off-diag`, for non-target pairs). The diagonal term is fixed at weight 1.0, while the off-diagonal weight `β(t)` follows a linear schedule starting at `β0=2` and decaying to a negative ratio `r=-0.8` over 20 epochs. Once `β(t)` becomes negative, the gradient on off-diagonal entries inverts, actively repelling the student from the teacher’s inter-class confusion structure after an initial attractive phase. Key hyperparameters: teacher temperature `τ_KD=5.0`, learning rate `10⁻⁵`, batch size 1024, coupled augmentation.

## Key Results
- Zero-shot HC18 validity: 88.6% (vs. teacher 83.5%)
- Brain sub-plane macro-F1: 0.784 (vs. teacher 0.702)
- On-device latency: 1.6 ms on iPhone 16 Pro; retains 97–98% of teacher linear probing performance.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: Decoupled Contrastive Distillation
**Claim:** Decomposing the contrastive KD loss into diagonal and off-diagonal components allows selective manipulation of the teacher’s similarity structure.  
**Mechanism:** `L_diag` preserves alignment of matched pairs, while `L_off-diag` is modulated by `β(t)` that decays negative, repelling the student from the teacher’s inter-class confusions. This decoupling adapts Decoupled KD from classification to the N×N contrastive setting.  
**Core assumption:** The teacher’s off-diagonal patterns encode inter-class confusions partly arising from ViT-L’s global self-attention artifacts, not purely visual ambiguity.  
**Evidence anchors:** Loss decomposition equations (Sec 3.4); diagonal protection ablation shows +4.2 pp HC18 loss when removed (Table 3).  
**Break condition:** Removing diagonal protection (coupled mode) significantly degrades performance, confirming preserved matched-pair alignment is critical.

### Mechanism 2: Negative Off-Diagonal Weighting
**Claim:** Setting `β(t) < 0` inverts the off-diagonal gradient, actively repelling the student’s non-target similarity away from the teacher’s.  
**Mechanism:** After an initial attractive phase (`β(t)>0`), `β(t)` linearly crosses zero (e.g., epoch ~11 for `r=-0.8`), switching to a repulsive phase that maximizes KL divergence on off-diagonal entries. This directed repulsion is more effective than undirected entropy maximization.  
**Core assumption:** The teacher’s inter-class confusion geometry provides a directional signal for building sharper class boundaries; reversing it guides the student better than uniform entropy penalties.  
**Evidence anchors:** Three-phase training description (Sec 3.3); confidence penalty (undirected) performs no better than no KD, while repulsive KD strongly outperforms (Sec 4.4).  
**Break condition:** Excessive repulsion (`r ≤ -1.6`) or amplification (`β0 ≥ 4`) collapses performance (Table 3), indicating the attractive phase is necessary to bootstrap learning.

### Mechanism 3: Forcing Native Feature Discovery
**Claim:** Selective repulsion compels the compact student to discover features suited to its hybrid architecture rather than mimic the teacher’s global attention patterns.  
**Mechanism:** By being repelled from the teacher’s off-diagonal structure while constrained on diagonals, the student must find alternative, compact discriminative cues (e.g., local textures, multi-scale features) compatible with FastViT’s inductive biases. The massive capacity gap creates “architectural incommensurability” that makes direct mimicry wasteful.  
**Core assumption:** The student cannot faithfully replicate the teacher’s distributed representations due to limited capacity; guiding it away from those patterns yields more efficient representations.  
**Evidence anchors:** Embedding analysis shows Selective Repulsive KD achieves highest effective dimensionality (`d_eff=10.0`) and uniformity, unlike coupled repulsion which concentrates features (`d_eff=6.4`) (Sec 4.6). Adding feature mimicry (`λ_feat=2000`) degrades HC18 from 79.4% to 75.9% (Table 3).  
**Break condition:** Feature alignment (distilling intermediate embeddings) harms performance, confirming pointwise imitation is counterproductive under large capacity gaps.

## Foundational Learning
- **Contrastive Vision-Language Pretraining (CLIP):** The distillation aligns student and teacher similarity logit matrices from contrastive pretraining. Understanding the N×N symmetric structure and temperature `τ` is fundamental.  
  *Quick check:* In the CLIP loss, diagonal entries represent matched image-text pairs; off-diagonals serve as negatives to enforce discrimination.
- **Knowledge Distillation (KD) with Logits:** The method modifies standard logit-based KD where the student mimics softened teacher logits via cross-entropy.  
  *Quick check:* Applying temperature `τ_KD` only to the teacher amplifies “dark knowledge” by softening probability distributions, revealing inter-class relationships.
- **Architectural Trade-offs: ViT vs. FastViT:** The core problem stems from ViT-L’s global self-attention versus FastViT’s convolutional-attention hybrid. The mechanism’s rationale depends on this incommensurability.  
  *Quick check:* A hybrid model may struggle to replicate ViT-L’s distributed representations because its local inductive biases and limited capacity favor more compact, spatially aware features.

## Architecture Onboarding
- Component map: Frozen Teacher (FetalCLIP ViT-L/14) and Student (MobileFetalCLIP FastViT) produce N×N similarity logit matrices S_T and S_S → KD Module decomposes row-wise cross-entropy H(p_T, q_S) into L_diag (j=i) and L_off-diag (j≠i) → Schedule Controller linearly adjusts β(t) from β0 to r → Total loss L = L_CLIP + L_diag + β(t)*L_off-diag → Backpropagation updates student parameters.
- Critical path: 1) Forward passes through teacher and student to compute S_T, S_S. 2) Compute L_CLIP. 3) Compute p_T = softmax(S_T/τ_KD), q_S = softmax(S_S). 4) For each row i: L_diag += -p_T[i,i] * log(q_S[i,i]); L_off-diag += sum_{j≠i} -p_T[i,j] * log(q_S[i,j]). 5) Apply current β(t) to L_off-diag. 6) Form total loss L = L_CLIP + L_diag + β(t)*L_off-diag. 7) Backpropagate L. 8) Update β(t) per schedule. 9) Monitor zero-shot validation metrics (HC18, F1-3Brain) after each epoch.
- Design tradeoffs: β0 (initial off-diagonal weight, >1 for amplification but too high causes collapse), r (minimum ratio, <0 needed for repulsion but not too negative), τ_KD (elevated to amplify repulsive signal), diagonal protection (critical, never decay L_diag weight), feature KD (avoid, adding it harms).
- Failure signatures: Early collapse after repulsion starts → |r| too large or β0 too high. No late surge (metrics plateau below teacher) → r ≥ 0 or β0 too low. Degraded plane classification → diagonal weakening (check L_diag weight). Feature collapse (low d_eff) → over-repulsion or coupled mode.
- First 3 experiments: 1) Static Logit KD Baseline: set β(t)=1.0 constant, establishes degradation ceiling (HC18 ~79.4%). 2) Coupled Repulsive KD: apply same schedule to full L_KD, shows repulsion helps but suboptimal without diagonal protection (HC18 84.4% > teacher). 3) Selective Repulsive KD Sweep: fix r=-0.8, sweep β0 to find optimal (β0=2); then fix β0=2, sweep r to confirm r=-0.8 best.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. **Generalization to other medical imaging domains:** Future work notes extension to echocardiography and general radiology; needs validation on diverse datasets (e.g., echocardiography image-text pairs, MIMIC-CXR).
2. **Robustness in prospective clinical deployment:** Current evaluation uses retrospective data; real-world factors (device variability, operator skill) may degrade performance. Requires multi-center trials with handheld POCUS devices.
3. **Teacher confusion artifact vs. semantic ambiguity:** The method assumes teacher confusions are architectural, but if they reflect true visual similarity, repulsion may force unnatural boundaries. Needs analysis of ambiguity levels or cross-architecture consensus to isolate artifact-driven confusions.

## Limitations
- Hyperparameters (optimizer, scheduler, precision) and data pipeline (preprocessing, augmentation) details are not fully specified.
- Core assumption that teacher’s off-diagonal confusion is an architectural artifact is untested and could conflate with genuine semantic ambiguity.
- Generalizability beyond fetal ultrasound to other domains and architectures remains unknown.

## Confidence
- Performance superiority: High (clear gains in HC18 and brain F1 reported)
- Selective repulsion mechanism: Medium (ablations support, but sensitive to β0/r and theoretical basis weak)
- Native feature discovery: Medium (embedding analysis suggests but not conclusive)
- Artifact hypothesis: Low (no direct evidence separating architectural from semantic confusion)

## Next Checks
1. Loss audit: Log β(t), L_diag, and L_off-diag during training; verify off-diagonal gradient sign flips when β<0.
2. Teacher architecture ablation: Replace ViT-L teacher with a CNN (ResNet-101) pretrained on same data; reapply selective KD and compare performance.
3. Capacity gap scaling: Distill from teachers of multiple sizes (ViT-B/16, ViT-L/14) to fixed FastViT student; sweep β0/r; measure if relative gain increases with gap.
