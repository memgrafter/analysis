# Thinking with Constructions: A Benchmark and Policy Optimization for Visual-Text Interleaved Geometric Reasoning

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.18662
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18662
- **Reference count:** 25
- **Primary result:** A2PO achieves a 3.51% accuracy gain over strong baselines like GRPO on GeoAux-Bench, with visual re-prompting alone contributing a +1.80% improvement.

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses a key limitation of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) in geometric reasoning: models passively rely on static diagrams without strategically creating auxiliary visual constructions. The authors introduce GeoAux-Bench, a benchmark of 4,334 problems that explicitly pairs textual construction steps with corresponding visual updates. Building on the insight that interleaved visual-textual reasoning reduces perplexity, they propose Action Applicability Policy Optimization (A2PO), a reinforcement learning method that teaches models when and how to construct effective visual aids. A2PO uses Tri-Partition Sampling and Adaptive Reward Shaping to incentivize strategic construction timing and quality, employing visual re-prompting to dynamically inject auxiliary diagrams. The method significantly outperforms baselines, demonstrating that rewarding construction timing and quality leads to more effective auxiliary line usage and lower reasoning perplexity.

## Method Summary
GeoAux-Bench is constructed from existing geometry datasets (Geomverse, Geometry3k) and includes 4,334 problems with 8,470 diagrams, each annotated with ground-truth auxiliary construction text (`T_aux`) and the corresponding updated diagram (`I_aux`). The core method, A2PO, extends Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO). It begins with supervised fine-tuning (SFT) on a mixed prompt dataset to establish basic controllability over generating `<aux>` commands. During A2PO training, for each query, three types of rollouts are generated: mandatory (`O⁺`), prohibited (`O⁻`), and natural (`O`). A timing reward (`r_time`) is computed from the accuracy gap between `O⁺` and `O⁻` rollouts (`Δ`), incentivizing constructions only when beneficial. A quality reward (`r_qual`) uses reasoning perplexity (`PPL`) to reward entropy-reducing constructions. When a valid `T_aux` is generated (verified by an external model), visual re-prompting injects the ground-truth `I_aux` to enable interleaved reasoning. The composite reward combines accuracy, format, timing, and quality terms.

## Key Results
- A2PO achieves a 3.51% absolute accuracy gain over GRPO on GeoAux-Bench.
- Visual re-prompting alone provides the largest single-component improvement (+1.80%).
- A2PO maintains lower reasoning perplexity compared to other RL methods, confirming constructions act as entropy reducers.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Entropy Reduction via Valid Constructions
- **Claim:** Valid auxiliary constructions reduce reasoning perplexity (PPL), acting as an entropy reducer that simplifies the solution path.
- **Mechanism:** A well-placed construction clarifies geometric relationships, narrowing the space of plausible next steps, which manifests as lower predictive uncertainty (lower PPL) in the subsequent reasoning chain.
- **Core assumption:** There is a strong positive correlation between reduced reasoning PPL and higher final accuracy for valid auxiliary construction steps.
- **Evidence anchors:** 
  - Pilot study shows interleaved settings (T_aux + I_aux) achieve best accuracy and competitive PPL.
  - Table 1 shows across settings, lower PPL correlates with higher accuracy.
  - A2PO maintains PPL near SFT while improving accuracy, unlike other RL methods where PPL rises.
- **Break condition:** If future experiments show no significant PPL difference between successful and failed construction steps across diverse problem sets, or if interventions that lower PPL do not improve accuracy.

### Mechanism 2: Timing Reward via Counterfactual Sampling
- **Claim:** Tri-Partition Sampling with mandatory (O⁺) and prohibited (O⁻) rollouts provides a counterfactual baseline to isolate the marginal utility of *when* to construct.
- **Mechanism:** By comparing the performance (advantage) of trajectories that *must* include a construction against those that *cannot*, the model learns a timing reward (`r_time`) that incentivizes constructions only in scenarios where they provide a net benefit (`Δ > τ`).
- **Core assumption:** The performance gap between forced and forbidden construction rollouts (`Δ`) meaningfully quantifies the strategic necessity of an auxiliary line for a given problem state.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - Tri-partition sampling explicitly creates O⁺, O⁻, and O subsets.
  - `r_time` is defined based on `Δ`, the accuracy gap between `E_O⁺[racc]` and `E_O⁻[racc]`.
  - Ablation shows removing the Timing Reward (TR) hurts performance (40.18% vs 42.97%).
- **Break condition:** If the `Δ` metric shows high variance or poor correlation with human judgments of construction necessity, or if `r_time` does not yield a statistically significant improvement over a model that always or never constructs.

### Mechanism 3: Visual Re-prompting for Interleaved Feedback
- **Claim:** Visual Re-prompting, by dynamically injecting the ground-truth auxiliary diagram upon a semantically correct construction intent, enables the interleaved reasoning loop essential for geometric "aha moments."
- **Mechanism:** The Aux Verifier triggers a context update (re-prompt) only when the textual construction command is equivalent to the ground-truth `T_aux`. This provides high-fidelity visual feedback (`I_aux`) exactly when the model has identified a valid manipulation, simulating the cognitive process of "seeing" the result of one's own construction.
- **Core assumption:** The primary bottleneck is not the model's ability to *propose* a construction textually, but its inability to *perceive* the geometric consequences of that construction within its static visual context. Injecting the correct diagram bridges this gap.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - Aux Verifier triggers re-prompting upon correct `T_aux` detection.
  - Ablation shows Visual Re-prompting (Vis) provides the largest single gain (+1.80%), from 41.17% to 42.97%.
  - Pilot study's "Interleaved Settings" (T_aux + I_aux) outperformed single-modality settings.
- **Break condition:** If native generative models (without retrieval) achieve comparable interleaved performance without re-prompting, or if re-prompting with *incorrect* diagrams (even if the text was correct) does not harm accuracy.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept: Chain-of-Thought (CoT) & Visual Chain-of-Thought (VCoT)**
  - **Why needed:** The framework extends standard textual CoT to a multimodal, interleaved format. Understanding the limitation of static, single-pass reasoning is prerequisite to grasping the need for dynamic construction.
  - **Quick check:** What is the key limitation of standard CoT in geometry that VCoT aims to solve, and why can't text-only descriptions fully substitute for diagrams?
- **Concept: Reinforcement Learning from Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO)**
  - **Why needed:** A2PO builds directly on GRPO as its optimization backbone. Understanding GRPO's group-normalized advantage estimation is necessary to see how A2PO's tri-partition sampling modifies it.
  - **Quick check:** How does GRPO estimate the baseline for advantage calculation without a separate value function, and what does this make it sensitive to?
- **Concept: Auxiliary Constructions in Geometry**
  - **Why needed:** The core task is learning *which* constructions to make and *when*. This requires domain knowledge about common geometric aids and their strategic purposes.
  - **Quick check:** Besides "drawing an auxiliary line," what makes a construction *strategically useful* versus merely valid? What geometric property might it be trying to reveal?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:**
  Policy Model (M) -> Tri-Partition Sampler -> [O⁺, O⁻, O] Rollouts -> Aux Verifier -> (if valid) Visual Re-prompting Module (injects I_aux) -> Full Reasoning Chain -> Answer Judge -> Adaptive Reward Calculator (composes R(o) with racc, rfmt, rtime, rqual) -> GRPO Loss + KL Penalty -> Update M. Reference Model (π_ref) provides KL baseline.
- **Critical path:**
  1. SFT Warm-up: Train policy on mixed (Standard + Prohibited) prompts to establish basic controllability over `<aux>` generation.
  2. A2PO Training Loop:
     a. For batch of queries, generate one rollout per subset (O⁺, O⁻, O) using the Sampler.
     b. For O rollouts: run generation. If `<aux>` generated, call Aux Verifier. If valid, trigger Visual Re-prompting (inject I_aux).
     c. For all O rollouts: call Answer Judge to get racc, rfmt. Compute PPL(o) for full reasoning chain.
     d. Gather group statistics: mean/std rewards for GRPO advantage; compute Δ = E_O⁺[racc] - E_O⁻[racc]; compute ¯P = E_O⁺[PPL].
     e. Calculate r_time and r_qual for each O rollout, compose R(o).
     f. Compute GRPO surrogate loss with advantages from normalized R(o), add KL penalty, update policy.
- **Design tradeoffs:**
  - Visual Re-prompting uses ground-truth I_aux for reliable feedback but breaks the generative loop and requires perfect diagram availability.
  - Reward component weights (w_time=0.15, w_qual=0.15) are heuristic and may not generalize across model scales or problem subsets.
  - Aux Verifier reliance introduces a point of failure if its semantic matching is too strict or lenient.
- **Failure signatures:**
  1. Visual Hallucination Propagation: Native diagram generation with structural errors (curved lines, wrong points) invalidates subsequent reasoning.
  2. Analytic Shortcut Overuse: Model overuses coordinate systems even for simple pure-geometry problems, leading to inefficient or incorrect algebra.
  3. Redundant Construction Reward Hacking: Without r_qual, model may insert valid but unnecessary constructions to trigger r_time bonus, increasing solution length without benefit.
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1. Pilot Modality Ablation (Table 1): Validate the irreplaceable complementarity of T_aux and I_aux, and the PPL-accuracy correlation.
  2. GeoAux-Bench Baseline (Table 2): Quantify difficulty gap across MLLMs and identify best base model for RL (e.g., Qwen2.5-VL-7B).
  3. A2PO vs. GRPO Ablation (Table 4): Systematically remove components (LR, TR, QR, Vis) to confirm each contribution, especially the +1.80% gain from Visual Re-prompting.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Open Question 1:** How can MLLMs generate geometrically accurate auxiliary diagrams natively? The current reliance on retrieval-based injection limits autonomy; future work needs a native diagram generator.
- **Open Question 2:** Why do MLLMs overuse coordinate systems ("analytic shortcut") and how to mitigate? The paper observes a strong preference for analytic geometry even on Junior-level tasks, but the root cause is unknown.
- **Open Question 3:** Is reasoning perplexity a reliable proxy for construction quality? A2PO's reward uses a PPL threshold assuming "entropy reduction," but correlation may not indicate true utility; low PPL could coincide with errors.
- **Open Question 4:** Can construction timing be learned without costly counterfactual rollouts? A2PO's Tri-Partition Sampling is sample-inefficient; a more efficient method (e.g., learned critic) is needed.

## Limitations
- Reliance on an external verifier and ground-truth diagrams for visual re-prompting breaks the generative loop and requires perfect diagram availability.
- Training and evaluation are limited to derived datasets (Geomverse, Geometry3k); generalization to novel geometry types or real-world diagrams is unproven.
- Heuristic reward component weights (w_time=0.15, w_qual=0.15) are tuned on a specific validation set; their stability across model scales or problem subsets is unknown.

## Confidence
- **Mechanism 1 (PPL as Entropy Reducer):** High – Strong internal evidence (Tables 1, 5) shows correlation between valid constructions, lower PPL, and higher accuracy; ablations confirm quality reward necessity.
- **Mechanism 2 (Tri-Partition Timing Reward):** Medium – Supported by ablation and methodological design, but robustness of `Δ` as a universal "strategic necessity" signal across diverse problem states requires external validation.
- **Mechanism 3 (Visual Re-prompting):** High – Ablation shows the largest single-gain component; pilot studies establish interleaved modality complementarity.

## Next Checks
1. **Stress-test the Aux Verifier:** Evaluate the semantic matching accuracy of the verifier (Qwen3-30B) on a held-out set of `T_aux` variations (paraphrases, minor errors). High error rates would undermine the re-prompting trigger’s reliability.
2. **Evaluate in a Generative Diagram Setting:** Retrain A2PO without access to ground-truth `I_aux`. Instead, condition the model to reason over its *own generated* diagrams (using a diagram generator). This tests policy transfer beyond the retrieval-based shortcut.
3. **Isolate Timing Reward Generalization:** Run cross-validation where `Δ` is computed only on a subset of problem types (e.g., circle theorems) and applied to train on another (e.g., triangle congruency). A significant drop would indicate `Δ` is not a transferable strategy signal.
