# Hán Dān Xué Bù (Mimicry) or Qīng Chū Yú Lán (Mastery)? A Cognitive Perspective on Reasoning Distillation in Large Language Models
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv:** 2601.05019  
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.05019  
- **References:** 3  
- **Core result:** SFT reasoning distillation collapses cognitive alignment (teacher *r*=0.64 → student *r*=0.34) and inflates token output by ≈2.44×, indicating superficial mimicry, not mastery.

## Executive Summary
Supervised fine‑tuning of chain‑of‑thought traces fails to preserve a teacher model’s alignment with human processing costs. Evaluating 14 models (DeepSeek‑R1 teacher, six distilled students, six base instruct models) on six reasoning tasks, students show a severe collapse (*r*=0.34) below even base models (*r*=0.53). They drift into a “Spurious Valley” far from teacher and human patterns and obey a linear inflation law multiplying base inefficiency by ≈2.44. Results support the Hán Dān Xué Bù hypothesis: distillation yields mimicry without dynamic resource allocation.

## Method Summary
Models: DeepSeek‑R1 teacher, six Qwen/Llama students, six instruct‑tuned bases. Tasks: arithmetic, syllogism, Logic‑ALE, relational, intuitive reasoning. For each item, generate CoT traces (teacher with `<think>`; base with “Let’s think step by step”). Count reasoning tokens; obtain human mean reaction times. Metrics: functional alignment (Pearson *r* between log tokens and log RT), representational similarity analysis (RSA) of task fingerprints, strategy divergence (DKL), inverse efficiency (IE), and linear inflation law (student IE ≈ *N* · base IE).

## Key Results
- Teacher alignment strong (*r*=0.64); students collapse (*r*=0.34), worse than bases (*r*=0.53) → negative transfer.  
- RSA shows students occupy an isolated “Spurious Valley”; surface verbosity similarity (DKL) low despite mimicry.  
- Linear inflation law holds: *N*≈2.44 (*p*<0.01), uniformly inflating token counts.  
- Conclusion: SFT CoT distillation produces Hán Dān Xué Bù (mimicry), not Qīng Chū Yú Lán (mastery).

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
SFT forces students to copy teacher tokens but cannot convey the credit‑assignment mechanism linking verbosity to difficulty. Consequently: (1) token length decouples from cognitive demand → alignment collapse; (2) catastrophic interference overwrites efficient base heuristics → negative transfer; (3) students obey linear inflation (≈2.44× base inefficiency). The outcome is superficial mimicry without mastering human‑like dynamic resource allocation.

## Foundational Learning
- **Cognitive Alignment** – metric linking token length to human RT; *quick check:* could a high *r* arise from non‑human strategies?  
- **CoT Distillation via SFT** – core SFT intervention; *quick check:* why does equal token weighting prevent learning when to think longer?  
- **Representational Similarity Analysis** – compares model “difficulty fingerprints” via correlation; *quick check:* what does high RSA between models imply?

## Architecture Onboarding
Components: Model Mgr → Inference → Cost Ext → Human Int → Align Calc → RSA Builder → Metrics → Reg.  
Critical path: Inference → Cost extraction → *r*_align → RSA → Regression.  
Design tradeoffs: deterministic vs stochastic; delimiter reliance; log‑linearity assumption.  
Failure signatures: (a) *r*_align<0.3 with high accuracy; (b) DKL<2.5 with low human alignment; (c) *N* deviating from ≈2.44; (d) isolated RSA “Spurious Valley”; (e) negative transfer (student *r* < base *r*).  
First experiments: (1) replicate alignment collapse (paired *t*‑test), (2) RSA waterfall (heatmap & 2‑D projection), (3) inflation law (constrained regression).

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. Why does SFT fail to transmit the teacher’s credit‑assignment mechanism? Could token‑level rewards or RL‑augmented distillation recover functional alignment?  
2. What alternative paradigms (e.g., cognitive foraging, active exploration) achieve true mastery and break the Linear Inflation Law?  
3. Is the Linear Inflation Law universal across teacher‑student pairs and CoT sources, or specific to DeepSeek‑R1→Qwen/Llama? Does *N* vary with model capacity or data distribution?

## Limitations
- Missing item‑level human RTs and ground truth; reconstruction may diverge from original.  
- Model checkpoints and APIs may have drifted, altering token counts and accuracy.  
- Underspecified base‑model prompts and task adaptations introduce confounds.

## Confidence
- Functional Alignment Collapse / Negative Transfer: **Medium**  
- Spurious Valley (RSA result): **Medium‑Low**  
- Linear Inflation Law: **Medium**
