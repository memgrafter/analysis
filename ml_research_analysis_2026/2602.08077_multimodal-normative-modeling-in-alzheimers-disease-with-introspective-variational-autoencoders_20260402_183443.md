# Multimodal normative modeling in Alzheimers Disease with introspective variational autoencoders

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.08077
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08077
- **Reference Count:** 40
- **Primary Result:** mmSIVAE with MOPOE achieves superior reconstruction accuracy on healthy controls and higher outlier detection performance in the Alzheimer’s spectrum using MRI and amyloid PET.

## Executive Summary
Normative modeling in Alzheimer’s disease aims to identify anomalies relative to a healthy reference but is limited by imperfect reference learning and weak multimodal fusion. This work introduces mmSIVAE, combining a soft-introspective variational autoencoder with Mixture-of-Product-of-Experts (MOPOE) posterior aggregation. Trained on ADNI data (MRI volumes and amyloid PET), mmSIVAE yields more accurate reconstructions for held-out healthy controls than baseline VAEs. When applied to an AD spectrum cohort, it produces higher Mahalanobis deviation likelihood ratios (up to 10.29 vs. 8.56 for MoE) and greater feature-space separation (Earth Mover’s Distance = 34.043). Latent deviation maps align with known AD pathology, improving clinical interpretability.

## Method Summary
The mmSIVAE architecture enhances the standard VAE with two innovations. First, a soft-introspective loss penalizes divergence between the learned prior and aggregate posterior, ensuring the normative distribution stays faithful to the healthy data. Second, MOPOE aggregates multimodal posteriors via a weighted mixture of products of experts from each modality, capturing both agreement and uncertainty. The model is trained solely on healthy controls to define a normative latent space. For any subject, deviation is quantified by the Mahalanobis distance of their latent representation to the normative mean, optionally visualized as spatial deviation maps.

## Key Results
- mmSIVAE with MOPOE outperforms baseline VAEs and multimodal variants (e.g., MoE, PoE) in reconstruction accuracy on held-out healthy controls.
- Outlier detection in the AD spectrum yields the highest Mahalanobis deviation likelihood ratios (up to 10.29 vs. 8.56 for MoE) and largest Earth Mover’s Distance (34.043).
- Latent deviation maps reveal region-level patterns consistent with established Alzheimer’s disease pathology.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The soft-introspective component maintains an accurate healthy reference by aligning the prior with the aggregate posterior. MOPOE robustly combines multimodal information: each modality acts as an expert, their product highlights regions of high agreement, and the mixture gracefully handles modality-specific uncertainty. This yields a precise joint posterior, improving reconstruction fidelity and sensitivity of deviation measures.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map:**  
Multimodal inputs (MRI, PET) → Modality-specific encoders (E_MRI, E_PET) → Posterior parameters (μ_k, σ_k) → MOPOE (product of experts + mixture) → Joint latent distribution q(z) → Sample z → Shared decoder → Reconstructions (MRI', PET').

**Critical Path:**  
Data flows through modality encoders to produce expert distributions. MOPOE aggregates these into a joint posterior from which z is sampled. The decoder reconstructs both modalities. The loss combines reconstruction error, KL divergence to the prior, and a soft-introspective penalty aligning the prior with the aggregate posterior.

**Design Tradeoffs:**  
- MOPOE improves multimodal fusion but adds computational cost and mixture weight hyperparameters.  
- Soft-introspection enhances fidelity but requires careful tuning of the penalty coefficient to avoid underfitting.

**Failure Signatures:**  
- Poor reconstruction on healthy controls indicates ineffective normative learning.  
- High variance in latent deviation scores across random seeds suggests unstable posterior aggregation.  
- Noisy or nonspecific deviation maps may reflect overfitting or mis‑calibrated introspection weight.

## Limitations
- Training and evaluation are restricted to ADNI; external validation on other cohorts is needed.
- Only MRI and amyloid PET are used; exclusion of tau, CSF, and genetic biomarkers limits clinical comprehensiveness.
- The soft-introspective VAE introduces extra hyperparameters whose robustness (e.g., to seeds and weight choices) is not thoroughly assessed.

## Confidence
- Reconstruction performance: Medium confidence
- Outlier detection metrics (Mahalanobis LR, EMD): Medium confidence
