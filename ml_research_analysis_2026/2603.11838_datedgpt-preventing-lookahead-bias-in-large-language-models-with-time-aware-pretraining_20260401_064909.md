# DatedGPT: Preventing Lookahead Bias in Large Language Models with Time-Aware Pretraining
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.11838
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11838
- **Reference count:** 18
- **Primary result:** DatedGPT-INSTRUCT achieves average scores up to 42.7 across tasks, with IFEval instruction accuracy up to 35.3, matching or exceeding similar-scale models like SmolLM-1.7B.

## Executive Summary
DatedGPT mitigates lookahead bias in financial forecasting by training twelve distinct 1.3B-parameter language models from scratch on annually partitioned web data with strict temporal cutoffs (2013–2024). Each model is exposed solely to text available up to its designated cutoff year. This is complemented by instruction fine-tuning on datasets curated to avoid post-cutoff content. Perplexity probing on quarterly news headlines confirms that model knowledge is effectively bounded by the cutoff, showing a clear reversal thereafter. Benchmark evaluations demonstrate competitive performance, and an interactive web demo facilitates temporal comparison of model outputs.

## Method Summary
The core method involves two stages. First, twelve base models are pretrained from scratch on approximately 100 billion tokens per year, where each training corpus is filtered from FineWeb-Edu to include only documents with crawl timestamps ≤ the model's cutoff year. Second, each base model is instruction fine-tuned on a mixture of general-domain instructions (filtered to remove time-sensitive content) and domain-specific finance tasks anchored to publication dates. Temporal validation is conducted by measuring perplexity on news data sliced by quarter; a consistent increase in perplexity after the cutoff indicates successful temporal isolation.

## Key Results
- Perplexity probing on news headlines shows a clear inflection point at each model's cutoff year, with scaled perplexity rising thereafter, confirming temporal knowledge bounding.
- DatedGPT-INSTRUCT achieves average benchmark scores up to 42.7 and IFEval instruction accuracy up to 35.3, competitive with similarly sized models such as SmolLM-1.7B.
- An interactive web demo (www.datedgpt.com) enables users to compare model responses across different temporal cutoffs.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Pretraining Temporal Cutoffs
- **Claim:** Training separate models from scratch on annually partitioned data with strict cutoffs prevents exposure to future information.
- **Mechanism:** Each 1.3B-parameter model is trained exclusively on FineWeb-Edu text filtered to include only documents with crawl timestamps ≤ its cutoff year, decoupling parametric knowledge from post-cutoff events.
- **Evidence:** Section 2.1 describes year-based filtering of FineWeb-Edu; section 3.2 specifies from-scratch training per year with a fixed iteration budget.
- **Break condition:** Inadvertent inclusion of post-cutoff documents due to crawl timestamp errors or dataset curation flaws.

### Mechanism 2: Instruction Fine-Tuning on Constrained Datasets
- **Claim:** Instruction fine-tuning on temporally curated datasets aligns model behavior without reintroducing leakage.
- **Mechanism:** General instructions are filtered via Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct to remove time-sensitive samples; finance tasks use data with precise temporal anchors (publication dates), forcing forward-looking reasoning within the information available at the cutoff.
- **Evidence:** Section 2.2 details the filtering of general instruction data and the curation of date-anchored finance tasks.
- **Break condition:** Filtering failures leaving subtle post-cutoff references, or finance task prompts inadvertently embedding future context.

### Mechanism 3: Perplexity Reversal Validation
- **Claim:** A reversal in perplexity on post-cutoff news data validates the effectiveness of temporal knowledge bounding.
- **Mechanism:** Model perplexity is measured on news headlines from each quarter; lower perplexity is expected for pre-cutoff periods, rising for post-cutoff periods due to unfamiliar linguistic patterns and event vocabulary.
- **Evidence:** Section 4.3 presents Figure 2 for DATEDGPT-BASE-2020, showing scaled perplexity increasing after the 2020 cutoff.
- **Break condition:** No consistent inflection at the cutoff due to data contamination, insufficient model capacity, or an ineffective probe.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept: Temporal Data Slicing**
  - **Why needed:** The approach requires constructing multiple datasets where each contains only information available up to a specific year, necessitating filtering based on timestamps and data provenance.
  - **Quick check:** If a webpage was crawled in 2021 but authored in 2018, should it be included in a 2020-cutoff model's training? The decision hinges on whether crawl date (included) or creation date (excluded) is the governing proxy.
- **Concept: Instruction Data Curation for Temporal Safety**
  - **Why needed:** Standard instruction datasets inherently contain time-sensitive knowledge; one must identify and remove samples that encode information from after a cutoff, requiring semantic understanding of temporal references.
  - **Quick check:** Is the query "Explain the significance of the COVID-19 pandemic" time-sensitive for a 2019-cutoff model? (Yes, the pandemic began in 2020). For a 2022-cutoff model? (Likely not, as the event is well-established by then.)
- **Concept: Validation via Distributional Shift Detection**
  - **Why needed:** Proving a model is temporally bounded requires showing its uncertainty or performance degrades on post-cutoff data, using metrics like perplexity as a detector of distributional shift.
  - **Quick check:** If a model's perplexity is high on both pre-cutoff and post-cutoff data, what does that suggest? It implies the probe may be insensitive or the model is uniformly uncertain, failing to confirm temporal separation.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Data Curator -> Pretraining Engine -> Instruction Tuner -> Temporal Validator -> Demo Backend
- **Critical path:** Per-year data curation → from-scratch pretraining → instruction fine-tuning → perplexity validation → model packaging. Each year's pipeline runs independently.
- **Design tradeoffs:**
  - *Token volume vs. temporal granularity:* 100B tokens/year is modest compared to trillion-scale pretraining, possibly limiting performance but ensuring manageable yearly retraining.
  - *Crawl date vs. creation date:* Using crawl date is reproducible but may include old pages crawled late, over-restricting early models or leaking new content if late-crawled pages reference post-cutoff events.
  - *General vs. domain instruction:* Mixing ensures broad usability but risks subtle leakage if general instruction filtering is imperfect.
- **Failure signatures:**
  - Perplexity curve shows no clear reversal at the cutoff year.
  - Model confidently generates accurate post-cutoff facts in qualitative tests (e.g., DatedGPT-2020 knows about ChatGPT).
  - Instruction-tuned model performance degrades significantly on pre-cutoff benchmarks compared to its base model, indicating catastrophic forgetting during tuning.
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1. Replicate the quarterly news perplexity probe for DATEDGPT-BASE-2024, testing on 2023 and 2025 news; expect lower perplexity for 2023, higher for 2025.
  2. Conduct a leakage test: query DATEDGPT-INSTRUCT-2020 with "What is the latest version of GPT?" The model should not know GPT-4 or ChatGPT-released details.
  3. Perform a non-time-sensitive task parity check: compare DATEDGPT-INSTRUCT-2020 and -2023 on HellaSwag; similar scores would confirm general capability is not drastically skewed by data recency.

## Limitations
- Crawl timestamps are imperfect proxies for creation dates; old content crawled late may over-restrict early models or leak new references if the content discusses post-cutoff events.
- Instruction data filtering via an LLM (Llama-3.3-70B) is heuristic and may miss subtle temporal references, risking reintroduction of lookahead bias.
- Perplexity reversal indicates temporal separation but does not guarantee absence of factual leakage—a model could memorize key post-cutoff entities while still showing high uncertainty on general post-cutoff text.

## Confidence
- **Mechanism 1 (Pretraining cutoffs):** Medium — execution is clear and reproducible, but the core assumption (crawl dates as sufficient proxies) remains weakly validated.
- **Mechanism 2 (Instruction tuning safety):** Low — relies on heuristic filtering; no direct evidence that all time-sensitive content is removed.
- **Mechanism 3 (Perplexity validation):** Medium — method is established, but its power to detect all forms of lookahead bias (e.g., entity memorization without linguistic shift) is unclear.

## Next Checks
1. Run the quarterly news perplexity test for all twelve models; confirm a consistent inflection at each cutoff year and quantify the magnitude of the post-cutoff increase.
2. Perform targeted leakage queries: test DatedGPT-2020 with "When was ChatGPT released?" and "What is GPT-4?"; any accurate post-2022 detail indicates leakage.
3. Compare all DatedGPT-INSTRUCT models on a non-time-sensitive benchmark like HellaSwag or MMLU; significant variance by year would suggest data volume or temporal skew impacts general capability, undermining isolation claims.
