# SACRED: A Faithful Annotated Multimedia Multimodal Multilingual Dataset for Classifying Connectedness Types in Online Spirituality

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.27331  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27331  
- **Reference count:** 0  
- **Primary result:** SACRED is a novel multimodal dataset for classifying spiritual connectedness, with DeepSeek-V3 achieving 79.19% text accuracy and GPT-4o-mini leading image F1 at 63.99%, outperforming traditional methods and revealing a new “connectedness to art” category.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces SACRED, the first high-quality multimodal dataset for studying online spirituality, annotated across text and images from Reddit, Instagram, and Quora. It defines five types of connectedness—four established (self, others, nature, transcendence) and one newly identified (art). The authors evaluate 13 models, finding that advanced LLMs like DeepSeek-V3 and GPT-4o-mini significantly outperform rule-based and fine-tuned BERT approaches. The work highlights the promise of zero-shot LLMs for nuanced spiritual sentiment classification while exposing reproducibility gaps in baseline implementations.

## Method Summary
The task is multimodal classification of online spirituality posts into five connectedness types using the SACRED dataset. Data sources include Reddit posts, Instagram images with captions, and Quora answers, annotated by five trained annotators. An evaluation subset of 447 text posts and 193 images was proportionally sampled by platform and hashtag. Models evaluated: one rule-based (keyword dictionaries), one fine-tuned BERT (30 epochs, lr=2e-5), and 11 LLMs under zero-shot and definition-injected prompting. Text performance is measured by accuracy; image performance by F1 score. Prompts are provided in the appendix.

## Key Results
- DeepSeek-V3 achieved 79.19% accuracy on Quora text classification, the best among all models.
- GPT-4o-mini led vision tasks with a 63.99% F1 score.
- Traditional rule-based and fine-tuned BERT models performed substantially worse than state-of-the-art LLMs.
- Analysis revealed a new “connectedness to art” category emerging from artistic engagement in posts.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
LLMs leverage extensive pretraining on diverse internet text to recognize subtle linguistic and contextual cues linked to spiritual connectedness, allowing zero-shot generalization beyond keyword matching. Their ability to integrate definitional prompts provides structured priors that improve classification without task-specific fine-tuning. For images, vision-language models align visual features with textual concepts of connectedness through multimodal training, though performance is limited by the abstract, subjective nature of spiritual themes.

## Foundational Learning
- **Multimodal classification** – Integrating text and image inputs for a single prediction task; needed because spiritual expression spans modalities.
- **Connectedness theory** – Psychological framework categorizing spiritual experiences (self, others, nature, transcendence); needed to ground annotation schema.
- **Zero-shot prompting** – Using natural language instructions without training; needed to compare LLM innate knowledge vs. fine-tuned baselines.
- **Annotation reliability** – Consistent labeling by multiple annotators; needed to ensure dataset quality but not reported.
- **Platform-specific bias** – Different user behaviors on Reddit/Instagram/Quora; needed to stratify sampling but not fully controlled.

## Architecture Onboarding
Dataset -> Annotation Schema -> Model Inference -> Evaluation Metrics  
Critical path: Data collection (Reddit/Instagram/Quora) → Multi-annotator labeling → Stratified test split → LLM/rule-based/BERT inference → Metric computation (accuracy/F1).  
Design tradeoffs: Zero-shot LLMs offer flexibility but hallucinate; fine-tuned BERT adapts but requires training data; rule-based is interpretable but brittle.  
Failure signatures: Invalid label outputs from LLMs; poor multimodal alignment on Instagram image-only task; long Quora answers causing theme dilution.

## Limitations
- Annotation reliability is unverified—no inter-annotator agreement statistics reported.
- Evaluation reproducibility is compromised—sampling seed/method for test subset not specified.
- Rule-based baseline results cannot be replicated—keyword dictionaries are absent.

## Confidence
- **High:** Existence of SACRED dataset and multimodal annotation schema; LLM evaluation protocol with prompts.
- **Medium:** Performance numbers for top LLMs (plausible but may vary with test split).
- **Low:** Results for rule-based and fine-tuned BERT models (missing implementation details).
