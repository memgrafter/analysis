# HEAL: Hindsight Entropy-Assisted Learning for Reasoning Distillation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2603.10359
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.10359
- Reference count: 9
- Primary result: HEAL achieves 61.68% average accuracy on Qwen2.5-14B versus 50.99% for SFT and 53.74% for Curriculum SFT, with a particularly strong improvement on AIME2024 (53.63% vs 36.27%).

## Executive Summary
HEAL introduces a three-component framework to overcome the "Teacher Ceiling" in reasoning distillation, where standard rejection sampling discards hard problems that the teacher cannot solve independently. GEAR uses entropy dynamics to locate reasoning dead-ends and applies targeted hindsight hints to repair failing trajectories. PURE filters out spurious shortcuts via a step-wise perplexity-uncertainty ratio. PACE organizes training into a three-stage curriculum that gradually introduces more complex data. Evaluated on AIME, MATH500, and OlympiadBench with Qwen2.5-14B and Qwen3-4B, HEAL demonstrates substantial gains, notably achieving 61.68% average accuracy on the 14B model, a >10 percentage-point improvement over SFT.

## Method Summary
HEAL constructs three complementary datasets from the teacher model's outputs. D_base consists of correct trajectories obtained via standard rejection sampling. D_hint provides global answer-conditioned trajectories for hard problems. D_repair is generated by GEAR: for a failed trajectory, it identifies a breakpoint via an entropy spike in the first third, injects the ground-truth answer as a local hint, and regenerates a corrected trajectory; the resulting set is then filtered by PURE to remove spurious shortcuts. Training proceeds with PACE: Stage I on D_base, Stage II on D_base ∪ D_hint, and Stage III on the full mixture including D_repair. This curriculum stabilizes learning and enables the student to master both foundational and frontier reasoning skills.

## Key Results
- HEAL reaches 61.68% average accuracy on Qwen2.5-14B across AIME, MATH500, and OlympiadBench, surpassing SFT (50.99%) and Curriculum SFT (53.74%).
- On the challenging AIME2024 benchmark, HEAL achieves 53.63%, a 17+ point gain over SFT's 36.27%.
- Improvements are consistent across different student model scales (14B and 4B), confirming the robustness of the approach.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** GEAR repairs failing reasoning trajectories by detecting reasoning dead-ends via entropy dynamics and injecting targeted hindsight hints for repair, thereby transforming previously unsolvable problems into valid training signals.
- **Mechanism:** During teacher sampling, GEAR monitors token-level entropy across the initial third of a trajectory. A sudden entropy increase (spike) indicates a "reasoning dead-end" where the model's confidence collapses. GEAR anchors intervention one step before this peak (the last stable state) and conditions subsequent generation on the ground-truth answer, providing a scaffold to bridge the logic gap without altering earlier correct reasoning.
- **Core assumption:** The teacher model possesses latent reasoning capability for the problem but fails due to an early structural error (within its Zone of Proximal Development). Providing the answer as a local hint at the breakpoint allows recovery of a valid trajectory.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "GEAR uses entropy dynamics to locate reasoning dead-ends and applies targeted hindsight hints for repair."
  - [section 4.1] Detailed formulation of critical breakpoint `t* = arg max_t {∇H(st)|1<t<L/3} -1` and its theoretical grounding in preventing error cascading via early-stage anchoring.
  - [corpus] Neighboring papers (e.g., "Long-Chain Reasoning Distillation via Adaptive Prefix Alignment," "RLKD") address distillation challenges but do not explore entropy-guided local repair; evidence is indirect and conceptual.
- **Break condition:** If the teacher lacks foundational knowledge for the problem (outside its ZPD), or if the entropy spike is ambiguous/missing, GEAR may insert hints at non-critical points, yielding incoherent trajectories. Also, reliance on ground-truth answers limits applicability to domains with verifiable solutions.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** PURE filters out spurious "shortcut" trajectories (where the model logically drifts to the known answer without valid derivation) by using a step-wise perplexity-uncertainty ratio, ensuring only logically sound reasoning paths are used for distillation.
- **Mechanism:** For each step (excluding final two), PURE computes the Suspicion Ratio `Rt = PPL(st|q,s<t) / (NLL(a|q,s≤t)+ϵ)`. Genuine reasoning shows gradual changes; shortcuts exhibit abrupt drops (low perplexity relative to answer uncertainty). The trajectory's maximum `Rt` (Anomaly Score) ranks paths, and the top λ% (e.g., 20%) are pruned.
- **Core assumption:** Spurious shortcuts manifest as locally low perplexity (easy token prediction) coupled with high answer confidence (low NLL) because the model leverages the known answer to "guess" coherent but logically baseless text. Genuine multi-step reasoning maintains higher perplexity throughout.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "PURE employs a perplexity-uncertainty ratio to filter spurious shortcuts."
  - [section 4.2] Full definition of `Rt` and the filtering protocol, including exclusion of final steps and ranking by `Atraj`.
  - [corpus] While neighboring works (e.g., "Distilling LLM Agent into Small Models") address data quality, none propose this specific ratio; evidence is weak and derived from the paper's own justification.
- **Break condition:** The ratio may misclassify valid high-perplexity steps (e.g., complex derivations) as shortcuts if answer uncertainty drops prematurely. The fixed λ threshold is also problem-sensitive; inappropriate values either leave noise or discard valid diversity.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** PACE stabilizes student training and prevents catastrophic forgetting by organizing distillation into three progressive stages (from teacher's independent solutions to hint-repaired corner cases), aligning data complexity with the student's growing capability.
- **Mechanism:** Stage I trains on `D_base` (standard rejection sampling) to build foundational patterns. Stage II mixes in `D_hint` (global answer-conditioned paths) to expand the solution space. Stage III integrates `D_repair` (GEAR-synthesized, PURE-filtered) to tackle the hardest corner cases. Each stage builds on the previous, avoiding sudden distribution shifts.
- **Core assumption:** The student model requires a curriculum that matches its learning trajectory; exposing it prematurely to complex, hint-repaired trajectories (with non-standard reasoning structures) disrupts the assimilation of basic skills.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "PACE organizes training in three progressive stages from basic to advanced trajectories."
  - [section 4.3] Explicit stage definitions and objectives (Foundation Alignment, Latent Expansion, Frontier Breakthrough).
  - [corpus] Curriculum learning is well-established (e.g., "Light-R1" uses curriculum SFT), but the specific three-tier categorization (`base`/`hint`/`repair`) is novel; neighboring evidence is conceptual.
- **Break condition:** If the stage transitions are too abrupt or the mixing proportions are suboptimal, the student may forget earlier skills (catastrophic interference) or fail to integrate advanced patterns. The method also assumes `D_base` is sufficiently large and high-quality to form a stable foundation.

## Foundational Learning
- **Rejection Sampling (in Reasoning Distillation)**
  - Why needed here: HEAL directly addresses rejection sampling's "Teacher Ceiling"—the discarding of hard problems where the teacher fails independently. Understanding the baseline is essential to appreciate HEAL's data synthesis innovations.
  - Quick check: What is the Teacher Ceiling, and why does it occur in standard rejection sampling?

- **Entropy Dynamics in Autoregressive Decoding**
  - Why needed here: GEAR's breakpoint detection relies on tracking token-level entropy spikes as indicators of reasoning dead-ends. This requires understanding how entropy reflects model confidence during generation.
  - Quick check: How does a sudden increase in token entropy during reasoning correlate with the model's cognitive state?

- **Perplexity and Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL)**
  - Why needed here: PURE's suspicion ratio uses step-wise perplexity (surprisal of generating the step) and answer NLL (confidence in the final answer given the context). These are fundamental metrics for evaluating coherence and logical consistency.
  - Quick check: Why would a spurious shortcut exhibit low perplexity for a reasoning step but low NLL for the final answer?

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:**
- Input Pipeline: problem set Q, teacher model π_T, ground-truth answers a.
- Data Synthesis:
  - D_base: independent trajectories via rejection sampling.
  - D_hint: global hindsight trajectories (answer in prompt) for hard problems.
  - D_repair: GEAR-synthesized trajectories (entropy-guided local hint) then filtered by PURE.
- Training: PACE three-stage curriculum (Stage I on D_base, Stage II on D_base ∪ D_hint, Stage III on D_base ∪ D_hint ∪ D_repair).
- Output: student model π_S.

**Critical path:** The most critical computational path is the GEAR-PURE pipeline for constructing D_repair: (1) generate failed trajectories, (2) compute entropy dynamics to find breakpoint t*, (3) regenerate with local hint at t*, (4) compute Rt for each step, (5) rank and prune by Atraj. Errors here directly affect the quality of the hardest training data.

**Design tradeoffs:**
- GEAR: Using the first third of the trajectory (t < L/3) balances early intervention with avoiding premature pruning, but may miss later structural errors.
- PURE: The smoothing term ϵ=0.01 prevents division by zero but introduces a hyperparameter; λ=0.2 was chosen via grid search and may not generalize.
- PACE: Stagewise training increases total computation and requires careful epoch allocation; the paper uses 5 epochs total without stage-specific weights.

**Failure signatures:**
- GEAR: No clear entropy spike across a trajectory → breakpoint detection fails → hint inserted arbitrarily → regenerated trajectory remains invalid.
- PURE: High Atraj for many trajectories → excessive pruning → insufficient D_repair samples; or low Atraj for obvious shortcuts → noise in training data.
- PACE: Degradation on D_base validation during Stage II/III → catastrophic forgetting; no improvement on AIME/OlympiadBench → curriculum misalignment.

**First 3 experiments:**
1. Ablation - Random Breakpoint: Replace GEAR's entropy-guided breakpoint with random selection within the first third. Expect significant drop on AIME2024 (from 53.63% to ~50.27%).
2. Ablation - w/o PURE: Train without PURE filtering on D_repair. Expect severe drop on AIME2024 (to 42.10%).
3. Ablation - w/o PACE: Train with monolithic mixing of all data (no stages). Expect drop on AIME2024 (to 43.27%).

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- How can the GEAR module be adapted for open-ended generation tasks where ground-truth answers are unavailable or ambiguous?
- How can HEAL be extended to handle problems that lie outside the teacher model’s zone of proximal development (ZPD), such as those requiring entirely novel reasoning primitives?
- What is the optimal strategy for integrating HEAL-generated distillation data with reinforcement learning (RL) methods to further boost reasoning capabilities?
- How does the optimal truncation threshold λ for PURE’s shortcut filtering generalize across diverse problem domains and model scales?

## Limitations
- **Undocumented implementation details**: Exact prompt templates for GEAR repairs, precise PACE stage epoch splits, and upsampling factors for D_repair are not specified, hampering exact replication.
- **Heuristic fragility**: GEAR's entropy-spike breakpoint detection may fail to capture later reasoning errors; PURE's suspicion ratio (R_t) can misclassify valid high-perplexity steps as spurious shortcuts due to fixed λ thresholds.
- **Scope limitations**: The framework assumes the teacher model possesses latent reasoning capability within its zone of proximal development and requires verifiable ground-truth answers, restricting use to well-defined domains like math and coding.
