# A Sobering Look at Tabular Data Generation via Probabilistic Circuits
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2603.23016
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23016
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Common fidelity metrics (TREND, C2ST with logistic regression) are flawed—a trivial fully-factorized model scores near perfectly; TabPC matches or exceeds SotA diffusion models on robust metrics (wNMIS, C2ST-XGB, alpha-precision) while training 1-2 orders faster.

## Executive Summary
The paper critically evaluates tabular data generation (TDG) by exposing flaws in widely used fidelity metrics (TREND, C2ST with logistic regression) that incorrectly rate trivial models as perfect. It introduces probabilistic circuits (PCs) as an efficient baseline and proposes stronger metrics (wNMIS, C2ST with XGBoost). The resulting model, TabPC, achieves state-of-the-art fidelity on robust metrics while being orders of magnitude faster than diffusion models and supporting exact conditional sampling and likelihood-based selection. The findings reveal that perceived saturation in TDG is largely due to inadequate metrics, leaving substantial room for improvement.

## Method Summary
TabPC constructs a probabilistic circuit (PC) from a Chow-Liu tree to define a region graph, then builds a smooth, decomposable circuit with overparameterized CP sum-product layers. The circuit is trained via maximum likelihood using RAdam with a learning rate scheduler, validated on held-out bits-per-dimension (BPD), and early-stopped. Preprocessing includes quantile normalization to standard Gaussian and handling of inflated values (new category or marginalization). The method yields exact inference for likelihoods and conditionals, enabling efficient generation and model selection without retraining.

## Key Results
- Common metrics (TREND, C2ST-LR) fail: a fully-factorized model scores near-perfectly despite modeling no dependencies.
- TabPC matches or exceeds diffusion models (TabDiff, TabSyn) on robust metrics (wNMIS, C2ST-XGB, α-precision) while training 1–2 orders faster.
- TabPC supports exact conditional sampling and likelihood-based model selection, unlike diffusion models.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Flawed Fidelity Metrics
Common fidelity metrics (TREND, C2ST with logistic regression) are inadequate because they assign high scores to trivial fully-factorized models that only match univariate marginals, failing to capture joint distribution dependencies. TREND averages linear correlations/contingencies, making it sensitive to univariate marginals; C2ST with logistic regression becomes a random classifier when real and synthetic means match (Theorem 2.1). The core assumption is that fidelity metrics must evaluate multivariate dependencies beyond marginals and linear relationships. Evidence comes from the paper's experiments and theory showing the fully-factorized model scores near perfectly on these metrics. A break condition would be if future metrics reduce saturation by correctly penalizing simple models, or if synthetic data generation advances beyond marginal matching.

### Mechanism 2: Robust Metrics (wNMIS, C2ST-XGB)
wNMIS (weighted normalized mutual information) and C2ST with XGBoost provide robust stratification, correctly identifying weak models (fully-factorized) while rewarding expressive ones (TabPC, diffusion SOTA). wNMIS emphasizes feature pairs with high dependency in real data and is invariant to marginal quality. C2ST with XGBoost uses a powerful non-linear classifier to avoid the logistic regression random-classifier pitfall, measuring distinguishability on the full joint distribution. The core assumption is that mutual information captures non-linear dependencies and a flexible classifier is necessary for a meaningful two-sample test. Evidence includes wNMIS's invariance to marginal transformations (Fig. 2) and experimental results showing fully-factorized fails under C2ST(XGB) but succeeds under C2ST(LR) (Fig. 3, Tabs 7–8). A break condition is if wNMIS saturates for all models or XGBoost overfits on small datasets.

### Mechanism 3: Probabilistic Circuits (TabPC) Efficacy
TabPC delivers SOTA-level fidelity on robust metrics with 1–2 orders of magnitude less training time than diffusion models, while enabling exact conditional sampling and likelihood-based model selection. It builds smooth, decomposable PCs from a Chow-Liu tree, using overparameterized tensorized CP layers to create deep hierarchical mixtures. Tractable inference (forward pass for likelihoods, ancestral sampling for conditionals) comes from structural guarantees; GPU tensorization accelerates training. The core assumption is that deep, overparameterized PCs can effectively model heterogeneous tabular data with tractable inference, and hierarchical depth is more parameter-efficient than shallow width. Evidence includes competitive scores on wNMIS/C2ST(XGB)/α-precision (Tabs 6, 8, 9), huge speedups (Tab. 12), and exact conditional sampling (Fig. 6). A break condition is if PCs cannot scale to very high feature counts or complex non-linear interactions beyond pairwise, or if training becomes unstable with depth.

## Architecture Onboarding
### Component map
Data → Preprocessing (missing handling, inflated value handling, quantile normalization) → Chow-Liu tree → Region graph → Tensorised PC (CP sum-product layers) → Training (RAdam, learning rate scheduler, early stopping on validation BPD) → Inference (exact likelihood, conditional sampling) → Evaluation (wNMIS, C2ST-XGB, timing).

### Critical path
Data preprocessing → Chow-Liu tree → Region graph compilation → Circuit folding → Training (RAdam, validation BPD) → Evaluation (wNMIS, C2ST-XGB, timing).

### Design tradeoffs
- Overparameterisation (K): Larger K increases expressivity (mimics wider mixtures) but raises memory/computation; depth vs. width efficiency.
- Tensorisation: Speeds GPU execution but may limit model size due to memory; folding required for parallel layers.
- Preprocessing: Quantile stabilises training but alters data scale; marginalisation of inflated values leverages PC tractability.

### Failure signatures
- Validation BPD stagnates: capacity too low, learning rate issues, or poor initialization.
- High SHAPE but low wNMIS/C2ST-XGB: model matches marginals but not dependencies; increase depth or K.
- Memory overflow: reduce K, batch size, or avoid excessive folding.
- C2ST-XGB near random: model distributions too similar; may indicate overfitting or inadequate training.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. **Sufficiency of robust metrics:** Are wNMIS and C2ST-XGB sensitive to high-order dependencies, or do they still saturate for expressive models?
2. **Parameter efficiency of PCs:** Why does TabPC achieve comparable fidelity to diffusion models despite having orders of magnitude more parameters? Is it due to the structure (Chow-Liu tree), overparameterization, or the mixture inductive bias?
3. **BPD as proxy for quality:** Does the correlation between validation BPD and C2ST-XGB hold for other generative model classes (e.g., diffusion) or only for tractable PCs?
4. **Conditional sampling utility:** Can exact conditional sampling of TabPC be practically leveraged for missing data imputation and outperform diffusion-based heuristics in real downstream tasks?
5. **Scalability limits:** How do PCs scale to hundreds of features or very small datasets compared to diffusion models?

## Limitations
- Implementation details missing: inflated value detection, quantile normalization parameters (n_quantiles), XGBoost hyperparameters for C2ST-XGB, wNMIS discretization method, and procedure for Chow-Liu tree on mixed data.
- Evaluation scope limited to seven UCI datasets; generalizability to other domains or larger feature sets unclear.
- Robust metrics may still saturate for highly expressive models, as acknowledged by the authors.

## Confidence
- Flawed metrics claim: High
- Robust metrics claim: Medium
- TabPC efficacy claim: Medium
