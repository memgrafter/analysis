# From Liar Paradox to Incongruent Sets: A Normal Form for Self-Reference

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.24527  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24527  
- **Reference count:** 21  
- **Primary result:** Self-referential paradoxes can be transformed into finite sets of individually satisfiable but jointly unsatisfiable non-self-referential sentences (incongruent normal form), preserving classical semantics while isolating incompatibility and linking knowledge to semantic incompleteness via model-theoretic informativeness and quantitative energy-entropy bounds.

## Executive Summary
This paper tackles semantic paradoxes from self-reference not by restricting language or truth values but by providing a structural solution. It introduces the **incongruent normal form (INF)**, a procedure that rewrites any self-referential sentence with finite truth-predicate unfolding into a finite set of non-self-referential sentences. Each sentence in the INF is locally satisfiable, but the entire set is unsatisfiable, capturing the original paradox’s obstruction within classical semantics. Using a model-theoretic notion of **informativeness** (a sentence is informative iff its truth set is neither empty nor universal), the authors prove that a semantically complete fragment cannot contain informative sentences; hence knowledge requires incompleteness. Furthermore, any consistent incomplete first-order theory admits a finite incongruent family. Quantitatively, semantic states are modeled on a finite group (Boolean cube), with **semantic energy** defined as total influence of truth-value functions. This yields uncertainty-style bounds: a single sentence’s knowledge content (binary entropy) is bounded by a function of its energy; for multiple sentences, total variance is bounded by total energy, and incongruence tightens this by forcing energy redistribution among incompatible alternatives. The work connects logic, model theory, and functional analysis to show that semantic determinacy, informativeness, and spectral simplicity cannot coexist.

## Method Summary
The core method is the **INF transformation**. For a self-referential sentence σ, one unfolds all semantic predicates (e.g., “True(⌜σ⌝)”) into fresh propositional symbols, building a finite dependency graph. From this graph, a linking theory Δ_σ is generated: biconditionals for an acyclic graph, implications for a cyclic one. The resulting set Φ_σ ∪ Δ_σ is the INF. Its correctness (Theorem 3.11/3.12) ensures that each sentence in Φ_σ is individually satisfiable (local realizability) while the conjunction of all Φ_σ is unsatisfiable under Δ_σ exactly when σ has no classical model. For quantitative analysis, truth-value assignments over a finite state space Ω (e.g., {0,1}^m) are treated as Boolean functions. Semantic energy is the **total influence** (sum of sensitivities to single-coordinate flips). Variance and entropy are bounded via the **Poincaré inequality** and Fourier analysis. For families, a matrix inequality Σ ⪯ ℰ (covariance vs. energy matrix) is derived, and incongruence (bounded width of simultaneously true sentences) suppresses covariance, tightening the bound.

## Key Results
- **INF construction:** Any self-referential sentence with finite unfolding can be replaced by a finite set of non-self-referential sentences that are individually satisfiable but jointly unsatisfiable, preserving the semantic obstruction.
- **Knowledge requires incompleteness:** In any model class, semantic completeness (every sentence in a fragment has trivial truth set) precludes informativeness (nontrivial truth set); therefore, informative knowledge is impossible in a complete system.
- **Quantitative uncertainty principle:** On a finite group, a sentence’s entropy K(f) ≤ (E(f)/2)·log(2e/E(f)); for a family, trace(Cov) ≤ trace(ℰ); incongruence (width w) further reduces aggregate variance.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: Removing self-reference while preserving obstruction
Self-referential paradoxes are converted into finite sets of non-self-referential sentences via unfolding. Each truth-predicate occurrence is replaced by a fresh symbol; constraints linking these symbols (biconditionals for acyclic dependencies, implications for cyclic ones) ensure that the set of commitments exactly captures the original sentence’s semantic requirements. Local satisfiability of each commitment is preserved, but joint satisfiability fails exactly when the original self-referential sentence has no classical model. This isolates the incompatibility structurally without altering local classical semantics.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract; Section 3 (procedure and Theorems 3.11/3.12).

### Mechanism 2: Semantic completeness destroys informativeness
Informativeness is defined model-theoretically: a sentence φ is informative relative to a class of models ℳ iff its truth set [[φ]]ℳ is neither ∅ nor ℳ. If a fragment is semantically complete over ℳ (every sentence in the fragment has either empty or universal truth set), then no sentence in that fragment can be informative. Thus any system capable of expressing informative knowledge must be semantically incomplete.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract; Section 4 (Lemma 4.1, Observation 4.3).

### Mechanism 3: Energy-entropy trade-off via Fourier analysis
Modeling semantic states as a finite abelian group (e.g., Boolean cube) with uniform measure, a sentence’s truth indicator f is a Boolean function. Its **semantic energy** E(f) is the total influence (sum of Fourier coefficients weighted by degree). The Poincaré inequality gives Var(f) ≤ E(f). Entropy K(f) = H(Pr[f=1]) is then bounded using the fact that for a bounded random variable, entropy ≤ minimal tail mass · log(1/tail mass). For multiple sentences, the covariance matrix Σ satisfies Σ ⪯ ℰ (the energy matrix), so trace(Σ) ≤ trace(ℰ). Incongruent families have bounded width (max number of simultaneously true sentences ≤ n−1), which suppresses off-diagonal covariances, further tightening the aggregate variance bound.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract; Section 5 (Theorems 5.4, 5.5, 5.7; Lemma 5.6).

## Architecture Onboarding
Unfolding module -> Linking theory generator -> INF validator -> Quantitative analyzer

**Critical path:** For a given self-referential σ: (1) Unfold to get dependency graph; (2) Classify as acyclic/cyclic and generate Δ_σ; (3) Check local satisfiability of Φ_σ and joint unsatisfiability of Φ_σ ∪ Δ_σ; (4) For quantitative analysis, encode sentences as Boolean functions over a finite state space, compute energy/entropy, and for families compute covariance and energy matrices and width.

**Design tradeoffs:**
- Exact unfolding vs. scalability: Exact unfolding guarantees correctness but may explode; approximate/bounded unfolding trades correctness for efficiency.
- State space representation: Explicit tables scale poorly; sampling or locality-sensitive hashing may be needed for larger dimensions.
- Measure assumptions: Theory assumes uniform measure over the Boolean cube; non-uniform priors require adjusting the Poincaré constant.
- Cycle handling: Using biconditionals on cycles yields inconsistency; implications preserve local satisfiability but may weaken constraints (only forbidding all-true assignment).

**Failure signatures:**
- Unfolding does not terminate: sentence likely outside finite-unfolding class (e.g., Yablo sequence).
- Δ_σ inconsistent for an acyclic graph: bug in topological ordering or extraction of linking constraints.
- Energy > 1 in entropy bound makes bound trivial for low-entropy functions: check state space size or function complexity.
- Width estimate w(Φ) > n−1 does not necessarily contradict incongruence; incongruence only requires full conjunction unsatisfiable. Use exact joint satisfiability check.

**First 3 experiments:**
1. **Liar INF:** Encode “This sentence is false” as σ. Unfold to get Φ={p1=“σ true”, p2=“σ false”}. Build cyclic Δ⁺={p1→¬p2, p2→¬p1}. Verify each p_i has a model (e.g., p1=1, p2=0) and that p1∧p2 is unsatisfiable under Δ⁺.
2. **Incongruence from incomplete theory:** Take FO theory T with one unary predicate P, constant c, and axiom ∀x (∀i≠j ¬(P_i(x)∧P_j(x))). Let φ_i = P_i(c). Each φ_i is satisfiable in a model of T (1-element model with exactly one P_i true), but no model satisfies all φ_i simultaneously → finite incongruent family without negations.
3. **Energy-entropy trade-off:** On {0,1}^m (m=4), compute for several f: p=Pr[f=1], K=H(p), E=total influence. Verify K ≤ min(1, (E/2)·log₂(2e/E)). For a family of 3 functions, compute centered functions g̃_i, matrices Σ and ℰ, and check Σ ⪯ ℰ via eigenvalues.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Open Question 1:** What are necessary and sufficient conditions for the linking theory Δ_σ in an INF to be minimal, and is it unique for a given σ? (Section 3.8, Remark 3.1.1a)
- **Open Question 2:** Can the INF transformation be extended to infinitary semantic paradoxes (e.g., Yablo sequences), which generate infinite dependency chains? (Remark 3.1.1a)
- **Open Question 3:** Do semantic uncertainty bounds hold for state spaces not isomorphic to a finite Boolean group (e.g., continuous spaces or non-product Markov kernels)? (Section 6.6)
- **Open Question 4:** How does the framework interact with inconsistency-tolerant logics (e.g., paraconsistent systems)? (Section 6.9)

## Limitations
- INF applies only to sentences with finite truth-predicate unfolding; infinitary paradoxes (Yablo) are excluded.
- Quantitative bounds assume a finite abelian group (Boolean cube) with uniform measure and single-coordinate flips; other state spaces require different analytic tools.
- Entropy bound becomes trivial for normalized energy > ~0.655; no empirical validation of the framework’s quantitative predictions exists yet.

## Confidence
- **High:** Semantic completeness → no informativeness (simple model-theoretic argument).
- **Medium:** INF correctness for finite unfoldings; existence of finite incongruent families for any consistent incomplete FO theory (proofs appear valid but effective generation is not fully detailed).
- **Low:** Energy-entropy uncertainty principle (sound under strong assumptions but tightness and practical significance unverified).
