# 360° Image Perception with MLLMs: A Comprehensive Benchmark and a Training-Free Method

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.16179
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16179
- **Reference Count:** 40
- **Primary Result:** Free360 improves its base MLLM's accuracy on 360° VQA by +7.3% overall, with up to +22.9% on spatial reasoning, while operating at 22.5 seconds per sample.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces the problem of multi-modal large language model (MLLM) perception of 360° images, which are challenged by geometric distortion and complex spatial relations. To evaluate progress, the authors present 360Bench, a comprehensive VQA benchmark for omnidirectional scenes. They also propose Free360, a training-free method that decomposes 360° question answering into four stepwise scene-graph-generation stages. Free360 leverages adaptive spherical transformations—entity-centered rotation and a hybrid CMP/ERP projection strategy—to capture fine-grained attributes and spatial relations. On 360Bench, human accuracy is 86.3% while the best MLLM (Gemini Pro 2.5) achieves only 46.5%. Free360 consistently improves its base model (Qwen2.5-VL-7B) by +7.3% overall, demonstrating the value of structured, transformation-aware reasoning for this challenging visual domain.

## Method Summary
Free360 is a training-free pipeline that enhances a base MLLM's ability to answer questions about 360° images. It operates by decomposing the task into four sequential modules that construct a textual scene graph. First, it identifies question-relevant entities on a cubemap projection (CMP) of the scene. Second, it extracts attributes by cropping around each detected entity. Third, it infers inter-entity spatial relations by rotating the original equirectangular projection (ERP) image so that the pair of interest is centered, simplifying the spatial judgment. Fourth, it maps each entity to one of six predefined "view" nodes (front, back, etc.) to capture viewer-centric relations. All extracted information is unified into a structured graph, which is then provided to the base MLLM to generate the final answer. A fallback to direct ERP questioning is used if the pipeline is unable to produce an answer.

## Key Results
- On the new 360Bench benchmark, the best MLLM (Gemini Pro 2.5) achieves 46.5% accuracy, far below the human ceiling of 86.3%.
- Free360 applied to Qwen2.5-VL-7B yields a **+7.3%** overall accuracy gain on 360Bench.
- Task-specific improvements are substantial, including **+22.9%** on spatial reasoning (SR-OV) and **+14.1%** on projection-distorted perception (PP-IR), achieved via the hybrid CMP/ERP strategy.
- The method incurs an inference cost of ~22.5 seconds per sample, a significant but manageable overhead compared to human response times.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: Stepwise Scene Graph Decomposition
- **Claim:** Decomposing 360° VQA into sequential scene graph generation (SGG) steps reduces cognitive load on the MLLM, allowing focused processing on sub-tasks.
- **Mechanism:** The process is split into four modular steps: (1) identify entities on a CMP image, (2) extract attributes from entity crops, (3) detect inter-entity spatial relations via entity-centered ERP rotation, (4) detect entity-view relations using predefined view nodes. The outputs are unified into a textual scene graph.
- **Core Assumption:** MLLMs perform better on focused, constrained sub-tasks (e.g., describing a cropped object) than on holistic, multi-faceted questions over a distorted 360° scene.
- **Evidence Anchors:** The paper details the four-step SGG process in Section 4.1. Table 4(a) shows ablations where removing any step (Crop, Rotate, EVR) degrades accuracy, confirming each component's necessity.

### Mechanism 2: Entity-Centered Rotation for Spatial Reasoning
- **Claim:** Adaptive spherical rotation (entity-centered view) mitigates the difficulty of reasoning about spatial relations in a fixed ERP frame by aligning the viewpoint with the entities of interest.
- **Mechanism:** For a pair of entities, the minimal spherical bounding box is computed. The ERP image is rotated so this box's center aligns with the image center (longitude 0°, latitude 0°). The rotated image, with highlighted entity bounding boxes, is presented to the MLLM.
- **Core Assumption:** Centering relevant entities simplifies spatial judgment by reducing the need to mentally project across a distorted spherical surface.
- **Evidence Anchors:** The abstract and Section 4.1 define the rotation matrix Rc. Table 4(a) shows "w/o Rotate" reduces accuracy by 0.7%, indicating a measurable contribution.

### Mechanism 3: Hybrid CMP/ERP Projection Strategy
- **Claim:** A hybrid projection strategy leverages complementary strengths: CMP's distortion resistance for recognition and ERP's spatial continuity for holistic reasoning.
- **Mechanism:** Step 1 (Entity ID) uses CMP, where objects appear less distorted. Step 3 (Inter-Entity Relation) uses ERP with spherical rotation, preserving continuous spatial layout for left/right/forward/behind judgments.
- **Core Assumption:** No single projection is optimal for all sub-tasks; adaptive format selection per step maximizes per-step accuracy.
- **Evidence Anchors:** The abstract states CMP excels on projection-distorted tasks while ERP benefits spatial reasoning. Table 3 empirically shows this split: CMP outperforms ERP on PP-IR/IC, while ERP outperforms CMP on SR-OV/DG.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept:** Scene Graph Generation (SGG)
  - **Why needed here:** Free360's core innovation is structuring the MLLM's output into a graph of nodes (entities/views) and edges (attributes/relations). This explicit structure integrates information from multiple, differently transformed image views before final reasoning.
  - **Quick check:** How does representing information as a graph with labeled edges ("in", "across from") help the MLLM answer a complex spatial question compared to just describing the image in a paragraph?

- **Concept:** Spherical Projections (ERP & CMP)
  - **Why needed here:** 360° images are native to a sphere. Projecting them to 2D (ERP, CMP) introduces specific distortions (stretching at poles, seams). Understanding these distortions is key to why certain steps use specific projections and how entity-centered rotation works.
  - **Quick check:** Why might an object that is a single contiguous entity in the real world appear as multiple fragments in an ERP image near the left/right boundaries, but as a single entity on a CMP cube face?

- **Concept:** Entity-Centered Coordinate Transformation
  - **Why needed here:** Spatial reasoning over a sphere is non-Euclidean. The rotation operation in Step 3 re-centers the spherical coordinate system on the entities of interest, converting a global "where is X relative to Y?" question into a local "is X left/right/above/below?" question in a locally Euclidean patch.
  - **Quick check:** What is the geometric effect of rotating an ERP image so that the midpoint between two objects' bounding boxes becomes the new center (longitude 0°, latitude 0°)?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Input Processor (ERP & CMP) -> Step 1 Module (Entity ID on CMP) -> Cropper -> Step 2 Module (Attribute Extraction) -> Rotation Engine -> Step 3 Module (Inter-Entity Relations on rotated ERP) -> View Mapper -> Step 4 Module (Entity-View Relations) -> Graph Serializer -> Answer Generator -> [Fallback: ERP direct query]
- **Critical path:** Step 1 (CMP Entity ID) → Cropper → Step 2 (Attributes) → Rotation Engine + Step 3 (ERP Inter-Entity Relations) → View Mapper + Step 4 → Graph Serializer → Answer Generator. The graph is the central data structure.
- **Design tradeoffs:**
  - **Training-free vs. Fine-tuning:** Avoids catastrophic forgetting and dataset labor but adds significant inference overhead (~22.5s vs base 2.1s) and is bounded by the base MLLM's capabilities.
  - **Cropping vs. Full Image:** Cropping improves focus and speed but risks losing contextual cues needed for some attributes.
  - **Single vs. Hybrid Projection:** Using a single format simplifies the pipeline but sacrifices task-specific accuracy. Hybrid adds complexity (format conversion, alignment) but yields net gains.
  - **MLLM-as-module vs. Unified Model:** Using the same MLLM for all steps is simple but may not be optimal (e.g., a small model for detection, large for reasoning). Free360 keeps one model for consistency.
- **Failure signatures:**
  - **Step 1 Failure:** No entities detected or irrelevant entities. Graph is empty/irrelevant. **Symptom:** Answer Generator outputs "CANNOT ANSWER" or guesses randomly.
  - **Step 3 Failure:** Nonsensical/contradictory spatial relations (e.g., "X is left of Y" and "Y is left of X"). **Symptom:** Final reasoning analysis contains incoherent spatial descriptions.
  - **View Mapping Failure:** Entity assigned to wrong view node (e.g., an object on the left edge of CMP mapped to "right view"). **Symptom:** Entity-view relations in graph are implausible.
  - **Alignment Failure:** Bounding boxes from CMP (Step 1) do not correctly map to positions in rotated ERP (Step 3). **Symptom:** Highlighted boxes in rotated image are misplaced, confusing the MLLM.
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1.  **Benchmark Baselines:** Evaluate the base MLLM and other SOTA models on 360Bench in both ERP and CMP formats. This establishes the performance gap and the projection format effect.
  2.  **Ablation Study:** Run Free360 with key components removed (w/o Crop, w/o Rotate, w/o EVR) to isolate each design choice's contribution.
  3.  **Scalability Test:** Apply Free360 with different base model scales (3B, 7B, 32B) to test if the framework's benefits generalize beyond a specific model size.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Open Question 1:** How to extend Free360 to 360° video QA? The current single-frame design lacks temporal reasoning.
- **Open Question 2:** How to integrate patch-wise retrieval into Free360 for finer-grained perception? Current cropping may miss subtle details.
- **Open Question 3:** Can we automatically choose between CMP and ERP projections per sample to maximize accuracy? The fixed hybrid assignment may be suboptimal for certain samples.

## Limitations
- **Underspecified prompts & transformations:** Exact prompts for each MLLM step and precise CMP-to-spherical coordinate mapping (for rotation and view assignment) are not provided, hindering exact replication.
- **Cascade failure sensitivity:** The pipeline's accuracy is brittle; errors in early steps (e.g., incorrect entity bounding boxes) propagate to the final graph and answer.
- **Training-free overhead:** The ~10x inference cost (22.5s vs 2.1s) versus the base model may limit practical deployment, and gains are bounded by the base MLLM's inherent capabilities.

## Confidence
- **High:** Empirical gains on 360Bench (+7.3% overall) and ablations confirming each module's contribution.
- **Medium:** The mechanism that step-wise decomposition reduces cognitive load and that hybrid projection exploits complementary strengths (supported by per-task results).
- **Low:** The specific geometric correctness of the entity-centered rotation and view-mapping; these rely on precise coordinate transformations not fully detailed.

## Next Checks
1.  **Prompt ablation:** Systematically vary the wording and in-context examples for each step to measure sensitivity and identify a robust prompt set.
2.  **Coordinate transformation audit:** Implement the described CMP layout and spherical rotation math; validate on a held-out set by checking if rotated ERP images correctly center entity pairs and if view-node assignments align with human intuition.
3.  **Error propagation analysis:** Run Free360 with gold-standard (human-annotated) entity boxes and relations for Step 1–3 to quantify the upper-bound performance and pinpoint the primary failure stage.
