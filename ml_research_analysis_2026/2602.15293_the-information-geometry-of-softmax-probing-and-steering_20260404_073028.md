# The Information Geometry of Softmax: Probing and Steering

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.15293
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.15293
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Dual steering achieves target concept probability ~1 while significantly preserving off-target distributions versus Euclidean steering, using a forward KL minimization principle and regularized Newton updates.

## Executive Summary
**Problem:** Steering softmax-based models (e.g., LLMs, CLIP) by adding vectors in the representation space (primal space) causes unintended off-target concept drift because the natural geometry of softmax is non-Euclidean (information geometry).  
**Method:** Dual steering operates in the dual space of expected unembedding vectors, adding the linear probe direction there. This corresponds to minimizing forward KL divergence subject to a target concept constraint, thereby preserving off-target distributions. A regularized Newton step is used to perform the update in the primal space while respecting the bounded dual space.

## Method Summary
The core method is dual steering, which treats the softmax output layer as a dually flat statistical manifold. Given a linear probe β_W that measures a binary concept W, the goal is to modify a context representation λ₀ to achieve a target concept value while minimizing change to the off-target distribution. Instead of adding β_W to λ₀ (Euclidean steering), dual steering updates the dual coordinate φ(λ) = E[γ|λ] (expected unembedding vectors) as φ(λₜ) = φ(λ₀) + t·β_W. This is equivalent to solving min_{λ: β_W⊤λ=c} D_KL(P_λ₀ || P_λ) under a concept-factorization assumption. Since φ(λ) is not directly manipulable, a regularized Newton step is employed: compute the covariance Σ = Cov[γ|λ] of unembedding vectors, solve (Σ + αI)v = β_W for step direction v, and update λ ← λ + η·v/||v||. The regularization α ensures numerical stability near the boundary of the dual space (convex hull of {γ_y}).

## Key Results
- Dual steering reliably drives target concept probability to ~1 while maintaining off-target distribution fidelity.
- It preserves the total probability mass on counterfactual pairs (e.g., "good"/"bad" token pairs) far better than Euclidean steering.
- Off-target KL divergence and token ranking instability are substantially reduced under dual steering across Gemma-3-4B and MetaClip-2.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

1. **Softmax parameter space inherently exhibits Bregman (dually flat) geometry.**  
   The logits λ induce a statistical manifold where KL divergence is a Bregman divergence generated by the log-normalizer A(λ) = log Σ_y exp(λ⊤γ_y). Primal coordinates λ and dual coordinates φ(λ) = ∇A(λ) = E[γ|λ] are related by Legendre duality, forming a dually flat manifold. This non-Euclidean structure makes Euclidean additions suboptimal for distributional preservation.

2. **Primal vs. dual interpolation encode distinct semantic operations (AND vs. OR).**  
   Primal interpolation (λ_t = (1-t)λ₀ + tλ₁) solves an e-geodesic that minimizes a weighted sum of reverse KL divergences, acting like an AND operator that amplifies only the intersection of high-probability regions. Dual interpolation (φ_t = (1-t)φ(λ₀)+tφ(λ₁)) solves an m-geodesic that minimizes a weighted sum of forward KL divergences, acting like an OR operator that unions the endpoint distributions. This explains why primal additions tend to suppress endpoint-specific outputs while dual additions preserve them.

3. **Dual steering minimizes off-target distribution shift via forward KL projection.**  
   The steering objective min_{λ: β_W⊤λ=c} D_KL(P_λ₀ || P_λ) ensures the modified distribution stays close to the original in a forward KL sense. Under concept-factorization (the output distribution cleanly separates into a concept part and an off-target part), the first-order optimality condition forces the dual step φ(λ) - φ(λ₀) to be parallel to β_W. Because the KL decomposes into a term dependent only on φ and a constant concept term on the constraint hyperplane, minimizing forward KL automatically preserves the off-target distribution P^Z.

## Foundational Learning

1. **Bregman Divergence & Dually Flat Manifolds**  
   *Why needed:* The KL divergence between softmax distributions is a Bregman divergence generated by the log-normalizer A(λ). This dually flat structure is the foundation for distinguishing primal vs. dual operations.  
   *Quick check:* For softmax, what is the dual coordinate φ(λ) in terms of the unembedding vectors {γ_y} and probabilities P_λ(y)? How does φ(λ) relate to ∇A(λ)? (Answer: φ(λ) = E[γ|λ] = Σ_y P_λ(y) γ_y = ∇A(λ). φ lies in the convex hull of {γ_y}.)

2. **Legendre Duality & Convex Conjugates**  
   *Why needed:* The bijection between primal logits λ and dual expectations φ is given by φ = ∇A(λ) and its inverse λ = ∇A^*(φ) via convex conjugation. This allows translating steering operations between spaces.  
   *Quick check:* If φ(λ)=E[γ|λ], what is the geometric interpretation of φ as a coordinate? What constraints must φ satisfy? (Answer: φ is an affine coordinate on the dual manifold; it must lie in the convex hull of the unembedding vectors {γ_y}.)

3. **Linear Probe & Concept Factorization**  
   *Why needed:* The steering method relies on a linear probe β_W that perfectly captures a binary concept, and on the assumption that the softmax distribution factorizes into independent concept and off-target components. This factorization is crucial for the off-target preservation guarantee.  
   *Quick check:* In concept-factorization, what does P^Z_λ represent? Why must the total probability mass over counterfactual pairs (for the concept) be constant on the hyperplane Λ_W(c) = {λ: β_W⊤λ = c}? (Answer: P^Z_λ is the distribution over off-target tokens/items. Constancy follows because the probe's output σ(β_W⊤λ+b_W) is constant on Λ_W(c), so the mass allocated to the concept's counterfactual pairs does not change, isolating the off-target KL term.)

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map:**  
  Softmax Output Layer (takes λ, outputs P_λ) -> Dual Space Φ (φ(λ)=E[γ|λ]) -> Linear Probe β_W (trained to predict binary concept W) -> Steering Controller (computes φ_target = φ(λ₀) + tβ_W and solves for λ update via regularized Newton step).

- **Critical path:**  
  1. Identify a softmax-based model (LLM final layer, CLIP contrastive head).  
  2. Obtain or train a linear probe β_W for the target binary concept.  
  3. For a given context embedding λ₀, compute φ(λ₀)=E[γ|λ₀] via full or approximate softmax.  
  4. Set target dual coordinate φ_target = φ(λ₀) + t·β_W based on desired concept shift.  
  5. Solve for λ update: find ∆λ such that ∇A(λ)∆λ ≈ β_W. Use Σ = Cov[γ|λ] as Hessian. Regularize: (Σ + αI)v = β_W, then λ ← λ + η·v/||v||.  
  6. Iterate until β_W⊤λ reaches target value c.

- **Design tradeoffs:**  
  - **Dual vs. Euclidean steering:** Dual preserves off-target distributions (forward KL optimal) but requires careful regularization to handle the bounded dual space (convex hull). Euclidean is simpler (direct addition) but minimizes reverse KL, causing off-target drift.  
  - **Regularization strength α:** Too small → singular Σ near dual boundary, step fails; too large → v deviates from true dual direction, slowing convergence or distorting off-target.  
  - **Covariance approximation:** Exact Cov[γ|λ] needs full softmax; Top-K approximations reduce compute but misestimate tail contributions, biasing the dual direction.  
  - **Probe quality:** Theoretical guarantee assumes a perfect, concept-factorizing probe; real probes (e.g., mean-difference vectors) are approximations, leading to performance gaps.

- **Failure signatures:**  
  - **Hessian rank-deficiency:** Σ near-singular when P_λ concentrates on few tokens; step v yields little movement in φ (low cos(∆φ, β_W)).  
  - **Off-target KL rise despite target success:** Target concept probability reaches 1 but off-target KL or counterfactual mass degrades; indicates probe β_W is entangled with off-target features or concept-factorization violated.  
  - **Rank instability:** Off-target token rankings change drastically even if total off-target mass is preserved, signaling distributional deformation within the off-target set.

- **First 3 experiments:**  
  1. **Interpolation validation:** For two context embeddings λ_A, λ_B, compute primal (λ_t) and dual (φ_t) interpolation paths at t=0.5. Inspect top-k outputs: primal midpoint should show intersection (AND) of A and B's top tokens; dual midpoint should show union (OR).  
  2. **Dual steering path geometry:** During iterative dual steering, track cos(φ(λ_{t+1}) - φ(λ_t), β_W). Expect low cosine initially (regularization pulls toward higher entropy), rising as variance increases, possibly dipping near boundary. Contrast with Euclidean steering where cosine is consistently lower.  
  3. **Off-target preservation comparison:** For test contexts, run Euclidean and dual steering until target probability ≈1. Plot (a) sum of counterfactual pair probabilities, (b) off-target KL D_KL(P^Z₀ || P^Z_t), and (c) weighted rank difference vs. target probability. Dual should maintain constant counterfactual mass and lower KL/rank difference; Euclidean should degrade these metrics as target increases.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. How does the information geometry of the final softmax layer propagate to intermediate layers in transformer models?
2. Under what specific conditions does dual steering fail to preserve off-target distributions when the probing assumption (constant concept probability on the hyperplane) is violated?
3. Can the dual steering framework be extended to architectures with multiple interacting softmax operations, such as multi-head attention?
4. How realistic is the concept-factorability assumption (Definition 2) for natural binary concepts in LLMs and CLIP?

## Limitations
- Probe quality: Linear probes may not capture abstract concepts; mean-difference directions are imperfect approximations.
- Concept factorization: Assumption that P^W_λ is constant on β_W⊤λ=c hyperplanes is fragile; entangled concepts cause off-target distortion.
- Dual boundary: Covariance Σ becomes singular near the convex hull boundary, requiring regularization that may bias the step direction.

## Confidence
- **Softmax geometry (Bregman/dually flat):** High (standard information geometry result).
- **Dual steering optimality (forward KL preservation):** Medium (relies on perfect probe and factorization; some empirical off-target drift observed).
- **AND/OR interpolation semantics:** Medium (experiments support but qualitative generalization uncertain).
