# SA-CycleGAN-2.5D: Self-Attention CycleGAN with Tri-Planar Context for Multi-Site MRI Harmonization

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.17219
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17219
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Reduces MMD by 99.1% (1.729 → 0.015) and degrades domain classifier accuracy to near-chance (59.7%) while maintaining cycle SSIM >0.92, effectively removing scanner biases without anatomical distortion.

## Executive Summary
SA-CycleGAN-2.5D introduces a self-attention enhanced CycleGAN for unpaired multi-site MRI harmonization. It processes 2.5D tri-planar slices to embed 3D context, uses a U-ResNet generator with dense self-attention to model global intensity shifts, and employs spectrally-normalized discriminators. Evaluated on 654 glioma scans from heterogeneous (BraTS) and homogeneous (UPenn-GBM) domains, it achieves near-perfect domain confusion (MMD ↓99.1%) and high structural fidelity (cycle SSIM >0.92). Ablation studies confirm self-attention is statistically essential for the challenging heterogeneous-to-homogeneous direction.

## Method Summary
The task is unpaired harmonization of multi-site MRI, translating BraTS (multi-scanner) to UPenn-GBM (single-scanner) images while preserving anatomy. Inputs are 12-channel 2.5D tri-planar slices (four modalities × adjacent z-slices) after standardized preprocessing (N4 bias correction, HD-BET skull-stripping, SRI24 co-registration, z-score normalization). The model uses two coupled SA-CycleGAN-2.5D networks: generators are U-ResNets with 3 encoder stages, 9 residual blocks (blocks 4–6 and a global module use self-attention, all blocks have CBAM), and decoders with skip connections; discriminators are multi-scale PatchGANs with spectral normalization. The composite loss combines LSGAN adversarial, L1 cycle and identity, and SSIM terms. Training uses Adam, cosine LR decay, mixed precision, and 200 epochs on 70/15/15 subject splits.

## Key Results
- MMD reduced by 99.1% (1.729 → 0.015), indicating near-complete domain alignment.
- Domain classifier accuracy drops to 59.7% (near-chance), confirming effective removal of scanner signatures.
- Cycle SSIM exceeds 0.92, ensuring structural preservation for downstream tasks.
- Ablation: Self-attention yields large, statistically significant improvement (Cohen’s d=1.32, p<0.001) for heterogeneous→homogeneous translation.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Self-attention enables the generator to capture long-range dependencies in scanner-induced field biases that are global and non-local, while the tri-planar 2.5D input provides immediate 3D context to maintain anatomical coherence. Spectral normalization stabilizes discriminator training, preventing mode collapse. The composite loss explicitly balances domain translation (adversarial), anatomical preservation (cycle L1/SSIM), and modality consistency (identity), allowing intensity remapping without structural distortion. CBAM modules further refine local features, complementing global self-attention.

## Foundational Learning
1. **CycleGAN** – Enables unpaired image translation via cyclic consistency; needed because multi-site data lacks paired scans. Quick check: Verify cycle loss reduces without paired data.
2. **Self-Attention** – Models voxel-wise global interactions; needed to capture widespread scanner biases. Quick check: Attention maps should highlight diffuse intensity patterns, not just local textures.
3. **U-ResNet** – Encoder-decoder with residual blocks for stable gradient flow; needed to preserve fine details during translation. Quick check: Skip connections should recover high-frequency details in reconstructed images.
4. **Tri-Planar 2.5D** – Stacks adjacent slices across planes; needed to approximate 3D context with 2D computation. Quick check: Visualize adjacent slice inputs to confirm contextual continuity.
5. **Spectral Normalization** – Constrains discriminator Lipschitz constant; needed to stabilize GAN training. Quick check: Discriminator loss should not oscillate wildly early in training.
6. **Multi-Scale PatchGAN** – Discriminates at multiple receptive fields; needed to align both global domain statistics and local textures. Quick check: Smaller-scale discriminators should capture fine details, larger scales capture overall intensity distributions.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:** Input (12-ch tri-planar slice) → U-ResNet Generator (conv stem → encoder with CBAM → residual blocks with CBAM/SA → global SA → decoder with skip connections → 4-ch output) → SA-CycleGAN-2.5D Translation ↔ Spectrally-normalized Multi-scale PatchGAN Discriminators (two scales) → Adversarial & Cycle Losses.

**Critical path:** Data preprocessing → Construct tri-planar inputs → Train coupled generators and discriminators with composite loss → Evaluate harmonization via MMD, domain classifier, and cycle SSIM/PSNR.

**Design tradeoffs:** 
- 2.5D vs. 3D: 2.5D reduces memory but may miss long-range 3D context; self-attention partially compensates.
- Self-attention placement: Global SA in bottlenecks captures domain biases but increases computation; limited to blocks 4–6 balances cost and benefit.
- Loss weights: High cycle λ (10) preserves anatomy but may impede translation; tuned empirically.

**Failure signatures:** 
- Discriminator accuracy stuck at 0 or 1 → training collapse; adjust loss weights or spectral norm.
- Cycle SSIM <0.85 → anatomical distortion; increase cycle loss weight or reduce model capacity.
- MMD remains high → insufficient domain confusion; check self-attention implementation and data preprocessing consistency.

**Three first experiments:**
1. Train baseline without self-attention (only CBAM) and compare cycle SSIM and MMD to full model.
2. Vary tri-planar padding strategy (mirror vs. zero vs. skip edges) and measure impact on MMD and edge artifacts.
3. Sweep cycle loss weight λ from 5 to 20 while monitoring discriminator accuracy and cycle SSIM to find stability sweet spot.

## Limitations
- Edge handling for tri-planar slices (padding vs. skipping) is unspecified, potentially affecting boundary quality.
- Exact self-attention variant (e.g., multi-head, positional encoding) is ambiguous, leading to implementation variability.
- Hyperparameter sensitivity (loss weights, spectral norm) not systematically analyzed; training stability may depend on seed and hardware.
