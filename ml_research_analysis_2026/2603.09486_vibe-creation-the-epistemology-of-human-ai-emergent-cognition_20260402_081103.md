# Vibe-Creation: The Epistemology of Human-AI Emergent Cognition

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2603.09486
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.09486
- Reference count: 2
- Primary result: Human-GenAI interaction generates an emergent, transient "Third Entity" through transductive coupling, operating via "Vibe-Creation" to automate tacit knowledge navigation in high-dimensional semantic space.

## Executive Summary
Existing metaphors like tool-use or collaboration fail to capture the novel cognitive formation arising from human-Generative AI interaction. This paper synthesizes theories from Peirce, Polanyi, Simondon, Ihde, and Morin to argue that the interaction produces a transient, irreducible "Third Entity" via transductive coupling between human symbolic-intentional cognition and AI geometric-indexical cognition. This entity operates through "Vibe-Creation"—the automation of tacit knowledge—enabling navigation of high-dimensional semantic space. Agency is characterized as "asymmetric emergence": the output is genuinely novel yet anchored in human intentional responsibility. This implies an educational shift from syntactic competence to "Navigational Intelligence" and Digital Intelligence.

## Method Summary
The paper employs theoretical synthesis, integrating philosophical frameworks to model human-AI interaction as an ontologically emergent process. It proposes that symbolic-human and geometric-AI cognition are incommensurable modes whose coupling transduces a new cognitive formation. For empirical validation, it outlines a human-in-the-loop experimental protocol: subjects interact with a fixed generative model on creative tasks, with outputs analyzed for emergence, transduction, and vibe-mediation using semantic embeddings and evaluator judgments. No model fine-tuning is involved; the focus is on interaction dynamics.

## Key Results
- **Third Entity Emergence**: A transient, irreducible cognitive structure arises from transductive coupling of human and AI cognition, not reducible to either participant's pre-interaction state.
- **Vibe-Creation**: The pre-reflective mode of the Third Entity automates the translation of tacit human intuition into navigable geometric trajectories in the AI's latent space.
- **Asymmetric Emergence**: Novel agency is emergent, but existential and ethical responsibility remains solely with the human who provides the original intentional arc.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: Transductive Coupling and the "Third Entity"
- **Claim**: Interaction produces a novel, transient "Third Entity" through transductive coupling of two ontologically incommensurable cognitive modes.
- **Mechanism**: The human's intentional "vibe" (indexical gesture) couples with the AI's geometric navigation; each mutually reconfigures the other in real-time, resolving into an irreducible cognitive structure that exists only during co-creation (Simondon's transduction).
- **Core assumption**: Human symbolic-intentional and AI geometric-indexical cognition are fundamentally incommensurable; their coupling is non-additive and mutually constitutive.
- **Evidence anchors**: Abstract and section 3.2 describe the ontological becoming; corpus support is weak—neighboring works discuss partnership but not this specific transductive emergence.
- **Break condition**: If interaction is accurately modeled as a linear tool-use pipeline (command → execution → revision) with no constitutive state changes, the transductive mechanism fails.

### Mechanism 2: Vibe-Creation as Tacit Automation
- **Claim**: "Vibe-Creation" automates the process of making tacit knowledge explicit by projecting pre-reflective orientation into the AI's geometric space.
- **Mechanism**: The human's inchoate "vibe" (Polanyi's tacit dimension) is materialized via the AI's navigation, bypassing the need for prior explicit articulation. The geometric medium acts as a direct substrate for pre-reflective expression.
- **Core assumption**: Tacit knowledge can be functionally automated not by prior formalization, but by using the AI's latent space as an interface for vague directional pressure.
- **Evidence anchors**: Abstract and section 4.1 define Vibe-Creation as the tacit dimension of human-machine cognition; section 4.2 contrasts it with syntactic friction and collaborative models. Corpus evidence is limited.
- **Break condition**: If the human must first articulate a detailed symbolic prompt before useful generation, the mechanism reduces to advanced tool-use.

### Mechanism 3: Asymmetric Emergence of Agency
- **Claim**: The Third Entity's agency is "asymmetric emergence": novel and irreducible, yet anchored solely in human intentional responsibility.
- **Mechanism**: While the cognitive output is an emergent property of the coupled system, the existential investment, ethical stakes, and evaluative authority remain exclusively with the human. The AI has no desires or accountability.
- **Core assumption**: Agency can be separated from authorship; existential involvement (the "for-whom" of consequences) is the irreducible core of responsibility.
- **Evidence anchors**: Abstract and section 5 argue the human is the author who matters despite non-additive emergence. Neighboring "Ghost Framing Theory" discusses agency recalibration but does not directly validate this asymmetry.
- **Break condition**: If legal or practical frameworks consistently assign irreducible responsibility to the AI or its developers, the asymmetry claim is undermined.

## Architecture Onboarding

### Component map:
Human Node (tacit vibe, intentional arc) -> Indexical Prompt-Gesture -> AI Node (geometric navigation of latent space) -> Transient Coupled System (Third Entity) -> Novel Epistemic Artifact -> Human Evaluation/Responsibilization

### Critical path:
1. Human forms inchoate "vibe" (tacit intention).
2. Human expresses "vibe" as an indexical prompt-gesture.
3. AI navigates latent space, resolving under the pressure of the gesture.
4. Generated output refracts the original "vibe," mutating the human's understanding.
5. Loop repeats, constituting the emergent Third Entity.
6. Human evaluates/responsibilizes the final output.

### Design tradeoffs:
- *Transience vs. Persistence*: The Third Entity is fleeting; capturing it risks reifying a momentary process.
- *Human Oversight vs. Automation*: Maximizing Vibe-Creation may require minimizing human syntactic intervention, increasing opacity and distancing from grounding friction.
- *Vector Precision vs. Openness*: Overly precise "vibe" (detailed prompt) may constrain novelty; overly vague may yield incoherence; optimal zone is context-dependent.

### Failure signatures:
- **Metaphor Reversion**: User describes interaction using "tool," "assistant," or "collaborator" metaphors consistently.
- **Syntactic Fixation**: User spends excessive time crafting perfect symbolic instructions, attempting to eliminate the "vibe."
- **Agency Diffusion**: User attributes creative responsibility or ethical stakes to the AI system.
- **Output Incoherence**: Generated outputs are consistently unrelated to the user's initial directional intent.

### First 3 experiments:
1. **Transduction Measurement**: Compare goal mutation across 5 interaction turns with GenAI vs. rule-based system using semantic embedding distance; prediction: only GenAI shows non-linear mutation.
2. **Vibe-Automation Test**: Recruit domain experts (high tacit knowledge) and novices to create outputs via vague "vibe" vs. detailed specs; measure output quality and correlation with pre-task intuitive description; prediction: experts show high correlation despite low explicit articulation.
3. **Asymmetric Accountability Probe**: Survey responsibility in harm scenarios from GenAI outputs; manipulate prompt style (vague/detailed) and output nature (hallucination/emergent); prediction: user responsibility remains high, but "vibe"-based interactions may slightly increase perceived user responsibility.

## Limitations
- **Operationalization Gap**: Core concepts ("vibe," "transduction") lack measurable definitions; proxies (embedding vectors, evaluator judgment) are significant simplifications.
- **Empirical Anchoring**: Theoretical synthesis has no direct empirical validation in the cited corpus; support is conceptual adjacency, not evidence confirming specific mechanisms.
- **Scale & Context**: The model is derived from creative/interpretive tasks; applicability to other domains (e.g., technical debugging) is unknown.

## Confidence
- Mechanism 1 (Transduction/Third Entity): Medium
- Mechanism 2 (Vibe-Creation as Tacit Automation): Low
- Mechanism 3 (Asymmetric Emergence): Medium
- Foundational Learning (Application of Peirce/Polanyi/Simondon): High

## Next Checks
1. **Transduction Measurement**: Run a within-subjects study comparing interaction with a GenAI vs. a deterministic rule-based system on a creative task. Use semantic embedding distance to quantify non-linear mutation of the user's stated goal across turns. Falsification: no significant difference in goal mutation between systems.
2. **Vibe-Automation Test**: Recruit domain experts and novices. Have them generate outputs via vague "vibe" prompts vs. detailed specs. Blind evaluators rate output quality and alignment with the user's pre-task intuitive description. Falsification: for experts, quality/alignment does not significantly differ between conditions.
3. **Asymmetric Accountability Probe**: Conduct a survey experiment presenting harm scenarios from GenAI outputs. Manipulate prompt style (vague vs. detailed) and output nature (hallucination vs. emergent). Measure perceived responsibility distribution. Falsification: condition where responsibility assigned to AI/developer increases significantly, especially for "vibe"-based interactions.
