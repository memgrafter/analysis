# Xuanwu: Evolving General Multimodal Models into an Industrial-Grade Foundation for Content Ecosystems
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2603.29211
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29211
- **Reference count**: 40
- **Primary result**: Xuanwu VL-2B achieves an average score of 67.90 across seven OpenCompass multimodal metrics, a 94.38% average recall on seven business moderation tasks, and an 82.82% weighted recall on adversarial OCR, outperforming InternVL 3.5 2B and Gemini-2.5-Pro within a 2B parameter budget.

## Executive Summary
Xuanwu VL-2B is a compact vision-language model designed for industrial content moderation and adversarial robustness. It combines a lightweight InternViT-300M vision encoder, an MLP connector, and the Qwen3 1.7B language model. A three-stage training pipeline (pre-training, mid-training, post-training) balances specialization with general capability retention. Results show state-of-the-art performance on specialized moderation and adversarial OCR benchmarks, while maintaining strong general multimodal scores.

## Method Summary
The architecture uses InternViT-300M to encode images into visual tokens, which are projected via an MLP into the language model’s embedding space. Qwen3 1.7B processes the combined multimodal representations for generation or classification. The three-stage training begins with large-scale general multimodal pre-training to establish alignment. Mid-training then introduces domain-specific data for content moderation and adversarial scenarios, focusing on fine-grained perception and noise robustness. Finally, post-training fine-tunes the model on task-specific supervised data and alignment objectives to optimize industrial performance while preserving broad competencies.

## Key Results
- OpenCompass average score: **67.90** across seven multimodal metrics, exceeding InternVL 3.5 2B (64.27).
- Business moderation tasks: **94.38%** average recall across seven tasks.
- Adversarial OCR: **82.82%** weighted recall, outperforming Gemini-2.5-Pro (76.72%) within the 2B parameter budget.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Generalist models often fail at fine-grained perception and long-tail noise handling in industrial moderation. Xuanwu’s staged training mitigates catastrophic forgetting: pre-training retains general knowledge, mid-training injects domain robustness, and post-training aligns the model to specific tasks. The compact architecture (InternViT-300M + MLP + Qwen3 1.7B) reduces overfitting and enables deployment under strict resource constraints. This combination yields a model that excels on targeted industrial benchmarks without sacrificing general multimodal competence.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: InternViT-300M -> MLP -> Qwen3 1.7B  
- **Critical path**: Image → InternViT-300M → visual tokens → MLP projection → combine with text query → Qwen3 1.7B → output. Training pipeline: pre‑train (general data) → mid‑train (moderation data) → post‑train (task-specific alignment).  
- **Design tradeoffs**:  
  - Compactness vs. capacity: smaller model reduces compute but may limit complex reasoning.  
  - Specialization vs. generality: mid‑training improves moderation but risks forgetting general knowledge; post‑training attempts to balance.  
  - Data dependency: model quality hinges on curated moderation datasets; bias in data leads to biased decisions.  
- **Failure signatures**:  
  - Degraded performance on simple natural images (overfitting to noise).  
  - High false‑negative rate on subtle adversarial content.  
  - Inability to handle out‑of‑domain visual styles (e.g., cartoons, memes).  
- **Three first experiments**:  
  1. Benchmark on the OpenCompass suite to verify the reported average score and compare with InternVL 3.5 2B.  
  2. Stress test on a comprehensive adversarial OCR benchmark (various fonts, backgrounds, distortions) to measure robustness.  
  3. Ablation study: remove mid‑training or post‑training stages to quantify their contributions to moderation recall and general metrics.
