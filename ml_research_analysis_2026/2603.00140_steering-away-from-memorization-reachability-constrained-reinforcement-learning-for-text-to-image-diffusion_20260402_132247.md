# Steering Away from Memorization: Reachability-Constrained Reinforcement Learning for Text-to-Image Diffusion

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2603.00140
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00140
- Reference count: 15
- Primary result: RADS achieves a superior Pareto frontier between memorization reduction, image quality, and prompt alignment on Stable Diffusion v1.4 without model retraining.

## Executive Summary
Text-to-image diffusion models frequently memorize training data, raising privacy and copyright concerns. Current mitigation strategies either degrade generation quality or require invasive model modifications. This paper introduces RADS (Reachability-Aware Diffusion Steering), a method that frames the denoising process as a controlled dynamical system and uses reachability analysis to identify a backward reachable tube (BRT) of states that inevitably lead to memorization. At inference time, a constrained reinforcement learning policy steers caption embeddings away from this unsafe region while maximizing prompt alignment. Operating entirely without altering the diffusion model’s weights, RADS attains state-of-the-art trade-offs on standard benchmarks, significantly reducing memorization (as measured by SSCD) while preserving image quality (FID) and text-image alignment (CLIP score).

## Method Summary
RADS comprises two offline-trained components: a VAE that compresses CLIP text embeddings into a 64-dimensional latent action space, and a reinforcement learning policy based on constrained soft actor-critic (SAC). The denoising process is formalized as a Markov decision process where the state consists of the current latent image, timestep, and VAE-encoded caption. A safety critic estimates the worst-case reachability value relative to a failure set defined by the classifier-free guidance norm, while a task critic estimates the CLIP-based reward. The policy learns perturbations to the caption embedding that maximize the task reward subject to a safety constraint (via a Lagrange multiplier), ensuring trajectories avoid the BRT. At inference, for each diffusion step, the policy samples a perturbation, which is added to the current caption latent, decoded, and fed to the frozen diffusion model to produce the next latent image.

## Key Results
- On Stable Diffusion v1.4 with the Webster dataset, RADS achieves SSCD_target of 0.2303, FID of 31.57, and CLIP score of 0.2917.
- RADS occupies the upper-right frontier of the SSCD–FID–CLIP trade-off, outperforming state-of-the-art baselines in balancing memorization reduction with generation fidelity.
- The method generalizes robustly to unseen prompts and unseen datasets (MemBench) with minimal added inference latency.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** RADS identifies latent states that inevitably lead to memorization via a backward reachable tube (BRT) computation.
- **Mechanism:** The denoising process is modeled as a controlled dynamical system \(s_{t+1} = f(s_t, u_t, \omega_t)\). Memorization is framed as a safety violation: a failure set \(\mathcal{F}\) is defined using the classifier-free guidance norm \(||\epsilon_\theta(x_\tau, e'_c) - \epsilon_\theta(x_\tau, \emptyset)||_2\). Reachability analysis computes the BRT \(\mathcal{B}\)—the set of initial states \(s_0\) from which memorization is unavoidable regardless of control.
- **Core assumption:** Once a trajectory enters \(\mathcal{B}\), it deterministically collapses into memorization; avoiding \(\mathcal{B}\) early suffices to prevent memorization.
- **Evidence anchors:** [abstract] "models the denoising process as a dynamical system... uses reachability analysis to identify the backward reachable tube of memorization." [section 4.2] Defines \(\mathcal{B}\) mathematically (Eq. 3) and instantiates the target function \(\ell(s)\) using guidance norm (Eq. 5). [corpus] Corpus shows related work on "attraction basins" (Jain et al., 2025) and memorization subspaces (paper 146922), but direct BRT computation is unique to RADS.
- **Break condition:** If memorization trajectories are highly stochastic or the guidance norm threshold fails to separate safe/unsafe states, the BRT approximation becomes unreliable and steering may fail.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** A constrained reinforcement learning policy learns minimal perturbations to caption embeddings that steer the denoising trajectory away from the BRT while preserving prompt alignment.
- **Mechanism:** The policy \(\pi_\phi\) outputs perturbations \(u_t\) in a compressed 64-D latent action space (via VAE). The optimization is a constrained MDP with a sparse terminal reward \(r(s_T)\) based on CLIP image-text similarity. A safety critic \(Q^{\text{safe}}_\psi\) estimates the worst-case reachability value and is enforced via a Lagrange multiplier \(\lambda\) to ensure \(Q^{\text{safe}} \geq \delta\).
- **Core assumption:** Perturbing caption embeddings (rather than latent states) can influence the trajectory early enough (within the first 2 steps, per Fig. 3) to escape memorization basins.
- **Evidence anchors:** [section 4.4] Details the CMDP formulation, SAC with Lagrangian, and the safety critic training (Eqs. 7–9). [section 4.1] Justifies caption space control: "memorization dominates the image latents within the first few steps." [corpus] Neighbors like Wagenmaker et al. (2025) use RL for steering, but RADS uniquely couples it with reachability constraints.
- **Break condition:** If the VAE compression loses critical steering directions or the CLIP reward misaligns with human perception, the policy may learn ineffective or semantically distorted perturbations.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** RADS mitigates memorization entirely at inference time without modifying the diffusion model’s weights.
- **Mechanism:** The pre-trained diffusion backbone \(f_{\text{DM}}\) remains frozen. Only the VAE and RL policy are trained offline. At inference, the policy provides time-varying perturbations to caption embeddings, actively steering each denoising step.
- **Core assumption:** Memorization can be circumvented solely by manipulating input conditioning; the model’s internal weights do not require unlearning.
- **Evidence anchors:** [abstract] "without modifying the diffusion backbone, offering a plug-and-play solution." [section 2] Contrasts with weight-based unlearning (e.g., Gandikota et al., 2023) and emphasizes inference-time nature. [section 5.1] Baselines are inference-time methods; training excludes retraining approaches. [corpus] Corpus includes unlearning methods (e.g., Kumari et al., 2023) that RADS explicitly avoids.
- **Break condition:** If memorization is deeply embedded in the model’s parameterization and cannot be overridden by input perturbations (e.g., for highly overfitted examples), steering may be insufficient.

## Foundational Learning
- **Dynamical systems formulation of diffusion**
  - Why needed: Reachability analysis applies to dynamical systems; modeling denoising as \(s_{t+1}=f(s_t,u_t)\) is prerequisite.
  - Quick check: Given state \(x_t\), caption embedding \(e_c\), and timestep \(t\), what is the update rule \(f_{\text{DM}}\)? How does perturbing \(e_c\) affect \(x_{t-1}\)?
- **Backward reachable tube (BRT)**
  - Why needed: BRT characterizes memorization basins; understanding it is central to the safety constraint.
  - Quick check: If failure set \(\mathcal{F} = \{ s \mid \ell(s) \leq 0 \}\), what does \(\mathcal{B} = \{ s_0 \mid \forall u, \exists \sigma: s_\sigma \in \mathcal{F} \}\) mean in words?
- **Constrained Markov Decision Process (CMDP)**
  - Why needed: RADS formulates mitigation as maximizing CLIP reward subject to \(Q^{\text{safe}} \geq \delta\).
  - Quick check: How does the Lagrange multiplier \(\lambda\) balance task reward and safety? What does \(\nabla_\lambda J = \mathbb{E}[Q^{\text{safe}}] - \delta\) do?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: VAE encoder -> z_t (64-D) -> policy π_φ outputs u_t -> z_t+u_t -> VAE decoder -> steered caption embedding e'_c -> frozen diffusion model f_DM -> next latent x_{t-1} -> state update (x_{T-t-1}, T-t-1, z_t+u_t). Critics Q_task and Q_safe observe state-action pairs.
- **Critical path**: 
  1. Input caption c, initial noise x_T, CLIP text embedding e_c.
  2. Encode: z_0 = Enc(e_c).
  3. For t = 0 to T-1 (T = total diffusion steps, e.g., 50):
     - State s_t = (x_{T-t}, T-t, z_t).
     - Sample action u_t ∼ π_φ(s_t).
     - Update latent: z_{t+1} = z_t + u_t.
     - Decode: e'_c = Dec(z_{t+1}).
     - Denoise: x_{T-t-1} = f_DM(x_{T-t}, e'_c, T-t).
  4. Terminal reward: CLIP score between final image and prompt c.
- **Design tradeoffs**:
  - *Caption vs. latent steering:* Caption chosen because memorization is determined in first ~2 steps (Fig 3); latent steering would be too late.
  - *Action space compression:* 64-D VAE balances expressiveness and sample efficiency; higher dims increase RL difficulty.
  - *Sparse terminal reward:* Avoids shaping artifacts but requires more exploration; SAC entropy term mitigates.
- **Failure signatures**:
  - High SSCD despite λ > 0: safety critic inaccurate or policy stuck.
  - Low CLIP score: λ too high (over-constraint) or VAE reconstruction loss poor.
  - High latency: VAE decoding overhead (mitigated by lightweight MLP policy).
- **First 3 experiments**:
  1. **Pareto frontier** (Table 1, Fig 4): Compare SSCD, FID, CLIP across baselines on Webster dataset; RADS occupies upper-right.
  2. **Diversity across seeds** (Fig 7): Show RADS generates varied outputs for same prompt; prior methods collapse.
  3. **Challenging prompts** (Fig 8): Test on hard memorization cases (e.g., "Bloodborne"); RADS consistently avoids memorization while preserving semantics.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Open Question 1:** How can RADS avoid semantic drift on unseen prompts when trained on limited memorized data?  
  Basis: "This small, skewed distribution can lead to overfitting ... causing semantic drift" (Section 8).  
  Why unresolved: The policy overfits to the 430 training prompts, leading to incorrect steering for out-of-distribution prompts sharing tokens.
- **Open Question 2:** Can RADS achieve zero-shot memorization mitigation without offline training?  
  Basis: "RADS requires a training phase... it introduces an offline dependency that zero-shot methods avoid" (Section 8).  
  Why unresolved: Training adds time and data requirements, while zero-shot baselines exist but trade off performance.
- **Open Question 3:** Is the RADS framework adaptable to other constraints (e.g., NSFW, copyright) beyond memorization?  
  Basis: "RADS establishes a general approach for safe generative control adaptable to other constraints, such as copyrighted or NSFW content" (Section 7).  
  Why unresolved: The target function ℓ and failure set are tailored to guidance magnitude, which may not generalize.
- **Open Question 4:** Does RADS offer theoretical safety guarantees regarding memorization avoidance?  
  Basis: RADS uses reachability analysis but approximates the BRT via a learned safety critic, so no formal bounds are provided.  
  Why unresolved: The learning-based approximation lacks provable properties; deployments may require high-confidence guarantees.

## Limitations
- Missing implementation details (VAE mix ratio, SAC hyperparameters, state representation, BRT computation specifics) hinder exact reproduction.
- The backward reachable tube approximation assumes deterministic memorization once entered, which may not hold under stochastic diffusion processes.
- The fixed guidance-norm threshold for failure set definition may be sensitive and not generalize across models or datasets.

## Confidence
- Reachability analysis identifies memorization basins: **Medium** (formulation clear but threshold and linearization limitations).
- RL steering preserves quality: **Medium** (strong results but reproduction issues and potential overfitting to training captions).
- Inference-time only mitigation: **High** (no weight modifications, experiments confirm).
