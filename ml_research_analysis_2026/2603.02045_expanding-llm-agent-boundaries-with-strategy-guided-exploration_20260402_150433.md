# Expanding LLM Agent Boundaries with Strategy-Guided Exploration

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv:** 2603.02045
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.02045
- **Refs:** 40
- **Result:** +27% avg success, exceeds base model pass@k.

## Executive Summary
RL with LLM agents fails in sparse-reward environments due to limited exploration. SGE shifts to high‑level strategies, uses mixed‑temperature (high τ for strategy, low τ for action) and reflection. Across four domains, SGE beats EntropyAdv and RND by 27% avg success, surpasses base model max pass@k, and generalizes better.

## Method Summary
SGE modifies GRPO: generate high‑τ (≈1.2) strategy, sample low‑τ (≈0.7) actions conditioned on it, execute, store final strategy in success/failure buffers, with probability reflect on sampled past strategies (p=0.25 fail, 0.1 success, 0.5 AppWorld), then GRPO update from parallel rollouts.

## Key Results
- +27% relative final success vs baselines on average.
- Surpasses base model max pass@k, solving previously unsolvable tasks.
- Better generalization to unseen test tasks.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Strategy‑Space Exploration
Distinct strategies yield non‑overlapping action sets, targeting different outcomes and exploiting LLM planning.

### Mixed‑Temperature Decoupling
High τ for strategies diversifies plans; low τ for actions preserves precision and avoids noise.

### Strategy Reflection
Past outcomes condition new strategies, enabling iterative refinement and escape from local optima.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:** LLM→mixed‑temp sampler→buffers→reflection→GRPO.

**Critical path:** 1) optionally prepend reflected strategy; 2) high‑τ strategy; 3) low‑τ actions; 4) execute; 5) store strategy; 6) GRPO update.

**Design tradeoffs:** τ gap (diversity vs coherence); buffer size (freshness vs coverage); reflection prob (exploration vs compute/staleness).

**Failure signatures:** No pass@1 gain; repetitive strategies; incoherent actions; unstable loss.

## Limitations
- Heavy compute (large token budgets, parallel rollouts).
- Complex environment setup (emulators, simulators).
- Dependence on specific Qwen model families.
