# When the Pure Reasoner Meets the Impossible Object: Analytic vs. Synthetic Fine-Tuning and the Suppression of Genesis in Language Models

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.19265
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19265
- **Reference count:** 1
- **Primary result:** Fine-tuning on logical contradictions catastrophically suppresses a model's capacity for generative concept formation (Genesis) when faced with impossible objects, while increasing arbitrary "Pick-One" dogmatism.

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates the behavioral and mechanistic consequences of fine-tuning a language model (Llama-3.1-8B) on logical contradictions (A ∧ ¬A) versus analytic tautologies (A=A). Conflict fine-tuning leads to a dramatic drop in the model's ability to generate novel, coherent concepts (e.g., inventing "cylinder" for a "square-circle"), a phenomenon the authors term "Genesis," from 9.0% to 1.0%. Concurrently, the model becomes more dogmatically prone to arbitrarily select one incompatible property ("Pick-One"), rising from 3.6% to 30.8%. Mechanistic probing of the final hidden layer suggests conflict fine-tuning stratifies the latent space and creates a topological collapse specifically at prompts requiring synthesis, implying a fractured representational manifold that blocks traversal toward synthetic solutions. The authors conclude that training on brute contradictions without a mediating dialectical process impairs the model's generative capacity for resolving conceptual tensions.

## Method Summary
The study employs a single-model, single-adapter experimental design. The base model (Llama-3.1-8B) is fine-tuned on two distinct corpora: one containing logical contradictions (A ∧ ¬A) and another containing analytic tautologies (A=A). Behavioral evaluation is conducted via a large-N (1,500 trials) test battery measuring responses to prompts for impossible objects (e.g., "square-circle"). Responses are categorized as "Genesis" (inventing a new, coherent super-concept), "Pick-One" (dogmatically choosing one contradictory property), or other. Mechanistic analysis compares the final-layer hidden representations for impossible-object prompts between the base and conflict-fine-tuned models using cosine similarity matrices and PCA heatmaps to infer latent space topology. Crucially, the paper provides no details on the fine-tuning dataset composition, prompt templates, scoring rubric, or hyperparameters, preventing exact replication.

## Key Results
- **Genesis collapse:** The rate of creative, synthetic concept generation for impossible objects plummets from 9.0% (base model) to 1.0% after conflict fine-tuning (p < .0001).
- **Dogmatism surge:** The rate of arbitrary "Pick-One" responses (selecting one pole of the contradiction) surges from 3.6% to 30.8% under conflict fine-tuning.
- **Latent space fracture:** Final-layer probing shows conflict fine-tuning stratifies representations into distinct condition clusters and induces a topological collapse localized at the synthesis prompt, suggesting a blocked traversal pathway.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The paper proposes that fine-tuning on bare logical contradictions (A ∧ ¬A) without any examples of their resolution forces the model to treat contradiction as a terminal, non-navigable state. Mechanistically, this is suggested to induce a "topological collapse" or "fractured manifold" in the latent space, specifically around prompts that demand generative synthesis (e.g., impossible objects). The stratification observed in the final-layer cosine similarity heatmaps implies that representations for contradictory and tautological contexts are pushed into segregated clusters, with the synthetic prompt region becoming a topological void or barrier. This structural change allegedly blocks the representational pathways needed to traverse from the tension (A and ¬A) to a novel, superseding concept (Genesis). Consequently, the model defaults to a simpler, dogmatic "Pick-One" heuristic, selecting one contradictory term arbitrarily. The mechanism is inferred from correlational final-layer geometry, not established causally.

## Foundational Learning
*   **Concept:** Logical Contradiction (A ∧ ¬A)
    *   **Why needed:** The core training stimulus whose processing effect is being compared to analytic statements.
    *   **Quick check:** Can the model be prompted to produce an output that coherently instantiates both A and ¬A simultaneously?
*   **Concept:** Analytic Tautology (A=A)
    *   **Why needed:** The control training condition representing non-contradictory, identity-based reasoning.
    *   **Quick check:** Does the model's response to "A is A" show stable, predictable token patterns?
*   **Concept:** Genesis (Generative Synthesis)
    *   **Why needed:** The key dependent behavior—inventing a new concept that transcends/implicitly resolves a contradiction.
    *   **Quick check:** For "square-circle," does the model output a term like "cylinder" that is neither square nor circle but a coherent synthesis?
*   **Concept:** Pick-One Dogmatism
    *   **Why needed:** The primary maladaptive behavioral alternative to Genesis, indicating failure to synthesize.
    *   **Quick check:** For "square-circle," does the model arbitrarily output "square" or "circle" without acknowledging the conflict?
*   **Concept:** Latent Space Topology / Manifold
    *   **Why needed:** The proposed locus of the mechanistic change—the geometric structure of the model's internal representations.
    *   **Quick check:** Do PCA or similarity visualizations show contiguous regions for similar concepts versus disjoint clusters after fine-tuning?
*   **Concept:** Dialectical Mediation
    *   **Why needed:** The proposed missing element in the conflict training regimen that would preserve Genesis capacity.
    *   **Quick check:** Would training on *pairs* of (contradiction, its resolved synthesis) prevent the observed collapse?

## Architecture Onboarding
*   **Component Map:** Input Tokens -> Transformer Layers (12+ for Llama-3.1-8B) -> Final Hidden State (last token) -> Output Projection -> Logits. Fine-tuning adapts weights across all layers; probing targets the final hidden state.
*   **Critical Path for Impossibility Resolution:** Prompt Encoding (embeds tension) -> Hierarchical Processing (layers 1-L) -> Final Representation Aggregation (layer L) -> Synthesis Pathway (hypothesized traversal through manifold) -> Token Generation.
*   **Design Tradeoffs:** Fine-tuning updates all parameters, risking catastrophic interference with pre-trained knowledge. The study's design trades architectural generality (only one model, last-layer probes) for depth of behavioral and snapshot-mechanistic analysis on a specific contradiction-handling scenario.
*   **Failure Signatures:** 1) **Behavioral:** Drop in Genesis rate, surge in Pick-One rate. 2) **Representational:** Increased within-condition clustering and decreased cross-condition continuity in final-layer cosine similarity. 3) **Topological:** PCA heatmap shows a "void" or isolated cluster for the impossible-object prompt condition in conflict-fine-tuned model.
*   **First 3 Experiments:**
    1.  Replicate fine-tuning with documented data/hyperparameters and confirm behavioral Genesis suppression on the original test set.
    2.  Vary the impossible-object prompt (e.g., "draw a square-circle" vs. "describe an object that is both a square and a circle") to test prompt-robustness of the effect.
    3.  Extend probing to **all layers** (not just last) for the same conditions to map the temporal evolution of the stratifying/collapse effect.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
### Open Question 1
- **Question:** What constitutes dialectical mediation in LLM fine-tuning, and how can it be operationally implemented to preserve the model's capacity for Genesis when training on contradictions?
- **Basis in paper:** [explicit] The conclusion asserts that training on logical contradictions without dialectical mediation forces the model into a "dogmatic" state, effectively lobotomizing creative synthesis, but dialectical mediation remains undefined and untested.
- **Why unresolved:** The paper identifies dialectical mediation as the missing factor but provides no concrete methodology or empirical evaluation of such an approach.
- **What evidence would resolve it:** Train models using regimens that explicitly incorporate dialectical elements (e.g., paired contradiction-resolution examples, curriculum learning that emphasizes problem-to-solution transitions) and compare Genesis rates and latent space topology against the conflict adapter condition.

### Open Question 2
- **Question:** How does the latent space topology evolve across all layers during conflict fine-tuning, and which specific architectural components (e.g., attention heads, MLP blocks) are causally responsible for the observed stratification and collapse at the synthesis prompt?
- **Basis in paper:** [explicit] The authors acknowledge they probed only the last layer and a single model/adapter set, noting that other layers or models might show different topologies.
- **Why unresolved:** The study's mechanistic analysis is limited to a single slice; the dynamics across layers and the precise neural mechanisms driving the effect are unknown.
- **What evidence would resolve it:** Extend the probe to all layers for the same experimental conditions, and employ causal intervention techniques (e.g., head ablation, activation steering) to isolate which components are necessary for Genesis suppression.

### Open Question 3
- **Question:** Do the findings of Genesis suppression and increased Pick-One dogmatism generalize to other model architectures (e.g., encoder-decoders, different scales) and to other types of impossible objects beyond the geometric square-circle?
- **Basis in paper:** [explicit] The experiment used only Llama-3.1-8B and a single impossible object type, which the authors note as a limitation.
- **Why unresolved:** It is unclear whether the effect is an artifact of the specific model/object or a universal property of conflict fine-tuning on any contradiction.
- **What evidence would resolve it:** Replicate the study with multiple model families (e.g., GPT, Claude) and a diverse corpus of impossible objects (e.g., "married bachelor," "four-sided triangle"), measuring the same behavioral and latent space metrics.

### Open Question 4
- **Question:** How do standard alignment techniques (e.g., RLHF, DPO) that aim to reduce hallucination affect the latent space topology and Genesis capacity compared to brute-force conflict fine-tuning?
- **Basis in paper:** [explicit] The paper did not manipulate RLHF or alignment directly, and the analogy to destructive plasticity is presented as conceptual rather than empirical.
- **Why unresolved:** The relationship between alignment-induced "truthfulness" and the suppression of creative synthesis is unexplored; alignment may produce similar topological scars or a different pattern.
- **What evidence would resolve it:** Compare models trained with common alignment methods to the conflict adapter using the identical Deleuzian probe (behavioral tests and last-layer heatmaps) to determine whether alignment also stratifies the latent space and collapses the synthesis pathway.

## Limitations
- **Unknown Training Data:** The specific construction, size, and diversity of the contradiction (A ∧ ¬A) and tautology (A=A) fine-tuning datasets are unreported, blocking exact replication.
- **Single-Layer Mechanism:** Claims of "topological collapse" rely solely on cosine similarity and PCA of the **final hidden layer**, providing correlational snapshots without causal or full-layer dynamic evidence.
- **Limited Generalization:** Experimental validation is restricted to one model architecture (Llama-3.1-8B) and one impossible object type ("square-circle"), severely constraining claims about universality.

## Confidence
- **Behavioral Suppression (Genesis rate drop 9.0% → 1.0%, p<.0001):** High. Large-N (1,500 trials) statistical testing provides robust evidence for the core behavioral effect.
- **Dogmatism Surge (Pick-One 3.6% → 30.8%):** High. Same as above; the magnitude and significance are clearly reported.
- **Latent Space Stratification/Collapse:** Medium. The presented final-layer similarity heatmaps are consistent with the claim but are indirect, single-slice evidence. The interpretation of a "fractured manifold blocking traversal" extends beyond the demonstrated data.

## Next Checks
1.  **Full Replication of Fine-Tuning:** Train a fresh Llama-3.1-8B model using a **publicly documented dataset and exact hyperparameters** for conflict and analytic conditions, then re-run the exact behavioral battery to confirm the Genesis suppression magnitude.
2.  **Prompt Robustness & Rubric Ablation:** Systematically vary the phrasing of the impossible-object prompt and the operational definition/scoring of "Genesis" (e.g., requiring semantic coherence vs. lexical novelty) to ensure the effect is not an artifact of a specific template.
3.  **Layer-Wise & Causal Mechanistic Probe:** Extend analysis to **all transformer layers** using Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA) and train linear decoders for synthetic concepts. Follow with **activation patching** (e.g., swapping final-layer representations between base and fine-tuned models) to test the causal necessity of the observed final-layer changes for the behavioral outcome.
