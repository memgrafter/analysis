# Beyond the Rabbit Hole: Mapping the Relational Harms of QAnon Radicalization

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2601.17658
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.17658
- **Reference Count:** 19
- **Primary Result:** Distinct QAnon radicalization pathways (personas), derived from loved ones' narratives, are significant predictors of specific emotional harms (anger, disgust, fear, sadness) experienced by those loved ones.

## Executive Summary
This study investigates the often-overlooked interpersonal harms of conspiracy radicalization by analyzing 12,747 personal narratives from the Reddit forum `r/QAnonCasualties`. It develops a data-driven framework to map how a loved one's journey into QAnon unfolds, identifies six probabilistic "radicalization personas" representing archetypal pathways, and statistically links these personas to the specific negative emotions reported by the narrators. The findings demonstrate that the *type* of radicalization—whether perceived as a deliberate ideological shift or a result of personal collapse—systematically influences the emotional toll on relationships, providing the first large-scale empirical quantification of these distinct relational harms.

## Method Summary
The analysis pipeline begins with filtering Reddit posts to retain narratives containing both a narrator and a distinct "object of narration" (the radicalized individual). These narratives are segmented into sentences and processed with BERTopic, a contextual embedding-based clustering method, to extract 461 initial thematic topics. An LLM co-annotation step filters these to 198 topics relevant to the radicalization trajectory, which are then manually organized into 50 thematic traits across three phases: pre-radicalization, trigger, and post-radicalization. Each narrative is represented as a distribution over these 50 traits. A Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) model is applied to this bag-of-traits representation to discover six mixed-membership "personas." The resulting persona probabilities for each narrative undergo an Isometric Log-Ratio (ILR) transformation to create orthogonal predictors, which are used in logistic regressions—alongside demographic controls—to model the binary presence of four narrator emotions (anger, fear, sadness, disgust) as outcomes.

## Key Results
- Six coherent "radicalization personas" emerge, combining traits from different phases (e.g., a "Political Grievance" persona blends pre-existing political disillusionment with post-radicalization aggression).
- Personas significantly predict narrator emotions. Dispositional personas (deliberate ideological choice) increase odds of narrator anger (OR=0.93) and disgust (OR=0.87), while Corporeal-based personas (personal/cognitive collapse) increase odds of fear (OR=1.09) and sadness (OR=1.11).
- The ILR transformation successfully creates interpretable balances between persona groups, enabling the isolation of specific thematic contrasts linked to emotional outcomes.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Thematic Trait Discovery via Contextual Topic Modeling
Recurring thematic traits (pre-conditions, triggers, post-radicalization characteristics) are extracted from heterogeneous personal narratives using BERTopic. Sentences are embedded, dimensionality reduced (UMAP), and clustered (HDBSCAN). An LLM co-annotation framework filters for relevance to the radicalization journey, yielding 50 structured traits. The core assumption is that semantic similarities captured by transformers correspond to meaningful, human-interpretable stages of a radicalization process as narrated. Evidence is provided by the paper's detailed pipeline description. A break condition is highly idiosyncratic, non-semantic language (e.g., heavy neologisms) causing clusters to group by superficial features instead of coherent themes.

### Mechanism 2: Persona Discovery via Mixed-Membership Graphical Modeling
Distinct, data-driven "radicalization personas" (archetypal co-occurrence patterns of traits) are uncovered by applying LDA to the bag-of-traits representation of individual profiles. Each profile's narrative is a distribution over 50 traits; LDA discovers latent topics (personas) explaining co-occurrence patterns. Mixed-membership allows a single profile to blend multiple personas. Model selection (k=6) is guided by topic coherence and qualitative interpretability. The core assumption is that observed radicalization pathways can be decomposed into a limited set of probabilistic generative "themes." Evidence is in the section detailing the LDA vs. NMF comparison and persona definitions. A break condition is if true pathways are not well-approximated by a fixed number of archetypes, leading to forced or incoherent personas.

### Mechanism 3: Linking Personas to Emotional Harm via Compositional Data Regression
The compositional persona probabilities (summing to 1 per profile) are transformed into orthogonal log-ratio balances via Isometric Log-Ratio (ILR). These balances encode nested thematic contrasts (e.g., Situational vs. Dispositional) and serve as independent variables in logistic regressions predicting binary narrator emotions, with controls for relationship and demographics. The core assumption is that emotional response is systematically related to the *type* of radicalization pathway (persona composition). Evidence is the reported odds ratios and the detailed ILR/regression section. Break conditions are: (1) LLM emotion detection fails to reliably distinguish narrator's own emotions from attributed emotions in multi-actor narratives; (2) unmeasured confounders (e.g., pre-existing mental health) correlate with persona composition, making associations spurious.

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept: Compositional Data Analysis (CoDa) and ILR Transformation**
  - **Why needed here:** Persona probabilities are compositional (parts of a whole). Using raw probabilities in regression violates independence assumptions. ILR transforms them into orthogonal, real-valued predictors (balances).
  - **Quick check:** If Profile X is 90% Persona A / 10% Persona B and Profile Y is 10% A / 90% B, a *positive* score on the A-vs-B ILR balance indicates Profile X has relatively more A (and less B) compared to Profile Y.

- **Concept: Mixed-Membership Topic Modeling (LDA) for Archetype Discovery**
  - **Why needed here:** Radicalization pathways are not mutually exclusive; one individual's journey can exhibit traits from multiple archetypes. LDA's mixed-membership naturally handles this by assigning each profile a distribution over latent topics (personas).
  - **Quick check:** In this paper's LDA, a "topic" (persona) is a probability distribution over the 50 thematic traits. A profile's "topic distribution" is its probabilistic blend of these six archetypes.

- **Concept: LLM-Assisted Annotation for Domain-Specific NLP Tasks**
  - **Why needed here:** Filtering radicalization-relevant topics and detecting narrator-specific emotions in niche, multi-actor narratives require deep contextual understanding. LLMs offer a scalable alternative to training custom classifiers.
  - **Quick check:** Two risks are: (1) the LLM conflates the narrator's stated emotions with emotions attributed to the radicalized person; (2) the prompt misses implicit but clear affect in sarcastic or indirect language. Mitigation involves careful prompt design and validation on a human-reviewed sample.

## Architecture Onboarding
Data Ingestion & Filtering -> Trajectory Extraction (BERTopic + LLM filter) -> Persona Modeling (LDA) -> Emotion & Control Annotation -> Statistical Linking (ILR + Logistic Regression). The core analytical flow is linear, with LLM annotation steps as parallel dependencies.

**Critical path:** Filtered Narratives -> BERTopic Traits -> LDA Personas -> ILR Balances -> Regression.

**Design tradeoffs:**
- Chose BERTopic over LDA/NMF for richer semantic clustering of traits, requiring a subsequent LLM relevance filter.
- Chose LDA over NMF for personas to enable probabilistic mixed-membership, accepting slower inference.
- Chose ILR over naive proportions for statistical validity in regression, at the cost of raw interpretability; the Sequential Binary Partition (SBP) design is a crucial, subjective interpretive layer.
- Used LLMs instead of human annotation for scale on emotion/demographics/topic filtering, trading some accuracy for feasibility.

**Failure signatures:**
- **Topic incoherence:** BERTopic yields trivial or semantically scattered topics that survive the LLM filter.
- **Persona degeneracy:** LDA at chosen *k* yields overlapping, non-distinct, or uninterpretable personas.
- **ILR anomaly:** The constructed SBP balances do not cleanly separate meaningful thematic groups, suggesting an incorrect hierarchical partition.
- **Emotion misclassification:** LLM emotion prompt systematically mislabels narrator vs. object-of-narration affect.

**First 3 experiments:**
1.  **Topic Model Validation:** Re-run BERTopic with varied `min_cluster_size`/`min_samples` and compute topic stability (e.g., NMI) across runs. Target: >0.7 average NMI for top 50 traits.
2.  **Persona Coherence & Separation:** Compute pairwise Jensen–Shannon divergence between the six persona-trait distributions. Target: all off-diagonal values >0.3. Manually review top 10 profiles per persona for narrative coherence.
3.  **ILR Orthogonality & SBP Verification:** Calculate the correlation matrix of the five ILR balances. Target: all off-diagonal |*r*| < 0.05. Verify the SBP tree hierarchy matches the textual description in Table 3.

## Limitations
- The domain fit of BERTopic for fragmented Reddit narratives lacks direct validation; performance may degrade with heavy code-switching or non-semantic noise.
- The LDA archetype assumption forces a fixed number (k=6) of mixed-membership personas; true pathways may be more continuous or unique, as suggested by "fractured" personas at higher *k*.
- The Sequential Binary Partition (SBP) tree for ILR is reconstructed from textual descriptions; an incorrect hierarchy would invalidate balance–emotion mappings.
- LLM emotion detection validation is limited to 100 posts; the prompt may conflate narrator and object-of-narration emotions in complex multi-actor stories.

## Confidence
- **Persona discovery (6 archetypes):** Medium. Supported by coherence metrics and qualitative review, but sensitive to *k* and preprocessing choices.
- **Persona–emotion linkage (OR results):** Medium. Statistically significant odds ratios are reported, but dependent on correct ILR construction and LLM emotion labels with limited validation.
- **Trajectory mapping (50 thematic traits):** Low–Medium. Thematic organization is manual post-LLM filtering; no direct evidence that BERTopic clusters perfectly align with human-defined radicalization stages.
