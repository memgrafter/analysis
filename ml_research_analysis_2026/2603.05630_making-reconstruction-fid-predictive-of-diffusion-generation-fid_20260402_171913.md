# Making Reconstruction FID Predictive of Diffusion Generation FID

## Quick Facts
- **ArXiv ID:** 2603.05630
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.05630
- **Reference Count:** 24
- **Primary Result:** Introduces interpolated FID (iFID), which replaces VAE latent reconstruction with linear interpolation to nearest neighbors, achieving strong correlation (Pearson ~0.85-0.89) with diffusion model generation FID (gFID), solving the reconstruction-generation dilemma.

## Executive Summary
The paper addresses the poor correlation between VAE reconstruction FID (rFID) and diffusion model generation FID (gFID), known as the reconstruction-generation dilemma. It proposes interpolated FID (iFID), which computes FID on images decoded from linear interpolations between each latent and its nearest neighbor in the latent space. Evaluated on 13 diverse VAE checkpoints and corresponding diffusion models, iFID shows high Pearson and Spearman correlations with gFID under various settings, including with classifier-free guidance. Additionally, rFID and iFID are found to correlate with different phases of diffusion sampling: rFID with refinement (small timesteps) and iFID with navigation (large timesteps), aligning with the theory that diffusion models generate samples via interpolation of training data.

## Method Summary
iFID modifies the standard rFID computation by replacing direct latent reconstruction (encoding an image, decoding back) with an interpolation step. For each latent vector `z` from an image encoder, its nearest neighbor `z_nn` in the latent training set is identified. Linear interpolation `z_interp = α*z + (1-α)*z_nn` (for `α` in [0,1], typically `α=0.5`) is applied, and the decoded interpolated images are used to compute FID against real data. This approximates the interpolation behavior of diffusion models during sampling. The method is evaluated by training diffusion models (SiT) on latents from various VAEs and correlating iFID with resulting gFID.

## Key Results
- iFID achieves Pearson correlation coefficients of 0.85-0.89 and Spearman ranks of 0.84-0.92 with gFID across 13 VAE checkpoints.
- rFID correlates with diffusion sample quality at small timesteps (refinement phase), while iFID correlates at large timesteps (navigation phase).
- iFID is the first metric demonstrating strong predictive power for diffusion generation FID, outperforming rFID and other latent-based metrics.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Diffusion models are theorized to generate new samples by interpolating between training data in latent space. rFID optimizes for separable, point-accurate latents that can cause hallucination when interpolated, hence poor correlation with gFID. iFID directly measures the validity of such interpolations by decoding midpoints between latents and their neighbors, aligning with the interpolation mechanism of diffusion sampling. This explains why iFID correlates with gFID during the navigation phase (large timesteps), while rFID correlates during refinement (small timesteps) where reconstruction fidelity matters more.

## Foundational Learning
- **Variational Autoencoder (VAE):** Compresses images to latent codes; needed as the latent space provider for diffusion training. Quick check: Encode a sample image and reconstruct it to verify decoder quality.
- **Frechet Inception Distance (FID):** Measures distribution similarity between generated and real images using Inception features; standard for generative model evaluation. Quick check: Compute FID between two small sets of real and generated images.
- **Diffusion Models:** Generative models that iteratively denoise from latent or pixel space; their sampling involves traversing latent manifolds. Quick check: Run a few sampling steps with a pretrained diffusion model.
- **Latent Interpolation:** Blending between two latent vectors to generate intermediate samples; core to understanding diffusion generation. Quick check: Interpolate between two latent codes and decode to observe smoothness.
- **Nearest Neighbor Search:** Finds closest latent vectors in a dataset; used in iFID to define interpolation partners. Quick check: Use cosine similarity to find nearest neighbors for a latent in a precomputed index.
- **Classifier-Free Guidance:** Technique to steer diffusion sampling; evaluated for its effect on iFID stability. Quick check: Sample with and without guidance scales from a diffusion model.

## Architecture Onboarding
Component map: Input Image -> VAE Encoder -> Latent z -> Nearest Neighbor Search (find z_nn) -> Linear Interpolation (z_interp) -> VAE Decoder -> Decoded Interpolates -> FID Computation vs Real Data.

Critical path: Encoding image to z, retrieving z_nn from latent training set, interpolating, decoding, and computing FID with real image statistics.

Design tradeoffs: Interpolation vs reconstruction—interpolation tests manifold smoothness but may introduce blur; nearest neighbor selection impacts relevance (evidenced by average false match rate 0.416). Decoder quality heavily influences iFID, as artifacts affect FID.

Failure signatures: High iFID but low gFID if latent space is smooth but diffusion overfits; low iFID but high gFID if interpolation partners are semantically mismatched; unstable iFID under high classifier-free guidance due to distribution shift.

First experiments:
1. Compute rFID and iFID for a single VAE (e.g., SD-VAE) and compare with a basic diffusion model's gFID to verify correlation direction.
2. Vary interpolation coefficient α (e.g., 0.3, 0.5, 0.7) and measure impact on iFID to assess sensitivity.
3. Test iFID on a subset of VAE checkpoints with known gFID ranges to establish baseline correlation strength.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
The paper does not explicitly call out open questions beyond suggesting future work on broader VAE diversity and guidance effects.

## Limitations
- Limited VAE diversity: Evaluated on only 13 checkpoints from specific families (SD-VAE, IN-VAE, FLUX-VAE), reducing generalizability.
- Imperfect correlation: Maximum Pearson 0.89 implies unexplained variance, possibly due to latent space or decoder artifacts.
- Unexplored effects: Impact of classifier-free guidance on iFID stability remains unanalyzed, and nearest neighbor relevance is moderate (avg FMR 0.416).

## Confidence
- **iFID-gFID correlation strength:** Medium-High (empirical evidence solid but limited model scope)
- **Phase-specific correlation (rFID vs iFID):** Medium (interpretive, requires more diffusion dynamics analysis)
- **Theoretical alignment with interpolation:** Medium (plausible but not independently validated)

## Next Checks
1. Evaluate iFID on at least 10 additional VAE architectures (e.g., VQ-VAE-2, NVAE) across multiple resolutions to test generalizability.
2. Test iFID predictive power for diffusion models trained with different samplers (DDIM, Euler) and varied guidance scales to assess robustness.
3. Measure correlation between iFID and latent space metrics (e.g., local neighborhood density, manifold smoothness) to validate the interpolation validity hypothesis.
