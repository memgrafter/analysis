# Autoregressive Guidance of Deep Spatially Selective Filters using Bayesian Tracking for Efficient Extraction of Moving Speakers

## Quick Facts
- **ArXiv ID:** 2603.23723
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.23723
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Autoregressive guidance reduces mean angular error by 70% (21.65° to 6.47°) and improves speech quality (PESQ 1.93→2.04) and intelligibility (ESTOI 78.2%→80.4%) with negligible computational overhead.

## Executive Summary
This paper tackles the problem of extracting a moving speaker's speech when only an initial direction-of-arrival (DoA) is available, requiring lightweight tracking. It proposes autoregressive (AR) guidance, where the previously enhanced speech from a deep spatial filter is incorporated into Bayesian tracking algorithms (Wrapped Kalman Filter and Bootstrap Particle Filter) to update the DoA estimate. This feedback loop refines the filter's steering direction over time. Evaluated on synthetic data with social force model trajectories and real-world recordings, AR guidance significantly lowers tracking error and boosts perceptual metrics compared to concatenative baselines, with minimal added compute.

## Method Summary
The system combines a deep spatially selective filter (SpatialNet) with Bayesian tracking. SpatialNet enhances speech from multichannel inputs given a DoA estimate. In AR guidance, the tracker uses the prior enhanced speech as an observation to update the DoA, which then steers SpatialNet for the next frame. Two AR variants: MISO-AR modifies the tracker's likelihood to use enhanced speech, while MIMO-AR replaces noisy inputs with multichannel enhanced speech from an extended MIMO SpatialNet. Training uses pseudo-AR fine-tuning, where open-loop results emulate AR during epochs. The method is validated on synthetic LibriSpeech mixtures with social force trajectories and real recordings in reverberant rooms.

## Key Results
- Mean angular error reduced from 21.65° to 6.47° using Bootstrap PF with MIMO-AR.
- PESQ improved from 1.93 to 2.04, ESTOI from 78.2% to 80.4%.
- AR methods consistently outperform concatenative baselines in regional accuracy and perceptual metrics across reverberation conditions.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: Autoregressive Feedback into Bayesian Filtering
Incorporating the previously enhanced speech estimate into the tracker's likelihood provides a cleaner observation than raw noisy input, reducing noise and interference influence on DoA estimation. The enhanced speech serves as a proxy for clean speech, improving tracking accuracy. However, if enhancement quality degrades, the contaminated estimate may mislead the tracker, causing divergence.

### Mechanism 2: MIMO Spatial Filter Extension for Trajectory-Aware Guidance
Extending the spatial filter to MIMO output preserves spatial cues across microphones, enabling the tracker to exploit inter-channel phase differences directly when using enhanced speech as observation. This avoids bandwidth limitations of linear-phase estimators like in the Wrapped KF. The MIMO filter requires fine-tuning with an IPD loss to maintain spatial selectivity; without it, cues degrade, harming tracking.

### Mechanism 3: Social Force Motion Model for Generalizable Tracking
Training on trajectories generated by a social force model—which simulates human-like movement with driving and repulsive forces—produces models that generalize better to unseen real-world motions. The model covers diverse velocities, curvatures, and evasive maneuvers, unlike simple synthetic paths, leading to robust performance across test datasets.

## Architecture Onboarding

*Component map:* SpatialNet (SSF) → Bayesian Tracker (TST) → Feedback Loop (Ŝ_{t-1} to tracker) → Noise Covariance Estimation (PF only)

*Critical path:* Y_t → Tracker (predicts p(θ_t|...)) → θ_t → SSF (F_k(Y_t, θ_t|z_{t-1})) → Ŝ_t → (if AR) Tracker (update with Ŝ_t) → θ_{t+1}. The AR configuration modifies the tracker's update step.

*Design tradeoffs:*
- MISO-AR retains simpler SSF but KF's linear-phase estimator limits bandwidth; MIMO-AR uses full spectrum but requires MIMO SSF fine-tuning with IPD loss.
- Wrapped KF is lightweight (~300k MACs/s) but less accurate; Bootstrap PF is heavier (~2.5M MACs/s) but more flexible.
- Pseudo-AR training avoids non-parallelizable closed-loop training but introduces training-inference mismatch.

*Failure signatures:*
- Tracking drift: MAE increases or spikes after speaker crossings, often due to tracker overfitting to noise or corrupted Ŝ_t.
- Spatial cue degradation: ESTOI/PESQ drop without MAE increase, indicating SSF lost spatial selectivity, common in MIMO without sufficient IPD loss.
- Generalization gap: Performance on real high-T60 data worse than synthetic; social force model may not cover all behaviors.

*First 3 experiments:*
1. Ablation on synthetic data: Compare concatenative, MISO-AR, MIMO-AR with both trackers; measure MAE, ACC, PESQ, ESTOI.
2. Computational cost vs. accuracy: Plot MACs vs. MAE/ACC for all configurations.
3. Real-world generalization: Evaluate on recordings with T60=200/350/800ms; compute Re-ACC, NISQA, WER.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. Can the Kalman filter's bandwidth limitation due to the linear-phase DoA estimator be overcome to use full-spectrum information?
2. How can the trade-off between spatial cue preservation and speech reconstruction in MIMO-AR SSFs be automatically optimized?
3. Does the autoregressive feedback cause error accumulation over long sequences, and can it be mitigated?

## Limitations
- Tracker hyperparameters (e.g., initial covariance, noise ratios) are unspecified, affecting replication.
- Real-world experiments are narrow, with only two speakers in one room.
- Social force model integration details (step size, boundary handling) are missing, hindering dataset reproduction.

## Confidence
- AR guidance improves tracking and enhancement: **High** (synthetic data shows large gains).
- MIMO SSF preserves spatial cues: **Medium** (depends on IPD fine-tuning; trade-offs observed).
- Social force model improves generalization: **Medium** (synthetic validation only; real-world tests limited).

## Next Checks
