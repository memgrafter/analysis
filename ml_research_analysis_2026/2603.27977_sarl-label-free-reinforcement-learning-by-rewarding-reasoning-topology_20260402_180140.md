# SARL: Label-Free Reinforcement Learning by Rewarding Reasoning Topology

## Executive Summary
Structure Aware Reinforcement Learning (SARL) proposes a novel, label-free reinforcement learning framework for training reasoning models. It eliminates the need for verifiable rewards or labeled supervision by constructing a "Reasoning Map" from the intermediate thinking steps of each response. This map is then rewarded for exhibiting a small-world network topology—characterized by high local clustering and short global path lengths—which the authors argue promotes both coherent local steps and efficient global reasoning trajectories. Experiments on the Qwen3-4B model show significant performance gains on math and open-ended tasks compared to ground-truth RL and prior label-free methods, alongside more stable training dynamics (lower KL divergence, higher policy entropy) and more concise responses.

## Key Results
*   On math tasks, SARL with Qwen3-4B achieves average gains of 9.1% (PPO) and 11.6% (GRPO) over ground-truth RL baselines.
*   On open-ended tasks, gains reach 34.6% (PPO) and 30.4% (GRPO) compared to prior label-free methods.
*   Training with SARL shows lower KL divergence and higher policy entropy, suggesting more stable and exploratory learning, and results in shorter model responses.
