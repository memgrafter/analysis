# MedScope: Incentivizing "Think with Videos" for Clinical Reasoning via Coarse-to-Fine Tool Calling
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.13332  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13332  
- **Reference count:** 40  
- **Primary result:** MedScope-7B-RL sets new open-source benchmarks on ClinVideo-Eval (e.g., 64.42 mIoU temporal grounding on OphVL, 65.58 mIoU grounded VQA, 61.20 mIoU on MedVideoCap with 39.80 accuracy) and achieves SOTA on SVU-31K (4.77 detail orientation, 4.36 fine-grained description, 4.56 CIDEr temporal reasoning, 10.93 CIDEr perception).

## Executive Summary
Long clinical videos contain sparse, decisive moments that are crucial for accurate reasoning. Passive frame sampling fails to capture these, limiting iterative evidence seeking and verification. MedScope introduces a tool-augmented LLM framework that interleaves textual reasoning with targeted calls to two simple tools: `crop_video` (dense frames in a time window) and `get_frame` (frames at a timestamp). This enables a coarse-to-fine loop: hypothesize, retrieve precise evidence, and revise. Training leverages ClinVideoSuite—a large-scale, automatically constructed dataset of localized QA and visual-CoT trajectories—and a three-stage pipeline (warm-up SFT, VCoT cold-start SFT, GA-GRPO RL). GA-GRPO reinforces grounding via composite rewards (answer correctness, format compliance, temporal alignment) and evidence‑weighted advantages. Across multiple clinical video benchmarks, MedScope significantly outperforms prior open‑source models, demonstrating effective “thinking with videos” through verifiable, tool‑integrated reasoning.

## Method Summary
MedScope builds on Qwen2.5‑VL‑7B‑Instruct by adding a function‑calling interface for video tools. The training pipeline comprises three stages: (1) **Warm‑up SFT** on mixed video/image data (Video‑R1 CoT, LongVideo‑Reason, surgical VQA) to establish general video reasoning; (2) **Cold‑start SFT** on ClinVideoSuite’s visual‑CoT trajectories (VCoT‑34K + CoT‑84K) to teach tool‐interleaved reasoning; (3) **GA‑GRPO RL** on ClinVideo‑RL data, optimizing a surrogate objective with group‑relative advantages modulated by the fidelity of the last tool call. The tool environment executes `crop_video` or `get_frame` on the input video and inserts returned frames into the conversation. ClinVideoSuite itself is synthesized in three phases: evidence‑centric captioning of video windows, QA generation that forces reliance on local captions (via multi‑model voting), and VCoT trajectory rollout using a teacher model. This end‑to‑end approach equips the policy to actively seek and verify temporal evidence.

## Key Results
- **SVU‑31K:** MedScope achieves SOTA among open‑source models: detail orientation 4.77, fine‑grained description 4.36, CIDEr temporal reasoning 4.56, CIDEr perception 10.93.
- **ClinVideo‑Eval:** MedScope‑7B‑RL reaches 64.42 mIoU (temporal grounding) and 65.58 mIoU (grounded VQA) on OphVL, 61.20 mIoU on MedVideoCap with 39.80 accuracy, and maintains robust out‑of‑domain performance on SurgVidLM.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
1. **Coarse‑to‑fine evidence retrieval** – The model alternates between textual reasoning and tool calls, mimicking a clinician’s skim‑then‑revisit pattern. Starting from a rough hypothesis, it invokes `crop_video` or `get_frame` to fetch temporally precise observations and iteratively refines its hypothesis, overcoming limitations of passive sampling.
2. **Grounding‑aware reinforcement** – GA‑GRPO explicitly rewards tool calls that align with ground‑truth temporal segments (IoU for crops, timestamp proximity for frames) and multiplies trajectory advantages by a fidelity factor. This directly shapes intermediate decisions, preventing reward hacking and encouraging verifiable evidence use.
3. **Evidence‑centric dataset** – ClinVideoSuite provides supervised trajectories linking questions to localized visual cues. Its automatic pipeline enforces grounding through cross‑model voting and multimodal confirmation, yielding high‑quality data for both SFT and RL stages.
Break conditions: if tool invocation frequency is mis‑calibrated (over/under‑use) or grounding reward thresholds are poorly set, evidence seeking may degrade or RL may become unstable.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:** Input Encoder -> Reasoning Policy -> Tool Environment -> Reward Manager -> Training Loop (SFT & RL)

**Critical path:**  
- Inference: Given video + question, the policy emits a sequence:` → optional `<tool call>` → `<tool response>` → repeat until `<answer>`. The tool environment executes `crop_video` or `get_frame` and returns frames.  
- Training: Warm‑up SFT initializes the policy on general video tasks. Cold‑start SFT imitates VCoT trajectories. RL (GA‑GRPO) samples n rollouts per query, computes composite reward (answer accuracy, format compliance, grounding), modulates advantages by last‑tool fidelity, and updates policy via PPO‑style surrogate loss.

**Design tradeoffs:**  
- Tools: `crop_video` gives dense context over an interval; `get_frame` provides sharp detail at a point. The model learns to chain them.  
- Reward shaping: Composite reward balances correctness, format, and grounding; evidence reward (IoU/ tolerance) improves precision but risks overfitting. An IoU‑bonus schedule dynamically adjusts thresholds.  
- Advantage modulation: Fidelity‑weighted advantages stabilize training but require careful tuning of scaling parameters.

**Failure signatures:**  
- Evidence‑overrule failure: Model notes mismatch between retrieved evidence and hypothesis but fails to retry, indicating weak re‑planning.  
- Text‑anchored entity binding drift: Model relies on textual overlays/narration instead of visual details, leading to incorrect grounding when visuals are ambiguous.  
- Tool call inefficiency: Excessive cropping/framing with low IoU suggests poor calibration of search stopping.

**First 3 experiments:**  
1. **Tool‑zero ablation** – Disable tool calls at test time; expect a large drop in grounding mIoU.  
2. **Reward ablation** – Remove `R_evidence` or IoU bonus; measure degradation in temporal precision.  
3. **VCoT cold‑start ablation** – Omit VCoT trajectories in SFT stage; expect lower tool‑use proficiency and unstable RL convergence.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- How can MedScope be extended to handle volumetric (3D) and multi‑view medical imaging?  
- What richer perception tools (segmentation, phase recognition, OCR, structured reporting) should be added, and how can the model learn to orchestrate them effectively?  
- How can physician‑verified evaluation be scaled across diverse institutions and procedures to ensure generalizability and safety?  
- How can reward shaping in GA‑GRPO be made more principled and uncertainty‑aware to improve training stability and prevent overconfident, ungrounded tool usage?

## Limitations
- **Dataset unreleased:** ClinVideoSuite is not public; its automatic synthesis quality and potential biases are difficult to audit.  
- **Hyperparameter opacity:** Exact GA‑GRPO parameters (fidelity modulation, evidence reward thresholds) are not disclosed, hindering exact replication.  
- **Proprietary teacher:** VCoT trajectories use Gemini‑2.5‑pro; open‑source substitutes may degrade quality.  
- **Evaluation scope:** Benchmarks are curated with limited expert input; generalization to unseen clinical settings remains unproven.

## Confidence
- **Tool‑augmented coarse‑to‑fine reasoning:** Medium (mechanism clear but clinical utility hinges on precise tool calibration)  
- **GA‑GRPO alignment:** Medium (novel formulation, but robustness unspecified)  
- **Dataset quality & generalization:** Low (gains coupled to unreleased, auto‑constructed data)

## Next Checks
- **Tool‑zero ablation on public benchmarks:** Evaluate MedScope with tool calls disabled on OphVL/MedVideoCap; a catastrophic drop in mIoU would confirm dependency on active tool use.  
- **Re‑synthesis audit with open‑source models:** Build a small public subset of ClinVideoSuite using open caption/QA models, train a mini‑MedScope, and compare tool‑use patterns to assess synthesis brittleness.  
- **Hyperparameter sweep for GA‑GRPO:** Systematically vary fidelity modulation (α, c, s_min) and evidence reward thresholds; monitor convergence stability, final mIoU, and tool‑call efficiency to identify a robust regime.
