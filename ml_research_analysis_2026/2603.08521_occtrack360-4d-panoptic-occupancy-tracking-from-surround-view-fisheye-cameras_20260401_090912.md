# OccTrack360: 4D Panoptic Occupancy Tracking from Surround-View Fisheye Cameras

## Quick Facts
*   **arXiv ID:** 2603.08521
*   **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08521
*   **Reference Count:** 40
*   **Primary Result:** Introduces OccTrack360, a new benchmark for 4D panoptic occupancy tracking from surround-view fisheye cameras, and proposes FoSOcc which achieves +20.7% OccSQ for general objects on Occ3D-Waymo and improved OccSQ-Overall on OccTrack360.

## Executive Summary
This paper tackles the challenge of 4D panopic occupancy tracking—predicting temporally consistent, instance-labeled 3D occupancy voxels—from surround-view fisheye cameras. The core contribution is the introduction of the **OccTrack360 benchmark**, built from KITTI-360, which provides long video sequences (174–2234 frames) with instance-level voxel annotations and principled masks for all-direction occlusion and fisheye field-of-view. To establish a baseline, the authors propose the **FoSOcc framework**, featuring a Center Focusing Module (CFM) for robust instance localization under distortion and a Spherical Lift Module (SLM) for accurate 2D-to-3D lifting using the fisheye camera model. Results show significant occupancy quality gains on Occ3D-Waymo and OccTrack360, though with a noted trade-off in tracking metrics.

## Method Summary
The task is defined as predicting a sequence of voxel grids (128×128×16, 0.2m resolution) where each voxel has a semantic class and a unique instance ID. Inputs are multi-view fisheye images, their MEI model intrinsics (with mirror parameter ξ), extrinsics, and corresponding labels. Key outputs are masks for ignored regions: an all-direction occlusion mask and a fisheye FoV mask based on the camera model.

The proposed **FoSOcc** framework processes multi-view features through two main modules. The **Spherical Lift Module (SLM)** lifts 2D image features to a 3D spherical voxel grid using the Unified Projection Model (MEI), explicitly handling fisheye distortion. The **Center Focusing Module (CFM)** then operates on these lifted features to enhance instance-aware localization; it generates a "supervised focus feature" from multi-directional voxel offsets and uses cross-attention to guide a query-based tracking decoder. Training uses standard losses for occupancy segmentation and tracking association, with AdamW (lr=2e-4, batch=4) for 24 epochs.

## Key Results
*   On **Occ3D-Waymo**, FoSOcc improves OccSQ for traffic signs by 11.1% and general objects by 20.7% over the TrackOcc baseline.
*   On the new **OccTrack360** benchmark, FoSOcc increases OccSQ-Overall from 12.90 to 13.54 (all FoV) and from 13.25 to 14.49 (within fisheye FoV), with strong gains on "parking", "fence", and "other structure" classes.
*   There is a **trade-off** between occupancy quality (OccSQ) and tracking metrics (OccSTQ/OccAQ); FoSOcc's gains in OccSQ come with a relative drop in these association metrics.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The improvement stems from two targeted design choices addressing fisheye-specific challenges. First, **SLM's explicit fisheye lifting** replaces naive pinhole-based lifting, reducing geometric errors from extreme distortion and providing a more accurate 3D feature initialization. Second, **CFM's focus mechanism** counteracts the smearing and localization ambiguity caused by fisheye projection by aggregating evidence from a "supervised focus" signal derived from multi-view offset consistency, leading to sharper, more instance-aware voxel features for the tracking decoder. This two-stage approach—accurate geometric lifting followed by instance-focused refinement—outperforms adapting a pinhole-based tracker.

## Foundational Learning
*   **Fisheye Camera Model (MEI):** Why needed: Standard pinhole models fail for >180° FoV; the Unified Projection Model (polynomial + mirror parameter ξ) accurately maps 3D sphere rays to 2D distorted pixels. *Quick check:* Validate reprojection error is lower than pinhole on a set of 3D-2D point correspondences.
*   **4D Panoptic Occupancy:** Why needed: Extends semantic occupancy to track instances over time, requiring consistent voxel labeling across frames. *Quick check:* Ensure a tracked instance's ID persists across adjacent frames in a short sequence, even under partial occlusion.
*   **2D-to-3D Lifting:** Why needed: Transforms dense 2D image features into a 3D volumetric representation for occupancy prediction. *Quick check:* Visualize lifted 3D features; known object surfaces should show higher activation than free space.
*   **Query-based Tracking:** Why needed: Maintains instance hypotheses across time using learnable queries and association metrics. *Quick check:* Verify query count is stable across a sequence; sudden spikes/drops indicate association failure.
*   **Occlusion & Visibility Masks:** Why needed: Prevents supervision from invalid regions (occluded areas, outside camera FoV). *Quick check:* Overlay mask on rendered occupancy; masked-out voxels should correspond to areas not visible from any camera.
*   **All-Direction Occlusion:** Why needed: In surround-view, an object can be occluded by another from any camera direction, not just along the viewing ray. *Quick check:* For a voxel, check line-of-sight to *all* cameras; if any is blocked, mark as occluded.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map:**
Multi-view Fisheye Images -> Backbone -> 2D Features -> SLM (Spherical Lift) -> Spherical Voxel Features -> CFM (Center Focusing) -> Focused Voxel Features -> Query-based Tracking Decoder -> 4D Panoptic Voxels

**Critical Path:** The core differentiable path is **Image -> SLM -> CFM -> Decoder**. SLM correctness is foundational; an error here propagates to CFM and tracking. CFM's cross-attention is the key refinement step targeting instance separation.

**Design Tradeoffs:**
*   **OccSQ vs. Tracking (OccSTQ/AQ):** Aggressive instance separation in CFM (e.g., high focus threshold) boosts occupancy segmentation (OccSQ) but may break weak associations, harming tracking quality.
*   **SLM Complexity vs. Speed:** Precise fisheye lifting is computationally heavier than pinhole lifting; gains in accuracy traded for throughput.
*   **Mask Strictness:** A strict occlusion/FoV mask removes uncertain Supervision but may also discard valid boundary voxels, potentially harming fine detail.

**Failure Signatures:**
*   **Blobby, Under-segmented Instances:** CFM failing to separate adjacent instances; check focus attention maps are diffuse.
*   **Instance ID Flickering/Swaps:** Tracking association failing; check query matching scores (τ₁, τ₂) for abrupt changes or many-to-one assignments.
*   **Structured Artifacts at FoV Boundaries:** SLM lifting inaccuracies near the fisheye image edge; visualize occupancy along the extreme horizontal FOV.
*   **High OccSQ but Low OccSTQ:** Classic trade-off; indicates strong segmentation but poor temporal consistency.

**First 3 Experiments:**
1.  **Validate Data Pipeline:** Process 1-2 KITTI-360 sequences. Visualize: (a) fisheye FoV mask cone against camera frustum, (b) occlusion mask on a scene with moving vehicles, (c) final voxel labels. Ensure masks correctly exclude invalid regions.
2.  **Reproduce Baseline Gains on Occ3D-Waymo:** Train FoSOcc (with SLM and CFM). Expect >10% OccSQ improvement on "sign" class and >15% on "general object" over a TrackOcc+SLM baseline. Also confirm the reported OccSTQ trade-off.
3.  **Ablate Core Modules:** Train three models: (a) FoSOcc w/o SLM (use pinhole lifting), (b) FoSOcc w/o CFM, (c) Full FoSOcc. Isolate SLM's contribution to OccSQ and CFM's to instance separation. Expect SLM ablation to hurt OccSQ significantly, CFM ablation to hurt OccSTQ and instance clarity.

## Limitations
*   Code and full data conversion pipeline for OccTrack360 not released, hindering exact replication and analysis of mask generation.
*   Hyperparameters for depth binning in SLM, query propagation rules, and CFM's cross-attention configuration are described conceptually but not numerically specified.
*   The observed trade-off between occupancy quality and tracking metrics lacks theoretical analysis; its root cause (e.g., association threshold sensitivity) is unclear.

## Confidence
*   **Benchmark Creation (long sequences, instance voxels, masks):** **Medium**. Sequence statistics are provided and plausible, but mask generation details and ground-truth accuracy cannot be externally verified without the pipeline code.
*   **FoSOcc Occupancy Gains (e.g., +20.7% on Occ3D-Waymo):** **Medium**. Results are based on a single reported evaluation; independent replication is needed to rule out implementation specifics or evaluation set splits.
*   **Tracking Trade-off (OccSTQ/AQ Drop):** **Medium**. The phenomenon is reported but not deeply analyzed; confidence is in the observation but not in understanding the cause.
