# Multi-Sourced, Multi-Agent Evidence Retrieval for Fact-Checking

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.00267  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00267  
- **Reference count:** 40  
- **Primary result:** Proposed WKGFC framework achieves 74.3% balanced accuracy, an absolute improvement of over 5% compared to prior best baseline (FIRE at 68.9%) and large-scale LLMs like Claude 3.5-Sonnet (66.5%).

## Executive Summary
The paper tackles inaccurate fact-checking caused by limited evidence retrieval, which often relies on textual similarity and fails to capture multi-hop semantic relations or achieve open-world coverage. It introduces WKGFC, a Web-enhanced Knowledge Graph Fact-Checking framework that reformulates the task as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP). An LLM-based agent dynamically decides between retrieving from an open knowledge graph (via an expand-and-prune strategy) and querying the web. Retrieved evidence is integrated into an augmented knowledge graph, and self-reflection with prompt optimization refines the retrieval policy without model fine-tuning. WKGFC demonstrates consistent performance gains across Wikipedia-based, web-sourced, and gold-evidence datasets, establishing a new state-of-the-art.

## Method Summary
WKGFC treats fact-checking as a sequential decision problem under partial observability. An LLM agent observes the current evidence state (a subgraph) and selects actions: `initKGRetrieval` (entity linking and seed KG query), `expandKG` (beam-search expansion and LLM-guided pruning), `webSearch` (BM25 retrieval, filtering, and conversion to KG-aligned triplets), or `verdict`. The agent iterates until confident, merging KG and web evidence into an augmented graph for the final verdict. Policy improvement is achieved via self-reflection on past trajectories (experience buffer) using TextGrad to optimize the system prompt, avoiding model fine-tuning. The expand-and-prune KG retrieval navigates multi-hop paths without requiring pre-specified SPARQL queries, while web retrieval is selectively triggered to complement KG gaps.

## Key Results
- **State-of-the-art accuracy:** WKGFC achieves 74.3% balanced accuracy across diverse benchmarks, surpassing the prior best (FIRE, 68.9%) and Claude 3.5-Sonnet (66.5%) by significant margins.
- **Ablation necessity:** Removing the agentic decision module, KG retrieval, or web retrieval all cause substantial performance drops, confirming the necessity of the integrated, dynamic approach.
- **Cross-dataset consistency:** Performance gains hold on Wikipedia-based (FEVER, HOVER), web-sourced (LIAR-New, AveriTeC), and gold-evidence (SummEval) datasets, demonstrating robustness to evidence source characteristics.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** Formulating fact-checking as a POMDP enables an LLM agent to dynamically decide *when* and *what* to retrieve across heterogeneous sources, adapting to evidence sufficiency rather than following a static pipeline.
- **Mechanism:** The agent’s state is the current retrieved evidence (partial observation). Its policy (LLM reasoning) selects actions (init/expand KG, webSearch, verdict) based on this state, with the environment (KG/web APIs) returning new observations. This sequential decision-making under partial observability allows adaptive evidence gathering until a confidence threshold is met.
- **Core assumption:** The LLM can reliably act as a policy network for retrieval decisions given natural language state descriptions.
- **Evidence anchors:** [abstract] "formulates the task as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP) where an LLM-based agent dynamically decides between retrieving from an open knowledge graph (via expand-and-prune) and querying the web." [section 3] "This interactive process can be formalized as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP)".

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** The "expand-and-prune" KG retrieval paradigm, guided by LLM reasoning at each hop, retrieves claim-relevant multi-hop subgraphs more effectively than generate-then-retrieve (SPARQL generation) methods by adapting to semantic relevance during traversal.
- **Mechanism:** Starting from seed entities mapped from the claim, the system performs beam search expansion: for each candidate entity/relation, the LLM scores its relevance to the claim. The top-*k* branches are retained per hop, creating a compact, semantically focused subgraph that captures multi-hop relations without requiring a pre-specified query structure.
- **Core assumption:** LLM-based scoring of KG entities/relations for semantic relevance is more robust than generating exact SPARQL queries in open-world settings where the evidence path is unknown.
- **Evidence anchors:** [abstract] "LLM-enabled retrieval is designed to assess the claims and retrieve the most relevant knowledge subgraphs... expand-and-prune search guided by LLM reasoning." [section 4.2] "We follows an expand-and-prune paradigm... in contrast to the generate-then-retrieve strategies... This strategy ensures multi-hop exploration while maintaining manageable graph size."

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** Controlled integration of web content—only when KG evidence is insufficient—and conversion of web passages into KG-aligned triplets creates a unified, high-precision augmented graph that adds coverage without overwhelming noise.
- **Mechanism:** Web retrieval is triggered based on the agent’s sufficiency assessment. Retrieved passages are filtered through a coarse-to-fine pipeline and converted into subject-predicate-object triplets aligned with the KG schema. This allows the LLM to reason over both sources using consistent relational logic, treating KG facts as precision anchors and web content as breadth-expanding context.
- **Core assumption:** Converting unstructured web text into KG schema triplets enables unified reasoning and avoids modality-confused reasoning.
- **Evidence anchors:** [abstract] "retrieved passages from the open web are filtered through a coarse-to-fine grained pipeline... converted into knowledge triplets aligned with the KG schema." [section 4.3] "To unify the structured and the unstructured evidence, selected web sentences are converted into knowledge triplets and annotations to be aligned with the KG schema."

## Foundational Learning
- **Concept:** Partially Observable Markov Decision Process (POMDP)  
  - **Why needed:** The agentic workflow—deciding when to expand the KG, when to search the web, when to stop—is a sequential decision problem under incomplete information (full truth not in any single retrieval step). Understanding POMDP clarifies why a *policy* (action-selection strategy) is needed over a fixed pipeline.  
  - **Quick check:** If the agent’s observation (current evidence subgraph) is partially observable, what does the policy π(𝑎|𝑠) try to infer about the hidden true state of “claim veracity” to choose the next action?
- **Concept:** Expand-and-Prun vs. Generate-then-Retrieve in KGQA  
  - **Why needed:** The paper’s innovation over prior KG-based fact-checking (e.g., ChatKBQA) is the retrieval strategy. Knowing the limitations of SPARQL generation (closed-world assumption) highlights why expand-and-prune is necessary for open-domain claims where the evidence path is unknown.  
  - **Quick check:** Why is generating a SPARQL query for a real-world, time-sensitive claim often unrealistic, and how does beam-search expansion avoid this pitfall?
- **Concept:** Prompt Optimization as Policy Gradient (TextGrad)  
  - **Why needed:** The system improves retrieval decisions without fine-tuning the LLM. This bridges reinforcement learning (policy improvement) and LLM prompting by treating the system prompt as the policy parameter and optimizing it via textual feedback (“gradients”) from self-reflection.  
  - **Quick check:** If the LLM weights are frozen, what component is actually being updated during the “prompt optimization” phase, and what signal drives this update?

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Input Processor (SpaCy NER + Wikidata API) -> Agent Controller (LLM-based POMDP policy) -> [KG Retriever (expand-and-prune via SPARQL) & Web Retriever (Serper API + LLM filter + triplet extraction)] -> Evidence Integrator (merge into augmented KG) -> Verdict Generator (LLM reasoning). Self-Reflection & Prompt Optimizer (TextGrad) loops back to update Agent Controller's prompt.
- **Critical path:** Claim → Entity Extraction/Linking → `initKGRetrieval` → Loop: [LLM Assess Sufficiency → (`expandKG` / `webSearch`)*] → `verdict`. The loop continues until verdict action is triggered or maximum steps exceeded.
- **Design tradeoffs:**  
  - KG vs. Web Trigger: KG is high-precision primary source; web is noisy but wide-coverage complement. Trade-off: Coverage vs. noise.  
  - Beam size (*k*) & Hops (*N*): Larger values increase recall but raise cost and noise risk. Paper uses *k=4*, *N=4*.  
  - Prompt Optimization Scope: Isolates policy learning for retrieval decisions but may miss end-to-end synergies with verdict generation.
- **Failure signatures:**  
  - **Insufficient KG:** Agent requests web search even after max KG expansion, indicating KG coverage gaps or poor exploration.  
  - **Exceeding Max Steps:** Agent exhausts all actions without confidence, suggesting weak stopping heuristic or conflicting evidence.  
  - **Over-confidence:** Agent makes premature verdict after initial KG only, common on complex claims.
- **First 3 experiments:**  
  1. **Unit Test - KG Retrieval Efficacy:** Run KG-only variant on FEVER (single-hop) and HOVER (multi-hop) with varying *k* and *N*. Expect performance increases with scope but diminishing returns; validate expand-and-prune beats single SPARQL baseline.
  2. **Integration Test - Web Trigger Logic:** Compare Web-only vs. non-agentic KG+Web vs. full WKGFC on LIAR-New. Expect WKGFC to outperform by showing agent learns to trigger web only when needed, avoiding unnecessary noisy calls.
  3. **Policy Improvement Validation:** Compare full WKGFC (with prompt optimization) vs. variant without optimization on held-out set. Expect optimized prompt to yield higher accuracy and lower error rates (over-confidence, insufficient KG), proving self-reflection helps.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Collaborative Multi-Agent Verification:** How can WKGFC extend to a system where agents specialize in evidence acquisition, credibility assessment, and causal reasoning? Current single-agent design is not evaluated for multi-agent coordination.
- **KG vs. Policy Bottleneck:** Are errors like "insufficient KG" due to Wikidata's inherent incompleteness or the agent's premature pruning? Ablations don't disentangle source completeness from policy effectiveness.
- **KG Source Portability:** How does switching the open knowledge graph (e.g., Wikidata → DBpedia → domain-specific KG) impact performance, especially for out-of-domain or temporal claims? All experiments use Wikidata only.
- **Prompt Optimization vs. Fine-Tuning:** Is prompt-based policy improvement sufficient, or would gradient-based fine-tuning (or training a separate retriever) yield better accuracy or efficiency? No comparison to weight-updating methods is provided.
- **Sufficiency Threshold Opacity:** What specific thresholds or criteria does the agent use to assess KG evidence sufficiency? Error modes (over-confidence, exceeding steps) suggest the decision logic is sensitive but undisclosed.

## Limitations
- Exact LLM prompt templates and sufficiency decision thresholds are not disclosed, hindering exact replication.
- TextGrad optimization hyperparameters (learning rate, batch size, epochs) are missing; robustness of prompt improvements is unclear.
- Evaluation uses only balanced accuracy without statistical significance testing or calibration analysis.
- Experiments are restricted to GPT-4o; generalizability to other LLMs (open-source or newer) is unknown.
- No quantification of web-to-KG triplet extraction precision or API cost implications.

## Confidence
- **C1: POMDP-based dynamic multi-source retrieval improves accuracy** – High. Consistent gains across four datasets and ablations removing the agent component support this.
- **C2: Expand-and-prune KG retrieval captures multi-hop evidence** – High. Parameter studies and comparison to generate-then-retrieve baselines validate the mechanism.
- **C3: Agent-controlled web search with KG-aligned fusion adds coverage without excess noise** – High. Ablations show necessity; web-only and un-agentized KG+Web underperform.
- **C4: Self-reflection and prompt optimization refine policy without fine-tuning** – Medium. Positive ablation observed, but hyperparameter sensitivity and potential overfitting remain unclear.
