# Exhaustive Circuit Mapping of a Single-Cell Foundation Model Reveals Massive Redundancy, Heavy-Tailed Hub Architecture, and Layer-Dependent Differentiation Control

## Quick Facts
*   **arXiv ID:** 2603.11940
*   **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11940
*   **Reference Count:** 12
*   **Primary Result:** Systematic biases in model interpretability are driven by selective feature sampling, pairwise-only testing, and observational analysis; exhaustive tracing reveals massive computational hubs lacking biological annotation, higher-order ablation shows deepening redundancy without synergy, and trajectory steering establishes layer-dependent directionality in differentiation control.

## Executive Summary
This study corrects three systematic biases in biological foundation model interpretability: annotation bias from selective feature sampling, pairwise-only interaction testing, and observational trajectory analysis. Using Geneformer, the authors apply exhaustive circuit tracing (causally ablating all active features), three-way combinatorial ablation, and causal trajectory steering. They find that exhaustive tracing uncovers a 27-fold increase in downstream edges with 40% of top computational hubs unannotated. Three-way ablation reveals monotonically deepening redundancy (ratio 0.59) and zero synergy. Trajectory steering shows late-layer features universally promote maturation while early/mid-layer features predominantly oppose it. These results demonstrate layer-dependent differentiation control and expose pervasive annotation bias in prior work.

## Method Summary
The study uses Geneformer V2 (18-layer transformer) with per-layer Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs; TopK, k=32, 4× overcomplete). **Exhaustive tracing** causally ablates all 4,065 active L5 features across 20 K562 cells, measuring downstream effects (Cohen's *d*) on L6/L11/L17. **Three-way ablation** tests 8 triplets (features from layers 0/5/11, same/cross pathways) with 7 conditions (singles, pairs, triplet) on 200 cells, computing redundancy ratios and marginal contributions on L17 outputs. **Trajectory steering** identifies "switch features" (clean activation correlates with pseudotime), amplifies their SAE coefficients (α=2,5) in early-pseudotime Tabula Sapiens immune cells, and measures logit shift toward maturity vs. progenitor gene signatures. Replication requires specific SAEs, active feature lists, triplet definitions, and pseudotime parameters.

## Key Results
*   Exhaustive tracing identified 1,393,850 significant downstream edges—a 27-fold increase over selective sampling—with 40% of the top 20 computational hubs lacking biological annotation; annotation status is not predictive of computational centrality.
*   Three-way ablation showed redundancy deepens monotonically (three-way ratio 0.59 vs. pairwise 0.74) with zero synergy (superadditive effects in only 0.14% of cases), confirming subadditivity at higher orders.
*   Trajectory steering demonstrated that all tested late-layer (L17) features (3/3) pushed cell states toward maturity (fraction positive = 1.0), while early- (L0, 5/5) and mid-layer (L11) features predominantly opposed maturation.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Exhaustive Circuit Tracing Corrects Annotation Bias
*   **Claim:** Selective tracing based on biological annotation systematically misses computationally central but unannotated hub features.
*   **Mechanism:** By causally ablating *all* active SAE features at a source layer (L5) across many cells and measuring downstream effect sizes (Cohen’s *d*), the complete computational graph is reconstructed without pre-filtering. Heavy-tailed connectivity emerges.
*   **Core Assumption:** Computational importance (number of significant downstream edges) is not predictable from biological annotation status.
*   **Evidence Anchors:**
    *   [abstract] "exhaustive tracing... yields 1,393,850 significant downstream edges—a 27-fold increase... 40% of the top 20 computational hubs lack biological annotation"
    *   [section 2.1.3] "40% of the top-20 hubs lack biological annotation... annotation status is not predictive of computational centrality"
*   **Break Condition:** If exhaustive tracing showed that annotated features overwhelmingly dominated the top connectivity ranks (e.g., >90% of top 20 annotated).

### Mechanism 2: Higher-Order Ablation Reveals Deepening Redundancy
*   **Claim:** Model circuits are fundamentally subadditive; redundancy deepens monotonically with interaction order, and synergy is absent.
*   **Mechanism:** Systematically ablating single features, pairs, and triplets from the same biological pathway, then comparing observed vs. summed effects via a redundancy ratio. Near-zero marginal contributions from a third feature confirm deep redundancy.
*   **Core Assumption:** Biological pathway representations are distributed such that multiple features carry largely overlapping information.
*   **Evidence Anchors:**
    *   [abstract] "three-way ablation... redundancy deepens monotonically (ratio 0.59 vs. pairwise 0.74) with zero synergy"
    *   [section 2.2.1 & 2.2.2] "three-way redundancy ratio... 0.59... versus pairwise ratio of 0.74... superadditive effects were observed for only 7 instances (0.14%)"
*   **Break Condition:** Discovery of significant superadditive effects in a non-trivial fraction of triplets.

### Mechanism 3: Trajectory Steering Establishes Layer-Dependent Directionality
*   **Claim:** Layer position causally determines whether a feature’s amplification pushes cell state toward or away from maturity; late layers universally drive maturation.
*   **Mechanism:** For "switch features" (clean activation correlates with pseudotime), amplify their SAE activations in early-pseudotime cells. The resulting change in the model’s logit vector, measured as a shift in similarity to maturity vs. progenitor gene signatures, indicates causal directionality.
*   **Core Assumption:** The direction of logit change upon feature amplification reflects the feature’s functional role in differentiation.
*   **Evidence Anchors:**
    *   [abstract] "late-layer (L17) features universally pushed cell states toward maturity (fraction positive = 1.0), while early- and mid-layer features (L0, L11) predominantly opposed maturation"
    *   [section 2.3.1] "L17 features (3/3 tested) produced positive state shifts in 100%... L0 features (5/5 tested) produced negative or mixed shifts"
*   **Break Condition:** If a significant number of L17 features produced negative shifts, or L0 features produced uniformly positive shifts.

## Foundational Learning
*   **Concept: Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs)**
    *   *Why needed:* SAEs decompose superposed transformer activations into interpretable, sparsely activating features. This is the foundational representation for all circuit analysis.
    *   *Quick Check:* If an SAE feature has high reconstruction error but clean activation patterns, what does that suggest about its utility for circuit analysis? (It may capture a computationally meaningful but linearly complex direction, limiting interpretability but not necessarily causal relevance.)
*   **Concept: Causal Mediation Analysis / Intervention**
    *   *Why needed:* To move from correlation (feature A activates when cell matures) to causation (amplifying feature A *makes* cells more mature). Ablation and steering are specific intervention forms.
    *   *Quick Check:* What is the key methodological difference between observational correlation (e.g., feature activation vs. pseudotime) and causal steering? (Steering actively manipulates the feature's activation to observe downstream effects, establishing causality.)
*   **Concept: Trajectory Inference & Pseudotime**
    *   *Why needed:* Provides a continuous ordering of cells from progenitor to mature states. This ordering defines "early" vs. "late" cells for steering experiments and identifies "switch features."
    *   *Quick Check:* Why is using *early-pseudotime* cells for steering experiments, rather than random cells, critical for testing differentiation control? (It targets cells where the system is most sensitive to perturbations that could alter the differentiation trajectory.)

## Architecture Onboarding
*   **Component Map:** Base Model (Geneformer V2) -> SAE Layer (per layer, k=32) -> Circuit Tracer -> Ablation Engine -> Steering Module. The SAE decomposes activations; the Tracer/Ablation Engine perform interventions; the Steering Module computes state shifts.
*   **Critical Path:**
    1.  Train/obtain SAEs for each layer.
    2.  Identify "active" features (min activation frequency).
    3.  **Exhaustive Tracing:** For each active feature at L5 → ablate → measure L6/L11/L17 effects (4k features × 20 cells).
    4.  **Combinatorial Ablation:** For pre-selected pathway-aligned triplets → run 7 ablation conditions × 200 cells → compute ratios.
    5.  **Steering:** For pre-identified "switch features" → find early-pseudotime cells → apply steering (α=2,5) → compute state shift Δs.
*   **Design Tradeoffs:**
    *   *Exhaustiveness vs. Compute:* Tracing all features uses fewer cells per feature (20 vs. 200) to manage O(N²) scaling, reducing power for small effects.
    *   *Steering Magnitude vs. Plausibility:* Large α (e.g., 5x) produces clearer directionality but may push activations into non-physiological ranges.
    *   *Downstream Layer Selection:* Tracing only L6/L11/L17 from L5 misses intermediate-layer effects (L7-10, L12-16).
*   **Failure Signatures:**
    *   *Exhaustive Tracing:* >50% of features with zero significant edges may indicate too strict thresholds or insufficient cell sampling.
    *   *Ablation:* Any superadditive instance in triplet tests contradicts "zero synergy" claim; requires checking inclusion-exclusion calculation errors.
    *   *Steering:* Uniformly near-zero Δs magnitudes could mean α is too small, the metric is insensitive, or features are not causally involved.
*   **First 3 Experiments:**
    1.  **Replicate Exhaustive Tracing on a Subset:** Pick 100 random L5 features. Run full tracing protocol. Verify edge count distribution matches paper’s mean/median. Check that 1-2 top hubs are unannotated.
    2.  **Run Pairwise then Three-Way Ablation on One Triplet:** Select a same-pathway triplet (e.g., Vesicle #1). Run all 7+1 conditions. Compute pairwise and three-way redundancy ratios. Confirm subadditivity (ratio <1) and near-zero C|AB.
    3.  **Steering on L0 and L17 Features:** Take one L0 and one L17 switch feature. On early-pseudotime cells, apply α=5 steering. Compute fraction positive shift. Expect L17 ~1.0, L0 <0.5. Validate gene-level logit changes for one top-up/down gene are biologically plausible.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
*   How do intermediate layers (L7–L10, L12–L16) contribute to the circuit architecture and signal propagation dynamics? Only three downstream layers were measured.
*   Does the pattern of deepening redundancy without synergy generalize to higher-order interactions (4-way and above) and a broader diversity of biological pathways? Only 8 triplets across 4 pathways were tested.
*   Are the identified architectural features—heavy-tailed hub distribution, annotation bias, and layer-dependent directionality—universal across single-cell and protein foundation models? All experiments were on Geneformer alone.

## Limitations
*   **Incomplete tracing scope:** Only L5→L6/L11/L17 edges mapped; intermediate layers (L7–10, L12–16) omitted, potentially missing key pathways.
*   **Small cell samples:** 20 cells for tracing may underpower detection of weak but widespread effects.
*   **Pseudotime ambiguity:** Critical parameters (HVG selection, PCA dimensions, neighbor graph size, root cell) not disclosed; mis-specification could shift "early" cell set and signature definitions.
*   **Metric definitions unclear:** "Consistency" threshold, aggregation method for redundancy ratios (sum vs. mean), and ablation significance metric require confirmation.
*   **Limited triplet diversity:** Only 8 triplets tested; results may not generalize across pathways or layers.
*   **Steering magnitude non-physiological:** α=5 may push activations into unrealistic regimes, risking artificial directionality.
*   **Domain shift:** SAEs trained on K562 activations applied to Tabula Sapiens immune cells; feature semantics may not transfer cleanly.

## Confidence
*   **Claim 1 (Exhaustive tracing reveals massive annotation bias):** **Medium**. Strong quantitative increase in edge count (27×) supports bias, but tracing limited to three downstream layers and "consistency" definition ambiguous.
*   **Claim 2 (Deepening redundancy, zero synergy):** **Medium**. Redundancy ratios consistent with prior pairwise work, but small triplet sample (8) and aggregation uncertainty temper confidence.
*   **Claim 3 (Layer-dependent differentiation control):** **Medium**. Directional steering results robust to α variations, but pseudotime misalignment risk and cross-dataset application raise biological validity concerns.
