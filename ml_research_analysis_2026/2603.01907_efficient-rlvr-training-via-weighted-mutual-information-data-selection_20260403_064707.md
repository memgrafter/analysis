# Efficient RLVR Training via Weighted Mutual Information Data Selection

## Quick Facts
- ArXiv ID: 2603.01907
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01907
- Reference count: 32
- Primary result: INSIGHT achieves +1.41 average gain on Planning & Mathematics, +1.01 on general reasoning, and up to ~2.2x training acceleration with negligible overhead.

## Executive Summary
Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) for LLMs often suffers from inefficient data selection due to heuristics that ignore epistemic uncertainty. This paper introduces INSIGHT, a method using a weighted mutual information acquisition score based on Bayesian posterior beliefs to jointly optimize task difficulty and accumulated evidence. It extends to multi-rollout RLVR settings without extra inference cost. Experiments across planning, math, and reasoning benchmarks with 0.6B to 7B models show consistent SOTA improvements and significant acceleration.

## Method Summary
INSIGHT selects data points by maximizing a weighted mutual information score that quantifies expected uncertainty reduction under Bayesian posterior approximations. This score balances task difficulty (via model performance variance) with accumulated evidence (from rollouts). The approach naturally extends to multi-rollout RLVR by aggregating evidence across rollouts, maintaining computational efficiency.

## Key Results
- Average gain of +1.41 on Planning & Mathematics benchmarks.
- Average gain of +1.01 on general reasoning benchmarks.
- Up to ~2.2x training acceleration compared to prior methods.

## Why This Works
The weighted mutual information score explicitly accounts for epistemic uncertainty from limited evidence, unlike difficulty-based heuristics. By jointly considering difficulty and evidence accumulation, it prioritizes data that maximally reduces model uncertainty, leading to more efficient learning per sample. The Bayesian grounding provides a principled exploration-exploitation trade-off in RLVR.

  - *Why needed*: Captures uncertainty due to limited training data, key for identifying informative samples.
  - *Quick check*: Measure correlation between epistemic uncertainty and learning gain on a validation set.
- **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF)**:
  - *Why needed*: Framework where INSIGHT optimizes data selection.
  - *Quick check*: Ensure baseline RLHF performance plateaus with random selection.
- **Multi-rollout RLVR**:
  - *Why needed*: Setting with multiple rollouts per prompt; INSIGHT aggregates evidence naturally.
  - *Quick check*: Test if evidence aggregation over multiple rollouts improves selection stability.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: Bayesian posterior approximator -> mutual information calculator -> data selector -> RL trainer (with multi-rollout aggregation)
- **Critical path**: For each candidate data point, compute weighted mutual information based on current posterior and rollout evidence; select top-k; update model; repeat.
- **Design tradeoffs**: Balances computational cost of posterior approximation vs. gain in sample efficiency; uses lightweight approximations to keep overhead low.
- **Failure signatures**: Degraded performance on out-of-distribution tasks if prior is misspecified; selection bias if evidence accumulation is insufficient.
- **First experiments**:
  1. Implement acquisition score on a synthetic Bayesian linear regression task to validate uncertainty reduction.
  2. Compare INSIGHT to random selection and difficulty-based baselines on a small RLVR dataset (e.g., 1k prompts).
  3. Measure wall-clock overhead vs. baseline on a single GPU for 100 selection steps.

## Limitations
- No code or algorithms provided for weighted mutual information calculation, hindering reproducibility.
- "Negligible computational overhead" lacks direct comparisons to SOTA online selection methods.
- Scalability untested beyond 7B models and standard context lengths.

## Confidence
- **High**: Empirical gains across diverse benchmarks and model scales.
- **Medium**: Theoretical motivation and extension to multi-rollout RLVR.
- **Low**: Claims of negligible overhead and broad superiority without full baseline comparisons.
