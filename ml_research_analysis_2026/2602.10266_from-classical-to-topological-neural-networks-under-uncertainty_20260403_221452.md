# From Classical to Topological Neural Networks Under Uncertainty

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.10266
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10266
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Integrating topological data analysis and Bayesian uncertainty with neural networks improves robustness, interpretability, and generalization across vision, time-series, and graph domains for military AI tasks.

## Executive Summary
This chapter addresses reliable decision-making from heterogeneous, noisy military data by combining topological data analysis (TDA) and Bayesian uncertainty within neural networks. It introduces topological convolutional neural networks (TCNNs) with manifold-based filters, Bayesian TCNNs (BTCNNs) for probabilistic weights, and Bayesian frameworks for persistence diagrams. These methods enhance model robustness, interpretability, and calibration, demonstrating significant gains in radar emitter identification, cross-dataset image/video classification, and EEG/biological network analysis.

## Method Summary
The proposed approach merges TDA and Bayesian inference with deep learning. TCNNs use fixed filters derived from manifold embeddings (e.g., circle, Klein bottle) to encode geometric inductive biases. BTCNNs apply variational Bayesian inference to TCNN weights, providing posterior distributions and calibrated uncertainty. For persistence diagrams, Bayesian Poisson point process models with Gaussian mixture priors enable uncertainty-aware classification. These architectures are trained end-to-end via stochastic gradient descent, optimizing the evidence lower bound (ELBO) for Bayesian variants and cross-entropy for deterministic baselines.

## Key Results
- An attention-GRU achieved >93% accuracy in radar emitter identification under low signal-to-noise ratio.
- TCNNs outperformed standard CNNs in accuracy, learning speed, and cross-dataset generalization on image/video tasks.
- BTCNNs delivered higher accuracy and better calibration, particularly with limited or perturbed data.
- Bayesian topological signal processing enabled accurate classification of EEG and biological network data with well-calibrated uncertainty estimates.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Topological Summaries for Robust Representations
Persistent homology tracks the birth and death of topological features (e.g., connected components, loops) across a filtration, such as a Vietoris–Rips complex. These summaries are stable under small perturbations (stability theorem) and vectorized via persistence images or kernels for machine learning. This provides noise-robust, multiscale representations that capture global structure, improving classification accuracy and interpretability compared to raw data or conventional features. Evidence includes the abstract’s claim about TDA uncovering hidden patterns and Section 3.1’s application to EEG and biological networks. Break condition: if data lacks topological structure or noise eliminates persistent features, performance may degrade.

### Bayesian Neural Networks for Calibrated Uncertainty
Bayesian neural networks place priors on weights and infer posteriors (e.g., via Bayes-by-Backprop with reparameterization), capturing epistemic uncertainty. This yields predictive distributions reflecting model confidence and improves generalization under data scarcity or distribution shift. The variational lower bound (ELBO) balances data fit and model complexity. Evidence includes the abstract’s emphasis on interpretability and robustness, and Section 2.5’s derivation and applications. Break condition: poor posterior approximation (e.g., restrictive mean-field) or misspecified prior leads to over- or under-confidence.

### Manifold-Based Filters in Topological CNNs
TCNNs replace standard convolutional kernels with fixed filters defined by embeddings of manifolds (e.g., primary circle, Klein bottle). These filters encode geometric priors like rotation equivariance, and variants like COL/KOL prune connections based on manifold distance, acting as a regularizer. This imposes a topological inductive bias that improves learning speed, generalization, and interpretability. Evidence includes the abstract’s claim and Section 3.2’s experiments showing TCNN superiority. Break condition: if the chosen manifold does not align with data geometry (e.g., Klein bottle for non-Möbius structures), fixed filters may fail to capture relevant features.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Input (raw data or topological summaries) -> Topological Feature Extractor (optional: compute persistence diagrams, vectorize) -> Topological Neural Layers (TCNN: CF/KF/COL/KOL; Simplicial: message passing with boundary operators; Sheaf: diffusion with restriction maps) -> Bayesian Wrapper (variational layers or PPP models) -> Output Head (task-specific) -> Loss (ELBO or cross-entropy)
- **Critical path (building a BTCNN for image classification):**
  1. Choose a manifold (e.g., primary circle) and layer type (COL).
  2. Implement topological conv layer: index slices by manifold coordinates, set nonzero connections via manifold metric, initialize weights.
  3. Add Bayesian dense layers after topological features; define prior (e.g., N(0,1)) and variational posterior (factorized Gaussian).
  4. Formulate ELBO loss: negative log-likelihood + KL divergence.
  5. Train with stochastic gradients, monitoring ELBO components.
  6. Calibrate uncertainty on held-out data (e.g., temperature scaling).
- **Design tradeoffs:**
  - TCNN vs. CNN: TCNN reduces parameters and embeds inductive bias but may lack flexibility if manifold misaligns with data.
  - Bayesian vs. deterministic: Bayesian yields uncertainty estimates but increases memory/computation.
  - Simplicial vs. graph: Simplicial networks capture higher-order interactions but require building complexes and handling orientations.
  - Vectorized PDs vs. end-to-end topological layers: Vectorization is modular but breaks gradient flow; topological layers allow joint optimization but need custom backprop.
- **Failure signatures:**
  - ELBO instability: KL divergence exploding indicates poor variational posterior; try smaller prior variance or more expressive posterior.
  - Over-smoothing in deep topological layers: Node/simplex representations become indistinguishable; reduce depth or add residuals.
  - Poor calibration: Confidence-accuracy mismatch; check prior, variational family, or apply post-hoc calibration.
  - Uninformative topological features: Low accuracy with PDs suggests features not predictive; try alternative vectorizations or switch to topological layers.
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1. **TCNN on MNIST:** Replace first convolutional layer with a circle filter (CF) layer; compare test accuracy and convergence epochs vs. CNN baseline.
  2. **BTCNN under label noise:** Train BTCNN on CIFAR-10 with 20% random label corruption; evaluate accuracy and expected calibration error (ECE) vs. deterministic CNN and standard BNN.
  3. **Bayesian topological signal processing on EEG:** Compute sublevel-set persistence diagrams, fit Bayesian PPP with Gaussian mixture prior, perform mental-state classification using Bayes factors; report accuracy and uncertainty estimates.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Class-conditional topological modeling:** How can the Bayesian PPP framework be extended to explicitly model class-conditional topological structures for multi-class tasks? The current generative approach lacks discriminative class modeling.
- **Dynamic simplicial networks:** To what extent can simplicial neural networks generalize to dynamic graph data, and what modifications are needed for temporal evolution of higher-order relationships? Existing SNNs assume fixed complexes or specific spike data.
- **Robustness of ToFU activations:** How does topology-based activation (ToFU) using persistence diagram dissimilarity impact robustness to adversarial perturbations and distribution shifts compared to standard activations? Robustness is implied but not measured.
- **Computational scalability of TCNNs:** What are the complexity and scalability trade-offs of TCNNs relative to CNNs as manifold discretization resolution increases? No empirical profiling is provided.

## Limitations
- Hyperparameters (learning rates, batch sizes, network depths) are not reported, hindering exact replication.
- Radar data generation pipeline (UPMOP extraction, noise models) and exact image/video datasets for TCNN tests are unspecified.
- Corpus evidence for the three main mechanisms is weak; most cited works are tangentially related, indicating limited external validation.

## Confidence
- Topological summaries for robustness: **Medium** (theory solid but limited empirical coverage).
- Bayesian NN for uncertainty: **Medium** (standard framework but sensitive to approximations).
- Manifold-based filters: **Low**–**Medium** (preliminary, depends on correct manifold choice).
