# QiMeng-CodeV-SVA: Training Specialized LLMs for Hardware Assertion Generation via RTL-Grounded Bidirectional Data Synthesis

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.14239  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14239  
- **Reference count:** 40  
- **Primary result:** CodeV‑SVA‑14B attains 75.8% Func.@1 on NL2SVA‑Human and 84.0% on NL2SVA‑Machine, matching or surpassing GPT‑5 and DeepSeek‑R1.

## Executive Summary
The paper addresses two key bottlenecks in training LLMs for natural‑language‑to‑SystemVerilog‑Assertion (NL2SVA) translation: the scarcity of high‑quality SVA data and unreliable semantic alignment methods. It introduces a comprehensive data‑synthesis framework that (1) generates SVAs from large‑scale open‑source RTLs, (2) uses formal verification to filter verifiable assertions, (3) applies bidirectional translation with equivalence checking to align NL and SVA, and (4) refines the dataset with LLM‑as‑a‑judge, difficulty filtering, and reasoning trajectory augmentation. Supervised fine‑tuning of Qwen3 on the resulting dataset yields CodeV‑SVA models that achieve state‑of‑the‑art results on FVEval benchmarks.

## Method Summary
The pipeline starts with ∼42K open‑source RTL designs containing clock and reset. A strong LLM (DeepSeek‑V3.1) produces multiple natural‑language properties and corresponding SVAs; JasperGold verifies each SVA under its RTL, keeping ∼159K valid pairs. Bidirectional translation (SVA→NL→SVA) with formal equivalence reduces this to ∼105K semantically aligned pairs. Data refinement removes problematic examples via an LLM‑as‑a‑judge (four error types), discards trivial instances using a weaker LLM, and adds reasoning traces from DeepSeek‑R1, resulting in ∼83K (NL, reasoning, SVA) triples. Finally, Qwen3 8B/14B is fine‑tuned: input = RTL + NL, output = reasoning tokens + SVA, using cross‑entropy loss (2 epochs, lr=2e‑5, batch=128).

## Key Results
- CodeV‑SVA‑14B reaches 75.8% Func.@1 on NL2SVA‑Human and 84.0% on NL2SVA‑Machine.
- Ablations show bidirectional selection contributes +12.3% and reasoning augmentation +8% to Func.@1.
- Models trained on synthetic data outperform those trained on existing corpora (e.g., VERT).

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1: RTL‑grounded synthesis
- **Claim:** Grounding SVA generation in real RTL yields high‑quality, verifiable data.  
- **Mechanism:** LLM analyses open‑source RTLs, generates NL properties and SVAs; JasperGold filters SVAs that hold on the RTL.  
- **Core assumption:** Verified SVAs are meaningful; diverse RTLs cover broad assertion patterns.  
- **Break condition:** Unrepresentative RTLs or flawed verification produce invalid SVAs.

### Mechanism 2: Bidirectional alignment
- **Claim:** Round‑trip translation with formal equivalence catches semantic misalignments.  
- **Mechanism:** SVA→NL→SVA; retain pairs where original and regenerated SVAs are equivalent.  
- **Core assumption:** Information loss during round‑trip indicates misalignment.  
- **Break condition:** Inconsistent translation or overly strict equivalence discards valid pairs.

### Mechanism 3: Reasoning augmentation
- **Claim:** Explicit reasoning trajectories improve NL2SVA accuracy.  
- **Mechanism:** DeepSeek‑R1 generates step‑by‑step reasoning and SVA; only consistent pairs are kept.  
- **Core assumption:** Reasoning decomposes the task, aiding generalization.  
- **Break condition:** Erroneous reasoning or increased sequence length.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:**  
RTL Curation → NL Property Generation → SVA Generation & Verification → Bidirectional Selection → Data Refinement → Supervised Fine‑Tuning  

**Critical path:** RTL → NL property → SVA → verification → bidirectionality → refinement → SFT; each stage reduces data volume while improving alignment.

**Design tradeoffs:**  
- Quality vs. quantity: formal verification and bidirectionality drastically cut data (159K→83K) but are necessary for correctness.  
- Tool dependency: JasperGold’s correctness and performance are critical; any limitations propagate.  
- Model capacity: reasoning augmentation increases context length (up to 32K tokens), raising memory demands.

**Failure signatures:**  
- Surge in vacuous assertions like `assert property(1'b1)`.  
- Large gap between Func.@1 and higher @k values.  
- Poor generalization on human‑written benchmarks, indicating RTL‑specific overfitting.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. **Residual misalignments in final dataset:** The authors validated a small sample (50 pairs) but did not audit the full 83k. Uncaught errors could degrade real‑task performance.  
2. **Generalization beyond CodeV:** Training data comes solely from CodeV; evaluation is on FVEval (likely distribution‑similar). No out‑of‑distribution test exists.  
3. **Computational cost of synthesis:** LLM inference and formal verification expenses are unreported; without cost metrics, scalability and accessibility remain unclear.

## Limitations
- Heavy reliance on proprietary JasperGold; configuration and equivalence‑checking details are undocumented, hindering replication.  
- Exact LLM prompts are missing; reproduced data may differ in quality and distribution.  
- Formal verification cost for >100k SVAs is high and not quantified.  
- LLM‑as‑a‑judge criteria are only conceptually described, lacking operational specifics.

## Confidence
- **RTL‑grounded synthesis:** Medium (supported by diversity metrics and verification, but limited by RTL representativeness and tool reliability).  
- **Bidirectional alignment:** Medium‑Low (ablation shows benefit, but effectiveness depends on LLM consistency and equivalence check details).  
- **Reasoning augmentation:** Medium (ablation indicates improvement, but risk of erroneous reasoning).

## Next Checks
1. Conduct a human audit on a stratified sample of the final NL‑SVA pairs to measure semantic correctness and alignment.  
2. Replace JasperGold with an open‑source verifier (e.g., Yosys+SVA) in the synthesis pipeline and compare model performance.  
3. Evaluate CodeV‑SVA on an out‑of‑distribution benchmark of proprietary or recent RTL designs.
