# ViMultiChoice: Toward a Method That Gives Explanation for Multiple-Choice Reading Comprehension in Vietnamese

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.09961  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09961  
- **Reference count:** 40  
- **Primary result:** Introduces ViMultiChoice, a method that jointly predicts answers and generates explanations for Vietnamese multiple-choice reading comprehension, achieving 64.38% accuracy on the new ViRCSoSciD dataset and outperforming prior methods and LLMs.

## Executive Summary
The paper tackles the shortage of explanation-capable models for Vietnamese multiple-choice reading comprehension (MCRC). It releases ViRCSoSciD, the first Vietnamese MCRC dataset with human-annotated explanations (12,819 questions), and proposes ViMultiChoice, which simultaneously selects the correct option and produces a textual justification. ViMultiChoice integrates a ViWordFormer module to capture Vietnamese phrasal lexemes and is trained with a multitask loss combining answer selection and explanation generation. Evaluated on ViMMRC 2.0 and ViRCSoSciD, it sets new state-of-the-art results, surpassing prior models and large language models like Mistral-Large. The approach also yields high-quality explanations as measured by BLEU-4 and ROUGE-L.

## Method Summary
ViMultiChoice is an encoder-based architecture. It first retrieves relevant textbook passages using VietBGE embeddings. A Vietnamese Text Encoder (based on ViT5) processes the question, four options, and the retrieved context, with three ViWordFormer modules enhancing representations by modeling phrasal cohesion. The Option Inference module (adapted from OCN) performs pairwise keep-eliminate comparisons among options, cross-attends with context, and refines each option’s representation via self-attention. An Option Head scores these vectors to predict the answer. Separately, an autoregressive Explanation Generator conditions on the concatenated features of the question, context, and the selected option’s vector to produce an explanation. The model is trained end-to-end with a multitask loss: `L = L_MC + L_E`, where `L_MC` is cross-entropy for answer selection and `L_E` for explanation generation. Optimization uses Adam (lr=5e-5, batch size=32) with early stopping on F1-macro.

## Key Results
- ViMultiChoice reaches 64.38% accuracy and F1-macro on ViRCSoSciD, exceeding OCN (54.64%) and Mistral-Large (64.49% F1).  
- For explanation generation, it attains BLEU-4 of 34.85 and ROUGE-L of 56.78, strongly outperforming baselines.  
- Ablation confirms the ViWordFormer module’s contribution and shows multitask training lifts accuracy from 62.98% to 64.38%.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

**Mechanism 1: High-quality explanation-annotated dataset (ViRCSoSciD)**  
- **Claim:** Enables training and evaluation of models that produce human-like justifications, addressing a key gap for Vietnamese.  
- **Mechanism:** High-school teachers (10+ years experience) annotated correct answers and explanations; high inter-annotator agreement (96%); options shuffled to reduce bias.  
- **Core assumption:** Human-annotated explanations are a valid and necessary training signal; the annotation captures authentic reasoning.  
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract states it is the first such dataset; Section 3.1 details annotation process; existingVietnamese MCRC datasets lack explanations.  
- **Break condition:** If explanations are stylistically inconsistent, overly brief, or not causally tied to answer selection, joint training may learn spurious correlations.

**Mechanism 2: ViWordFormer module for Vietnamese phrasal lexemes**  
- **Claim:** Improves Vietnamese text representation by explicitly modeling compound words that span multiple space-separated tokens.  
- **Mechanism:** Computes a Phrasal Score matrix `P` estimating adjacent token cohesion, then multiplies it element‑wise with the standard attention matrix `A` (`A' = A ⊙ P`). This refines self‑attention to favor intra‑phrase connections.  
- **Core assumption:** Vietnamese compound words function as semantic phrases; standard tokenization fragments these concepts.  
- **Evidence anchors:** Section 4.3 gives the mathematical formulation; ablations show removal drops accuracy (e.g., multitask: 64.38% → 62.51%) and BLEU‑4 (34.85 → 20.23).  
- **Break condition:** If `P` misaligns with true linguistic boundaries or the gain does not hold across diverse question types, the module’s utility is questionable.

**Mechanism 3: Multitask training unifies answer selection and explanation generation**  
- **Claim:** Joint training improves answer accuracy by forcing deeper understanding through explanation generation.  
- **Mechanism:** Shared encoder and option inference modules minimize `L = L_MC(θ) + L_E(θ)`. Explanation generation is conditioned on the selected option’s vector `f_k`.  
- **Core assumption:** Generating a faithful explanation requires a coherent reasoning trace that benefits answer selection.  
- **Evidence anchors:** Section 4.6 defines the loss; Table 6/7 show multitask (64.38% Acc) beats single‑task (62.98%); explanation metrics also improve with ViWordFormer under multitask.  
- **Break condition:** If `L_E` dominates or conflicts with `L_MC`, answer accuracy can degrade or explanations become generic.

## Foundational Learning

**Concept: Vietnamese Phrasal Linguistics & Tokenization**  
- **Why needed:** ViWordFormer relies on the linguistic reality that Vietnamese “words” are often phrasal constructs written with spaces between syllables. Standard tokenization treats each space‑separated unit as a word, which splits compound meanings like “địa bàn kinh doanh”.  
- **Quick check:** Why might training on syllable‑tokenized Vietnamese text lead to suboptimal representations for such compounds?

**Concept: Multitask Learning in Encoder‑Decoder Architectures**  
- **Why needed:** ViMultiChoice’s core innovation is joint training for classification (answer) and generation (explanation). Understanding how shared representations serve both objectives and potential negative transfer is essential.  
- **Quick check:** In the architecture `f_enc = [f_Q, f_P, f_k]` used for explanation, why is the vector of the *chosen* option (not all options) used? What does this assume about the relationship between decision and explanation?

**Concept: Transformer Self‑Attention as a Flexible Routing Mechanism**  
- **Why needed:** ViWordFormer modifies the attention matrix by element‑wise multiplication with a Phrasal Score matrix. Comprehending this requires a mental model of how `Attention(Q,K,V) = softmax(QKᵀ/√d)V` computes weighted sums and how `A ⊙ P` alters token‑to‑token routing.  
- **Quick check:** If `P` has near‑zero values for token pairs believed to be in *different* phrases, what is the effect of `A' = A ⊙ P` on their attention weight? How does this differ from simply adding `P` to `A`?

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component map:**  
Retrieved context -> Vietnamese Text Encoder (ViT5 + ViWordFormer modules for question, each option, and context) -> feature sets `f_Q`, `f_O_k`, `f_P` -> Option Inference (keep‑eliminate comparison, cross‑attention with `f_P`, self‑attention) -> option‑specific vectors `f_k` -> Option Head (max‑pool + linear + softmax) -> answer prediction; separately `f_{k_max}` (chosen option) + `f_Q` + `f_P` -> Explanation Generator (autoregressive decoder) -> explanation text.

**Critical path:** Retrieval → ViWordFormer encoding → Option comparison & context integration → Option scoring → Explanation generation from selected option’s representation.

**Design tradeoffs:**  
- ViWordFormer adds O(n²) phrasal score computation; ablation shows benefit but increases memory.  
- Retrieval quality bounds performance; VietBGE P@15=38.12% may limit the model.  
- Multitask training uses loss weight λ=1.0 without tuning; imbalance could harm one task.  
- Option comparison uses OCN‑style pairwise interactions (12 comparisons), which is costlier than independent encoding.

**Failure signatures:**  
- Answer accuracy gaps across grades (e.g., Grade 11 67.43% vs. Grade 10 60.12%) and subjects (e.g., Literature 66.19% vs. Civic Ed 70.18%) indicate domain sensitivity.  
- Low BLEU‑4/ROUGE‑L on some samples suggests generic or unfaithful explanations.  
- Visualizing ViWordFormer’s `P` matrix may reveal low phrasal cohesion scores for known Vietnamese compounds, indicating mis‑groupings.  
- Multitask collapse: if explanation loss dominates, answer accuracy may plateau or degrade.

**First 3 experiments:**  
1. Retriever sanity: Run VietBGE on a sample of 50 questions, manually inspect top‑k retrieved passages for relevance. Confirm the context contains necessary information to answer.  
2. ViWordFormer ablation: Train two models on a small subset: (a) without ViWordFormer, (b) full model. Compare validation accuracy and explanation BLEU‑4. Expect a clear gap.  
3. Multitask vs. single‑task: Train with `L = L_MC` only and with `L = L_MC + L_E`. Use the same selected‑option vector for explanation generation during evaluation. Validate that multitask yields higher answer accuracy; if not, adjust loss weighting.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

- **Open Q1:** How can the option comparison module be enhanced to handle confusing distractors? The authors note ViMultiChoice fails on questions with closely similar information, and further improvement should focus on option comparison.  
- **Open Q2:** What is the mechanism behind multitask training’s accuracy gain, and is it generalizable beyond the proposed architecture? The paper shows empirical improvement but does not analyze whether it stems from representation learning, regularization, or forced reasoning.  
- **Open Q3:** To what extent does ViMultiChoice generalize to other low‑resource languages with different linguistic structures? The ViWordFormer is tailored to Vietnamese phrasal lexemes; language‑specific components need investigation.  
- **Open Q4:** How does the quality and source of retrieved context affect performance, especially for out‑of‑curriculum knowledge? The model relies on textbook passages; robustness to broader or unstructured contexts is unknown.  
- **Open Q5:** Why do LLMs achieve high answer accuracy but significantly lower explanation quality on Vietnamese MCRC? The paper observes this gap but does not explore causes such as tokenization, pretraining data, or task mismatch.

## Limitations
- Dataset construction depends on high‑school teachers, potentially introducing bias toward textbook phrasing and limiting diversity of reasoning strategies.  
- ViWordFormer’s phrasal segmentation is learned without explicit supervision, risking mis‑grouping for ambiguous compounds.  
- Multitask training uses equal loss weighting (`λ=1.0`) without sensitivity analysis; automatic metrics (BLEU/ROUGE) may not reflect human‑judged explanation quality or faithfulness.

## Next Checks
1. **Phrasal Structure Validation:** Sample 200 Vietnamese compound words; compute average Phrasal Score `P` for within‑phrase vs. cross‑phrase token pairs. Expect high within‑phrase scores and low cross‑phrase scores.  
2. **Explanation Faithfulness Probe:** For 100 predictions, have a human evaluator judge whether the generated explanation logically supports the selected option (yes/no). Compare agreement with automatic BLEU/ROUGE scores.  
3. **Multitask Loss Sensitivity Sweep:** Train models with λ ∈ {0.1, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0} on a small subset; plot answer accuracy vs. explanation BLEU to identify optimal trade‑off and confirm that λ=1.0 yields the best balance.
