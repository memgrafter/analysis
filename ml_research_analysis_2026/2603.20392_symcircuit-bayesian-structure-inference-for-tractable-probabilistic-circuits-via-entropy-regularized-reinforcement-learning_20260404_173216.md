# SymCircuit: Bayesian Structure Inference for Tractable Probabilistic Circuits via Entropy-Regularized Reinforcement Learning

## Quick Facts
- **ArXiv ID:** 2603.20392  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.20392  
- **Reference count:** 3  
- **Primary result:** On NLTCS, SymCircuit closes 93% of the performance gap to LearnSPN (−6.105 vs. −6.093 nats) with 33× higher sample efficiency; option-level REINFORCE improves SNR by 3–5×. Preliminary Plants (69 vars) closes 19% of the gap.

## Executive Summary
SymCircuit replaces greedy probabilistic circuit structure search with a differentiable, amortized approach using entropy-regularized reinforcement learning. A grammar-constrained Transformer (SymFormer) generates valid PC structures, and the training objective – an expected log-likelihood plus a KL divergence to a prior – corresponds to approximating a Bayesian posterior over structures when the temperature is set inversely to the dataset size. Parameter optimization for each sampled circuit uses a hybrid scheme: flow-weighted EM (Anemone) for sum weights and Adam for leaves. Option-level REINFORCE updates only at structural decision points (sum-node arities), dramatically improving gradient signal-to-noise ratio.

On NLTCS, SymCircuit achieves near state-of-the-art test log-likelihood (−6.105 nats), closing 93% of the LearnSPN gap (−6.093 nats) while using 33× fewer sampled circuits. Preliminary experiments on the 69-variable Plants dataset indicate scalability, closing 19% of the gap. Uncertainty analysis attributes 85% of predictive variance on NLTCS to structural diversity, underscoring the benefit of Bayesian model averaging over circuits.

## Method Summary
SymCircuit has two training phases. **Phase 1 (Supervised pretraining):** The SymFormer policy is initialized by imitation learning on token sequences produced by LearnSPN, using cross-entropy loss. The policy generates sequences that obey a context-free grammar enforcing smoothness and decomposability, guaranteeing valid PC structures. **Phase 2 (RL fine-tuning):** The objective `J(ψ) = E[S∼πψ][R(S)] - α·D_KL(πψ ∥ P0)` is maximized, where `R(S) = (1/N) log p(D|S)` is the average validation log-likelihood of structure `S` after parameter optimization, and `P0` is the pretrained prior. Setting `α = 1/N` recovers the exact Bayesian posterior in the infinite-capacity limit. Policy gradients are computed with **option-level REINFORCE**, restricting updates to the `D` tokens that determine sum-node arities. This reduces gradient variance by a factor of `√(T/D)` relative to token-level updates (`T` = total tokens). For each sampled circuit, parameters are optimized via a hybrid scheme: sum weights use Anemone’s flow-weighted EM (exploiting the block-diagonal Fisher structure for natural gradient efficiency), while leaf parameters use Adam because EM degrades under random sum-weight initialization. Training employs ε-greedy exploration and a replay buffer.

## Key Results
- On NLTCS (16 variables), SymCircuit attains test log-likelihood **−6.105 nats**, closing **93%** of the LearnSPN gap (−6.093 nats) with **33× higher sample efficiency**.
- **Option-level REINFORCE** improves gradient signal-to-noise ratio by **3–5×** compared to token-level variants.
- Preliminary results on the **69‑variable Plants** dataset show scalability, closing **19%** of the performance gap; three-layer uncertainty decomposition attributes **85%** of predictive variance on NLTCS to structural diversity.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
### Mechanism 1: Entropy‑regularized RL as Bayesian structure inference
The objective `J(ψ) = E[S∼πψ][R(S)] - α·D_KL(πψ ∥ P0)` is maximized. Proposition 1 shows its unique maximizer is `π*(S) ∝ P0(S)·p(D|S)^(1/(Nα))`. Setting `α = 1/N` recovers the exact Bayesian posterior `P(S|D)`. Thus the trained policy `πψ` approximates a tempered posterior. *Core assumption:* The grammar’s hypothesis space `𝒢` contains high‑likelihood circuits and `πψ` has sufficient capacity. *Break condition:* If `𝒢` excludes the MAP structure or optimization fails, the Bayesian interpretation weakens.

### Mechanism 2: Option‑level REINFORCE boosts SNR
In a circuit of length `T`, only `D` tokens (sum‑node arities) carry meaningful gradient signal; others are nearly deterministic under the grammar. Option‑level REINFORCE averages gradients over these `D` decisions, while token-level averages over `T`. Proposition 2 proves `SNR(option)/SNR(token) = √(T/D)` under simplifying assumptions. *Core assumption:* Signal is concentrated exclusively in structural tokens and per‑token noise variances are comparable. *Break condition:* If leaf or product choices also carry signal (e.g., stochastic grammar), the gain diminishes; if `D` scales with `T`, the ratio may be modest.

### Mechanism 3: Hybrid parameter optimization (Anemone + Adam)
For fixed structures, sum weights are updated via flow‑weighted EM (Anemone), exploiting the block‑diagonal Fisher information of PC trees to approximate natural gradient descent efficiently. Leaf parameters use Adam because EM degrades when sum weights are randomly initialized (poor flow statistics). *Core assumption:* The Fisher matrix is block‑diagonal for tree‑structured circuits, and cross‑information between sum weights and leaves is negligible under random initialization. *Break condition:* For circuits with shared subgraphs or deep interactions, block‑diagonality may fail; poor sum‑weight initialization can also harm Anemone.

## Foundational Learning
- **Probabilistic Circuits (PCs):** Tractable models with decomposability (product children disjoint scopes) and smoothness (sum children same scope), guaranteeing linear‑time marginalization. *Why needed:* SymCircuit generates and evaluates PC structures; these constraints ensure validity and efficiency. *Quick check:* Why does decomposability guarantee that a product node’s computation factors over disjoint variable sets?
- **Reinforcement Learning as Inference:** An entropy‑regularized RL policy corresponds to a posterior distribution over actions. *Why needed:* This frames structure learning as Bayesian inference, justifying entropy regularization and the tempered posterior. *Quick check:* How does adding `-α·D_KL(π∥P0)` to the expected reward change the optimal policy from MAP to a distribution?
- **Autoregressive Transformers with Grammar Constraints:** A Transformer that generates token sequences subject to a grammar via dynamic masking and tree‑relative attention biases. *Why needed:* SymFormer is the policy network; understanding grammar enforcement and architectural biases is key to implementation. *Quick check:* Why is a fixed‑arity token vocabulary (e.g., `Factorize3`) necessary for unambiguous parsing?

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:** Grammar (valid tokens per state) → SymFormer (Transformer with grammar mask) → Parsed circuit → Option selector (identifies sum‑arity tokens) → Parameter optimizer (Anemone for sum weights, Adam for leaves) → Reward calculator (validation LL) → RL trainer (option‑level REINFORCE update). Replay buffer stores circuits for stable learning.

**Critical path:** Grammar definition → SymFormer architecture (masking, tree‑relative attention) → Supervised pretraining on LearnSPN sequences → RL fine‑tuning loop (sample structure → optimize parameters → compute reward → option‑level gradient → update policy). Per‑circuit parameter optimization is the primary bottleneck.

**Design tradeoffs:**
- *Grammar expressivity vs. action space:* Fixed arity simplifies parsing but may limit expressiveness; larger vocab increases search difficulty.
- *Option‑level vs. token‑level:* Option‑level improves SNR but relies on correct identification of structural tokens; including more decisions could capture additional signal at the cost of variance.
- *Anemone steps per circuit:* More EM steps yield a more accurate reward but increase runtime; the paper uses 30–100, with 30 sufficient for ranking on Plants.

**Failure signatures:**
- Invalid circuits: grammar masking bug → check `𝒱(σ_t)` computation.
- No RL improvement: reward not varying or wrong `ℐ(S)` → monitor gradient norms and reward variance; increase exploration ε or entropy α.
- Parameter instability (NaN, collapse): ensure Anemone applied only to sum weights; leaves use Adam; clamp flow probabilities.
- High gradient variance: policy collapse or too few structural tokens → inspect token distribution; consider expanding grammar.

**First 3 experiments:**
1. **Grammar & SymFormer smoke test:** Generate 100 sequences with high ε‑greedy from an untrained model; parse each and assert 100% validity; verify `ℐ(S)` picks only sum‑arity tokens.
2. **Supervised pretraining convergence:** Train on LearnSPN sequences for 50 epochs; cross‑entropy loss on a held‑out set should approach zero.
3. **Option‑level gradient sanity check:** Sample 10 circuits, compute `R(S)` with brief optimization; for one circuit, manually compute gradient contributions for a sum‑arity token and a leaf token; confirm sum‑arity tokens have non‑trivial magnitude and the option gradient is finite.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- **Full DEBD benchmark evaluation:** How does SymCircuit perform across all 20 DEBD datasets? Current evaluation is limited to NLTCS and Plants.
- **Grammar vocabulary expansion:** Does including Sum4/Sum5 close the residual 0.011‑nat gap on NLTCS? What are the effects on action space size and sample efficiency?
- **Scalability to Plants:** Will training on the 69‑variable Plants dataset converge to competitive performance, and does the 33× sample efficiency advantage hold for larger circuits?
- **Theoretical amortization gap:** Can we derive bounds on the KL divergence between the optimal tempered posterior and the learned policy in terms of grammar complexity or model capacity?

## Limitations
- Hyperparameters (SymFormer size, EM steps, RL rates) are not fully specified, affecting reproducibility and potentially performance.
- The grammar’s partition strategy for `Factorize_k` (splitting scopes) is ambiguous and influences structural diversity.
- Evaluation is limited to two datasets (NLTCS, Plants); claims about generality and scalability remain provisional.

## Confidence
