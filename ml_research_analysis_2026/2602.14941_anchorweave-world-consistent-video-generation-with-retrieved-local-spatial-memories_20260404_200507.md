# AnchorWeave: World-Consistent Video Generation with Retrieved Local Spatial Memories

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.14941
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14941
- **Reference count:** 38
- **Primary result:** Conditioning on local point clouds yields PSNR 20.96 and SSIM 0.6727, substantially outperforming global point cloud baseline (16.31 PSNR, 0.5345 SSIM); multi-anchor (K=4) further improves over single-anchor.

## Executive Summary
AnchorWeave tackles spatial inconsistency in long-horizon, camera-controlled video generation caused by cross-view misalignment in globally fused 3D scene reconstructions. It introduces a memory-augmented framework that replaces a single global point cloud with multiple per-frame local geometric memories. A coverage-driven retrieval strategy selects complementary local memories aligned with the target camera trajectory, and a multi-anchor weaving controller fuses their rendered anchor videos via shared cross-attention and pose-guided importance weighting to reconcile residual misalignments. Evaluated on RealEstate10K and DL3DV, AnchorWeave significantly improves long-term scene consistency over prior memory-augmented methods.

## Method Summary
AnchorWeave constructs a bank of per-frame local point clouds from historical video using a 3D estimator (e.g., TTT3R). For a given target camera trajectory, it retrieves a diverse set of local memories via a greedy visibility coverage maximization algorithm. Each retrieved memory is rasterized along the trajectory to produce an anchor video and a sequence of retrieved-to-target relative camera poses. A multi-anchor weaving controller then processes all anchor latents: it applies shared cross-attention over concatenated anchor tokens and fuses them using MLP-encoded pose weights. The fused control signal is injected into a frozen video diffusion backbone (e.g., CogVideoX, Wan2.2) during early denoising steps. The process iterates: newly generated frames are added to the history, new local memories are created, and the cycle repeats for long-horizon generation.

## Key Results
- Conditioning on local point clouds achieves PSNR 20.96 and SSIM 0.6727, substantially outperforming global point cloud baseline (16.31 PSNR, 0.5345 SSIM).
- Using multiple anchors (K=4) improves over the single-anchor variant, demonstrating the effectiveness of multi-anchor conditioning.
- AnchorWeave significantly improves long-term scene consistency over prior memory-augmented methods on RealEstate10K and DL3DV.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
- **Local point clouds reduce cross-view misalignment:** Global fusion accumulates pose and depth estimation errors, causing "ghosting." Local point clouds, built independently per frame, avoid this artifact, providing cleaner geometric signals for rendering-based conditioning. Evidence: abstract and Sec. 1; quantitative gap from local vs global ablation.
- **Coverage-driven retrieval selects complementary anchors:** A greedy algorithm iteratively picks memories that maximize newly visible surface area along the target trajectory, ensuring diverse spatial guidance rather than redundant views. Evidence: Sec. 3.3; PSNR/SSIM improve with more anchors.
- **Multi-anchor weaving controller reconciles inconsistencies:** Shared cross-attention enables information exchange across anchors to aggregate correct geometry and suppress noise. Pose-guided fusion downweights anchors with large relative pose discrepancies, mitigating residual misalignment. Evidence: Sec. 3.4; ablations confirm benefits of joint attention and pose weighting.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** 3D Estimator -> Memory Bank -> Coverage-Driven Retriever -> Anchor Renderer -> Multi-anchor Weaving Controller -> Backbone Video Diffusion -> (loop back: new frames -> 3D Estimator)
- **Critical path (one segment):** 
  1. Given history and next T-frame target trajectory, partition into D-frame chunks.
  2. For each chunk: run retriever (candidate FoV filter -> greedy coverage) to get ≤K memories.
  3. Render each memory as anchor video; compute retrieved-to-target poses.
  4. Concatenate anchors/poses across chunks to full length.
  5. For each early denoising timestep: controller takes anchor latents & poses -> shared attention -> pose-weighted fusion -> inject fused latent + target pose into backbone.
  6. Decode latent -> output segment.
  7. Update memory bank with new segment; repeat.
- **Design tradeoffs:** 
  - K (retrievals): higher improves consistency but increases cost; paper finds optimal K=4.
  - Controller injection depth: first 1/3 layers balances guidance and backbone priors.
  - Chunk length D: tradeoff between retrieval cost and temporal precision.
  - Local vs global: local avoids fusion artifacts but may need more retrievals.
- **Failure signatures:** 
  - Ghosting/double edges: anchors misaligned; pose proxy fails or K too low.
  - Blurry incoherent structure: shared attention fails to aggregate; check separate attention ablation.
  - Camera drift: no anchor coverage for novel views; monitor coverage.
  - Chunk boundary artifacts: stitching issues between anchor clips.
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1. Replicate core quantitative finding: train K=1 and K=4 on small subset; validate K=4 improves PSNR/SSIM on partial-revisit test set.
  2. Isolate local memory benefit: train two K=1 models (global vs local anchors); compare metrics to confirm local superiority.
  3. Test pose-guided fusion ablation: replace pose-weighted fusion with uniform average in trained K=4 model; compare visual quality (ghosting) and LPIPS.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- How robust is AnchorWeave to inaccuracies in per-frame depth and pose estimation from the 3D reconstruction model?
- Is the greedy, coverage-driven retrieval strategy optimal, or could alternative criteria improve performance?
- How well does AnchorWeave handle scenes with dynamic objects or non-rigid motion, given its static geometry assumption?
- What is the computational cost of AnchorWeave, and how does it scale with video length, resolution, or number of retrieved memories?

## Limitations
- Implementation details unspecified (TTT3R specifics, coverage grid resolution, pose encoding MLP, control injection mechanics) hinder exact replication.
- Pose-guided fusion assumes relative camera pose reliably indicates geometric misalignment; this may fail for non-linear distortions or noisy pose estimation.
- Greedy coverage maximization may select irrelevant or redundant anchors when trajectory prediction is uncertain.
- No anchor coverage for novel target views leads to hallucination and camera drift.

## Confidence
- **Local point cloud superiority (reduces cross-view misalignment):** High
- **Coverage-driven retrieval (selects complementary anchors):** Medium
- **Multi-anchor weaving controller (reconciles inconsistencies):** Medium-High
