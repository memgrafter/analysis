# Do Post-Training Algorithms Actually Differ? A Controlled Study Across Model Scales Uncovers Scale-Dependent Ranking Inversions
## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.19335
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19335
- **Reference count:** 21
- **Primary result:** Algorithm rankings invert with model scale—SimPO is worst at ≤3B but best at 7B on GSM8K—while DPO loss modifications yield negligible gains and algorithm leverage collapses on out-of-distribution tasks.

## Executive Summary
This study introduces OXRL, a unified framework implementing 51 post-training algorithms with identical infrastructure to enable apples-to-apples comparisons. Evaluating 8 algorithms across 4 model scales (0.5B–7B) on GSM8K reveals three headline findings: rankings invert dramatically with scale (e.g., SimPO reverses from worst to best), modifications to the DPO loss produce no significant improvements after multiple testing correction, and the performance spread among algorithms collapses on harder or out-of-distribution tasks. A hierarchical decomposition shows model scale dominates (~50 pp), followed by training paradigm (~10 pp), online/offline distinction (~9 pp), and loss function (~1 pp).

## Method Summary
The OXRL framework standardizes training infrastructure—including DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, fixed hyperparameters (AdamW, cosine LR schedule), and evaluation via LM Eval Harness—to isolate algorithmic differences. Experiments use Qwen 2.5 Instruct checkpoints (0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B) trained on synthetic preference pairs generated via model self-play on GSM8K’s training set. Core comparisons include SFT, DPO, SimPO, IPO, KTO, and online RL methods (SGRPO, GSPO, CISPO). For scale comparisons, 3B is evaluated with both full fine-tuning and LoRA to disentangle scale from parameter-efficient tuning. A sweep of 20 DPO variants at 1.5B uses 5 seeds each, with Bonferroni-corrected t-tests for statistical rigor. Primary metric is strict exact-match accuracy on GSM8K test set; cross-task evaluation uses MATH and general-domain benchmarks (ARC, HellaSwag, WinoGrande).

## Key Results
- **Scale-dependent ranking inversion:** On GSM8K, SGRPO is best at 1.5B (58.0% ±0.57) while SimPO is worst; at 7B, SimPO becomes best (85.8%), a 49.8 pp reversal driven by scale, not LoRA.
- **DPO loss variants ineffective:** None of 20 variants significantly outperform vanilla DPO after Bonferroni correction; SimPO is significantly worse (−11.5 pp, p<10⁻⁴).
- **Task-specific leverage collapse:** Algorithm spread on GSM8K (19.3 pp) collapses on MATH (0.54 pp, 36× reduction) and general-domain benchmarks (0.47 pp, 41× reduction).

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
**Scale-dependent inversion due to shifting bottlenecks:** At small scales (≤3B), post-training is bottlenecked by fundamental reasoning ability; reference-based methods (DPO, IPO, KTO) and SFT cluster because the model cannot reliably generate correct solutions. At 7B, reasoning capacity is sufficient; the primary limiter becomes **format compliance**—producing answers in the exact expected structure. Reference-free methods like SimPO, which encourage autonomous behavior without a reference model’s implicit formatting, learn to produce well-formatted answers, while reference-based methods inherit formatting biases. The strict exact-match metric heavily penalizes format violations, creating the inversion. Evidence: SimPO’s strict-match exceeds flexible-extract by +4.9 pp at 7B (excellent formatting), while SFT hides −3.9 pp behind non-compliance; this gap is negligible at 1.5B. If strict-match were replaced with flexible extraction, the inversion would diminish.

**DPO loss tweaks are low-leverage:** All 20 variants approximate the same offline preference learning objective; small modifications (divergence measure, weighting, reference usage) change optimization dynamics marginally but do not alter fundamental inductive bias or data efficiency. The dominant drivers are upstream: model capacity (scale) and training paradigm (online RL vs. static pairs). Evidence: No variant significantly beats vanilla DPO after Bonferroni correction; only SimPO is significantly worse. The 15.7 pp spread among variants is driven entirely by underperformance; the upside is noise. The mechanism applies only to within-family loss tweaks on the same static dataset; a variant introducing a fundamentally new inductive bias (e.g., explicit reasoning steps) might break the ~1 pp ceiling.

**Task-specific collapse from narrow training distribution:** Post-training algorithms optimize for success on the training distribution (GSM8K-style math). Their learned adjustments—in reasoning pattern, format, or response style—are narrowly tailored and do not transfer to harder but related tasks (MATH) or general-domain benchmarks. The model’s base knowledge and general reasoning, shaped during pretraining, dominate outside the training distribution. Evidence: Spread compresses 36× on MATH and 41× on general domain; rank correlation with GSM8K drops from 1.0 to 0.0 (MATH) to +0.6 (general domain). The collapse depends on a narrow train/test distribution gap; diverse training data or near-perfect test subsets would reduce it.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** OXRL -> BaseAlgorithm接口 -> 共享组件: Model Loader, Data Pipeline, DeepSpeed ZeRO-3, vLLM Rollouts, Eval Harness; 每算法自定义: Loss Function.
- **Critical path:** To add a new algorithm, implement its loss computation within the `BaseAlgorithm` interface using standardized log-probability calculations from `πθ` and `πref`. No changes to training loop, optimizer, or data loading are needed.
- **Design tradeoffs:**
  - 统一基础设施保障公平比较，但强制对所有算法使用相同超参（LR、β、训练轮数），可能抑制各算法的峰值性能；权衡是受控比较优先于每算法最优调优。
  - 早期版本因 `DistributedSampler` 种子问题导致随机种子下 SL 运行完全确定；修复后暴露真实随机性（如 DPO 在 3B 的标准差 2.01 pp），现需多种子运行以捕捉不确定性。
- **Failure signatures:**
  - 隐藏确定性: 不同随机种子产生完全相同的结果 → 检查 `DistributedSampler` 是否传播种子。
  - 训练发散: CISPO、BetaDPO、CalDPO 等变体可能出现 NaN 或损失曲线 spikes → 监控训练损失。
  - 格式崩溃: 基础模型在 3B 的 strict-flex 差距达 −52.2 pp；若后训练未改善格式合规，strict-match 提升会极小 → 检查各算法的 format compliance gap（表 9）。
- **First 3 experiments:**
  1. 重现规模反转: 在 0.5B、1.5B、3B（全 FT）和 7B（LoRA）上训练 SFT、DPO、SimPO、IPO、KTO、SGRPO、GSPO、CISPO；验证 SimPO 在 1.5B 最差（~58% 附近 SGRPO 最佳）、7B 最佳（~85.8%），且 LoRA 在 3B 影响可忽略。
  2. DPO 变体扫描: 在 1.5B 上运行全部 20 种变体各 5 种子；进行 Bonferroni 校正的 Welch’s t 检验（α=0.0026）对比 vanilla DPO；预期无显著胜者，SimPO 应显著更差。
  3. 跨任务坍塌验证: 评估所有 1.5B 检查点在 MATH 和通用基准（ARC、HellaSwag、WinoGrande）；确认 spread 从 GSM8K 的 ~19.3 pp 压缩至 MATH 的 ~0.54 pp 和通用域的 ~0.47 pp，且无算法显著超越基础模型在通用域的表现。

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
- 其他模型家族（如 Llama）和 >7B 规模下是否观察到相同的排名反转？
- 人类偏好数据集（vs 合成自弈数据）是否改变算法排名和 DPO 变体效果？
- 基于采样的评估（pass@k、majority）是否改变测量到的算法 spread 相对于贪心解码？
- 在代码生成（MBPP）和指令遵循任务中是否可复现任务特定的 spread 坍塌？

## Limitations
- 仅测试单一模型家族（Qwen 2.5）至 7B 参数；推广至其他架构或更大尺度未知。
- 使用合成偏好数据和贪心解码；人类数据或采样评估可能产生不同排名。
- 统一超参确保公平性，但可能抑制每算法可达到的峰值性能；结果反映受控条件下的相对排名。

## Confidence
- Scale-dependent inversion: **Medium** – 受控跨规模实验显示强模式，但机制依赖严格匹配评估的格式敏感性；灵活评分可能减弱反转。
- DPO variants ineffective: **High** – 20 变体扫描加 Bonferroni 校正显示无显著收益；仅 SimPO 显著更差。
- Task-specific leverage collapse: **High** – 跨任务 36–41× spread 压缩在不同规模和算法中一致。

## Next Checks
1. 用灵活答案提取重做核心规模比较；若排名反转减弱，则格式合规是主驱动力。
2. 实现具有新归纳偏置的 DPO 变体（如显式多步推理）；若其突破 ~1 pp 天花板，则损失微调本身是低杠杆因素。
3. 在多领域偏好数据（如 GSM8K + 代码 + 问答）上训练；若跨任务 spread 不坍塌，则任务特定坍塌源于窄训练分布。
