# Improving Text-to-Image Generation with Intrinsic Self-Confidence Rewards

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2603.00918
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00918
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** SOLACE improves compositional generation (GenEval from 0.65 to 0.71), text rendering (OCR from 0.61 to 0.67), and text-image alignment (CLIPScore from 0.282 to 0.288) while matching human-preference scores of baseline models

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces SOLACE, an intrinsic reward framework for text-to-image generation that eliminates the need for external reward models. SOLACE works by measuring a model's self-confidence through re-noising its generated latents and evaluating how well it recovers the injected noise across timesteps; this noise recovery error is transformed into a scalar reward. The framework performs reinforcement learning post-training without any external supervision, achieving improvements in compositional generation, text rendering, and text-image alignment while matching human-preference scores of baseline models. Notably, SOLACE also combines with external rewards, boosting non-target metrics while only mildly reducing the target reward, demonstrating complementarity and mitigation of reward hacking.

## Method Summary
SOLACE introduces an intrinsic reward mechanism that measures self-confidence by re-noising generated latents and evaluating noise recovery performance. The framework transforms this noise recovery error into a scalar reward signal for reinforcement learning post-training. Unlike traditional approaches that rely on external reward models, SOLACE operates entirely without supervision, making it a fully unsupervised method for improving text-to-image generation quality. The approach is designed to address reward hacking issues common in post-training while improving specific capabilities like compositional generation and text rendering.

## Key Results
- Compositional generation improved from 0.65 to 0.71 on GenEval benchmark
- Text rendering improved from 0.61 to 0.67 on OCR benchmark
- Text-image alignment improved from 0.282 to 0.288 on CLIPScore
- Performance matches human-preference scores (PickScore ~22.4) of baseline SD3.5-M models
- Gains comparable to three-times-larger SD3.5-L model

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The intrinsic reward signal in SOLACE works by measuring the model's ability to recover injected noise in its own generated latents. When a model can successfully recover noise that was intentionally added to its outputs, it demonstrates self-consistency and confidence in its generation process. This self-confidence signal serves as a proxy for generation quality, allowing the model to improve without external supervision. The noise recovery evaluation across timesteps captures the model's understanding of the underlying structure and relationships in the generated images, which correlates with better compositional and text rendering capabilities.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Text prompt -> Image generation model -> Latent space manipulation -> Noise injection -> Self-recovery evaluation -> Intrinsic reward computation -> RL weight updates
- **Critical path:** Text prompt → generation → noise injection → self-recovery evaluation → reward computation → weight update (the core loop that drives improvement)
- **Design tradeoffs:** SOLACE trades computational overhead of self-evaluation for elimination of external reward model training, and accepts potentially noisier intrinsic signals for greater robustness against reward hacking
- **Failure signatures:** Poor noise recovery leading to weak rewards, reward collapse from overly aggressive noise recovery penalties, or instability in RL training due to poorly calibrated intrinsic signals
- **Three first experiments:** 1) Validate noise recovery metric correlates with generation quality, 2) Test intrinsic reward signal stability across different noise levels, 3) Compare performance with and without noise recovery preprocessing

## Limitations
- The claim of being "fully unsupervised" lacks clarity regarding potential implicit supervision in training data or preprocessing
- Performance improvements, while demonstrated, are modest in magnitude and may not be statistically significant
- The framework's robustness to out-of-distribution prompts and long-tail compositional concepts remains untested
- The specific mechanisms by which SOLACE addresses reward hacking versus general reward shaping are not fully explored

## Confidence
- SOLACE provides effective intrinsic rewards without external supervision: **Medium** - Novel approach but lacks extensive ablation studies
- SOLACE improves compositional generation, text rendering, and text-image alignment: **Medium** - Improvements shown but modest magnitude, no statistical significance testing
- SOLACE is comparable to three-times-larger models: **Low** - Based on single metric without considering other quality dimensions or computational costs
- SOLACE mitigates reward hacking when combined with external rewards: **Medium** - Demonstrated through complementary performance, but specific mechanisms unclear
