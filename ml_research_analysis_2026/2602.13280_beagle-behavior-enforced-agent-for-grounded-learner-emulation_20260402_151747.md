# BEAGLE: Behavior-Enforced Agent for Grounded Learner Emulation

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2602.13280  
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.13280  
- **Reference count:** 40  
- **Primary result:** BEAGLE-generated traces are perceptually indistinguishable from real student data (human Turing test accuracy 52.8%, d′ = 0.15) and significantly outperform LLM baselines on behavioral, epistemic, and perceptual fidelity metrics.

## Executive Summary
BEAGLE addresses the "competency bias" in LLM-based student simulation—where models default to efficient, expert-like correctness instead of the erratic, iterative struggle of novices. It introduces a neuro-symbolic framework that enforces behavioral, epistemic, and perceptual fidelity through three core mechanisms: (1) a semi-Markov controller that governs realistic timing and transitions of metacognitive and cognitive states; (2) Bayesian Knowledge Tracing with explicit flaw injection to simulate knowledge gaps and "unknown unknowns"; and (3) a decoupled Strategist/Executor architecture that prevents silent self-correction. Evaluated on Python programming tasks, BEAGLE achieves near-perfect alignment with real student behavior, as confirmed by both automated metrics and a human Turing test with 71 participants.

## Method Summary
BEAGLE is a neuro-symbolic simulator that combines a rule-based symbolic control layer with LLM-generated actions, without any model fine-tuning. The symbolic layer manages a semi-Markov process (state transitions with Gamma-distributed durations) to model metacognitive and cognitive dynamics, a Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) engine to track per-concept mastery, and an explicit flaw injection (EFI) mechanism that converts low-mastery states into hard constraints blocking concept usage. The neural layer uses a decoupled two-LLM design: a Strategist produces high-level directives, and an Executor strictly implements them in code or monologue, with separate memory buffers to enforce role separation. The system is configured with a student profile (behavioral dynamics ρ_behav and linguistic persona ρ_persona) and a problem domain with 12 knowledge components. Execution occurs in a gated environment where observations are filtered (e.g., errors hidden during ENACTING) to shape perception.

## Key Results
- Achieved human-indistinguishable realism: LLM-as-judge score 2.68/3.0 (vs. ≤1.74 for baselines); human Turing test accuracy 52.8% (chance-level, p=0.053), d′ = 0.15.
- Behavioral divergence (D_KL) against real student distributions dropped to 0.43 (vs. ≥0.97 for CoT, 3.97 for Vanilla).
- Error recurrence rate reached 86.2%, matching real data (92.0%) and far exceeding Vanilla (7.8%).
- Differentiated skill levels: 40% solve rate gap between HIGH and LOW profiles (vs. 0% for Vanilla).

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Semi-Markov Behavioral Controller
- **Claim:** Enforces realistic temporal patterns by modeling state durations with variable (Gamma) distributions, overcoming geometric limitations of standard Markov chains.
- **Mechanism:** A two-level model: metacognitive states (PLANNING, ENACTING, etc.) follow a first-order Markov chain; each segment’s duration is sampled from a Gamma distribution fitted to real data. This captures overdispersed durations (e.g., students stuck in ENACTING).
- **Core assumption:** Real student behavior exhibits state-dependent duration variability that is not memoryless.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract and Section 3.2/A.2 show empirical deviation from geometric constraints and fitted parameters; ablation removing semi-Markov increases D_KL catastrophically (to 6.76).
- **Break condition:** If real durations are memoryless or Gamma parameters are poorly estimated, temporal dynamics diverge; independence between duration and next transition may be violated.

### Bayesian Knowledge Tracing with Explicit Flaw Injection (EFI)
- **Claim:** Enforces epistemic fidelity by simulating the "curse of incompetence"—novices cannot access concepts they haven’t mastered, even if present in the LLM’s knowledge.
- **Mechanism:** BKT tracks continuous mastery probabilities P(L_k) for each knowledge component k. Mastery is discretized (Unknown/Partial/Mastered) and translated into natural language constraints κ_t. When mastery is low, EFI injects a hard constraint: "You have NEVER heard of and CANNOT use: [concept]."
- **Core assumption:** Authentic novices are metacognitively blind to missing concepts; they cannot retrieve expert knowledge even if latent.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract and Section 3.2/B.2 describe BKT/EFI; Figure 8 shows EFI forces incorrect approximations; error recurrence rate (86.2%) indicates stable misconceptions.
- **Break condition:** If the LLM overrides constraints due to strong pretraining or constraint wording is ambiguous, EFI fails; fixed BKT parameters may not match the domain.

### Decoupled Strategist/Executor Architecture
- **Claim:** Prevents silent self-correction by separating strategy formulation from code generation, ensuring the Executor follows flawed directives instead of diagnosing errors.
- **Mechanism:** The Strategist produces a (goal, mindset, directive) tuple from the symbolic state; the Executor generates actions strictly to implement the directive. Memory buffers separate their contexts, and task framing prohibits diagnostic reasoning.
- **Core assumption:** LLMs inherently possess debugging skills; without architectural constraints, they will correct errors even when instructed to act as novices.
- **Evidence anchors:** Abstract and Section 3.3 detail the design; ablation merging them reduces realism by 21% and error recurrence by 19%.
- **Break condition:** If Strategist’s directive includes implicit diagnostic cues or buffers leak, Executor may self-correct; fine-tuned models might override role instructions.

## Foundational Learning
- **Self-Regulated Learning (SRL) Theory:** Explains the cyclic metacognitive states (PLANNING, ENACTING, MONITORING, REFLECTING). Quick check: A student who immediately starts coding without planning maps to ENACTING.
- **Semi-Markov Processes:** Captures variable state durations via Gamma distributions. Quick check: Why can’t a standard Markov chain capture prolonged debugging loops? Because it imposes geometric durations with fixed coefficient of variation, while real data shows overdispersion (CV > 1).
- **Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT):** Models latent knowledge states and response parameters (slip/guess). Quick check: If a student errs despite knowing a concept, which BKT parameter accounts for it? Slip probability P(S).

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map:** Symbolic Control -> Neural Action -> Environment. Specifically: Semi-Markov Controller & BKT Engine -> Strategist (LLM + Γ_Strat) -> Executor (LLM + Γ_Exec) -> IDE Oracle. Memory buffers (Γ_Strat, Γ_Exec) and observation filtering sit between layers.
- **Critical path:** 1. Sample M_t, C_t, D_t from semi-Markov; check interrupts. 2. Update BKT (if applicable), generate κ_t via EFI. 3. Build prompts for Strategist (System: Static⊕Mandate; User: Task_Strat⊕Mem⊕Context). 4. Strategist outputs (g,m,d) → Executor prompt (Profile⊕Strategy⊕Task_Exec⊕Mem⊕Context). 5. Executor generates (c_t, u_t). 6. Environment executes c_t → raw o_t; Symbolic Control filters to ˜o_t. 7. Update buffers, proceed to next step.
- **Design tradeoffs:** Fidelity vs. diversity (strict constraints may reduce output variety); Behavioral accuracy vs. performance (intentional handicapping lowers solve rates); Modularity vs. coupling (decoupling prevents self-correction but risks misalignment).
- **Failure signatures:** D_KL > 0.5 indicates semi-Markov mis-specification; error recurrence near 0% indicates EFI failure; realism score < 1.5 suggests persona/rules missing; no interrupts or uncorrelated BKT mastery indicate control bugs.
- **First 3 experiments:** 1. Validate semi-Markov: run with fixed seed, compute D_KL vs. real; ablate Gamma durations, observe D_KL spike. 2. Test EFI: enable/disable block for a specific KC (e.g., math library); inspect code for correct vs. approximate usage. 3. Verify decoupling: compare monolithic (single LLM) vs. decoupled on error recurrence and realism; expect drop for monolithic.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
1. Does BEAGLE generalize beyond Python physics tasks to block-based programming, math, or language learning?
2. Can the architecture simulate collaborative multi-agent classroom dynamics?
3. How dependent are results on the choice of base LLM (Gemini Flash vs. GPT-4, Claude, open-weights)?
4. Do synthetic trajectories effectively train adaptive tutoring policies that transfer to real students?
5. How do the orthogonal controls for behavioral dynamics (ρ_behav) and linguistic persona (ρ_persona) hold up on more complex, multi-step problems?

## Limitations
- Missing critical artifacts: full knowledge component definitions, complete transition matrices, exact prompt templates for mandate/profile blocks, and tutor prompts.
- Domain specificity: evaluation only on Python particle-physics tasks; generalizability unknown.
- LLM dependence: all experiments used Gemini Flash; other models may not respect constraints equivalently.

## Confidence
- Overall realism & human indistinguishability: **High** (converging metrics: LLM-as-judge, human test, D_KL, error recurrence).
- Semi-Markov temporal model: **Medium** (ablation shows necessity, but parameters/transition matrix incomplete; independence assumption untested).
- Explicit flaw injection (EFI): **Medium** (high error recurrence suggests effectiveness, but direct constraint adherence not measured).
- Decoupled Strategist/Executor: **Medium** (ablation indicates benefit, but causal isolation from other design choices not fully shown).

## Next Checks
1. Test semi-Markov independence assumption on real student data: compute P(M_next | M_current, duration) vs. P(M_next | M_current) via chi-square.
2. Measure EFI constraint violations: programmatically scan generated code for usage of blocked concepts across 100 seeds; compute violation rate.
3. Isolate decoupling impact: implement monolithic agent (single LLM) and compare error recurrence, D_KL, and human realism scores against decoupled version with identical seeds.
