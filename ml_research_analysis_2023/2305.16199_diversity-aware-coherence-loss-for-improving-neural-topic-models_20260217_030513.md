# Diversity-Aware Coherence Loss for Improving Neural Topic Models

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.16199
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16199
- Reference count: 33
- Key outcome: Novel diversity-aware coherence loss significantly improves neural topic model coherence and diversity without pretraining or extra parameters

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a diversity-aware coherence loss function for neural topic models that explicitly incorporates corpus-level coherence scores during training while maintaining topic diversity. The method addresses a key limitation in existing neural topic models where improvements in coherence often come at the cost of reduced topic diversity. By integrating an auxiliary loss that balances both objectives, the proposed approach achieves superior performance on standard evaluation metrics including NPMI and topic uniqueness.

## Method Summary
The diversity-aware coherence loss integrates corpus-level coherence scores into the training process through an auxiliary loss function. This approach modifies the standard training objective of neural topic models (specifically VAEs) to explicitly account for both coherence and diversity. The loss function is designed to be parameter-free and does not require pretraining, making it straightforward to implement within existing neural topic modeling frameworks. The method maintains the core architecture while enhancing the training objective to optimize for both coherence and diversity simultaneously.

## Key Results
- The proposed method achieves significantly higher NPMI scores compared to baseline neural topic models
- Topic uniqueness scores are improved, indicating better topic diversity maintenance
- Outperforms basic coherence loss approaches that sacrifice diversity for coherence gains
- Demonstrates consistent improvements across three different datasets

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The diversity-aware coherence loss works by explicitly incorporating corpus-level coherence information into the training objective while simultaneously preserving topic diversity through a balanced loss formulation. By using an auxiliary loss that considers both coherence and diversity metrics during training, the model learns to generate topics that are both semantically coherent and topically distinct. This dual optimization prevents the model from optimizing solely for coherence at the expense of diversity, which is a common failure mode in neural topic models.
