# A Categorical Archive of ChatGPT Failures

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.03494
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03494

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a comprehensive analysis of ChatGPT failures across ten distinct categories: reasoning (spatial, physical, temporal, psychological), logic, math/arithmetic, factual errors, bias/discrimination, humor, coding, syntax/grammar, and self-awareness. The analysis draws examples from Twitter to illustrate the model's limitations in handling complex reasoning tasks, mathematical expressions, and factual knowledge. The paper highlights significant risks around transparency, security, privacy, and environmental impact associated with these failures. By cataloging these limitations, the work serves as a valuable benchmark for evaluating future iterations of large language models and emphasizes the critical need for continued research to address fundamental architectural and implementation challenges.

## Method Summary
The paper analyzes ChatGPT failures through examples collected from Twitter posts, providing a categorical archive of model limitations. However, the methodology lacks systematic sampling, controlled testing conditions, or quantitative measurement of failure rates. The analysis is primarily qualitative, drawing from publicly available examples that may represent memorable or dramatic failures rather than a representative distribution of model errors. The study does not specify ChatGPT versions, prompting strategies, or testing protocols, limiting the ability to assess whether failures are persistent architectural limitations or transient implementation issues.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT demonstrates significant limitations in spatial, physical, temporal, and psychological reasoning tasks
- The model is susceptible to factual errors and can produce biased outputs across multiple categories
- Failures span coding, syntax/grammar, mathematical expressions, and self-awareness capabilities
- The analysis reveals risks around transparency, security, privacy, and environmental impact

## Limitations
