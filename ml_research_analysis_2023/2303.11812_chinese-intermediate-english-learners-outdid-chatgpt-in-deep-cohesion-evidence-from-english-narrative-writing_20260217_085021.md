# Chinese Intermediate English Learners outdid ChatGPT in deep cohesion: Evidence from English narrative writing

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.11812
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11812
- Reference count: 0
- ChatGPT outperformed Chinese intermediate English learners in narrativity, word concreteness, and referential cohesion, but lagged in syntactic simplicity and deep cohesion.

## Executive Summary
This study compares the narrative writing quality of Chinese intermediate English (CIE) learners and ChatGPT using automated Coh-Metrix analysis. ChatGPT demonstrated superiority in narrativity, word concreteness, and referential cohesion, suggesting strong performance in surface-level cohesion and tangible language use. However, CIE learners outperformed ChatGPT in deep cohesion, indicating better use of causal and intentional connectors to create coherent narratives. These findings reveal complementary strengths: ChatGPT excels in generating concrete, story-like prose, while human writers maintain superior deep coherence through metalinguistic awareness and targeted instruction.

## Method Summary
The study collected 40 narrative essays from CIE learners (native Chinese speakers, intermediate English level) and 40 essays generated by ChatGPT on the topic "Write a personal experience." All essays were analyzed using Coh-Metrix 3.0 to extract scores for five discourse components: narrativity, syntactic simplicity, word concreteness, referential cohesion, and deep cohesion. Group differences were examined using MANOVA, followed by ANOVA for individual components, and correlations between components were calculated within each group.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT outperformed CIE learners in narrativity, word concreteness, and referential cohesion.
- CIE learners outperformed ChatGPT in deep cohesion and syntactic simplicity.
- After revision commands, ChatGPT improved syntactic simplicity but still underperformed in deep cohesion compared to CIE learners.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
ChatGPT's superiority in narrativity and word concreteness stems from its training data bias toward simpler, action-oriented language rather than abstract or personal expression. ChatGPT's pre-training corpus contains a higher proportion of narrative and expository texts that use concrete verbs and relatable events, enabling it to generate more "story-like" and tangible prose. This is supported by its tendency to use plain and straightforward words to describe personal experiences that occur frequently in daily life, such as public speaking and game playing. If the model is fine-tuned on abstract or academic texts, its output would shift toward lower narrativity and concreteness scores.

### Mechanism 2
CIE learners' stronger deep cohesion arises from human-developed metalinguistic awareness and explicit instruction in causal connectors. Human writers, especially English majors, receive targeted instruction on using causal and intentional connectors, resulting in higher deep cohesion scores than ChatGPT. This is evidenced by CIE learners' use of more casual connectives (e.g., as a result, therefore) compared to ChatGPT's overuse of "and." If ChatGPT receives targeted fine-tuning on causal connectors, its deep cohesion score would increase significantly.

### Mechanism 3
ChatGPT's referential cohesion is high due to its strong theory of mind capabilities, allowing it to track referents and maintain clear pronoun usage. The model's ability to simulate perspective-taking and track antecedents results in fewer ambiguous references and higher referential cohesion. This is supported by ChatGPT's consistent pronoun usage, never repeating pronouns like "we/they/I" more than twice, while CIE learners overused them. If the model is prompted with ambiguous referents or complex tracking tasks, its referential cohesion may drop.

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: Essay collection -> Coh-Metrix analysis -> MANOVA/Correlation -> Result interpretation. Key components: participant essays, ChatGPT-generated essays, Coh-Metrix 3.0, R statistical environment, bruceR and PerformanceAnalytics packages.
- Critical path: Essay collection -> automated linguistic feature extraction -> statistical comparison -> interpretation of group differences and correlations.
- Design tradeoffs: Using automated metrics ensures objectivity but may miss nuanced meaning; focusing on CIE learners limits generalizability; comparing only two writer types simplifies analysis but omits native speaker benchmarks.
- Failure signatures: Low inter-rater reliability on Coh-Metrix output; MANOVA assumptions violated (e.g., unequal covariance matrices); high correlation instability across groups.
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Run Coh-Metrix on a small sample of both CIE and ChatGPT essays to verify metric extraction.
  2. Conduct MANOVA on the full dataset and check assumptions (e.g., Box's M test).
  3. Perform post-hoc Tukey tests for significant group differences and document effect sizes.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
How can ChatGPT's deep cohesion performance be improved to match or exceed human writers in narrative writing? The paper notes that ChatGPT lagged behind CIE learners in deep cohesion even after revision commands. This remains unresolved as the study only attempted one round of revision commands focused on detailed discourse components, without exploring other potential improvement strategies. Testing various training approaches or fine-tuning methods specifically targeting causal and intentional connectors, and measuring the impact on deep cohesion scores, would help resolve this question.

### Open Question 2
What specific linguistic features distinguish ChatGPT's word concreteness advantage from human writers' abstract expression in narrative writing? The paper notes ChatGPT used more action words while CIE learners used more abstract concepts. This remains unresolved as the study did not conduct a detailed linguistic analysis comparing the specific types of concrete versus abstract vocabulary used by each group. A comprehensive lexical analysis categorizing the types of concrete and abstract words used, and their distribution across different narrative elements, would help resolve this question.

### Open Question 3
How do ChatGPT's narrative writing capabilities compare to human writers across different cultural contexts and writing topics? The study only examined narrative writing on one specific topic ("Write a personal experience") by Chinese intermediate English learners. This remains unresolved as the limited scope of topics and cultural background of participants may not represent ChatGPT's general narrative writing capabilities. Comparative studies using diverse cultural contexts, writing topics, and participant backgrounds to assess ChatGPT's adaptability and performance would help resolve this question.

## Limitations
- The study only compares CIE learners with ChatGPT, lacking native English speaker benchmarks for comprehensive assessment.
- No information is provided about the specific prompts or instructions given to ChatGPT, which could significantly influence the generated essays.
- The revision commands mentioned in the study are not detailed, making it difficult to reproduce the exact comparison conditions.

## Confidence
- High Confidence: ChatGPT's superiority in narrativity, word concreteness, and referential cohesion compared to CIE learners.
- Medium Confidence: CIE learners' advantage in deep cohesion due to educational instruction, as this relies on indirect evidence and assumptions about teaching methods.
- Low Confidence: Mechanism 2's claim about CIE learners' deep cohesion stemming from explicit instruction in causal connectors, as there is no direct corpus evidence supporting this mechanism.

## Next Checks
1. Replicate the study using a larger and more diverse sample of CIE learners and ChatGPT-generated essays to verify the consistency of the results.
2. Include native English speakers in the comparison to establish a more comprehensive benchmark for evaluating the writing quality of both CIE learners and ChatGPT.
3. Investigate the specific revision commands used in the study to ensure accurate reproduction of the comparison conditions and validate the findings.
