# Bayesian Optimization over High-Dimensional Combinatorial Spaces via Dictionary-based Embeddings

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2303.01774
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01774
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Dictionary-based embeddings enable Bayesian optimization over high-dimensional combinatorial spaces using standard Gaussian process models.

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of Bayesian optimization (BO) over high-dimensional combinatorial spaces, which traditional methods struggle with due to the discrete nature of the search space. The authors propose a novel dictionary-based embedding approach that maps discrete structures to an ordinal space via Hamming distance, allowing the use of continuous-space Gaussian process models. By constructing dictionaries using binary wavelets for binary spaces and randomized methods for categorical spaces, the approach theoretically reduces the cardinality of the input space and achieves improved regret bounds. Empirical results on diverse benchmarks demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods.

## Method Summary
The proposed method constructs a dictionary of discrete structures from the input space and uses Hamming distance to define an ordinal embedding. This embedding transforms the high-dimensional combinatorial space into a continuous ordinal space where standard Gaussian process models can be applied. For binary spaces, binary wavelets are used to construct the dictionary, while a randomized approach is employed for categorical spaces. The theoretical analysis shows that this embedding reduces the effective cardinality of the search space, leading to improved regret bounds. The approach is validated through experiments on various benchmarks, demonstrating its effectiveness across different combinatorial domains.

## Key Results
- Dictionary-based embeddings enable effective Bayesian optimization over high-dimensional combinatorial spaces
- The approach outperforms state-of-the-art methods on diverse benchmarks
- Theoretical analysis provides regret bounds that improve with dictionary quality
- Dictionary construction methods are developed for both binary and categorical input spaces

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by creating a bridge between discrete combinatorial spaces and continuous optimization frameworks. By selecting a dictionary of representative discrete structures and defining an ordinal embedding via Hamming distance, the approach transforms the discrete optimization problem into a continuous one that can leverage Gaussian process models. The dictionary construction methods ensure that the embedding captures the essential structure of the combinatorial space while reducing its effective dimensionality. This allows standard BO techniques to be applied while maintaining theoretical guarantees through improved regret bounds.

- Why needed: Provide uncertainty quantification and optimization capabilities in the embedded space
- Quick check: Ensure GP hyperparameters are appropriately tuned for the ordinal embedding
