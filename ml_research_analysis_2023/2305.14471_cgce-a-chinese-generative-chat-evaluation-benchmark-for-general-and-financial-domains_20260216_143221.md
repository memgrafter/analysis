# CGCE: A Chinese Generative Chat Evaluation Benchmark for General and Financial Domains

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.14471
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14471

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces the Chinese Generative Chat Evaluation (CGCE) benchmark to address the lack of standardized evaluation frameworks for Chinese generative chat models, particularly in general and financial domains. The benchmark includes 200 questions covering 13 dimensions in the general domain and 150 specialized questions across four areas in the financial domain. Manual scoring evaluates accuracy, coherence, expression clarity, and completeness to provide a comprehensive assessment framework for Chinese generative chat models.

## Method Summary
The CGCE benchmark consists of 350 questions total: 200 for general domain evaluation across 13 dimensions and 150 specialized questions for financial domain covering banking, investment, insurance, and securities. Manual scoring methodology evaluates responses across four key dimensions: accuracy, coherence, expression clarity, and completeness. The benchmark aims to provide standardized evaluation criteria for Chinese generative chat models, addressing the gap in Chinese-language assessment frameworks that exist for English-language models.

## Key Results
- Introduces CGCE benchmark with 200 general domain questions across 13 evaluation dimensions
- Provides 150 specialized financial domain questions across four key areas (banking, investment, insurance, securities)
- Establishes manual scoring framework evaluating accuracy, coherence, expression clarity, and completeness
- Addresses gap in standardized evaluation frameworks for Chinese generative chat models

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The benchmark provides standardized evaluation criteria specifically designed for Chinese generative chat models, addressing the lack of culturally and linguistically appropriate assessment frameworks that exist for English-language models. By covering both general and specialized financial domains with carefully curated questions and multi-dimensional scoring criteria, CGCE enables more comprehensive and nuanced evaluation of model performance across different conversational contexts and domain-specific knowledge requirements.

## Limitations
- Manual scoring methodology lacks transparency with no inter-rater reliability metrics or validation procedures
- Benchmark scope limited to 350 questions, potentially insufficient for comprehensive real-world conversational coverage
- Financial domain evaluation requires specialized expertise, potentially restricting evaluator pool and long-term maintenance

## Confidence

High confidence: Benchmark addresses clear gap in Chinese generative chat evaluation
Medium confidence: Manual scoring methodology effectiveness without reliability metrics
Low confidence: Long-term adoption and maintenance feasibility
