# Explanation Graph Generation via Generative Pre-training over Synthetic Graphs

## Quick Facts

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces EG3P (Explanation Graph Generation via Generative Pre-training over Synthetic Graphs), a novel framework designed to bridge the gap between unstructured natural language queries and structured explanation graphs. The core innovation lies in a text-to-graph pre-training task combined with an automatic corpus synthesis strategy that generates large-scale synthetic data from ConceptNet. The approach is evaluated on the ExplaGraphs benchmark and shows substantial improvements in both structural correctness accuracy (StCA) and semantic correctness accuracy (SeCA) compared to baseline models, demonstrating its effectiveness in generating high-quality explanation graphs for reasoning tasks.

## Method Summary
The EG3P framework consists of two main stages: synthetic corpus generation and model pre-training. First, an automatic corpus synthesis strategy is employed to construct large-scale synthetic data by generating explanation graphs and corresponding queries from ConceptNet. The generated corpus includes queries of varying difficulty (easy, normal, hard) to ensure diversity. Second, a BART-Large backbone is pre-trained on this synthetic corpus using a "text-to-graph" task formulation, where the model learns to generate explanation graphs from queries and simulated knowledge sources. Finally, the pre-trained model is fine-tuned on downstream datasets such as ExplaGraphs, CommonsenseQA, and OpenbookQA, demonstrating significant improvements in structural and semantic correctness accuracy.

## Key Results
- Improves Structural Correctness Accuracy (StCA) by 12.56% over BART-Large without pre-training.
- Improves Semantic Correctness Accuracy (SeCA) by 11.3% over BART-Large without pre-training.
- Achieves improvements in semantic accuracy by 10.5 and 12.0 points on CommonsenseQA and OpenbookQA, respectively.
