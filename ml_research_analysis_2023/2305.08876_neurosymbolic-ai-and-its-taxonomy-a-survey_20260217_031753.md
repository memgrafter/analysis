# Neurosymbolic AI and its Taxonomy: a survey

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2305.08876
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08876
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Key outcome:** This survey presents a taxonomy of Neurosymbolic AI systems, which combine symbolic reasoning with neural learning to achieve Artificial General Intelligence.

## Executive Summary
This survey presents a taxonomy of Neurosymbolic AI systems, which combine symbolic reasoning with neural learning to achieve Artificial General Intelligence. The paper analyzes recent research from 2019 onwards, covering six major approaches: Logic Tensor Networks (LTN), Neural Logic Machines (NLM), Logical Boltzmann Machines (LBM), Logic Neural Networks (LNN), Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner (NS-CL), and Generative Neurosymbolic Machines (GNM). Each system's knowledge representation, learning process, reasoning mechanisms, and explainability are examined. Applications include fairness in AI, semantic image interpretation, bearing fault diagnosis, and question answering.

## Method Summary
The survey systematically categorizes Neurosymbolic AI systems by examining their core components: knowledge representation, learning mechanisms, reasoning processes, and explainability features. Each of the six surveyed systems is analyzed through this framework to identify commonalities and differences. The taxonomy provides a structured approach to understanding how neural networks and symbolic reasoning are integrated across different architectures.

## Key Results
- Neurosymbolic systems may underperform pure deep learning in some metrics but offer advantages in data efficiency, explainability, and integration of prior knowledge
- Six major neurosymbolic approaches were identified and analyzed: LTN, NLM, LBM, LNN, NS-CL, and GNM
- Applications span multiple domains including fairness in AI, semantic image interpretation, bearing fault diagnosis, and question answering

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Neurosymbolic AI systems work by integrating the complementary strengths of neural networks and symbolic reasoning. Neural networks provide robust pattern recognition and learning from data, while symbolic reasoning offers interpretability, logical inference, and the ability to incorporate prior knowledge. This integration enables systems to learn from data while maintaining the ability to reason symbolically, leading to more interpretable and knowledge-efficient AI systems that can leverage structured domain knowledge.

## Foundational Learning
- **Symbolic Logic and Reasoning**: Understanding formal logic systems (propositional, first-order logic) is essential as neurosymbolic systems use these to represent knowledge and perform inference. Quick check: Can you trace a logical inference chain in a first-order logic system?
- **Neural Network Fundamentals**: Knowledge of deep learning architectures, backpropagation, and optimization is crucial since neurosymbolic systems embed neural components. Quick check: Can you explain how a neural network learns from labeled examples?
- **Knowledge Representation**: Understanding how knowledge is structured and represented (ontologies, knowledge graphs) is needed as neurosymbolic systems combine symbolic knowledge with learned representations. Quick check: Can you describe the difference between symbolic and sub-symbolic knowledge representations?
- **Multi-modal Learning**: Many neurosymbolic applications involve learning from different data types (text, images, structured data), requiring understanding of multi-modal integration. Quick check: Can you explain how vision and language can be jointly represented in a learning system?
- **Explainable AI**: Since explainability is a key advantage of neurosymbolic systems, understanding interpretability techniques is important. Quick check: Can you distinguish between local and global interpretability methods?

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map:** Input Data → Neural Learning Module → Symbolic Reasoning Engine → Output Reasoning/Prediction

**Critical Path:** Data preprocessing → Neural feature extraction → Symbolic knowledge integration → Logical inference → Final output generation

**Design Tradeoffs:** The primary tradeoff is between the expressive power of symbolic reasoning and the learning efficiency of neural networks. Systems must balance computational complexity, explainability, and performance.

**Failure Signatures:** Common failures include inability to scale symbolic reasoning with large knowledge bases, neural components learning spurious correlations, and difficulties in learning rules that require long chains of symbolic reasoning.

**First 3 Experiments:** 1) Implement a simple Logic Tensor Network to classify objects using both visual features and logical rules, 2) Build a Neural Logic Machine to perform simple arithmetic reasoning, 3) Create a Neuro-Symbolic Concept Learner that answers questions about simple scenes using both perception and reasoning.

## Limitations
- The review covers research from 2019 onwards, potentially missing earlier foundational work that shaped current approaches
- The selection of six major systems, while representative, may not capture the full diversity of neurosymbolic approaches in the field
- The relative performance comparisons between neurosymbolic and pure deep learning approaches lack quantitative benchmarks

## Confidence
- Survey coverage of neurosymbolic approaches: **Medium** - Comprehensive but may miss some systems
- Analysis of system components: **High** - Systematic examination of knowledge representation, learning, reasoning, and explainability
- Performance comparison claims: **Low** - Limited empirical evidence for claimed trade-offs with pure deep learning
