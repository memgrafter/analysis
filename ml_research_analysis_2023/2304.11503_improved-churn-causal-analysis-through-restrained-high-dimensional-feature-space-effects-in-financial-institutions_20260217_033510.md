# Improved Churn Causal Analysis Through Restrained High-Dimensional Feature Space Effects in Financial Institutions

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.11503
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11503
- Reference count: 35
- Key result: Ensemble ANN model achieved 86% accuracy on customer churn prediction

## Executive Summary
This study addresses customer churn prediction in financial institutions using high-dimensional data. The research proposes a novel framework combining SMOTE sampling, ensemble artificial neural networks (ANNs), and Bayesian networks to overcome challenges of data imbalance and high dimensionality. The methodology employs Recursive Feature Elimination (RFE) to select the most influential features. Experiments on real-world superannuation fund data demonstrate that the ensemble ANN model outperforms other classifiers including Random Forest, achieving 86% accuracy.

## Method Summary
The methodology integrates multiple advanced techniques for churn prediction. SMOTE sampling addresses class imbalance by generating synthetic samples. Ensemble ANNs are employed for prediction, while Bayesian networks facilitate causal analysis. The Recursive Feature Elimination method is used to reduce dimensionality by selecting the most influential features. The framework processes high-dimensional data from financial institutions to identify churn patterns and causal relationships.

## Key Results
- Ensemble ANN model achieved 86% accuracy on churn prediction
- Model outperformed Random Forest and other classifiers
- Identified key causal factors: account balance changes, contribution levels, and account growth

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The framework's effectiveness stems from addressing multiple challenges simultaneously. SMOTE sampling balances the dataset, enabling better model training on minority churn cases. Ensemble ANNs capture complex nonlinear relationships in high-dimensional financial data. Bayesian networks provide interpretable causal insights beyond simple correlation. RFE ensures only the most relevant features are used, reducing noise and improving model performance.
