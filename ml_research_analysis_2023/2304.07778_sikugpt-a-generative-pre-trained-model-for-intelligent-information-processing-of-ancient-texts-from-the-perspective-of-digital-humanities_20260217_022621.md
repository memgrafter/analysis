# SikuGPT: A Generative Pre-trained Model for Intelligent Information Processing of Ancient Texts from the Perspective of Digital Humanities

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.07778
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07778
- Reference count: 5
- SikuGPT achieved BLEU-1 of 0.765 and BLEU-2 of 0.593 in text translation tasks for ancient Chinese texts

## Executive Summary
SikuGPT is a generative pre-trained model based on GPT2, specifically designed for processing ancient Chinese texts from the Siku Quanshu corpus. The model was enhanced with over 5,000 traditional Chinese characters and evaluated on two core tasks: text translation and text classification. Results demonstrated superior performance compared to baseline models GPT2-base-Chinese and GPT2-chinese-ancient, with BLEU scores reaching 0.765 (BLEU-1) and 0.593 (BLEU-2) in translation tasks, and an F1-score of 90.30% across 14 text categories in classification tasks. The study highlights the effectiveness of domain-specific pre-training for specialized digital humanities applications.

## Method Summary
The SikuGPT model was developed by pre-training a GPT2 architecture on the Siku Quanshu corpus, a comprehensive collection of ancient Chinese texts. The model was enhanced with traditional Chinese character embeddings to improve handling of archaic vocabulary and syntax. Two primary tasks were evaluated: text translation, measuring BLEU scores across multiple n-gram levels, and text classification, assessing performance across 14 ancient text categories using F1-score. The approach leverages domain-specific fine-tuning to address the unique linguistic challenges of ancient Chinese, including classical grammar structures and historical terminology.

## Key Results
- Text translation BLEU-1: 0.765, BLEU-2: 0.593, BLEU-3: 0.488, BLEU-4: 0.413
- Text classification F1-score: 90.30% across 14 ancient text categories
- Outperformed baseline models GPT2-base-Chinese and GPT2-chinese-ancient on both tasks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The effectiveness of SikuGPT stems from domain-specific pre-training on the Siku Quanshu corpus, which contains rich linguistic patterns unique to ancient Chinese texts. By incorporating over 5,000 traditional Chinese characters and fine-tuning on classical literature, the model develops specialized representations for archaic vocabulary and syntactic structures that general Chinese language models struggle with. The transformer-based architecture enables effective capture of long-range dependencies in classical Chinese sentence structures, while the pre-training process allows the model to learn contextual embeddings specific to historical document genres and writing styles.
