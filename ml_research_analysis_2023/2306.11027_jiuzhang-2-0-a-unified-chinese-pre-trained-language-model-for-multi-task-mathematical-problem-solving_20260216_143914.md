# JiuZhang 2.0: A Unified Chinese Pre-trained Language Model for Multi-task Mathematical Problem Solving

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.11027
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.11027
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: JiuZhang 2.0 achieves 53.5% win rate over previous version in online A/B testing

## Executive Summary
JiuZhang 2.0 is a unified Chinese pre-trained language model designed for multi-task mathematical problem solving. It addresses the limitations of existing PLMs in handling complex mathematical problems and reduces maintenance costs for multi-task deployment. The model combines Mixture-of-Experts architecture with multi-task continual pre-training and fine-tuning strategies, enhanced by iterative refinement using large language models. Experimental results show consistent performance improvements across eight tasks, demonstrating effective knowledge sharing and practical utility in real-world applications.

## Method Summary
JiuZhang 2.0 employs a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with multi-task continual pre-training and fine-tuning strategies to capture and share mathematical knowledge across tasks. The model leverages large language models (LLMs) through iterative refinement to improve solution quality. This unified approach addresses the limitations of existing PLMs in handling complex mathematical problems while maintaining computational efficiency for multi-task deployment.

## Key Results
- Achieves 53.5% win rate over previous version in online A/B testing
- Consistently outperforms competitive baselines including LLM-based methods across eight tasks
- Effectively balances performance and efficiency for real-world mathematical problem-solving applications

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The model's effectiveness stems from its ability to capture mathematical knowledge through multi-task continual pre-training while maintaining task-specific capabilities through fine-tuning. The MoE architecture allows efficient scaling by activating only relevant expert parameters for each task. Iterative refinement using LLMs further enhances solution quality by providing additional reasoning capabilities without requiring full model retraining.
