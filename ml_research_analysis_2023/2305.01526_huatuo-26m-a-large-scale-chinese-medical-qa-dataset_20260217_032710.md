# Huatuo-26M, a Large-scale Chinese Medical QA Dataset

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.01526
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.01526
- Reference count: 12
- Key outcome: Huatuo-26M is the largest Chinese medical QA dataset with 26 million QA pairs, improving transfer learning and model performance on medical tasks.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces Huatuo-26M, a large-scale Chinese medical question-answering dataset containing 26 million QA pairs constructed from online medical consultation records, medical encyclopedias, and medical knowledge bases. The dataset is the largest Chinese medical QA dataset to date, expanding existing datasets by more than two orders of magnitude. The paper benchmarks several retrieval and generation models on the dataset, finding that existing models still perform poorly, indicating the challenging nature of the task. Additionally, the paper demonstrates three applications of the dataset: transferring to other QA datasets, serving as external knowledge for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and improving pre-trained language models.

## Method Summary
The dataset is collected from three sources: online medical consultation records (31.6M pairs before cleaning), medical encyclopedias (automatically extracted from Chinese Wikipedia and other sources), and medical knowledge bases (from CPubMed-KG, 39Health-KG, and Xywy-KG). After text cleaning and deduplication, the dataset contains 26,504,088 QA pairs. Benchmark models include sparse retrieval (BM25, DeepCT) and dense retrieval (DPR) methods, as well as generation models (T5, GPT2). The dataset is also used for transfer learning, RAG, and as a pre-training corpus for BERT and RoBERTa models.

## Key Results
- Huatuo-26M is the largest Chinese medical QA dataset, containing 26 million QA pairs
- Existing models perform poorly on this challenging dataset, indicating room for improvement
- The dataset improves transfer learning to other medical QA datasets and enhances pre-trained language models

## Limitations

- Data Quality and Coverage Gaps: The dataset may contain noise from colloquial language and incomplete information, and its focus on Chinese medical data may limit generalizability.
- Evaluation Framework Constraints: The benchmark focuses on retrieval and generation metrics, lacking clinical validation of model outputs and comparison with global standards.
- Model Performance and Scalability: The paper does not provide comprehensive analysis of computational requirements or scalability limitations, and the performance improvements are demonstrated but not extensively validated.

## Confidence

**High Confidence Claims**
- The dataset contains 26 million QA pairs and is the largest Chinese medical QA dataset available (supported by clear documentation of the collection process and deduplication steps)
- Models trained on this dataset show improved performance on medical QA tasks compared to smaller datasets (demonstrated through controlled experiments)

**Medium Confidence Claims**
- The dataset's effectiveness for transfer learning to other medical QA datasets (supported by experiments but limited to specific target datasets)
- The benefits of using Huatuo-26M for retrieval-augmented generation (demonstrated but with room for more extensive validation)

**Low Confidence Claims**
- Generalizability of results to other languages and medical systems (not tested)
- Long-term stability and effectiveness of models trained on this dataset (no longitudinal studies presented)
