# Cluster Flow: how a hierarchical clustering layer make allows deep-NNs more resilient to hacking, more human-like and easily implements relational reasoning

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.14081
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14081
- Reference count: 17
- Key outcome: Hierarchical clustering framework adds relational reasoning to pre-trained CNNs, improving adversarial resilience, confidence calibration, and analogical reasoning capabilities

## Executive Summary
ClusterFlow introduces a hierarchical clustering framework that enhances pre-trained convolutional neural networks with relational reasoning capabilities by clustering high-dimensional activation vectors from the pre-SoftMax layer. This approach creates hyperspatial maps of classes and features, enabling CNNs to become more resilient to adversarial attacks, provide more accurate confidence measures, and perform relational reasoning tasks. The method demonstrates that modern deep CNNs can be made more human-like in their reasoning without retraining, suggesting ClusterFlow as a valuable tool for enhancing neural network performance across various domains including image classification and chemistry datasets with missing values.

## Method Summary
ClusterFlow operates by extracting high-dimensional activation vectors from the pre-SoftMax layer of pre-trained CNNs and applying hierarchical clustering to build hyperspatial maps of classes and features. The method creates a cluster flow that represents the relationships between different feature activations and class boundaries in the activation space. This clustering layer is added to existing CNN architectures without requiring retraining of the original model. The hierarchical structure allows for multi-level reasoning, where clusters at different depths capture varying levels of abstraction and feature relationships. The framework processes these clustered representations to enhance the network's decision-making capabilities, enabling it to perform relational reasoning tasks and improve resilience to adversarial examples while maintaining the original CNN's classification performance.

## Key Results
- Demonstrated improved resilience to adversarial "fooling images" through enhanced activation space clustering
- Successfully replicated infant-like asymmetry in cat/dog categorization, showing more human-like reasoning patterns
- Performed relational reasoning on image sets including novel objects through hierarchical cluster analysis
- Extended capability to non-NN data with missing values, validated on chemistry datasets

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The hierarchical clustering approach works by capturing the intrinsic structure of the activation space in pre-trained CNNs. By clustering high-dimensional activation vectors from the pre-SoftMax layer, ClusterFlow identifies natural groupings and relationships between different features and classes that exist in the learned representation space. This clustering creates a hyperspatial map that preserves the semantic relationships encoded in the CNN's weights while adding a layer of relational understanding. The hierarchical nature allows the system to reason at multiple levels of abstraction, from fine-grained feature relationships to broader class-level patterns. This structural understanding enables the network to better distinguish between genuine patterns and adversarial noise, as well as to perform analogical reasoning by recognizing relationships between different clusters across the hierarchy.

## Foundational Learning
- **Hierarchical Clustering**: A method of cluster analysis that builds a hierarchy of clusters, either agglomerative (bottom-up) or divisive (top-down). Why needed: Enables multi-level abstraction and relational reasoning across different feature scales. Quick check: Can the clustering algorithm handle varying cluster densities and shapes in high-dimensional spaces?

- **Activation Space Analysis**: Examining the high-dimensional feature representations learned by neural networks. Why needed: Provides insight into how CNNs encode semantic relationships and class boundaries. Quick check: Do the activation vectors maintain semantic coherence within clusters?

- **Relational Reasoning**: The ability to infer relationships between entities and apply them to new situations. Why needed: Enables transfer of learned patterns to novel contexts and analogical thinking. Quick check: Can the system correctly identify and apply relationships in unseen analogy problems?

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map**: Pre-trained CNN -> Pre-SoftMax Layer -> Activation Vector Extraction -> Hierarchical Clustering -> Cluster Flow Processing -> Enhanced Decision Layer

**Critical Path**: The most computationally intensive step is the hierarchical clustering of activation vectors, which scales with both the number of samples and the dimensionality of the activation space. The design tradeoff involves balancing clustering granularity with computational efficiency - finer clusters provide more detailed relational reasoning but increase computational overhead.

**Design Tradeoffs**: The primary tradeoff is between the depth of the hierarchical clustering and computational requirements. Deeper hierarchies capture more complex relationships but require more processing power and may overfit to specific patterns. The method also trades off between preserving the original CNN's decision boundaries and introducing new relational reasoning capabilities.

**Failure Signatures**: The system may fail when clusters become too diffuse in high-dimensional spaces, leading to ambiguous relational mappings. Over-clustering can cause the system to focus on spurious patterns rather than meaningful relationships. Under-clustering may miss important feature relationships needed for accurate reasoning.
