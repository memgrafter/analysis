# Croatian Film Review Dataset (Cro-FiReDa): A Sentiment Annotated Dataset of Film Reviews

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2305.08173
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.08173
- **Reference count:** 10
- **Primary result:** Sentiment-annotated dataset of over 10,000 Croatian film review sentences with benchmark F1 score of 79.78% and accuracy of 84.71%

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces Cro-FiReDa, a sentiment-annotated dataset for Croatian focused on movie reviews. The dataset contains over 10,000 sentences annotated at the sentence level, addressing the scarcity of sentiment analysis resources for the Croatian language. The authors describe their annotation process and present benchmark results using a transformer-based fine-tuning approach with the CroSloEngual BERT model. The dataset will be made publicly available to support further research in Croatian sentiment analysis.

## Method Summary
The authors constructed the Cro-FiReDa dataset by collecting Croatian film reviews and annotating them at the sentence level for sentiment polarity. The annotation process involved multiple annotators to ensure quality, though specific inter-annotator agreement metrics are not reported. For benchmarking, the authors employed a fine-tuning approach using the CroSloEngual BERT model, a transformer-based architecture specifically designed for Croatian and Slovenian languages. The fine-tuning process involved standard hyperparameter tuning and evaluation on a held-out test set.

## Key Results
- Dataset contains over 10,000 sentiment-annotated sentences from Croatian film reviews
- CroSloEngual BERT achieved F1 score of 79.78% and accuracy of 84.71% on the sentiment classification task
- The dataset will be publicly released to support Croatian natural language processing research

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The transformer-based approach leverages pre-trained language models that capture contextual representations of Croatian text, enabling effective sentiment classification through fine-tuning on the domain-specific movie review data. The sentence-level annotation granularity allows for precise sentiment labeling while maintaining manageable annotation costs.
