# MALM: Mask Augmentation based Local Matching for Food-Recipe Retrieval

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.11327
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11327
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Achieves new state-of-the-art performance on Recipe1M for both image-to-recipe and recipe-to-image retrieval tasks

## Executive Summary
MALM addresses supervision collapse in image-to-recipe retrieval by proposing a mask-augmentation-based local matching network. The approach combines an image-text matching module that aligns paired images and recipes at local and global levels with a masked self-distillation module for learning general-purpose image representations. Experimental results demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art methods on the Recipe1M dataset, achieving new benchmarks in cross-modal food recipe retrieval.

## Method Summary
MALM introduces a novel architecture that leverages mask augmentation to mitigate supervision collapse in paired data learning. The method consists of two main components: an image-text matching module that performs cross-modal alignment at both local and global levels, and a masked self-distillation module that learns robust image representations through self-supervised learning. The approach processes food images and their corresponding recipes by first extracting local features, then applying strategic mask augmentation to prevent overfitting to specific visual patterns. This allows the model to learn more generalizable representations that capture the semantic relationships between food images and recipes.

## Key Results
- Outperforms state-of-the-art methods on Recipe1M dataset
- Achieves new state-of-the-art performance on both image-to-recipe and recipe-to-image retrieval tasks
- Demonstrates effectiveness in addressing supervision collapse through mask augmentation

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The paper doesn't explicitly detail the mechanism, but the approach appears to work by preventing the model from overfitting to specific visual patterns through strategic mask augmentation. By forcing the model to learn from partial information and self-distill masked representations, MALM encourages more robust and generalizable feature learning. The local matching component ensures fine-grained alignment between image regions and recipe components, while the global matching captures broader semantic relationships.
