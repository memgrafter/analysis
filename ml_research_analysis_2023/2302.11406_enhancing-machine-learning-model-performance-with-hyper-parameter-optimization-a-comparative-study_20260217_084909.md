# Enhancing Machine Learning Model Performance with Hyper Parameter Optimization: A Comparative Study

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.11406
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.11406
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: PSO achieved 92.6% test accuracy, outperforming grid search, random search, Bayesian optimization, and genetic algorithms for Random Forest HPO

## Executive Summary
This study evaluates classical and meta-heuristic hyper parameter optimization methods for Random Forest classifiers on a binary classification task from the Australian electricity market. The researchers compared grid search, random search, Bayesian optimization, genetic algorithms, and particle swarm optimization (PSO) across four key hyper parameters. PSO demonstrated superior performance with 92.6% test accuracy and 99.3% training accuracy while maintaining reasonable computational efficiency. The study provides empirical evidence that meta-heuristic approaches, particularly PSO, can significantly enhance model performance compared to traditional optimization methods.

## Method Summary
The study employs the ELEC2 dataset from the Australian electricity market (45,312 samples, 30-minute intervals, 1996-1998) with 80% training and 20% testing split. Five hyper parameter optimization methods are compared: grid search, random search, Bayesian optimization, genetic algorithms, and particle swarm optimization. All methods optimize four Random Forest hyper parameters: n_estimators (50-400), max_features (4-10), max_depth (4-10), and criterion (entropy/gini). Performance is evaluated using accuracy, precision, recall, and F1-score with 5-fold cross-validation. Each method is run 15 times to ensure statistical reliability, and CPU times are recorded for computational efficiency analysis.

## Key Results
- PSO achieved highest test accuracy at 92.6% and training accuracy at 99.3%
- PSO delivered superior precision and recall for both UP and DOWN classes
- PSO maintained competitive computational time compared to other meta-heuristic methods
- Traditional methods (grid search, random search) showed significantly lower performance metrics

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Assumption: PSO's superior performance likely stems from its population-based search strategy that effectively balances exploration and exploitation across the hyper parameter space. The algorithm's velocity and position updates enable it to escape local optima more efficiently than gradient-based Bayesian optimization while requiring fewer function evaluations than exhaustive grid search. PSO's social learning component, where particles share information about promising regions, may help identify optimal parameter combinations that single-point search methods miss.

## Foundational Learning
- Hyper Parameter Optimization (HPO): Systematic search for optimal model parameters that are not learned during training; needed to maximize model performance beyond default settings
- Meta-heuristic Algorithms: High-level problem-independent search procedures that guide other heuristics to find sufficiently good solutions; required for exploring complex, non-convex search spaces efficiently
- Particle Swarm Optimization: Population-based stochastic optimization technique inspired by social behavior of bird flocking; effective for continuous parameter spaces with multiple local optima
- Random Forest Hyper Parameters: n_estimators (number of trees), max_features (features per split), max_depth (tree depth), criterion (split quality); critical for controlling model complexity and generalization
