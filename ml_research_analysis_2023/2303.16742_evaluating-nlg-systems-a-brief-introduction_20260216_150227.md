# Evaluating NLG systems: A brief introduction

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.16742
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16742
- Reference count: 6
- This paper provides a practical guide for evaluating Natural Language Generation (NLG) systems, addressing the need for standardized and rigorous evaluation practices in the field.

## Executive Summary
This paper serves as a comprehensive introduction to evaluating Natural Language Generation systems, offering a practical framework for researchers and practitioners. The guide emphasizes the importance of standardized evaluation practices in NLG, which has historically lacked rigorous assessment methods. The paper introduces fundamental concepts such as intrinsic vs. extrinsic evaluation and human vs. automatic evaluation, providing readers with a solid foundation for understanding NLG evaluation approaches.

The paper presents a structured 10-step framework for planning NLG evaluations, focusing on understanding target audiences, identifying relevant variables, ensuring metric validity, and maintaining transparency in methods and results. Rather than introducing new metrics or presenting empirical data, the paper synthesizes established evaluation practices and directs readers to additional resources for more detailed guidance in specific areas.

## Method Summary
This paper does not present experimental results but rather synthesizes existing evaluation practices in the NLG field. It provides a methodological framework based on established evaluation concepts and practices, drawing from the broader NLG research community's collective experience. The approach is primarily theoretical and prescriptive, offering guidance on how to plan and conduct NLG evaluations rather than presenting new data or metrics.

## Key Results
- Introduces key evaluation concepts including intrinsic vs. extrinsic evaluation and human vs. automatic evaluation
- Presents a 10-step framework for planning NLG evaluations
- Emphasizes importance of understanding target audiences and ensuring metric validity
- Provides guidance on transparency in evaluation methods and results
- Directs readers to additional resources for more detailed evaluation guidance

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
This guide works by establishing a systematic approach to NLG evaluation that bridges theoretical understanding with practical implementation. By distinguishing between intrinsic and extrinsic evaluation methods, and human versus automatic evaluation, it provides a comprehensive framework that can be adapted to various NLG contexts. The mechanism relies on structured planning and clear definition of evaluation goals, which helps ensure that the assessment methods align with the intended use cases and user needs.

## Foundational Learning

**Intrinsic vs. Extrinsic Evaluation**
- Why needed: To understand whether to evaluate the generated text itself or its impact on downstream tasks
- Quick check: Ask "Are we measuring the output quality or the system's effect on task completion?"

**Human vs. Automatic Evaluation**
- Why needed: To balance practical constraints with the need for reliable assessment
- Quick check: Consider available resources and required evaluation depth

**Evaluation Metrics Validity**
- Why needed: To ensure assessment measures what it claims to measure
- Quick check: Verify metrics align with intended evaluation goals

**Target Audience Understanding**
- Why needed: To ensure evaluation methods match user needs and expectations
- Quick check: Document who will use the system and their requirements

**Transparency in Methods**
- Why needed: To enable reproducibility and comparison across studies
- Quick check: Review if all evaluation steps are clearly documented
