# Provable Data Subset Selection For Efficient Neural Network Training

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.05151
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05151
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Introduces first algorithm to construct coresets for radial basis function neural networks (RBFNNs)

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a novel approach to data subset selection for efficient neural network training through coreset construction for radial basis function neural networks. The method leverages sensitivity sampling to create provably representative subsets of data, potentially reducing training time by up to 90% while maintaining or improving model performance. The approach is theoretically grounded and demonstrates empirical success across multiple standard benchmark datasets.

## Method Summary
The paper introduces an algorithm for constructing coresets specifically designed for radial basis function neural networks. The method utilizes sensitivity sampling to bound the importance of individual data points and efficiently sample representative subsets. By approximating the loss of input data on any RBFNN through these coresets, the approach enables more efficient training of deep neural networks while maintaining performance guarantees.

## Key Results
- Training times reduced by up to 90% while maintaining or improving model performance
- Significant improvements in accuracy and efficiency compared to state-of-the-art methods
- Successful evaluation on CIFAR10, CIFAR100, MNIST, and ImageNet datasets
- Model-independent approach works across multiple neural network architectures without repeated computations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The approach works by constructing coresets that approximate the loss function of radial basis function neural networks. Sensitivity sampling is used to identify and bound the importance of individual data points, allowing for efficient selection of representative subsets. This mathematical approximation enables training on smaller, more informative datasets while preserving the performance characteristics of models trained on full datasets.

## Foundational Learning
- Sensitivity Sampling: A technique for identifying important data points based on their contribution to the overall model; needed to efficiently select representative subsets; quick check: verify the sensitivity bounds hold for different data distributions
- Coreset Construction: The process of building a weighted subset that approximates the full dataset's properties; needed to enable efficient training on smaller datasets; quick check: confirm the coreset maintains statistical properties of original data
- Radial Basis Function Networks: Neural networks that use radial basis functions as activation functions; needed as the theoretical foundation for the approximation method; quick check: validate RBFNN properties match theoretical assumptions

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map:**
Data -> Sensitivity Analysis -> Coreset Construction -> Training Optimization

**Critical Path:**
The critical path involves sensitivity analysis to identify important data points, followed by coreset construction to create the representative subset, which is then used for efficient model training.

**Design Tradeoffs:**
- Accuracy vs. computational efficiency in coreset size selection
- Theoretical guarantees vs. practical implementation complexity
- Generalization across architectures vs. specialization for specific models

**Failure Signatures:**
- Performance degradation when dataset has high variance in data point importance
- Increased training instability when coreset approximation bounds are violated
- Reduced effectiveness on non-standard data distributions

**First Experiments:**
