# Transfer Learning for Underrepresented Music Generation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.00281
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00281
- Reference count: 6
- Primary result: CE-MCTS achieved 83.97% reconstruction accuracy on Iranian folk music test data, outperforming standard finetuning

## Executive Summary
This work addresses the challenge of adapting large music generation models to underrepresented genres through transfer learning. The authors identified Iranian folk music as an out-of-distribution genre for Google Magenta's MusicVAE model and collected a dataset of 100 Iranian folk MIDI files. They evaluated multiple transfer learning approaches, including standard finetuning and a combinational creativity-based method called CE-MCTS. The study demonstrates that CE-MCTS achieved the highest reconstruction accuracy (83.97%) on test data, showing that combinational creativity-based transfer learning can effectively adapt large music generation models to underrepresented genres by combining source domain features to approximate target domain patterns.

## Method Summary
The authors collected 100 Iranian folk MIDI files to create a dataset for an underrepresented musical genre. They then adapted Google Magenta's MusicVAE model using several transfer learning approaches: standard finetuning, and a combinational creativity-based method called CE-MCTS. The CE-MCTS approach combines features from the source domain (Western music) to approximate patterns in the target domain (Iranian folk music). Performance was evaluated using reconstruction accuracy on held-out test data, comparing the adapted models against the pre-trained MusicVAE model.

## Key Results
- CE-MCTS achieved 83.97% reconstruction accuracy on test data, outperforming standard finetuning approaches
- The pre-trained MusicVAE model showed lower performance on Iranian folk music compared to adapted models
- Combinational creativity-based transfer learning successfully captured patterns in underrepresented musical genres using limited data

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The CE-MCTS approach works by combining features from the source domain (Western music patterns in the pre-trained model) to approximate the target domain (Iranian folk music). This combinational creativity mechanism allows the model to leverage learned musical structures while adapting to new rhythmic, melodic, and harmonic patterns characteristic of underrepresented genres. By systematically combining and recombining source features, the approach can generate representations that approximate the statistical properties of the target genre without requiring extensive training data.

## Limitations
- Limited to one specific underrepresented genre (Iranian folk music), raising generalizability concerns
- Small dataset size (100 MIDI files) may not represent scalability challenges
- Reconstruction accuracy metric lacks benchmarking against human performance
- Combinational creativity approach may not translate to genres with fundamentally different musical structures

## Confidence

**CE-MCTS outperforms standard finetuning**: Medium confidence - demonstrated on specific dataset but limited to one case study

**Combinational creativity-based transfer learning effectiveness**: Medium confidence - promising results but requires validation across diverse genres

**Addressing representational bias in music generation**: Low confidence - insufficient evidence across diverse musical traditions
