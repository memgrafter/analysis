# Are Neural Architecture Search Benchmarks Well Designed? A Deeper Look Into Operation Importance

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.16938
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16938
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Convolutional layers and skip connections are the most impactful operations in NAS benchmarks

## Executive Summary
This paper critically evaluates three popular NAS benchmarks (NAS-Bench-101, NAS-Bench-201, and TransNAS-Bench-101) by analyzing the importance of different operations in neural architecture search. The authors systematically examine operation presence, occurrence, positioning, and combinations to understand what drives high performance. Their analysis reveals that convolutional layers and skip connections consistently dominate the performance landscape, while a small subset of operations is sufficient to generate top-performing architectures. The study highlights that both the cell structure and operation positioning significantly influence final performance, suggesting current benchmarks may be biased toward specific operation types.

## Method Summary
The authors conduct an empirical analysis of NAS benchmarks by examining precomputed tabular information on thousands of trained neural networks. They analyze performance distributions, operation presence across architectures, and the positioning of operations within cell structures. The study evaluates generability and transferability by computing inter-data set/task correlations using Kendall's tau correlation. Through statistical analysis and visualization techniques, they identify patterns in operation importance and how these relate to architecture performance across different benchmarks.

## Key Results
- Convolutional layers (C3×3) and skip connections (SC) are consistently the most impactful operations for achieving high performance
- Only a small subset of operations is required to generate architectures close to the upper-bound of performance ranges
- Cell structure and operation positioning significantly influence final performance, with certain positions benefiting more from specific operations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Convolutional layers and skip connections are consistently most impactful
- Mechanism: These operations directly shape feature extraction depth and gradient flow, with C3×3 providing rich spatial features and SC maintaining gradient flow
- Core assumption: Operation choice determines performance differences in fixed cell structures
- Evidence anchors: Abstract finding on convolution layers having highest impact; section analysis confirming this pattern
- Break condition: If benchmarks allow more diverse cell structures or fundamentally different operations

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Small subset of operations sufficient for top performance
- Mechanism: Benchmarks are biased toward convolutions and SC, so focusing on these achieves top results without full exploration
- Core assumption: Performance distribution is negatively skewed with high density near upper bound
- Evidence anchors: Abstract claim about subset sufficiency; analysis showing NAS-Bench-101 has negatively skewed distribution
- Break condition: If operation pool expands to include equally or more important diverse operations

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Cell structure and operation positioning significantly influence performance
- Mechanism: Specific edge positions benefit more from certain operations, with C3×3 performing best across all positions
- Core assumption: Cell structure is not agnostic to operation choice; some positions are more sensitive
- Evidence anchors: Abstract statement on structure and positioning influence; section analysis of edge position performance
- Break condition: If cell structure becomes more flexible or operation positioning importance reduces

## Limitations

- Analysis limited to three specific NAS benchmarks, which may not capture full diversity of NAS search spaces
- Study focuses on convolutional neural networks and may not apply to other architecture types like transformers
- Does not consider impact of hyperparameter optimization or other factors influencing architecture performance

## Confidence

- High: Consistent impact of convolutional layers and skip connections across all three benchmarks
- Medium: Finding that small operation subset sufficient for top performance, limited by specific benchmarks studied
- Medium: Influence of cell structure and operation positioning on performance, limited by fixed cell structure of benchmarks
