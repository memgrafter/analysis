# IDOL: Indicator-oriented Logic Pre-training for Logical Reasoning

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2306.15273
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.15273
- **Reference count**: 10
- **Primary result**: IDOL improves logical reasoning in language models through six types of logical indicators and custom dataset LGP

## Executive Summary
IDOL is a further pre-training method designed to enhance logical reasoning capabilities in language models. It leverages six types of logical indicators and a custom dataset (LGP) to improve performance on logical reasoning benchmarks. The method demonstrates effectiveness across multiple pre-trained model architectures while maintaining strong performance on general NLP tasks.

## Method Summary
IDOL employs a further pre-training approach that incorporates six types of logical indicators to improve logical reasoning in language models. The method uses a custom dataset called LGP for this additional training phase. By focusing on logical indicators during pre-training, IDOL aims to develop stronger reasoning capabilities while preserving the model's general language understanding.

## Key Results
- IDOL outperforms existing methods on logical reasoning benchmarks ReClor and LogiQA
- Maintains strong performance on general NLP tasks like RACE and SQuAD 2.0
- Achieves higher accuracy in logical reasoning tasks compared to ChatGPT
- Effective across multiple pre-trained models including BERT, RoBERTa, ALBERT, and DeBERTa

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Assumption: IDOL works by incorporating logical indicators during the pre-training phase, which helps the model develop stronger logical reasoning patterns. The six types of logical indicators likely include various logical operators, conditional statements, and reasoning patterns that are explicitly represented in the training data. This focused exposure to logical structures during pre-training enables the model to better recognize and process logical relationships when performing reasoning tasks.
