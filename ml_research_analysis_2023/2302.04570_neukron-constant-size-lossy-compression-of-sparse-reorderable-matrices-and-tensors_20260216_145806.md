# NeuKron: Constant-Size Lossy Compression of Sparse Reorderable Matrices and Tensors
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.04570
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04570
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: NeuKron achieves up to 5 orders of magnitude more compact compression than competitors with similar accuracy on sparse matrices

## Executive Summary
NeuKron introduces a novel approach for compressing sparse matrices and tensors using a constant-size lossy representation based on recurrent neural networks and Kronecker product generalizations. The method updates parameters to approximate a given matrix through product operations while reordering rows and columns to facilitate the approximation. With linear-time updates proportional to non-zero entries and logarithmic retrieval time for approximations, NeuKron demonstrates exceptional compression ratios and accuracy across ten real-world datasets.

## Method Summary
NeuKron employs a recurrent neural network with a constant number of parameters to generalize Kronecker products for compressing sparse reorderable matrices and tensors. The method works by iteratively updating network parameters to approximate the input matrix through product operations, while simultaneously reordering rows and columns to optimize the approximation quality. The compression process operates in linear time relative to the number of non-zero entries, and individual entry approximations can be retrieved in logarithmic time. This approach enables the creation of highly compact representations while maintaining controllable approximation error levels.

## Key Results
- Achieves up to five orders of magnitude more compact compression than best competitors with similar approximation errors
- Demonstrates up to 10x higher accuracy compared to existing methods
- Scales effectively to matrices with over 230 million non-zero entries
- Maintains linear update time relative to non-zero entries and logarithmic retrieval time

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
NeuKron leverages the structural properties of Kronecker products to create compact representations of sparse matrices. By using a recurrent neural network with constant parameters, the method generalizes the Kronecker product framework to handle arbitrary sparse matrices through iterative approximation. The key insight is that the network can learn to approximate complex matrix structures through a series of simple product operations, while the reordering optimization ensures that the approximation process is facilitated by optimal row and column arrangements. This combination of neural network generalization and structural optimization enables the creation of constant-size representations that capture essential matrix information.
