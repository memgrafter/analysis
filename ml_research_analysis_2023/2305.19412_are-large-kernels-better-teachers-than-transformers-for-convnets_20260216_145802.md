# Are Large Kernels Better Teachers than Transformers for ConvNets?

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2305.19412
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19412
- **Reference count:** 22
- **Primary result:** Large-kernel ConvNets (e.g., SLaK, ConvNeXt) outperform Vision Transformers as distillation teachers for small-kernel ConvNets, achieving state-of-the-art ConvNet accuracy under 30M parameters (83.1% top-1 on ImageNet).

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates whether large-kernel ConvNets serve as more effective knowledge distillation teachers compared to Vision Transformers for small-kernel ConvNets. Through extensive experiments, the authors demonstrate that large-kernel teachers (such as ConvNeXt and SLaK) consistently outperform transformer-based teachers (ViT, Swin, CSWin) when distilling knowledge to ResNet-50 and ConvNeXt-T students. The study shows that the benefits of large kernels—such as improved effective receptive fields—can be successfully transferred to small-kernel architectures via distillation, setting a new state-of-the-art for pure ConvNets under 30M parameters.

## Method Summary
The authors compare the effectiveness of large-kernel ConvNets and Vision Transformers as teachers in knowledge distillation settings. Experiments involve distilling knowledge from large-kernel teachers (ConvNeXt and SLaK) and transformer teachers (ViT, Swin, CSWin) to small-kernel students (ResNet-50 and ConvNeXt-T) on ImageNet-1K. Distillation performance is evaluated using standard metrics such as top-1 accuracy, and the effective receptive fields of the resulting models are analyzed. The authors also report the best-ever pure ConvNet under 30M parameters (83.1% top-1 accuracy) achieved by distilling SLaK-T (51x51 kernels) into ConvNeXt-T (7x7 kernels).

## Key Results
- Students distilled from large-kernel teachers (ConvNeXt, SLaK) outperform those from transformer-based teachers (ViT, Swin, CSWin) in accuracy and effective receptive fields.
- SLaK-T (51x51 kernels) distilled into ConvNeXt-T (7x7 kernels) achieves 83.1% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet, the best-ever pure ConvNet under 30M parameters.
- Large-kernel ConvNets as teachers provide more effective knowledge transfer to small-kernel ConvNets compared to Vision Transformers.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Large-kernel ConvNets capture broader spatial context and have larger effective receptive fields, which facilitates better feature representation and knowledge transfer during distillation. The distillation process allows small-kernel ConvNets to inherit these benefits, resulting in improved performance.

## Foundational Learning
- **Knowledge Distillation:** Transfer of knowledge from a large (teacher) model to a smaller (student) model; needed to train efficient models; check: student performance improves when trained with teacher outputs.
- **Effective Receptive Field (ERF):** The region of the input that significantly influences a neuron's output; larger ERF helps capture more context; check: visualization of ERF shows broader coverage for large kernels.
- **ConvNet Architecture:** Convolutional neural networks; fundamental for image processing; check: basic understanding of convolution, pooling, and feature extraction.
- **Transformer Architecture:** Attention-based models that have become standard for vision; check: familiarity with self-attention and multi-head mechanisms.
- **ImageNet Dataset:** Standard benchmark for image classification; check: understanding of dataset size, classes, and typical evaluation metrics.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map:** Large-kernel ConvNet Teacher (ConvNeXt/SLaK) -> Knowledge Distillation Loss -> Small-kernel ConvNet Student (ResNet-50/ConvNeXt-T) -> ImageNet Evaluation

**Critical Path:** Teacher forward pass -> soft label generation -> student forward pass -> distillation loss computation (e.g., KL divergence) -> student backpropagation

**Design Tradeoffs:** Large kernels improve representational power and ERF but increase computational cost; distillation mitigates this by transferring benefits to smaller kernels.

**Failure Signatures:** If teacher and student architectures are too dissimilar, distillation may not be effective; transformer teachers may not align well with ConvNet inductive biases.

## Limitations
- Results are only evaluated on ImageNet-1K, limiting generalization to other datasets or downstream tasks.
- The comparison is constrained to fixed student architectures, so the advantage of large-kernel teachers may be specific to these models.
- No ablation studies on distillation hyper-parameters or student model scales to isolate the source of performance gains.

## Confidence
- Effectiveness of large-kernel teachers on ImageNet-1K: **High**
- Claims about effective receptive field improvements: **Medium**
- Generalization of findings to other datasets, student architectures, or distillation settings: **Low**

## Next Checks

3. Compare large-kernel teacher performance when distilled to both ConvNet and transformer-based student architectures to assess the generality of the teacher advantage.
