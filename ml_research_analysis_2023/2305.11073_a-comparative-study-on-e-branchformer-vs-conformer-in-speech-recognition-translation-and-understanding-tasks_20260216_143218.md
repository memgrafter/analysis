# A Comparative Study on E-Branchformer vs Conformer in Speech Recognition, Translation, and Understanding Tasks

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.11073
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11073
- Reference count: 0
- This work investigates the effectiveness of E-Branchformer in various speech processing tasks, including ASR, ST, and SLU, and compares it to Conformer using different E2E frameworks. Our extensive experiments in publicly available benchmarks have shown that E-Branchformer outperforms Conformer in a wide variety of tasks, and can be more stable when training with large model size or on small datasets. In addition, we share various training tips and we will release our training configurations and pre-trained models to ensure full reproducibility, which can greatly benefit the speech community.

## Executive Summary
This study presents a comprehensive comparison between E-Branchformer and Conformer architectures across multiple speech processing tasks including Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Speech Translation (ST), and Spoken Language Understanding (SLU). The authors conduct extensive experiments using publicly available benchmarks to evaluate performance differences between these two architectures. E-Branchformer demonstrates superior performance across a wide variety of tasks while showing improved stability when training with large model sizes or on small datasets.

The research contributes valuable insights to the speech processing community by sharing various training tips and committing to release training configurations and pre-trained models to ensure full reproducibility. This approach aims to facilitate further research and development in the field by providing accessible resources for the community.
