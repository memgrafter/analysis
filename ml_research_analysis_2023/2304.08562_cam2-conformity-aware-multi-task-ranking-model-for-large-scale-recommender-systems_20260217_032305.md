# CAM2: Conformity-Aware Multi-Task Ranking Model for Large-Scale Recommender Systems

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.08562
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.08562
- Reference count: 21
- Primary result: CAM2 improves aggregated user engagement by 0.50% and daily active users by 0.21% on Facebook Watch through disentangling conformity and relevance in large-scale recommender systems.

## Executive Summary
The paper addresses conformity bias in large-scale recommender systems, where user interactions are influenced by popularity rather than true interests. CAM2 is proposed as a conformity-aware multi-task ranking model that disentangles conformity and relevance by learning separate causal embeddings. The model is trained on the entire dataset without data partitioning, leveraging auxiliary tasks for conformity and relevance. Offline experiments show improved prediction performance across tasks, while online A/B tests demonstrate significant improvements in user engagement and long-tail content exposure.

## Method Summary
CAM2 employs a multi-task learning approach with causal embeddings to disentangle conformity and relevance signals in user-item interactions. The model learns separate embeddings for conformity and relevance through a novel loss formulation and input features partitioning. By training on the entire dataset without partitioning, CAM2 leverages auxiliary tasks to jointly optimize for both conformity and relevance objectives. The causal embeddings are learned through a combination of supervised and self-supervised learning, allowing the model to capture the underlying causal relationships between user preferences and conformity bias.

## Key Results
- CAM2 achieves a 0.50% increase in aggregated user engagement and a 0.21% increase in daily active users on Facebook Watch through online A/B testing.
- Offline experiments show improved prediction performance across multiple tasks, including conformity and relevance prediction.
- The model increases exposure to long-tail content and improves performance for casual users.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
CAM2 addresses conformity bias by learning separate causal embeddings for conformity and relevance signals. The multi-task learning approach allows the model to jointly optimize for both objectives, while the input features partitioning ensures that conformity and relevance signals are learned from distinct sets of features. This disentanglement enables the model to better capture user preferences that are not influenced by conformity bias, leading to improved recommendations and increased engagement.

## Limitations
- The paper lacks detailed ablation studies demonstrating the individual contributions of the key components to the overall performance improvement.
- The reported online A/B test results, while statistically significant, represent relatively modest gains that may not fully justify the added model complexity.
- The computational overhead introduced by CAM2 and its scalability with extremely large item catalogs are not thoroughly discussed.

## Confidence
- High confidence in the offline experimental results and methodology, as the paper provides clear metrics and comparative analysis against baselines.
- Medium confidence in the online A/B test results due to the lack of statistical significance testing details and potential confounding factors in the real-world deployment.
- Medium confidence in the claims about increased long-tail content exposure, as the paper does not provide specific metrics or examples of how the model achieves this.
