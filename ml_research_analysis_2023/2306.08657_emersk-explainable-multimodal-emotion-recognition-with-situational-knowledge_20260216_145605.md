# EMERSK -- Explainable Multimodal Emotion Recognition with Situational Knowledge

## Executive Summary
This paper presents EMERSK, a generalized and modular system for human emotion recognition and explanation using visual information. The system handles multiple modalities, including facial expressions, posture, and gait, in a flexible and modular manner. EMERSK leverages situational knowledge derived from the location type and adjective-noun pair (ANP) extracted from the scene, as well as the spatio-temporal average distribution of emotions, to generate explanations. The multimodal approach significantly improves overall recognition performance, and the system provides human-interpretable explanations for the classification results.

## Method Summary
EMERSK utilizes a two-stream network architecture with convolutional neural networks (CNNs) and encoder-decoder style attention mechanisms to extract deep features from face images. Similarly, CNNs and recurrent neural networks (RNNs) with Long Short-term Memory (LSTM) are employed to extract features from posture and gait data. The system also incorporates deep features from the background as contextual information for the learning process. The deep features from each module are fused using an early fusion network. Furthermore, EMERSK leverages situational knowledge derived from the location type and adjective-noun pair (ANP) extracted from the scene, as well as the spatio-temporal average distribution of emotions, to generate explanations.

## Key Results
- The proposed EMERSK system achieves superior performance compared to other state-of-the-art methods on various benchmark datasets, including GroupWalk.
- The multimodal approach significantly improves overall recognition performance.
- The system provides human-interpretable explanations for the classification results.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The effectiveness of EMERSK stems from its ability to capture rich, complementary information from multiple modalities (face, posture, gait, and background) and integrate situational knowledge for more accurate and explainable emotion recognition. The attention mechanisms help focus on relevant features within each modality, while the early fusion of deep features enables the system to leverage the combined strengths of the different information sources.

## Limitations
- The reliance on external scene parsing and syntactic parsing tools for situational knowledge extraction introduces potential points of failure and performance variability.
- The early fusion approach assumes all modalities are always available, which may not reflect practical deployment conditions where sensor failures or occlusion could occur.
- The paper does not thoroughly explore the impact of varying the number of modalities on overall system performance.
