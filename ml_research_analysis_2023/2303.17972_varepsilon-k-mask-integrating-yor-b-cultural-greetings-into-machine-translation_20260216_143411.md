# $\varepsilon$ KÚ <MASK>: Integrating Yorùbá cultural greetings into machine translation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.17972
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17972

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of accurately translating Yorùbá cultural greetings using multilingual neural machine translation (NMT) systems. The authors introduce IkiniYorùbá, a novel dataset containing 960 parallel sentences of Yorùbá greetings and their use cases, to evaluate the performance of existing NMT models like Google Translate, NLLB, and M2M-100. Their results show that these models struggle with translating Yorùbá greetings, with BLEU scores as low as 4.3, despite high performance on conversational data. The authors then fine-tune M2M-100 on IkiniYorùbá, achieving a BLEU score of 29.67, demonstrating the effectiveness of domain-specific fine-tuning. Human evaluation further confirms the model's improved ability to preserve cultural content and semantic meaning.

## Method Summary
The authors collected 960 parallel sentences of Yorùbá greetings and their use cases to create the IkiniYorùbá dataset. They evaluated the performance of existing NMT models (Google Translate, NLLB, M2M-100) on this dataset, finding poor results with BLEU scores as low as 4.3. To improve performance, they fine-tuned the M2M-100 model on IkiniYorùbá, achieving a BLEU score of 29.67. Human evaluation was conducted to assess the model's ability to preserve cultural content and semantic meaning in translations.

## Key Results
- Existing NMT models (Google Translate, NLLB, M2M-100) achieve BLEU scores as low as 4.3 on Yorùbá cultural greetings
- Fine-tuning M2M-100 on IkiniYorùbá dataset improves BLEU score to 29.67
- Human evaluation confirms improved cultural content preservation and semantic meaning in fine-tuned model translations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Fine-tuning domain-specific models on culturally relevant data allows the model to learn and adapt to the unique linguistic patterns and cultural nuances present in Yorùbá greetings, which are not well-captured by general multilingual NMT models trained on broader conversational data.

- Reliance on BLEU scores may not fully capture semantic and cultural nuances in translation
- Human evaluation process lacks detailed description of rater expertise and agreement metrics
