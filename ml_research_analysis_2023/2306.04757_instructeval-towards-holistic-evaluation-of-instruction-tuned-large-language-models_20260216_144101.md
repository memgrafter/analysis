# INSTRUCTEVAL: Towards Holistic Evaluation of Instruction-Tuned Large Language Models

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2306.04757
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04757
- **Reference count**: 15
- **Primary result**: INSTRUCTEVAL provides a comprehensive evaluation suite for instruction-tuned large language models, highlighting that instruction data quality is the most crucial factor in scaling model performance.

## Executive Summary
INSTRUCTEVAL introduces a holistic evaluation framework for instruction-tuned large language models, addressing the gap in comprehensive assessment by focusing on problem-solving, writing ability, and alignment to human values. The study systematically examines the impact of pretraining foundations, instruction-tuning data, and training methods on model performance. Results indicate that while open-source models demonstrate impressive writing capabilities, they still have substantial room for improvement in problem-solving and alignment tasks. The evaluation underscores the critical importance of high-quality instruction data in scaling model performance.

## Method Summary
The authors developed INSTRUCTEVAL as a comprehensive evaluation suite that assesses instruction-tuned large language models across three key dimensions: problem-solving, writing ability, and alignment to human values. The framework systematically examines how different factors—including pretraining foundations, instruction-tuning data quality, and training methodologies—influence overall model performance. The evaluation employs a diverse set of benchmark tasks and metrics to provide a holistic assessment of model capabilities.

## Key Results
- Instruction data quality emerges as the most crucial factor in scaling model performance
- Open-source models show impressive writing abilities across multiple tasks
- Substantial room for improvement remains in problem-solving and alignment capabilities

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The evaluation framework works by systematically isolating and measuring the impact of three key factors on model performance: pretraining foundations, instruction-tuning data, and training methods. By using standardized benchmark tasks across problem-solving, writing, and alignment dimensions, the framework can identify which factors most strongly influence performance outcomes. The emphasis on instruction data quality as a critical scaling factor suggests that models' ability to follow and respond to instructions is fundamentally tied to the quality and diversity of the data used during instruction-tuning.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map**: Pretraining Data -> Instruction-Tuning Data -> Training Method -> Model Performance (Problem-solving, Writing, Alignment)

**Critical Path**: Instruction-Tuning Data -> Model Performance (most direct impact)

**Design Tradeoffs**: The framework prioritizes comprehensive assessment over computational efficiency, using multiple benchmarks rather than single-task evaluations. This provides richer insights but requires more resources to implement.

**Failure Signatures**: Models may show strong writing but weak problem-solving, indicating imbalanced instruction-tuning data. Poor alignment despite good technical performance suggests misalignment between training objectives and human values.

**First Experiments**: 1) Evaluate a model's performance across all three dimensions to establish baseline capabilities; 2) Conduct ablation study by varying instruction-tuning data quality while holding other factors constant; 3) Compare performance across different pretraining foundations with identical instruction-tuning approaches.

## Limitations
- The evaluation focuses primarily on English-language tasks and may not fully capture performance across diverse linguistic contexts
- Problem-solving and alignment assessments rely on specific benchmark datasets that may not represent real-world challenges
- Findings about instruction data quality being most crucial are based on correlational analysis without full control for confounding variables

## Confidence

- Problem-solving and alignment have substantial room for improvement: **Medium**
- Instruction data quality is the most crucial scaling factor: **Medium**
