# AIwriting: Relations Between Image Generation and Digital Writing

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.10834
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10834
- Reference count: 0
- The paper presents five artists' explorations of AI text-to-image systems as literary writing environments at ISEA 2023

## Executive Summary
AIwriting was a panel at ISEA 2023 presenting five artists' and theorists' explorations of AI text-to-image systems as literary writing environments. The works included abstract explorations of latent space aesthetics, AI-assisted digital poetry and interactive narratives, a visual memoir of cancer treatment using AI imagery, political satire using text-to-image generation, and critical analysis of training dataset biases. The primary outcome was establishing that AI image generation should be understood as a form of literary writing rather than merely a visual tool, with these systems functioning as environments for hybrid human-machine literary expression.

## Method Summary
The paper describes five different AI text-to-image generation experiments as literary writing environments, exploring how human-machine interactions produce visual narratives and new literary genres through iterative interlocution processes. Various AI systems (GPT-3, DALL-E 2, Stable Diffusion) were used as writing environments with text prompts created by artists. The methodology involved iterative prompt refinement between human creators and AI systems, examining resulting visual outputs as literary artifacts, and critical analysis of training dataset biases and their effects on generated content.

## Key Results
- AI text-to-image systems function as literary writing environments rather than mere visual tools
- New literary genres emerge from iterative human-AI collaboration and interlocution
- Training dataset biases significantly influence the literary qualities of generated visual narratives

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism relies on treating AI text-to-image systems as collaborative writing environments where human authors engage in iterative dialogue with machine learning models. Through prompt refinement and analysis of generated outputs, creators can explore latent space aesthetics and develop new forms of literary expression that blend human intent with machine interpretation. The systems function as environments that respond to linguistic input with visual output, creating a hybrid creative space.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component map**: Human author -> Text prompt creation -> AI text-to-image model -> Generated imagery -> Literary analysis -> Iterative refinement

**Critical path**: Prompt creation → AI generation → Output analysis → Refinement iteration → Final literary interpretation

**Design tradeoffs**: The systems prioritize visual coherence over semantic accuracy, requiring extensive prompt refinement to achieve literary goals. The lack of transparency in training data composition creates unpredictable biases that must be navigated through trial and error rather than systematic control.

**Failure signatures**: 
- Outputs fail to capture intended literary themes despite semantically clear prompts
- Generated imagery exhibits unexpected cultural or stylistic biases from training data
- Iterative refinement cycles produce diminishing returns in achieving desired literary qualities
- Technical limitations of specific AI systems constrain creative expression in genre-specific ways
