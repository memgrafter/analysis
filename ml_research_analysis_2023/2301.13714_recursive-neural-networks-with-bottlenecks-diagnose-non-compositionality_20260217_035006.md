# Recursive Neural Networks with Bottlenecks Diagnose (Non-)Compositionality

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.13714
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.13714
- Authors: 
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: BCM metric ranks examples by compositionality using bottlenecked recursive neural networks, validated on arithmetic and sentiment analysis tasks

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces the Bottleneck Compositionality Metric (BCM), a novel approach to quantify compositionality in data representations by leveraging recursive neural networks with information bottlenecks. The core insight is that bottlenecks force models to process inputs more locally, disproportionately affecting non-compositional examples. BCM compares representations from bottlenecked and non-bottlenecked models to rank examples by compositionality. The method is validated on synthetic arithmetic expressions and natural language sentiment analysis, demonstrating its ability to distinguish compositional from non-compositional patterns and correlate with memorization-based metrics.

## Method Summary
BCM operates by training recursive neural networks with and without bottlenecks on the target task, then measuring the performance degradation when bottlenecks are applied. The key hypothesis is that bottlenecks, which constrain information flow through hidden states, will more severely impact non-compositional examples that rely on global patterns rather than local feature composition. The method computes a compositionality score for each example based on the difference in task performance (e.g., MSE for regression, accuracy for classification) between bottlenecked and non-bottlenecked models. Examples with larger performance drops are ranked as more non-compositional. The approach is demonstrated on arithmetic expression evaluation and sentiment analysis, with rankings validated against memorization-based baselines and known non-compositional patterns.

## Key Results
- Arithmetic: Bottlenecks reduce performance on non-compositional exceptions by up to 60% MSE
- Sentiment: BCM rankings show strong correlations (ρ up to 0.46) with memorization-based metrics
- Training on more compositional examples improves model performance when data is limited

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Bottlenecks constrain the information capacity of hidden states, forcing the model to rely more heavily on local feature composition rather than global patterns. Non-compositional examples depend on memorizing specific input-output mappings or leveraging global context, so they suffer more when information flow is restricted. Compositional examples, which can be solved by combining local features according to syntactic or semantic rules, are less affected by bottlenecks. By comparing model performance with and without bottlenecks, BCM quantifies the degree to which each example relies on compositional versus non-compositional processing.

## Foundational Learning
- **Compositionality in neural networks**: The ability to systematically combine learned features to generalize to novel inputs
  - Why needed: Core concept being measured by BCM
  - Quick check: Can the model generalize to unseen combinations of known components?
- **Information bottlenecks**: Constraints on hidden state dimensionality or connectivity that force compression of representations
  - Why needed: Key mechanism for differentially affecting compositional vs. non-compositional examples
  - Quick check: Does bottleneck size/placement significantly impact performance on compositional vs. non-compositional examples?
- **Recursive neural networks**: Models that apply the same transformation recursively over input structure (e.g., expression trees)
  - Why needed: Natural fit for tasks with inherent hierarchical structure (arithmetic, syntax)
  - Quick check: Does the model correctly evaluate or classify simple compositional examples?

## Architecture Onboarding

Component map:
Input -> Recursive Neural Network -> Task Output (with and without bottleneck)

Critical path: Input expression/sentence → Recursive encoding → Bottleneck (optional) → Output prediction

Design tradeoffs:
- Bottleneck size: Smaller bottlenecks increase sensitivity to non-compositionality but may harm overall performance
- Recursive depth: Deeper recursion may better capture hierarchical structure but increases computational cost
- Task type: Regression (MSE) vs. classification (accuracy) metrics for performance comparison

Failure signatures:
- BCM rankings not correlated with known compositionality patterns
- Similar performance degradation for compositional and non-compositional examples
- Bottlenecked model performs worse than non-bottlenecked model on all examples

First experiments:
1. Evaluate BCM on synthetic arithmetic expressions with known compositional structure
2. Apply BCM to a sentiment analysis dataset with labeled idioms and non-compositional phrases
3. Train a model on ranked data (most compositional first) and measure generalization performance

## Limitations
- Evaluation primarily on synthetic arithmetic and single sentiment analysis task
- No exploration of how different bottleneck architectures affect results
- Limited investigation of BCM's relationship to downstream task performance across domains

## Confidence

High:
- BCM successfully distinguishes compositional from non-compositional examples in arithmetic dataset with ground truth labels
- Strong correlation (ρ up to 0.46) between BCM rankings and memorization-based metrics in sentiment analysis

Medium:
- Validation of BCM rankings in natural language tasks relies on indirect correlations rather than ground truth compositionality labels
- Relationship between BCM rankings and downstream task performance demonstrated but not thoroughly explored

## Next Checks
1. Test BCM across diverse natural language understanding tasks beyond sentiment analysis (e.g., NLI, semantic parsing) to establish generalizability
2. Conduct ablation studies varying bottleneck architecture (position, size, type) to determine sensitivity and optimal configurations
3. Validate BCM rankings against human judgments of compositionality in natural language examples, particularly for ambiguous cases where automatic metrics may disagree
