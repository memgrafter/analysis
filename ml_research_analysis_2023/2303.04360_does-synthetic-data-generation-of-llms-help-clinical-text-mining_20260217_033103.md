# Does Synthetic Data Generation of LLMs Help Clinical Text Mining?
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.04360
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04360
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: Synthetic data generation improves NER F1 from 23.37% to 63.99% and RE F1 from 75.86% to 83.59%

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of clinical text mining by proposing a novel approach that uses ChatGPT to generate synthetic labeled data for training local models. The authors demonstrate that directly applying ChatGPT to clinical named entity recognition (NER) and relation extraction (RE) tasks yields poor performance and raises privacy concerns when processing patient data. Their proposed solution involves generating high-quality synthetic data through carefully engineered prompts, then fine-tuning local models on this synthetic data. This approach not only significantly improves performance metrics but also reduces the need for extensive data collection and labeling while maintaining patient privacy.

## Method Summary
The authors propose a synthetic data generation framework for clinical text mining that leverages ChatGPT to create labeled training data. The method involves two main phases: first, using ChatGPT with carefully engineered prompts to generate synthetic clinical text samples with annotations for NER and RE tasks; second, fine-tuning local transformer-based models on this synthetic data for downstream applications. The approach addresses both performance limitations of direct ChatGPT application (23.37% NER F1) and privacy concerns associated with processing sensitive patient data. The synthetic data generation process includes human-in-the-loop validation to ensure quality, creating a scalable pipeline for producing large volumes of labeled clinical data without requiring manual annotation of real patient records.

## Key Results
- NER performance improved from 23.37% to 63.99% F1-score using synthetic data approach
- RE performance improved from 75.86% to 83.59% F1-score with fine-tuned local models
- The method significantly reduces data collection and labeling efforts while maintaining patient privacy

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The approach works by leveraging ChatGPT's ability to generate high-quality, contextually appropriate synthetic clinical text that captures the linguistic patterns and domain-specific terminology of real clinical data. By generating labeled examples rather than processing real patient data, the method avoids privacy risks while still providing rich training material. The synthetic data contains realistic entity annotations and relationship structures that mirror actual clinical documentation, allowing fine-tuned local models to learn effective representations for downstream tasks. The human-in-the-loop validation ensures the synthetic data maintains high quality and relevance to the target clinical domain.
