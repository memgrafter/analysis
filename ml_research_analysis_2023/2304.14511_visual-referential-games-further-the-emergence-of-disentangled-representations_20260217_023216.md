# Visual Referential Games Further the Emergence of Disentangled Representations

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.14511
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.14511
- Authors: 
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: Obverter-based visual referential games outperform FactorVAE on disentanglement metrics while producing more compositional languages

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates how visual referential games influence the emergence of disentangled and compositional representations. The authors compare Obverter and STGS-based agent architectures in visual referential games, finding that Obverter approaches produce significantly better disentanglement scores than FactorVAE while generating more compositional languages. The work introduces a speaker-centered version of the Positional Disentanglement metric and examines the complex relationship between systematicity and both disentanglement and compositionality metrics, revealing that correlation directions depend on measurement timing.

## Method Summary
The paper employs visual referential games where agents communicate about 3DShapesPyBullet images containing five shapes, five colors, and ten viewpoints. Two agent architectures are compared: STGS (using softmax over stimuli) and Obverter (using a mental simulation approach with confidence thresholding). Both use shared visual modules with convolutional layers and batch normalization, followed by language modules (LSTM for STGS, GRU for Obverter). Training runs for 4,000 epochs with Adam optimizer, measuring disentanglement metrics (FactorVAE Score, MIG, Modularity), compositionality metrics (PosDis, BosDis), and systematicity through zero-shot compositional learning tests.

## Key Results
- Obverter-based visual referential games outperform FactorVAE on major disentanglement metrics
- Speaker-centered positional disentanglement better captures informativeness and completeness than listener-centered variants
- Dropout layers before decision modules improve systematicity and FactorVAE-style disentanglement
- Correlation between systematicity and disentanglement/compositionality metrics shows both positive and negative coefficients depending on timing

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Visual referential games with Obverter architecture produce more disentangled representations than FactorVAE
- Mechanism: The Obverter architecture forces agents to reason about meaning from their own perspective, creating internal representations that naturally separate underlying factors (shape, color, viewpoint) during communication
- Core assumption: The Obverter's "mental simulation" process creates stronger regularization on learned representations than standard VAE reconstruction losses
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "we find that visual referential games that are based on the Obverter architecture outperforms state-of-the-art unsupervised learning approach in terms of many major disentanglement metrics"
- Break condition: If the mental simulation assumption fails (agents don't actually reason from their own perspective) or if the regularization effect is not stronger than reconstruction-based approaches

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Speaker-centered positional disentanglement better captures informativeness and completeness than listener-centered posdis
- Mechanism: By measuring mutual information from the speaker's perspective, the metric penalizes languages that don't capture all ground truth factors or use redundant symbol positions
- Core assumption: Mutual information is symmetric, but compositionality metrics should be asymmetric to capture different aspects of language quality
- Evidence anchors: [section] "the speaker-centred formulation cares about the informativeness... of the language with respect to all the ground truth factors"
- Break condition: If the symmetry assumption about mutual information breaks down in practice, or if completeness/informativeness aren't actually important for the downstream tasks being considered

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Dropout layers before decision modules improve systematicity and FactorVAE-style disentanglement
- Mechanism: Dropout acts as a regularizer that forces the network to learn more robust, factorized representations that generalize better to unseen combinations
- Core assumption: Dropout's noise injection specifically promotes FactorVAE-style disentanglement rather than just general regularization
- Evidence anchors: [section] "the presence of the Dropout layer... was highlighted earlier as a mechanism furthering high disentanglement of the learned representations in the sense of the FactorV AE score"
- Break condition: If dropout's effect is primarily through general regularization rather than specifically promoting FactorVAE-style disentanglement, or if the systematicity improvements are coincidental

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: Visual Module -> Language Module -> Decision Module -> Game Outcome
- Critical path: Visual module → Language module → Decision module → Game outcome → Representation learning signal

  - STGS vs Obverter: Richer gradient signal vs stronger compositional bias

  - Shared visual module: Improves sample efficiency but may limit specialization
- Failure signatures:
  - Training doesn't converge: Check batch norm layers and learning rate

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
- Question: Does the Obverter architecture consistently outperform the STGS approach in visual referential games across different datasets and task complexities?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] The paper finds that Obverter-based visual referential games outperform the state-of-the-art FactorV AE approach in terms of many major disentanglement metrics
- Why unresolved: The experiments were conducted on a specific dataset (3DShapesPyBullet) with a limited set of attributes and task complexity. It is unclear if these results generalize to other datasets or more complex tasks
- What evidence would resolve it: Experiments comparing Obverter and STGS approaches on a diverse set of datasets with varying task complexities, including those with more attributes and higher-dimensional latent spaces

### Open Question 2
- Question: What is the specific mechanism by which the Obverter architecture promotes disentanglement in learned representations?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] The paper observes that Obverter-based approaches outperform FactorV AE in terms of disentanglement metrics, but the specific mechanism is not fully explained
- Why unresolved: The paper does not provide a detailed analysis of the architectural differences between Obverter and other approaches that might contribute to its superior disentanglement performance
- What evidence would resolve it: A detailed ablation study comparing different components of the Obverter architecture (e.g., batch normalization, confidence threshold) and their impact on disentanglement metrics

### Open Question 3
- Question: How do the different compositional language metrics (topsim, posdis, bosdis) correlate with each other and with systematicity in visual referential games?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] The paper uses multiple compositional language metrics and investigates their correlation with systematicity, finding statistically significant but inconsistent correlations
- Why unresolved: The paper does not provide a comprehensive analysis of the relationships between all compositional language metrics and their predictive power for systematicity
- What evidence would resolve it: A systematic study examining the correlations between all compositional language metrics and their ability to predict systematicity across a range of visual referential game settings and datasets

## Limitations

- Evaluation confined to a single synthetic dataset (3DShapesPyBullet), limiting generalizability
- The mechanism by which Obverter architecture produces better disentanglement remains largely theoretical
- Expanded PosDis metric lacks comprehensive evaluation across diverse compositional tasks

## Confidence

**High Confidence**: The correlation findings between systematicity and disentanglement/compositionality metrics. The statistical analysis is straightforward and the observed patterns (both positive and negative correlations depending on timing) are clearly documented.

**Medium Confidence**: The superiority of Obverter-based visual referential games for disentanglement. While the results show better scores on established metrics, the underlying mechanism is not fully explained, and the comparison is limited to one unsupervised baseline (FactorVAE).

**Low Confidence**: The effectiveness of speaker-centered positional disentanglement as a compositionality metric. The theoretical motivation is clear, but practical validation across diverse compositional languages is limited.

## Next Checks

1. **Dataset Generalization**: Replicate the key findings on additional compositional datasets (e.g., CLEVR, dSprites) to verify that Obverter advantages extend beyond the 3DShapesPyBullet domain

2. **Mechanism Validation**: Design controlled experiments to isolate the specific components of Obverter architecture (mental simulation, confidence thresholding) that contribute to improved disentanglement

3. **Metric Benchmarking**: Conduct a comprehensive comparison of speaker-centered vs listener-centered PosDis across multiple compositional language tasks to establish when each formulation is more appropriate
