# Task-oriented Memory-efficient Pruning-Adapter

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.14704
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.14704
- Reference count: 10
- Key outcome: Novel method combining Adapters and Pruning achieves high memory efficiency and training speed while maintaining accuracy on GLUE tasks

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes Task-oriented Memory-efficient Pruning-Adapter, a novel approach to address the challenge of efficiently training and deploying large language models on resource-constrained devices. The method combines the advantages of Adapters and Pruning to achieve both training and inference efficiency simultaneously. By introducing Task-oriented Importance and Importance-oriented Rank-Varied LoRA, the authors demonstrate significant improvements in memory efficiency and training time while maintaining high accuracy across GLUE tasks.

## Method Summary
The proposed Task-oriented Pruning-Adapter method consists of three main components: Task-oriented Importance calculation, Importance-oriented Pruning, and Importance-oriented Rank-Varied LoRA. First, Task-oriented Importance is calculated by using head masks of Multi-head Attention (MHA) to identify the sensitivity of different Transformer structures to various types of data. Then, Importance-oriented Pruning is applied to prune Multi-head Attention structures that are less important to the model based on the calculated importance scores. Finally, Importance-oriented Rank-Varied LoRA is implemented by assigning higher ranks to more important blocks, taking into account their importance in the specific task. This approach achieves significant memory efficiency during both training and inference while maintaining high accuracy on GLUE tasks.

## Key Results
- Excellent accuracy performance with no significant decrease across GLUE tasks
- Significant memory efficiency, reducing memory consumption and model parameters during both training and inference
- Improved training time efficiency by sharply reducing trainable parameters

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The proposed method works by combining the advantages of Adapters and Pruning techniques. Task-oriented Importance identifies the sensitivity of different Transformer structures to various types of data, enabling targeted pruning of less important Multi-head Attention structures. This targeted pruning reduces the model's size and computational requirements without sacrificing performance. Additionally, Importance-oriented Rank-Varied LoRA assigns higher ranks to more important blocks based on their significance in specific tasks, further optimizing the model's efficiency and accuracy.

## Limitations
- The paper does not provide specific details on the threshold value for pruning Multi-head Attention structures, which may need to be adjusted for specific task requirements.
