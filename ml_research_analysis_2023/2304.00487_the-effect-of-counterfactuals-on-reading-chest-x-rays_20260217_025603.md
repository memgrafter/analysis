# The Effect of Counterfactuals on Reading Chest X-rays
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.00487
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00487
- Reference count: 6
- Two radiologists found counterfactual explanations increased confidence in true positive chest X-ray predictions by 0.15 points (p=0.01) but had no significant impact on false positives

## Executive Summary
This study evaluates how counterfactual explanations impact radiologists' interpretation of chest X-ray predictions compared to traditional attribution methods. Two radiologists assessed 240 chest X-ray predictions using both traditional explanations (input gradients, Guided Backprop, Integrated Gradients) and counterfactual explanations via the Latent Shift method. The study found that counterfactual explanations significantly increased radiologists' confidence in true positive predictions by 0.15 points (p=0.01), with no significant impact on false positive predictions (0.04 increase, p=0.57). Radiologists found counterfactual explanations more intuitive and helpful for identifying correct model features, particularly for Mass and Atelectasis prediction tasks.

## Method Summary
The study employed a controlled experimental design where two radiologists evaluated 240 chest X-ray predictions across three prediction tasks. Each prediction was explained using traditional attribution methods and counterfactual explanations generated through the Latent Shift method. Radiologists rated their confidence in model predictions on a 5-point scale, with half the predictions being false positives. The study compared confidence ratings between traditional and counterfactual explanations to assess the impact on diagnostic confidence.

## Key Results
- Counterfactual explanations increased confidence in true positive predictions by 0.15 points (p=0.01)
- No significant impact on false positive predictions (0.04 increase, p=0.57)
- Mass and Atelectasis prediction tasks showed the most improvement with counterfactual explanations
- Radiologists found counterfactual explanations more intuitive and helpful for identifying correct model features

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism by which counterfactual explanations improve radiologist confidence appears to be through providing more intuitive and actionable insights about model predictions. Unlike traditional attribution methods that highlight which pixels contributed to predictions, counterfactual explanations show what changes would make the prediction different, helping radiologists better understand the model's reasoning process and identify relevant features more effectively.

## Limitations
- Small sample size of only two radiologists limits generalizability to broader radiology community
- Confidence ratings are subjective and may be influenced by factors unrelated to explanation quality
- Lack of external validation on different datasets or clinical settings constrains real-world applicability

## Confidence
- High: Counterfactual explanations improve confidence in true positive predictions (0.15 increase, p=0.01)
- Medium: Null effect on false positive predictions (0.04 increase, p=0.57)
- Low: Long-term impacts on diagnostic accuracy and radiologist learning curves
