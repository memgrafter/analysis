# Differentiate ChatGPT-generated and Human-written Medical Texts

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.11567
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11567
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: BERT-based detection achieves F1 score over 95% for distinguishing ChatGPT-generated medical texts from human-written texts

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of detecting ChatGPT-generated medical texts by analyzing linguistic differences between human and AI-generated content. The authors create two datasets containing medical abstracts and radiology reports, comparing human-written texts with those generated by ChatGPT. Through comprehensive linguistic analysis covering vocabulary, part-of-speech, dependency structures, sentiment, and perplexity, the study reveals that ChatGPT-generated medical texts are more logical and fluent but less specific in content and language style compared to human-written texts. The research develops machine learning detection methods, with BERT-based models achieving high accuracy in distinguishing between the two text types.

## Method Summary
The study constructs two datasets containing medical abstracts and radiology reports, with each dataset comprising both human-written and ChatGPT-generated texts. The authors conduct a comprehensive linguistic analysis examining vocabulary diversity, part-of-speech distributions, dependency structures, sentiment patterns, and perplexity scores. Machine learning detection methods are developed using traditional classifiers and transformer-based models, with BERT achieving the highest performance. The detection system is evaluated using standard metrics including precision, recall, and F1 score, with experiments conducted to compare performance across different model architectures and text types.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT-generated medical texts show higher logical coherence and fluency but lower specificity compared to human-written texts
- BERT-based detection models achieve F1 scores exceeding 95% for identifying ChatGPT-generated medical texts
- Significant linguistic differences exist between human and AI-generated medical texts across multiple dimensions including vocabulary diversity and structural patterns

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The detection mechanism leverages systematic linguistic differences between human and AI-generated medical texts. ChatGPT produces text with more uniform vocabulary usage, consistent syntactic structures, and higher perplexity scores compared to the diverse, context-specific patterns found in human writing. The BERT model effectively captures these subtle but consistent differences in language patterns, dependencies, and semantic structures that distinguish AI-generated content from human-authored medical texts.

## Limitations
- Detection methods may not generalize well to other medical text types beyond abstracts and radiology reports
- Current detection performance may not hold against newer versions of ChatGPT or other advanced LLM models
- Does not account for potential adversarial techniques that could evade detection through text modification
