# Hierarchical learning, forecasting coherent spatio-temporal individual and aggregated building loads

## Executive Summary
This work introduces a novel machine-learning based hierarchical forecasting approach for building electrical loads that unifies traditional forecasting and reconciliation steps. The method employs a custom loss function incorporating hierarchical reconciliation taxonomy to ensure coherency across spatial, temporal, and spatio-temporal dimensions. The approach is evaluated on two real-world case studies involving 383 and 140 buildings respectively, using a deep neural network regressor.

The research demonstrates that hierarchical-coherent learning can provide value in certain settings, particularly in the spatial hierarchy of case study 1. The unified framework enables coherent forecasts across all examined dimensions, which can support better decision-making in smart grid operations. However, the approach faces significant challenges including computational complexity when handling large hierarchical structures and complications from scaled trees, highlighting the need for further research in scalability and non-uniform hierarchical structures.

## Method Summary
The paper proposes a hierarchical forecasting framework that integrates traditional forecasting with reconciliation steps through a custom loss function. The approach leverages established hierarchical reconciliation taxonomy to ensure coherency across multiple dimensions - spatial (individual buildings and aggregated loads), temporal (different time scales), and their combination (spatio-temporal). The method uses a deep neural network regressor trained to optimize for hierarchical coherency, effectively unifying what were previously separate forecasting and reconciliation steps into a single learning process. The custom loss function incorporates constraints that ensure the forecast hierarchy maintains mathematical consistency across all levels, preventing the common problem of incoherent forecasts that require post-hoc reconciliation.

## Key Results
- Best performance achieved in spatial hierarchy of case study 1 with 383 buildings
- Method provides unified and coherent forecasts across spatial, temporal, and spatio-temporal dimensions
- Faces challenges with curse of dimensionality and coherency-learning complications from scaled trees

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The approach works by embedding hierarchical reconciliation constraints directly into the learning objective through a custom loss function. This ensures that the model learns forecasts that are inherently coherent across all levels of the hierarchy, rather than requiring separate reconciliation steps. By treating forecasting and reconciliation as a unified problem, the method can leverage the relationships between different hierarchy levels during training, leading to improved overall coherence and potentially better accuracy at both individual and aggregated levels.
