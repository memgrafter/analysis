# Exact Count of Boundary Pieces of ReLU Classifiers: Towards the Proper Complexity Measure for Classification

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.08805
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.08805
- Reference count: 40
- Main claim: Boundary piece count is an independent and meaningful complexity measure for ReLU classifiers

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a novel approach to measure decision boundary complexity of ReLU classifiers by counting boundary pieces using tropical geometry. The authors develop an explicit algorithm to compute exact counts of both boundary pieces and total affine pieces, representing ReLU networks as differences of convex piecewise-affine functions. The method leverages duality and convex hull properties to identify boundary pieces as edges connecting points from positive and negative parts of the function decomposition.

## Method Summary
The authors propose a tropical geometry-based algorithm to count exact boundary pieces in ReLU classifiers. The method represents ReLU networks as differences of convex piecewise-affine functions, then uses duality and convex hull properties to identify boundary pieces as edges connecting points from positive and negative parts. This approach provides an exact count rather than approximations, enabling precise measurement of decision boundary complexity.

## Key Results
- The boundary piece count algorithm successfully computes exact counts for ReLU networks
- Boundary piece count shows negative correlation with robustness measures
- Adversarial training and noise injection significantly reduce boundary pieces in synthetic 2D data

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The tropical geometry approach works because ReLU networks can be represented as piecewise-affine functions with polyhedral domains. By decomposing these functions into differences of convex parts, the boundary pieces naturally emerge as edges connecting the positive and negative components. The duality framework allows systematic identification of these edges through convex hull operations.
