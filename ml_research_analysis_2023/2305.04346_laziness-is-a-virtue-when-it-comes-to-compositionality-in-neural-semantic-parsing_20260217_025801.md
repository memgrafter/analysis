# Laziness Is a Virtue When It Comes to Compositionality in Neural Semantic Parsing
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.04346
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04346
- Reference count: 19
- Novel bottom-up semantic parsing approach with lazy evaluation achieves strong compositional generalization performance

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a bottom-up semantic parsing approach that constructs logical forms from leaves upward, contrasting with traditional top-down autoregressive methods. The key innovation is a lazy evaluation scheme that expands only the most promising parse candidates at each generation step, significantly reducing computational overhead while maintaining exploration of a large hypothesis space. The method is evaluated on CFQ, Text-to-SQL, and Geoquery datasets, showing competitive performance against both general and specialized parsers while demonstrating strong compositional generalization capabilities.

## Method Summary
The proposed approach addresses compositional generalization challenges in neural semantic parsing through a bottom-up decoding strategy. Instead of generating logical forms top-down as in traditional autoregressive models, this method builds expressions from the leaves upward, using a lazy expansion mechanism. At each step, only the most promising parse candidates are expanded, reducing computational requirements while maintaining the ability to explore diverse parse hypotheses. The approach is designed to be compatible with different encoders and demonstrates consistent performance improvements across various semantic parsing tasks.

## Key Results
- Bottom-up semantic parser outperforms general-purpose semantic parsers on compositional generalization tasks
- Competitive performance with specialized neural parsers on CFQ, Text-to-SQL, and Geoquery datasets
- Method demonstrates strong flexibility by working effectively with different encoder configurations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The lazy bottom-up approach works by constructing logical forms from the ground up rather than top-down. This reversal of the generation order allows the model to focus computational resources on the most promising parse candidates at each step, rather than exploring the entire tree space. The lazy evaluation scheme means that expansions only occur when they appear to be on promising paths, reducing unnecessary computation while maintaining coverage of the hypothesis space. This targeted expansion strategy is particularly effective for compositional generalization, where the ability to recombine known components in novel ways is crucial.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component map:** Input sentence → Encoder → Bottom-up decoder with lazy expansion → Logical form candidates → Output

**Critical path:** The critical computational path involves encoding the input, generating initial leaf candidates, and then iteratively expanding the most promising candidates through the lazy evaluation mechanism until complete logical forms are produced.

**Design tradeoffs:** The approach trades the simplicity and directness of top-down generation for computational efficiency and improved compositional generalization. The lazy expansion mechanism introduces complexity but provides significant efficiency gains by avoiding exploration of unpromising paths.

**Failure signatures:** The method may struggle with tasks requiring deep logical forms or those with very large candidate spaces where lazy evaluation might prematurely prune promising paths. It also depends on the quality of the encoder's representations for guiding the expansion decisions.

## Limitations
- Evaluation limited to three datasets with constrained logical form spaces, unclear generalization to open-domain tasks
- Computational efficiency claims lack detailed ablation studies comparing lazy strategy to alternative pruning methods
