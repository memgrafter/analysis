# Improved Visual Story Generation with Adaptive Context Modeling

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.16811
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16811
- Reference count: 40
- The proposed ACM-VSG model achieves state-of-the-art FID scores: 15.36 on PororoSV story visualization, 15.48 on PororoSV story continuation, and 18.41 on FlintstonesSV story continuation.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces an adaptive context modeling approach for visual story generation that improves global consistency by selectively incorporating relevant historical text-image pairs. The method leverages CLIP and BLIP encoders with cross attention to identify and encode contextually relevant information, which is then used as additional guidance during diffusion sampling. The approach is evaluated on PororoSV and FlintstonesSV datasets for both story visualization and continuation tasks, demonstrating superior FID scores compared to existing methods.

## Method Summary
The proposed method employs an adaptive context modeling framework that selectively incorporates relevant historical text-image pairs to improve global consistency in visual story generation. The core mechanism uses a CLIP text encoder and BLIP text-image encoder with cross attention to identify and encode relevant context from previous story elements. This contextual information is then integrated as additional guidance during the diffusion sampling process, allowing the model to maintain better coherence across generated images in a visual story sequence.

## Key Results
- Achieved state-of-the-art FID score of 15.36 on PororoSV story visualization task
- Achieved state-of-the-art FID score of 15.48 on PororoSV story continuation task
- Achieved state-of-the-art FID score of 18.41 on FlintstonesSV story continuation task

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The adaptive context modeling approach works by selectively incorporating relevant historical text-image pairs during visual story generation. By using CLIP and BLIP encoders with cross attention, the model can identify which previous context elements are most relevant to the current generation step. This targeted incorporation of context provides additional guidance during diffusion sampling, helping maintain global consistency across the visual story while avoiding the pitfalls of including irrelevant or contradictory information from the full history.

## Architecture Onboarding
- Component map: CLIP text encoder -> BLIP text-image encoder with cross attention -> Adaptive context selection -> Diffusion sampling guidance
- Critical path: Current text prompt → CLIP encoder → Context relevance scoring → BLIP encoding of selected context → Cross attention integration → Diffusion sampling
- Design tradeoffs: Selective context incorporation vs. full context usage - balances relevance with computational efficiency
- Failure signatures: Irrelevant context selection leading to incoherent visuals; Over-selection causing generation instability
- First experiments: 1) Test context selection accuracy on held-out validation pairs, 2) Ablation study on number of context elements included, 3) Qualitative analysis of global consistency improvements

## Limitations
- The method's generalization to visual storytelling domains beyond PororoSV and FlintstonesSV datasets remains untested
- The computational overhead introduced by the adaptive context modeling module during inference is not addressed
- The sensitivity of results to encoder choice or parameter settings is not explored

## Confidence
- High: The proposed adaptive context modeling framework and its integration with diffusion sampling is technically sound and well-motivated
- Medium: The reported FID scores and their comparison with baselines are credible within the scope of the evaluated datasets and tasks
- Low: Claims about generalization to broader visual storytelling applications and the method's robustness to context quality are not sufficiently supported
