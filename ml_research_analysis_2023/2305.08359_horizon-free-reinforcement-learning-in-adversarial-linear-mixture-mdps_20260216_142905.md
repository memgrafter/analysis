# Horizon-free Reinforcement Learning in Adversarial Linear Mixture MDPs
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.08359

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of horizon-free reinforcement learning in adversarial linear mixture Markov decision processes (MDPs). The authors develop a novel algorithm, HF-O2PS, that achieves a near-optimal regret bound without polynomial dependence on the planning horizon. By leveraging occupancy measures as a proxy for policies and employing online mirror descent on these measures, the algorithm effectively handles the adversarial nature of rewards while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Method Summary
The HF-O2PS algorithm employs occupancy measures as a proxy for policies and conducts online mirror descent on these measures. The key innovation is the use of a variance-uncertainty-aware weighted least square estimator and a high-order moment estimator for learning the transition kernel. This approach allows the algorithm to achieve a horizon-free regret bound of $\tilde{O}((d+\log(|S|^2|A|))\sqrt{K} + d^2)$, significantly improving upon existing methods that suffer from polynomial dependence on the planning horizon.

## Key Results
- Achieves a horizon-free regret bound of $\tilde{O}((d+\log(|S|^2|A|))\sqrt{K} + d^2)$
- Removes polynomial dependence on the planning horizon, a significant theoretical advance
- Provides hardness results and regret lower bounds justifying the near optimality of the algorithm

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
