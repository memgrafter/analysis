# Non-convex approaches for low-rank tensor completion under tubal sampling
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.12721
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12721
- Reference count: 0
- Primary result: TL12 achieves higher accuracy but with higher computational cost, while TCCUR is more efficient but with slightly lower accuracy

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces two non-convex methods for tensor completion under tubal sampling. The tensor L1-L2 (TL12) method employs a novel regularization combining L1 and L2 norms of singular values from the t-SVD to promote low-rankness. The tensor completion via CUR (TCCUR) method utilizes CUR decomposition on Fourier-transformed frontal slices, converting the problem into a series of matrix completion subproblems. Both methods are designed to leverage the specific structure of tubal sampling, offering different trade-offs between accuracy and computational efficiency.

## Method Summary
The paper proposes two complementary approaches for tubal sampling tensor completion. TL12 combines L1 and L2 norms of t-SVD singular values to enforce low-rank structure through non-convex regularization. TCCUR decomposes the problem using CUR factorization on Fourier-transformed slices, exploiting the tubal sampling structure to reduce computational complexity. The methods represent different design philosophies: TL12 prioritizes accuracy through sophisticated regularization, while TCCUR emphasizes computational efficiency through decomposition.

## Key Results
- TL12 method achieves higher completion accuracy compared to TCCUR
- TCCUR method demonstrates superior computational efficiency
- Both methods outperform classical approaches in their respective domains
- Trade-off exists between accuracy (TL12) and efficiency (TCCUR)

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The TL12 method works by combining L1 and L2 regularization on singular values from the t-SVD, which promotes low-rank structure while maintaining convexity properties in the transformed domain. The non-convex combination provides stronger low-rank promotion than either norm alone. TCCUR leverages the tubal sampling structure by transforming the problem via Fourier analysis and applying CUR decomposition, which exploits the inherent redundancy in tensor data while reducing computational complexity through matrix completion subproblems.
