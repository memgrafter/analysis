# Deterministic Online Classification: Non-iteratively Reweighted Recursive Least-Squares for Binary Class Rebalancing

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a novel deterministic online classification algorithm called Non-iteratively Reweighted Recursive Least-Squares (NR-RLS) for binary class rebalancing. The method addresses the challenge of exact reweighting in online settings of weighted least-squares, which is necessary to converge to batch settings. Existing iterative reweighting approaches have linearly growing time complexity, making them inefficient for online learning. NR-RLS introduces a non-iterative, recursive formulation that achieves constant time complexity and exact convergence to the batch formulation. The algorithm uses a total-error-rate metric with two different weights for positive and negative classes, allowing it to handle unbalanced data distributions. Experiments on 31 real-world datasets show that NR-RLS outperforms state-of-the-art stochastic online binary classification algorithms in terms of accuracy, while maintaining constant computational time.

## Method Summary
The NR-RLS algorithm presents a new approach to online binary classification that overcomes the limitations of existing iterative reweighting methods. It achieves exact convergence to batch formulations while maintaining constant time complexity through a non-iterative, recursive formulation. The method uses a total-error-rate metric with different weights for positive and negative classes, enabling effective handling of unbalanced data distributions. The key innovation lies in the recursive update mechanism that avoids the linear time complexity growth of iterative approaches while maintaining deterministic behavior.

The deterministic nature ensures consistent behavior across runs, while the constant time complexity enables scalability to large datasets.
