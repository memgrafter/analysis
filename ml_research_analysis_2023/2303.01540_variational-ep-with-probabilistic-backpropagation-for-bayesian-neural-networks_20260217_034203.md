# Variational EP with Probabilistic Backpropagation for Bayesian Neural Networks

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.01540
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.01540
- Reference count: 8
- Key outcome: Novel VEP approach combines EP and VB for Bayesian neural networks, showing improved performance over standard methods

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes Variational Expectation Propagation (VEP), a hybrid approach that combines the global consistency of Variational Bayes with the local refinement of Expectation Propagation for approximate Bayesian inference in neural networks. The method uses a modified KL divergence that incorporates EP-style cavity distributions and tilted posteriors into the variational framework, while maintaining computational tractability through factorized Gaussian approximations. Hierarchical ARD priors are employed to encourage sparsity in the network weights, and Probabilistic Backpropagation enables scalable inference by propagating uncertainty through the network layers.

## Method Summary
The VEP method implements Bayesian inference for two-layer neural networks with hierarchical ARD priors on weights. It performs E-steps to compute approximate posteriors using cavity distributions and tilted posteriors, followed by M-steps to update hyperparameters through probabilistic backpropagation. The approach combines EP and VB through a hybrid algorithm that maintains the tractability of variational inference while incorporating EP's local refinement capabilities. The method is designed for binary classification with logistic regression likelihood and uses factorized Gaussian approximations to keep computational complexity manageable.

## Key Results
- Combines global VB consistency with local EP refinement through modified KL divergence
- Uses hierarchical ARD priors for sparsity while maintaining computational efficiency
- Employs Probabilistic Backpropagation for scalable uncertainty propagation

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: VEP combines VB's global consistency with EP's local refinement for more accurate posterior approximation
- Mechanism: Modified KL divergence balances global VB updates with local EP-style refinements using cavity distributions and tilted posteriors
- Core assumption: Smooth interpolation between VB and EP behaviors preserves tractability while improving accuracy
- Evidence anchors: Abstract description of combining EP and VB strengths; section explaining VEP as unification of algorithms

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Hierarchical ARD priors with factorized Gaussian approximations enable effective sparsity
- Mechanism: ARD priors assign separate variance hyperparameters to each weight, allowing automatic identification of irrelevant features while factorized approximation maintains computational efficiency
- Core assumption: Sparsity-promoting effect of ARD priors is preserved under factorized approximation
- Evidence anchors: Abstract mentioning hierarchical ARD priors and factorized Gaussian approximation; section on combining linear models with sparsity-promoting priors

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Probabilistic Backpropagation enables scalable inference with uncertainty quantification
- Mechanism: PBP replaces point estimates with Gaussian distributions and propagates these through network, approximating intractable distributions with one-dimensional Gaussians matching marginal means and variances
- Core assumption: One-dimensional Gaussian approximations adequately capture posterior uncertainty structure
- Evidence anchors: Section describing PBP using one-dimensional Gaussian approximations; explanation of computing marginal probability of target variable

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: VEP algorithm (VB+EP hybrid) -> Hierarchical ARD priors (sparsity) -> Probabilistic Backpropagation (scalable inference)
- Critical path: Forward propagation through network using PBP -> Backward propagation of gradients for parameter updates -> VEP updates with cavity distributions and tilted posteriors
- Design tradeoffs: Trades some posterior accuracy for computational tractability via factorized approximations; balances global consistency with local refinement
- Failure signatures: Underestimated uncertainty (VB dominance), slow convergence (excessive local updates), failure to achieve sparsity (ineffective ARD priors), computational bottlenecks in deep networks
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Binary classification on simple dataset (e.g., Iris binary task) to verify basic functionality and compare against standard VB/EP
  2. High-dimensional sparse regression to test ARD prior effectiveness in feature selection
  3. Deep network classification on MNIST/CIFAR-10 to evaluate PBP scalability and overall VEP approach

- Why unresolved: No detailed analysis of computational cost scaling with network size or empirical results on runtime for varying sizes

- Basis in paper: Limited to binary classification with logistic regression; no comparison to other Bayesian methods on complex datasets
- Why unresolved: Experimental evaluation limited to simple binary classification; comprehensive comparison needed on diverse, high-dimensional datasets
- What evidence would resolve it: Empirical results comparing VEP to HMC, VI on various real-world datasets with metrics for accuracy, uncertainty calibration, and computational efficiency

### Open Question 3
- Question: How sensitive is VEP to hyperparameter choices (prior variance, lower bound parameters), and what strategies select these automatically?
- Basis in paper: Uses hierarchical ARD priors and logistic likelihood lower bound but doesn't discuss hyperparameter selection or sensitivity
- Why unresolved: Performance may depend critically on hyperparameters but paper provides no guidance on selection or impact analysis
- What evidence would resolve it: Sensitivity analysis showing performance variation with different hyperparameter settings, plus results using automatic selection strategies
