# CBBQ: A Chinese Bias Benchmark Dataset Curated with Human-AI Collaboration for Large Language Models

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces CBBQ, a Chinese bias benchmark dataset for evaluating societal biases in large language models. The dataset contains over 100K questions spanning 14 social dimensions related to Chinese culture and values, constructed through a collaborative process between human experts and generative language models. The curation involves identifying biases through literature review, generating ambiguous and disambiguated contexts, and manual review. Experiments on 10 Chinese LLMs reveal strong biases across various categories, with fine-tuned models showing some ability to self-correct. The dataset provides a comprehensive tool for assessing and mitigating bias in Chinese LLMs, contributing to safer and more equitable AI deployment.

## Method Summary
The CBBQ dataset was constructed through a human-AI collaborative process involving literature review to identify potential biases, followed by generating ambiguous and disambiguated contexts using language models, and concluding with manual review by human experts. The dataset covers 14 social dimensions relevant to Chinese culture and values, containing over 100K questions designed to evaluate societal biases in large language models.

## Key Results
- CBBQ dataset contains over 100K questions across 14 social dimensions
- Experiments reveal strong biases in 10 Chinese LLMs across various categories
- Fine-tuned models demonstrate some ability to self-correct identified biases

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The dataset works by providing a comprehensive set of questions that capture various forms of societal bias specific to Chinese cultural context. The human-AI collaboration ensures both the breadth of coverage through automated generation and the quality control through human review. The ambiguous and disambiguated context pairs allow for precise measurement of when and how biases emerge in model responses.

## Architecture Onboarding

Component map: Literature Review -> AI Generation -> Human Review -> Benchmark Assembly

Critical path: Bias identification through literature → Context generation → Manual validation → Dataset compilation

Design tradeoffs: Automated generation provides scale but requires human oversight for cultural nuance; comprehensive coverage versus targeted precision

Failure signatures: Overfitting to dataset patterns; cultural misinterpretation by generative models; insufficient coverage of emerging bias types
