# Towards Visual Foundational Models of Physical Scenes

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.03727
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03727
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: NeRF Diffusion outperforms baselines in FID, KID, IS metrics while maintaining competitive PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS scores

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes a theoretical framework and empirical validation for foundational models of physical scenes using NeRFs combined with Diffusion Models (termed NeRF Diffusion). The key idea is to represent physical scenes as abstract concepts defined by minimal sufficient statistics of measurements, then show that NeRF Diffusion can serve as a viable representation satisfying these conditions. The approach combines NeRF's geometric structure with Diffusion Models' ability to hallucinate unseen details, resulting in improved realism while remaining faithful to the underlying measurements.

## Method Summary
The method combines NeRFs with Diffusion Models through a process called NeRF Diffusion. The approach trains a NeRF on posed images to learn geometry and radiance fields, then fine-tunes a pre-trained Stable Diffusion model on NeRF renderings to learn priors for denoising and extrapolating details. This combined model is evaluated on ObjectScans and Mip-NeRF 360 datasets using distribution metrics (FID, KID, IS) for realism and similarity metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) for faithfulness to measurements.

## Key Results
- NeRF Diffusion outperforms baseline methods in distribution metrics (FID, KID, IS) on ObjectScans and Mip-NeRF 360 datasets
- Maintains competitive similarity scores (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS) indicating faithfulness to measurements
- Successfully generates visually plausible images that respect underlying scene measurements
- Demonstrates improved phenomenological quality (realism) of rendering compared to NeRF alone

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: NeRF Diffusion can represent physical scenes by combining NeRF's geometry with Diffusion Model's ability to hallucinate unseen details
- Mechanism: NeRF provides geometric structure and radiance field, while Diffusion Model learns to denoise and extrapolate missing details
- Core assumption: Diffusion Models can learn statistical priors of natural images to hallucinate realistic details where NeRF is insufficient
- Evidence anchors: Abstract states NeRFs can be combined with Diffusion Models as unsupervised representations; Section 3.2 shows composition can be viable representation
- Break condition: If Diffusion Model cannot learn image priors or NeRF rendering is too aliased for effective denoising

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Physical scene is an abstract concept uniquely defined and inferred from measurements
- Mechanism: Physical scene defined as minimal sufficient statistic of measurements, ensuring uniqueness and unambiguous representation
- Core assumption: Measurements are exchangeable and can be extended to infinite exchangeable sequence
- Evidence anchors: Abstract defines scene as abstract concept from agent's observations; Section defines unique minimal sufficient scene for given measurements
- Break condition: If measurements are not exchangeable or cannot be extended to infinite sequence

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: NeRF Diffusion generates visually plausible images respecting underlying measurements
- Mechanism: Model trained to denoise NeRF views and extrapolate details not manifest therein
- Core assumption: NeRF Diffusion can learn distribution of NeRF rendering artifacts and map aliased renderings to calibrated images
- Evidence anchors: Abstract reports outperforming baselines in distribution metrics while maintaining similarity scores; Section discusses need to show ability to "hallucinate" unseen details perceptually
- Break condition: If NeRF Diffusion cannot learn artifact distribution or extrapolated details are unrealistic

## Foundational Learning

- Concept: Exchangeability of measurements
  - Why needed here: Theory relies on measurements being exchangeable to define scene as abstract concept
  - Quick check question: Can measurements be permuted without changing joint distribution?

- Concept: Minimal sufficient statistic
  - Why needed here: Physical scene defined as minimal sufficient statistic of measurements, ensuring uniqueness
  - Quick check question: Is minimal sufficient statistic unique for given measurements?

- Concept: Diffusion Models and their ability to learn statistical priors
  - Why needed here: Diffusion Model used to denoise and extrapolate details, requiring learning statistical priors of natural images
  - Quick check question: Can Diffusion Model learn statistical priors of natural images effectively?

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: NeRF -> Diffusion Model -> NeRF Diffusion
- Critical path:
  1. Train NeRF on posed images to learn geometry and radiance field
  2. Train Diffusion Model on NeRF renderings to learn artifact distribution and denoise renderings
  3. Combine NeRF and Diffusion Model to form NeRF Diffusion
  4. Evaluate NeRF Diffusion on unseen data to assess physical scene representation capability
- Design tradeoffs:
  - Training time: Both NeRF and Diffusion Model training can be computationally intensive
  - Memory usage: Storing and processing large amounts of image data and model parameters
  - Generalization: Ensuring NeRF Diffusion handles wide range of scenes and variations
- Failure signatures:
  - Poor reconstruction quality in NeRF rendering
  - Diffusion Model fails to denoise and extrapolate details effectively
  - NeRF Diffusion produces unrealistic or inconsistent images
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Train NeRF on simple scene (e.g., single object) and evaluate reconstruction quality
  2. Train Diffusion Model on NeRF renderings from simple scene and assess denoising/extrapolation ability
  3. Combine NeRF and Diffusion Model to form NeRF Diffusion and evaluate on unseen views of simple scene

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
- Question: How can proposed theoretical framework be extended to incorporate active perception and multi-modal data beyond passive visual data?
- Basis in paper: Explicit acknowledgment in discussion section about analysis being limited to vision
- Why unresolved: Current framework focuses solely on visual data without addressing adaptation to other sensory modalities or active perception
- What evidence would resolve it: Developing and validating framework incorporating active perception and multi-modal data, demonstrating effectiveness in representing physical scenes

### Open Question 2
- Question: How can proposed method be improved to better handle out-of-distribution data and generalize to unseen scenes?
- Basis in paper: Inferred from mention that diffusion model pre-trained on ObjectScans struggles to generalize to Mip-NeRF 360 dataset
- Why unresolved: Current method relies on fine-tuning pre-trained diffusion model which may not generalize well to scenes with different characteristics
- What evidence would resolve it: Developing method that better handles out-of-distribution data, potentially through sophisticated fine-tuning strategies or domain adaptation techniques

### Open Question 3
- Question: How can proposed method be scaled to handle larger and more complex scenes while maintaining computational efficiency?
- Basis in paper: Explicit acknowledgment that diffusion models require considerable training data and have high computational costs for larger scenes
- Why unresolved: Current method relies on pre-trained diffusion model and fine-tuning on smaller datasets which may not scale well
- What evidence would resolve it: Developing method that efficiently handles larger scenes through more efficient training strategies, model architectures, or hardware optimizations

## Limitations
- Theoretical framework relies on abstract definitions without empirical validation of exchangeability assumption across diverse real-world datasets
- Claim that NeRF Diffusion uniquely satisfies all eight conditions lacks comparison against alternative approaches like GAN-based or variational methods
- Empirical evaluation focuses on synthetic/curated datasets without testing generalization to complex, uncontrolled environments

## Confidence
- **High Confidence**: Technical implementation of NeRF Diffusion architecture and core empirical methodology are well-specified and reproducible
- **Medium Confidence**: Theoretical framework for defining physical scenes as abstract concepts is internally consistent but may not fully capture practical measurement scenarios
- **Medium Confidence**: Empirical results showing NeRF Diffusion outperforming baselines are convincing though choice of comparison methods could be expanded

## Next Checks
1. Test exchangeability assumption by systematically permuting measurements and measuring degradation in NeRF Diffusion performance across different scene types
2. Implement and evaluate GAN-based or variational approaches on same datasets to determine if NeRF Diffusion's claimed advantages are unique
3. Apply NeRF Diffusion to uncontrolled, real-world datasets (e.g., ScanNet, MegaDepth) to assess robustness beyond curated object scans and synthetic 360° environments
