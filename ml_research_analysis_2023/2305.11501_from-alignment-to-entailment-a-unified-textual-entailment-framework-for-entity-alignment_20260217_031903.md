# From Alignment to Entailment: A Unified Textual Entailment Framework for Entity Alignment

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes a unified textual entailment framework for entity alignment that transforms relational and attribute triples into unified textual sequences. The key innovation is modeling entity alignment as a bi-directional textual entailment task between cross-knowledge graph entities. The method addresses the limitation of existing approaches that encode relational and attribute triples in heterogeneous embedding spaces, preventing them from helping each other. By unifying these into textual sequences and using PLM-based aligners, the framework captures fine-grained interactions and enables mutual enhancement of heterogeneous information.

## Method Summary
The framework transforms relational triples (h,r,t) and attribute triples (e,a,v) into unified textual sequences, then models entity alignment as a bi-directional textual entailment task. Two PLM-based aligners are proposed: NSP-Aligner using Next Sentence Prediction and MLM-Aligner using Masked Language Modeling. The model is trained with both entailment losses and embedding-alignment objectives, using embeddings for candidate selection and entailment probability for re-ranking. This approach enables the model to capture common correlation patterns between relational and attribute information that would otherwise be separated in different embedding spaces.

## Key Results
- TEA achieves up to 4.4% improvement in Hits@1 and 11% in MRR on DBPZH-EN dataset
- The framework consistently outperforms state-of-the-art entity alignment methods on five cross-lingual datasets
- Re-ranking strategy with confidence threshold of 0.5 shows effectiveness in improving alignment accuracy

## Limitations
- The model requires substantial training data to effectively model fine-grained interactions between cross-KG entities, making it potentially ineffective for datasets with fewer triples.
- The re-ranking phase incurs significant computational overhead as it requires processing each entity pair, which could be prohibitive for large-scale knowledge graphs.
- The specific design of templates and their impact on performance across different KG structures remains underexplored.

## Confidence
**High Confidence**: The core mechanism of transforming KG triples into textual sequences and modeling entity alignment as bi-directional textual entailment is well-supported by the experimental results, particularly the consistent improvements across multiple datasets and the effectiveness of the re-ranking strategy.
