# Heteroskedastic Geospatial Tracking with Distributed Camera Networks

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2306.02407
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02407
- **Reference count:** 9
- **Primary result:** Distributed multi-camera tracking framework with heteroskedastic detection and Kalman filter fusion

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a distributed framework for tracking objects across multiple camera views in geospatial coordinates. Each camera independently detects objects and outputs Gaussian distributions over geospatial locations, which are then fused using a multi-observation Kalman filter. The approach introduces a custom backbone model that balances performance and latency, along with calibration techniques to improve covariance estimation and Kalman filter tuning, particularly enhancing performance in low-light conditions.

## Method Summary
The proposed system operates through a distributed architecture where each camera runs an independent heteroskedastic detector that outputs probabilistic geospatial locations. These detections are fused across the network using a multi-observation Kalman filter. A custom backbone model is introduced to balance computational efficiency with detection accuracy. The framework includes post-hoc calibration of detection covariances and Kalman filter fine-tuning to enhance tracking performance, especially under challenging conditions like low-light environments.

## Key Results
- Custom backbone achieves competitive performance to ResNet50 and DETR while maintaining lower latency
- Post-hoc calibration of detection covariances and Kalman filter fine-tuning improves overall tracking accuracy
- System shows particular strength in low-light conditions where DETR excels

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The framework leverages heteroskedastic detection to provide uncertainty-aware object localization across distributed cameras. By outputting Gaussian distributions instead of point estimates, the system can properly weight detections based on their confidence levels. The multi-observation Kalman filter effectively fuses these probabilistic detections over time, maintaining consistent tracking despite camera view changes. The custom backbone model provides a computational sweet spot between heavy architectures like ResNet50 and specialized models like DETR, while the calibration and fine-tuning steps address systematic biases in uncertainty estimation.
