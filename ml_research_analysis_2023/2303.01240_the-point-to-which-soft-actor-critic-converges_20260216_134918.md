# The Point to Which Soft Actor-Critic Converges

## Executive Summary
This paper establishes that Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) converges to the same optimal policy as Soft Q-Learning (SQL) when using entropy regularization, despite their different optimization approaches. The authors provide a theoretical framework showing that SAC's iterative policy evaluation and improvement steps ultimately optimize the same objective as SQL's direct optimization of the LogSumExp term. This theoretical insight explains SAC's empirical success and demonstrates that its tractable approach achieves the same convergence guarantees as SQL while being more practical to implement.

## Method Summary
The paper analyzes convergence by characterizing the optimal regularized value function and policy through Lagrangian optimization. The authors show that SAC's soft Bellman operator is a contraction mapping and that its fixed point corresponds to the optimal action-value function of the regularized MDP. The proof demonstrates that repeatedly applying soft policy evaluation and improvement converges to a policy where the Q-function is optimal for the regularized MDP. This framework also extends to other regularizers like KL divergence.

## Key Results
- SAC and SQL converge to identical optimal policies under entropy regularization
- The soft Bellman operator is proven to be a contraction mapping
- Convergence result extends to regularizers beyond entropy, including KL divergence
- Optimal policy follows softmax distribution over exponentiated Q-values

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: SAC converges to same solution as SQL in limit with entropy regularization
- Mechanism: SAC's soft Bellman operator approaches same fixed point as SQL's LogSumExp optimization through iterative policy evaluation and improvement
- Core assumption: Soft Bellman operator is a contraction mapping with modified reward
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "we prove that in the limit they converge to the same solution"
  - [section] "Repeatedly application of soft policy evaluation and soft policy improvement to any π ∈ Π converges to a policy π⋆"
  - [corpus] Weak evidence - no direct mention of SAC-SQL equivalence in neighboring papers
- Break condition: If soft Bellman operator is not a contraction, repeated application may not converge

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Optimal regularized value function and policy satisfy LogSumExp equations
- Mechanism: Lagrangian optimization with entropy regularization yields optimal policy as softmax over Q-values and optimal value as log of summed exponentiated values
- Core assumption: Objective is linear and entropy is strictly convex, satisfying Slater's condition
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section] "Lemma 1. For all(s, a) ∈ S × A, the optimal value function V⋆(s) and the optimal policy π⋆(a|s), satisfy V⋆(s) = η log ∑a exp(1/η(r(s,a) + γE_{s'∼p}[V⋆(s')]))"
  - [section] "π⋆(a|s) = exp(1/η(r(s,a) + γE_{s'∼p}[V⋆(s')])) / ∑s∈A exp(1/η(r(s,a) + γE_{s'∼p}[V⋆(s')]))"
  - [corpus] Weak evidence - no direct mention of Lagrangian optimization in neighboring papers
- Break condition: If objective not linear or entropy not strictly convex, Lagrangian approach may not yield optimal solution

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Convergence result extends to other regularizers like KL divergence
- Mechanism: Proof technique generalizes to other regularizers by modifying Lagrangian formulation and optimal policy equations
- Core assumption: Regularizer satisfies properties similar to entropy (linearity, strict convexity)
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "The same justification can also be applied to other regularizers such as KL divergence"
  - [section] "Optimizing a generalized type of regularizer such as the KL divergence, we can follow another optimization procedure"
  - [corpus] Weak evidence - no direct mention of KL divergence or other regularizers in neighboring papers
- Break condition: If regularizer doesn't satisfy required properties, convergence proof may not hold
