# QICHWABASE: A Quechua Language and Knowledge Base for Quechua Communities

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.06173
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.06173
- Reference count: 6
- Primary result: QICHWABASE contains 1,030,825 triples, lexemes in various Quechua dialects, and sense descriptions in English, German, and Spanish

## Executive Summary
QICHWABASE is a Wikibase-based knowledge base designed to address the lack of structured Web resources for the Quechua language and its communities. The project follows a systematic knowledge graph generation process and aims to increase the presence of minority communities on the Web through collaborative knowledge building. The knowledge base contains extensive linguistic data across multiple Quechua dialects and language descriptions.

## Method Summary
The authors developed QICHWABASE through a knowledge graph generation process that included creation, hosting, curation, and deployment phases. The knowledge base was deployed on Wikibase Cloud and populated with linguistic data including lexemes, senses, and translations. The project incorporates community outreach through workshops and tutorials while integrating with Wikibase services to enhance functionality.

## Key Results
- 1,030,825 triples of structured data
- Lexemes and sense descriptions across multiple Quechua dialects
- Support for English, German, and Spanish language descriptions
- Integration with Wikibase Cloud platform

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
QICHWABASE leverages the Wikibase platform's proven infrastructure for collaborative knowledge management, providing a structured framework for Quechua language data. The use of a standardized knowledge graph approach enables interoperability and facilitates community contributions. The multi-language support for sense descriptions expands accessibility beyond native Quechua speakers.
