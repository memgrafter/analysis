# Designing explainable artificial intelligence with active inference: A framework for transparent introspection and decision-making

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.04025
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04025
- Reference count: 40

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a theoretical framework for designing explainable artificial intelligence systems using active inference principles and hierarchical generative models. The core innovation is an architecture that enables AI systems to track and explain their own decision-making processes through explicit representation of causal structure and uncertainty. By integrating active inference with introspection mechanisms, attention systems, and natural language interfaces, the proposed framework addresses the "black box" problem in AI by providing transparent, auditable decision-making capabilities that can foster trust and collaboration between AI and human users.

## Method Summary
The proposed approach uses hierarchical generative models that explicitly encode causal relationships and uncertainty, combined with active inference principles for decision-making. The architecture implements a three-level generative model for introspection (overt actions, covert attention, meta-awareness) and integrates attention mechanisms that dynamically focus on relevant factors. Natural language interfaces translate internal states into human-understandable explanations. The system performs precision-weighted perceptual inference and policy selection based on expected free energy, while maintaining the ability to self-report its internal states and reasoning processes.

## Key Results
- Active inference's hierarchical generative models provide built-in explainability through explicit causal structure representation
- Three-level architecture enables self-modeling and introspection by implementing overt actions, covert attention, and meta-awareness layers
- Attention mechanisms dynamically focus on relevant factors in decision-making, enhancing the system's ability to provide targeted explanations

  1. Build hierarchical generative model with explicit causal structure
  2. Implement attention mechanisms for dynamic focus
  3. Develop introspection interface for self-reporting
  4. Test and validate explainability and introspection capabilities
- Design tradeoffs:
  - Complexity vs. Explainability: More complex models may be harder to explain
  - Real-time Performance vs. Detailed Explanations: More detailed explanations may impact real-time performance
  - Generalization vs. Specificity: More specific models may be less generalizable
- Failure signatures:
  - Inability to provide clear explanations for decisions
  - Performance degradation due to complex introspection mechanisms
  - Bias or unfairness in decision-making or explanations

  1. Test the hierarchical generative model with a simple decision-making task
