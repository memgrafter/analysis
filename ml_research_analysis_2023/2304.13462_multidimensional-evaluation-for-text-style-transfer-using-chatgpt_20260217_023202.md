# Multidimensional Evaluation for Text Style Transfer Using ChatGPT

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.13462
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13462
- Reference count: 14
- Key outcome: ChatGPT serves as a competitive multidimensional evaluator for text style transfer across style strength, content preservation, and fluency dimensions

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates the use of ChatGPT as a zero-shot evaluator for text style transfer (TST) tasks, comparing its performance to existing automatic metrics and human judgments. The study focuses on three evaluation dimensions—style strength, content preservation, and fluency—using a simple prompt template that requests separate evaluations for each dimension. Results show that ChatGPT achieves competitive correlations with human judgments, particularly excelling at the most challenging dataset-level evaluation. The study suggests ChatGPT has significant potential as a multidimensional evaluator for TST without requiring task-specific training.

## Method Summary
The study evaluates 80 source sentences (40 formal→informal, 40 informal→formal) with outputs from 8 systems plus references, using human judgments as ground truth. ChatGPT is prompted with task-specific instructions for each of the three evaluation dimensions using zero-shot prompting. The evaluation framework calculates correlations at three levels: system-level (pairwise accuracy), sample-level (sentence-level Kendall's Tau), and dataset-level (overall Kendall's Tau). The authors compare ChatGPT's performance against existing automatic metrics including BLEU, ROUGE, BERTScore, and others.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT achieves competitive correlations with human judgments compared to existing automatic metrics
- Simple prompt templates with separate requests for each dimension perform best
- ChatGPT particularly excels at dataset-level evaluation, outperforming other metrics in almost all cases
- The model shows strong performance across both transfer directions (informal-to-formal and formal-to-informal)

## Limitations
- The study's reliance on ChatGPT's zero-shot evaluation introduces uncertainty about consistency and potential refusal issues with certain content types
- ChatGPT cannot ensure evaluation consistency across sentences, which could impact reliability
- The evaluation is limited to formality transfer tasks only, limiting generalizability to other style transfer domains
