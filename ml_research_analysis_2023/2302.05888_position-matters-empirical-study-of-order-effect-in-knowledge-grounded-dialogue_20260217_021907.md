# Position Matters! Empirical Study of Order Effect in Knowledge-grounded Dialogue

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.05888
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05888
- Reference count: 9
- This paper investigates how the order of knowledge sentences affects autoregressive dialogue systems

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates the impact of knowledge sentence order on autoregressive dialogue systems' performance. The authors find that models exhibit imbalanced attention to input knowledge based on its position, leading to order effects in knowledge-grounded dialogue generation. To address this, they propose modifying position embeddings to treat each knowledge statement equally during training. The experimental results show that this approach reduces order effects while maintaining response quality, demonstrating that position embeddings can be adapted to mitigate position-based biases in dialogue systems.

## Method Summary
The authors conducted experiments on two dialogue datasets using GPT and GPT-2 models to study how position affects knowledge utilization in dialogue generation. They implemented a modified position embedding approach that aims to treat all knowledge statements equally regardless of their position in the input. The experimental design involved comparing standard position embeddings against their proposed modification across multiple model sizes and datasets, measuring both order effect reduction and response quality maintenance.

## Key Results
- Models exhibit imbalanced attention to input knowledge based on position, creating order effects
- Modified position embeddings can treat each knowledge statement equally during training
- The proposed method reduces order effects while maintaining response quality

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The proposed position embedding modification works by normalizing or adjusting the positional information such that the model cannot rely on position as a proxy for importance. By decoupling the semantic relevance of knowledge statements from their positional encoding, the model is forced to attend to content rather than position, thereby reducing positional bias in knowledge utilization.

**Critical Path**
Input knowledge sentences → Position encoding → Self-attention computation → Response token prediction

**Design Tradeoffs**
The modification trades positional information for uniform attention, potentially losing natural ordering cues but gaining fairness in knowledge consideration.

**Failure Signatures**
- Model overfits to position rather than content
- Knowledge statements at certain positions are systematically ignored
- Response quality degrades despite reduced order effects

**First Experiments**

2. Test response consistency when knowledge order is randomly permuted

## Limitations
- Results may not generalize to larger language models beyond GPT-2
- Limited exploration of whether uniform attention might override genuinely important contextual knowledge
- Evaluation focuses primarily on position effect reduction rather than comprehensive dialogue quality metrics

## Confidence
- High confidence in the empirical observation of position effects across tested models and datasets
- Medium confidence in the proposed position embedding solution's effectiveness within the tested scope
- Medium confidence in the claim that uniform attention improves knowledge-grounded dialogue generation

## Next Checks
1. Test the position embedding modification on larger language models (GPT-3, GPT-4) and additional dialogue datasets to assess scalability and generalizability
2. Conduct ablation studies to determine whether the uniform attention achieved might occasionally override genuinely important contextual knowledge, potentially degrading response quality
3. Evaluate the modified models in interactive dialogue settings where knowledge relevance may dynamically shift based on conversation flow, rather than static position-based ordering
