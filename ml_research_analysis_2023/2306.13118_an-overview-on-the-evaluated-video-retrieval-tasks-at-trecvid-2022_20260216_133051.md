# An overview on the evaluated video retrieval tasks at TRECVID 2022

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.13118
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13118
- Reference count: 40
- The top system in Ad-hoc Video Search (AVS) achieved a mean xInfAP of 0.179

## Executive Summary
This paper provides a comprehensive overview of the six video retrieval tasks evaluated at TRECVID 2022: Ad-hoc Video Search (AVS), Video to Text (VTT), Deep Video Understanding (DVU), Disaster Scene Description and Indexing (DSDI), Activities in Extended Video (ActEV), and Movie Summarization (MSUM). The evaluation involved multiple datasets including Vimeo Creative Commons Collection, KinoLorberEdu movies, MEVA, and LADI. Various performance metrics were employed, ranging from automatic metrics like xInfAP and CIDEr to human evaluation methods like Direct Assessment. The paper details the task definitions, datasets used, evaluation methodologies, and presents the results for each task, highlighting the challenges and performance trends observed.

## Method Summary
The TRECVID 2022 evaluation employed multiple video datasets and implemented a multi-faceted evaluation approach combining automatic metrics with human judgment. Systems submitted runs for each task, which were then pooled and judged according to task-specific protocols. Sampling strategies were used to estimate performance metrics like xInfAP without requiring exhaustive judgments. Human evaluation via Direct Assessment was employed for tasks like VTT where automatic metrics may be insufficient. The evaluation workflow included shot boundary definition, feature extraction, indexing, query processing, pooling of results, human judgment collection, and metric calculation.

## Key Results
- AVS task: Top system achieved xInfAP of 0.179 using a sampling strategy that pooled top 300 clips at 100% and beyond 300 at 25%
- VTT task: Top system achieved CIDEr score of 0.381 and DA score of 0.542
- DVU task: Top system achieved overall score of 0.256 on movie understanding
- DSDI task: Top system achieved MAP of 0.369 for disaster feature detection
- ActEV task: Top system achieved µnAUDC of 0.671 for human activity detection
- MSUM task: Top system achieved objective score of 0.133 and subjective score of 0.200

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** Retrieval performance in Ad-hoc Video Search (AVS) can be estimated reliably using inferred average precision (xInfAP) instead of requiring exhaustive human judgments.
- **Mechanism:** The xInfAP metric uses a sampling strategy that pools results across all runs at different rank depths (e.g., top 300 clips fully judged, 25% sampling beyond rank 300), allowing accurate performance estimation with fewer judgments.
- **Core assumption:** The sampled clips are representative of the full result set and that performance trends are preserved under sampling.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "The evaluation used mean extended inferred average precision (mean xinfAP), which permits sampling density to vary... This allowed the evaluation to be more sensitive to clips returned below the lowest rank ( ≈300) previously pooled and judged."
  - [section] "A workflow was adopted to pool results from runs submitted... For each query topic, a top pool was created using 100% of clips at ranks 1 to 300 across all submissions after removing duplicates. A second pool was created using a sampling rate at 25% of clips at ranks 301 to 1000..."
- **Break condition:** If the sampling strategy introduces significant bias (e.g., systematic exclusion of hard-to-retrieve clips), or if the distribution of relevant items changes drastically across different rank segments, xInfAP will no longer accurately estimate full-judgment performance.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** Novelty run submissions in AVS can identify unique relevant shots not found by other runs, and this can be quantified using a weighted uniqueness metric.
- **Mechanism:** Each relevant shot is assigned a weight inversely proportional to the number of runs retrieving it: weight = 1 - (N/M), where N is the number of runs retrieving the shot and M is the total number of runs. The novelty score is the mean of these weights over all relevant shots.
- **Core assumption:** Shots retrieved by fewer runs are inherently more unique and valuable, and the weighting scheme appropriately balances uniqueness and relevance.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "A Novelty run type was also allowed to be submitted within the main task. The goal of this run type is to encourage systems to submit novel and unique relevant shots not easily discovered by other runs."
  - [section] "A weight was given to each topic and shot pair such as follows: TopicX ShotYweight(x) = 1 − N/M where N is the number of times Shot Y was retrieved for topic X by any run submission, and M is the number of total runs submitted by all teams."
- **Break condition:** If the weighting overemphasizes extremely rare shots that are irrelevant or if multiple runs discover the same novel shot independently, the metric may not accurately reflect true novelty.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** Direct Assessment (DA) for video captioning provides reliable human evaluation by using large-scale ratings with quality control measures.
- **Mechanism:** AMT workers rate captions on a 0-100 scale after watching the video, with built-in quality control by inserting "polluted" captions (e.g., replacing a noun with an unrelated one) to identify low-quality raters.
- **Core assumption:** AMT workers can consistently judge caption quality, and the quality control measures effectively filter out unreliable ratings.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "Direct Assessment (DA), a method shown to produce highly reliable human evaluation results for MT and Natural Language Generation... Human assessors are presented with a video and a single caption. After watching the video, assessors rate how well the caption describes what took place in the video on a 0-100 rating scale."
  - [section] "Large numbers of ratings are collected for captions before ratings are combined into an overall average system rating... Quality control measures applied to filter out or downgrade the weightings from workers unable to demonstrate the ability to rate good captions higher than lower quality captions."
- **Break condition:** If AMT workers are not sufficiently motivated or trained, or if the quality control measures fail to detect subtle biases, DA scores may not accurately reflect true caption quality.

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map:** Data Ingestion (V3C, KinoLorberEdu, MEVA, LADI) -> Processing Pipeline (shot segmentation, feature extraction, indexing) -> Evaluation Framework (pooling, judging, metric calculation) -> Submission & Scoring (run submission, automatic scoring, human evaluation)
- **Critical path:** 1. Data preparation and shot boundary definition. 2. System run submission and pooling. 3. Human judgment collection and verification. 4. Metric calculation and result aggregation. 5. Result analysis and reporting.
- **Design tradeoffs:** Sampling vs. exhaustive judging: Balancing evaluation accuracy with resource constraints. Automatic vs. human evaluation: Combining metric-based scoring with human judgment for robustness. Dataset diversity vs. annotation cost: Choosing datasets that are representative but feasible to annotate.
- **Failure signatures:** Low agreement between human judges: Indicates ambiguous queries or poor shot boundary definitions. Large discrepancies between automatic and human metrics: Suggests limitations of automatic metrics or biases in human evaluation. Novelty runs not scoring high: May indicate that the novelty metric is not effectively capturing true novelty or that runs are not exploring sufficiently diverse retrieval strategies.
- **First 3 experiments:** 1. **Pool size sensitivity:** Vary the number of clips pooled at different rank depths and measure the impact on xInfAP accuracy. 2. **Human judgment agreement:** Calculate inter-annotator agreement for a subset of queries to assess the reliability of human judgments. 3. **Novelty metric validation:** Analyze the distribution of novelty scores across runs and assess whether high-scoring runs are indeed retrieving unique and relevant shots.
