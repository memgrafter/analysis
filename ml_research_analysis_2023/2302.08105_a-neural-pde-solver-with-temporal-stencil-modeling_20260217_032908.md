# A Neural PDE Solver with Temporal Stencil Modeling

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.08105
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.08105
- Reference count: 33
- Achieves state-of-the-art simulation accuracy for 2-D incompressible Navier-Stokes turbulent flows, outperforming previous methods by 19.9% in duration time and reducing inference latency by 80%

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces Temporal Stencil Modeling (TSM), a novel approach for solving time-dependent partial differential equations (PDEs) by leveraging temporal information from trajectories. TSM combines stencil learning with HiPPO-based time series modeling to recover lost details in low-resolution down-sampled features. The method generalizes classic finite volume solvers like WENO by predicting interpolation coefficients using abundant temporal information from previous trajectories. Experimental results demonstrate significant performance improvements over existing methods while maintaining strong generalization capabilities.

## Method Summary
TSM introduces a novel approach to neural PDE solving that combines stencil learning with temporal modeling. The method leverages HiPPO-based time series modeling to capture temporal dynamics while using stencil learning to predict interpolation coefficients. This integration allows TSM to recover fine details lost in low-resolution down-sampling of PDE solutions. The approach builds upon classic finite volume methods but extends them by incorporating learned temporal relationships from previous simulation trajectories, enabling more accurate and stable predictions.

## Key Results
- Achieves 19.9% improvement in duration time for 2-D incompressible Navier-Stokes turbulent flows
- Reduces inference latency by 80% compared to previous methods
- Demonstrates strong generalization to out-of-distribution turbulent flow settings and works well on 1-D Kuramoto-Sivashinsky equations and 3-D Navier-Stokes equations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
TSM works by integrating temporal information from previous simulation trajectories with spatial stencil learning. The HiPPO-based time series modeling captures long-term temporal dependencies that are crucial for accurate PDE solving, while the stencil learning component predicts interpolation coefficients that determine how spatial information is combined. This dual approach allows TSM to recover fine details that would otherwise be lost in down-sampling operations, leading to more accurate and stable simulations over extended time periods.

## Architecture Onboarding

Component map: Input features -> Temporal modeling (HiPPO) -> Stencil prediction -> Interpolation coefficients -> Output solution

Critical path: The core computational pipeline flows from input features through temporal modeling to stencil prediction and finally to solution generation. The HiPPO layer is critical for capturing long-term dependencies, while the stencil prediction layer determines the spatial accuracy of the solution.

Design tradeoffs: TSM trades increased model complexity and training time for improved inference speed and solution accuracy. The integration of temporal modeling adds computational overhead during training but enables faster inference and better long-term stability.

Failure signatures: Potential failure modes include temporal instability when HiPPO parameters are not properly tuned, spatial inaccuracies when stencil predictions diverge from true interpolation coefficients, and overall solution divergence in highly turbulent regimes where temporal correlations become too complex to capture.
