# Spawrious: A Benchmark for Fine Control of Spurious Correlation Biases

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.05470
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.05470
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: State-of-the-art group robustness methods achieve below 60% accuracy on hardest splits of Spawrious benchmark

## Executive Summary
Spawrious is a new benchmark designed to evaluate spurious correlation biases in image classification. It introduces both one-to-one and many-to-many spurious correlation challenges with varying difficulty levels, containing 152,000 synthetic images of four dog breeds with diverse backgrounds. The benchmark reveals that current state-of-the-art group robustness methods struggle significantly with the hardest splits, with none achieving over 60% accuracy, indicating that Spawrious presents a substantial challenge for driving future research in addressing spurious correlation biases.

## Method Summary
The Spawrious benchmark is constructed using a pipeline that combines text-to-image generation with image captioning-based filtering. Text-to-image models (Stable Diffusion v1.4) generate photo-realistic synthetic images, while image-to-text captioning models (ViT-GPT2) filter out unsuitable samples by checking for class-relevant keywords. The dataset contains two types of spurious correlations (O2O and M2M) across three difficulty levels, creating 152,064 total images. The benchmark evaluates methods using worst-group accuracy across these challenging scenarios.

## Key Results
- Current state-of-the-art group robustness methods achieve below 60% accuracy on the hardest Spawrious splits
- All methods perform significantly worse on many-to-many (M2M) spurious correlations compared to one-to-one (O2O) correlations
- Larger model architectures (ResNet50, ViT-B/16) consistently underperform ResNet18 despite hyperparameter tuning

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim:** The dataset construction pipeline ensures photo-realistic images while filtering out low-quality samples, maintaining high fidelity backgrounds necessary for spurious correlation evaluation.
- **Mechanism:** Text-to-image generation via Stable Diffusion v1.4 produces realistic synthetic images, while image-to-text captioning models (ViT-GPT2) filter out unsuitable images by checking for presence of class-relevant keywords like "dog" in generated captions.
- **Core assumption:** The combination of text-to-image generation and captioning-based filtering can produce high-quality images with diverse, complex backgrounds while removing samples lacking class objects.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "To create this dataset, we employ a text-to-image model to generate photo-realistic images and an image captioning model to filter out unsuitable ones."
  - [section] "We pass the raw, generated images to an image-to-text (I2T) model to extract a concise description; here, we use the ViT-GPT2 image captioning model [55]."

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim:** The two types of spurious correlations (O2O and M2M) present qualitatively different challenges that cannot be reduced to each other.
- **Mechanism:** O2O-SC involves one background correlating with one class, while M2M-SC involves groups of backgrounds correlating with groups of classes. The data-generating processes for each are fundamentally different, creating distinct spurious structures.
- **Core assumption:** M2M-SC is not an aggregation of O2O-SC and contains richer spurious structures that require different mitigation strategies.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "Previous SC benchmark datasets suffer from varying issues, e.g., over-saturation or only containing one-to-one (O2O) SCs, but no many-to-many (M2M) SCs arising between groups of spurious attributes and classes."
  - [section] "We believe that M2M SCs are an important generalization failure mode currently overlooked in current benchmarks. We emphasize that M2M-SCs are not an aggregation of O2O-SCs and cannot be expressed or decomposed in the form of the latter."

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim:** The difficulty progression (Easy → Medium → Hard) creates increasingly challenging spurious correlation scenarios that reveal limitations of current methods.
- **Mechanism:** The dataset contains three difficulty levels with different background-class correlation patterns. The Hard splits create scenarios where spurious correlations are stronger and more complex, making them harder for current methods to disentangle.
- **Core assumption:** Different difficulty levels can be constructed by varying the strength and complexity of spurious correlations while maintaining class balance.
- **Evidence anchors:**
  - [abstract] "The dataset contains 152,000 synthetic images of four dog breeds with diverse backgrounds, generated using text-to-image models and filtered using image captioning models."
  - [section] "We construct two datasets for each SC type with 3168 images per background-class combination, thus 2 SC types × 4 environments × 6 difficulties × 3168 = 152,064 images in total."

## Foundational Learning

- **Concept:** Spurious correlation and its distinction from genuine causal features
  - Why needed here: Understanding the difference between spurious correlations and true predictive features is essential for designing methods to mitigate spurious correlations and for interpreting benchmark results
  - Quick check question: Can you explain why a classifier might learn to associate camels with desert backgrounds even though the background is not causally related to the camel class?

- **Concept:** Domain generalization and its relationship to spurious correlation
  - Why needed here: The benchmark evaluates performance across different training and test environments, requiring understanding of how domain shifts affect spurious correlation learning
  - Quick check question: How does the presence of multiple training environments with different spurious correlation patterns help evaluate a method's ability to learn invariant features?

- **Concept:** Text-to-image generation and image captioning models
  - Why needed here: The dataset construction relies on these technologies, so understanding their capabilities and limitations is crucial for interpreting the benchmark
  - Quick check question: What are the potential failure modes of using image captioning models to filter generated images, and how might these affect dataset quality?

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map:** Text-to-image generation (Stable Diffusion) -> Image-to-text filtering (ViT-GPT2) -> Benchmark evaluation framework -> Group robustness methods
- **Critical path:** Data generation → Data cleaning → Benchmark evaluation → Method comparison
- **Design tradeoffs:** High-quality synthetic data vs. computational cost of generation; balanced classes vs. realistic spurious correlations; multiple difficulty levels vs. dataset size
- **Failure signatures:** Methods failing on Hard splits with accuracy below 60%; ERM performing significantly worse than specialized methods; no improvement when scaling model architecture

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
- Question: Why does scaling up model architectures (ResNet50, ViT-B/16) consistently worsen performance on Spawrious, despite tuning hyperparameters for the new architectures?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] Table 4 shows that ResNet50 and ViT-B/16 generally underperform compared to ResNet18 across different methods
- Why unresolved: The paper notes this surprising result but does not provide a clear explanation for why larger models perform worse
- What evidence would resolve it: Systematic ablation studies comparing different architectural components (depth, width, attention mechanisms) and their impact on spurious correlation robustness

### Open Question 2
- Question: What specific properties of many-to-many (M2M) spurious correlations make them significantly more challenging than one-to-one (O2O) spurious correlations for current robustness methods?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] The paper states "M2M-SCs are not an aggregation of O2O-SCs and cannot be expressed or decomposed in the form of the latter" and shows M2M-Hard splits are notably harder
- Why unresolved: While the paper identifies M2M as qualitatively different, it doesn't specify the exact mechanisms that make them harder to learn
- What evidence would resolve it: Detailed analysis of model representations and decision boundaries for O2O vs M2M cases to identify what additional complexity M2M introduces

### Open Question 3
- Question: Why do CORAL and CausIRL perform similarly despite having fundamentally different robustness regularization approaches?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] The paper notes "CORAL [64], CausIRL [7] perform about the same, despite much different robustness regularization"
- Why unresolved: The paper states this is "left for future work" and provides no explanation for this unexpected result
- What evidence would resolve it: Comparative analysis of how each method affects the learned feature distributions and whether they converge to similar solutions despite different optimization objectives

## Limitations
- The benchmark relies entirely on synthetic data, which may not fully capture real-world spurious correlation complexity
- The filtering pipeline using image captioning models may introduce bias or miss subtle spurious features
- The claim that M2M-SC is fundamentally different from O2O-SC is empirically supported but lacks theoretical justification

## Confidence
- **High Confidence**: Benchmark construction methodology and dataset statistics are well-documented and reproducible; performance on Hard splits is directly measurable
- **Medium Confidence**: The assertion that M2M-SC presents fundamentally different challenges than O2O-SC is supported by performance differences but needs additional theoretical analysis
- **Low Confidence**: Generalization of findings from synthetic images to real-world scenarios remains uncertain due to potential artifacts in text-to-image generation

## Next Checks
1. Apply the same group robustness methods to real-world datasets with known spurious correlations (like Waterbirds or CelebA) to assess whether performance trends on Spawrious generalize to natural images
2. Remove the image captioning filtering step and retrain methods on unfiltered generated images to quantify how much the filtering pipeline contributes to benchmark difficulty and quality
3. Design controlled experiments that systematically decompose M2M-SC scenarios into O2O components to empirically test whether the claimed fundamental difference between these correlation types holds under varying conditions
