# Sound Design Strategies for Latent Audio Space Explorations Using Deep Learning Architectures

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.15571
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15571
- Reference count: 0
- This paper proposes three strategies for exploring latent audio spaces generated by variational autoencoders (VAEs) applied to raw audio data.

## Executive Summary
This paper presents three strategies for navigating latent audio spaces created by variational autoencoders trained on raw audio data. The work focuses on enabling musically meaningful interpolation between audio inputs through a lightweight VAE architecture that supports real-time synthesis. Developed during an artistic residency focused on live coding performances, the strategies include stepwise interpolation, dynamic interpolation over concatenated audio, and extended duration audio generation. The paper aims to bridge technical audio synthesis approaches with artistic practice by providing algorithmic methods for traversing latent audio spaces in real-time performance contexts.

## Method Summary
The paper proposes a lightweight raw audio VAE architecture enabling real-time synthesis for live coding applications. Three interpolation strategies are developed: stepwise interpolation between audio points, dynamic interpolation over longer concatenated sequences, and extended duration generation through repeated latent space sampling. The architecture and strategies were developed through artistic practice rather than formal experimental methodology, focusing on practical usability in live performance contexts.

## Key Results
- Three distinct strategies for musically meaningful navigation of latent audio spaces
- Development of a lightweight VAE architecture supporting real-time audio synthesis
- Successful integration of latent space exploration into live coding performance contexts
- Demonstration of practical approaches for interpolating between audio inputs in learned latent representations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The strategies work by leveraging the learned manifold structure of audio representations in latent space. The VAE learns to compress audio waveforms into a lower-dimensional latent space where similar-sounding audio clusters together. By interpolating linearly between latent vectors representing different audio inputs, the model generates intermediate audio that smoothly transitions between the source sounds. The lightweight architecture trades some representation fidelity for computational efficiency, enabling real-time synthesis necessary for live coding applications.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map**: Raw Audio -> Encoder -> Latent Vector -> Decoder -> Synthesized Audio

**Critical Path**: The encoder processes raw audio waveforms through convolutional layers to produce a mean and variance vector in latent space. The decoder reconstructs audio from sampled latent vectors through transposed convolutional layers. The interpolation strategies operate on the latent vectors before decoding.

**Design Tradeoffs**: Lightweight architecture prioritizes real-time performance over maximum audio fidelity. Linear interpolation assumes latent space relationships are well-behaved. Artistic residency context prioritizes musical utility over technical optimization.

**Failure Signatures**: Audio artifacts at interpolation boundaries, loss of temporal coherence in extended generation, computational bottlenecks preventing real-time synthesis, and non-musical intermediate sounds during interpolation.

3. Evaluate extended duration generation for continuity and artifact introduction
