# Hierarchical Diffusion Autoencoders and Disentangled Image Manipulation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.11829
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11829
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: HDAE achieves 0.793 LPIPS on face reconstruction versus DAE's 0.073, with superior disentanglement and editing capabilities

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces Hierarchical Diffusion Autoencoders (HDAE) that create a multi-level semantic latent space for diffusion models by extracting features at different scales from the semantic encoder. The hierarchical approach captures fine-to-abstract and low-to-high level semantics, providing richer representations compared to prior Diffusion Autoencoders (DAE). The method enables applications including style mixing, controllable interpolation, and multi-modal semantic synthesis. To address attribute entanglement, the authors propose a truncated-feature-based approach for disentangled image manipulation. Experimental results show HDAE(U) achieves superior reconstruction quality with 0.793 LPIPS on face reconstruction, outperforming DAE (0.073) while preserving details. Human perceptual studies confirm HDAE's superior image manipulation quality and better disentanglement compared to baseline methods.

## Method Summary
HDAE builds a hierarchical latent space by extracting multi-scale features from the semantic encoder, creating a pyramid of representations from fine-grained to abstract semantics. The architecture maintains separate diffusion processes at each hierarchical level, allowing controlled manipulation at different semantic granularities. The disentanglement approach uses truncated features to isolate specific attributes during manipulation, reducing unwanted correlations between different image properties. The model is trained end-to-end with a reconstruction objective and diffusion-based prior, enabling both high-fidelity image synthesis and controllable editing through the hierarchical latent space.

## Key Results
- HDAE(U) achieves 0.793 LPIPS on face reconstruction versus DAE's 0.073
- Human perceptual studies confirm superior disentanglement quality compared to baseline methods
- Enables style mixing, controllable interpolation, and multi-modal semantic synthesis through hierarchical latent space

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The hierarchical design works by decomposing image semantics into multiple levels, each capturing different scales of information from fine details to high-level concepts. This multi-scale representation allows more precise control over different aspects of the image during manipulation. The truncated-feature approach isolates specific attribute information by removing correlated features from other levels, effectively reducing entanglement between different image properties.

- **Feature Truncation**: Methods for removing specific feature components; needed for the disentanglement approach
- **Perceptual Quality Metrics**: LPIPS and human studies for evaluating reconstruction and manipulation quality; needed for quantitative and qualitative assessment

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map**: Input Image -> Semantic Encoder -> Multi-level Feature Extraction -> Hierarchical Latent Space -> Diffusion Processes (per level) -> Reconstruction

**Critical Path**: Semantic Encoder → Multi-scale Feature Extraction → Hierarchical Latent Space → Diffusion Processes → Reconstruction Decoder

**Design Tradeoffs**: The hierarchical approach increases model complexity and computational cost but provides richer semantic representations and better disentanglement compared to single-level approaches.

**Failure Signatures**: Poor reconstruction quality when hierarchical levels are not properly balanced; disentanglement failures when feature truncation removes too much or too little information; training instability when diffusion processes at different levels are not synchronized.

**3 First Experiments**: 1) Ablation study on number of hierarchical levels and their impact on reconstruction quality, 2) Comparison of different feature truncation strategies for disentanglement, 3) Evaluation of style mixing quality across different hierarchical levels.

## Limitations

- No ablation studies on impact of different hierarchical levels
