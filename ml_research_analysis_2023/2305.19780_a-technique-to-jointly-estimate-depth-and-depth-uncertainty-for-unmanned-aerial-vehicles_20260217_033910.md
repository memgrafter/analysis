# A technique to jointly estimate depth and depth uncertainty for unmanned aerial vehicles

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.19780
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.19780
- Reference count: 32
- A technique to jointly estimate depth and depth uncertainty for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) applications

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a method to jointly estimate depth and its uncertainty for UAV applications using M4Depth, a state-of-the-art depth estimation network. The key innovation is a novel approach to convert parallax-related uncertainty estimates into depth-related uncertainty estimates, outperforming standard probabilistic methods. The proposed method demonstrates consistent performance across various datasets, including zero-shot transfer scenarios, while being 2.5 times faster and causal compared to other multi-view depth estimation methods.

## Method Summary
The method adapts M4Depth, a state-of-the-art depth estimation network, to infer uncertainty estimates alongside depth. A novel approach is introduced to convert uncertainty estimates related to parallax into uncertainty estimates related to depth. This conversion technique outperforms the standard probabilistic approach in uncertainty estimation. The method is designed to be causal and faster than existing multi-view depth estimation methods while maintaining competitive performance on benchmark datasets.

## Key Results
- Achieves 2.5x faster inference compared to other multi-view depth estimation methods
- Maintains causal processing while achieving similar performance to non-causal methods
- Demonstrates consistent performance in zero-shot transfer scenarios across multiple datasets

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by leveraging M4Depth's ability to generate parallax-related uncertainty estimates and converting them into depth-related uncertainty estimates. This conversion process is more effective than standard probabilistic approaches for uncertainty quantification in depth estimation. The causal nature of the method allows for real-time applications in UAV scenarios where future frames are not available.

Critical path:
Input frames -> M4Depth feature extraction -> Parallax computation -> Uncertainty conversion -> Depth and uncertainty prediction

Design tradeoffs:
- Speed vs accuracy: Causal processing sacrifices some accuracy for real-time performance
- Generalization vs specialization: Zero-shot transfer capability versus fine-tuned performance on specific datasets

Failure signatures:
- Inconsistent uncertainty estimates in textureless regions
- Propagation of parallax estimation errors to depth uncertainty
- Reduced accuracy in highly dynamic scenes with moving objects
