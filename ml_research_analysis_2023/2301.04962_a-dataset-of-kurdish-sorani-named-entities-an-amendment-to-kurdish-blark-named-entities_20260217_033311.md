# A Dataset of Kurdish (Sorani) Named Entities -- An Amendment to Kurdish-BLARK Named Entities

## Executive Summary
This work addresses the critical shortage of Named Entity Recognition (NER) resources for the Kurdish (Sorani) language by creating a comprehensive dataset of 33,261 named entity entries spanning 11 categories including persons, locations, villages, caves, mountains, rivers, universities, institutes, companies, ministries, and schools. The dataset was collected from authoritative sources such as government ministries, chambers of commerce, books, and online resources, then carefully preprocessed to remove noise, punctuation, duplicates, and transliterate Latin script entries into Persian-Arabic script. This dataset represents a significant expansion of the Kurdish BLARK (Basic Language Resource Kit) and is made publicly available under a CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license for non-commercial use.

## Method Summary
The authors collected named entity data from multiple authoritative sources including the Ministry of Higher Education of Kurdistan Regional Government, chambers of commerce in Erbil and Duhok, published books, and various online resources. The raw data underwent extensive preprocessing including noise removal, punctuation cleaning, duplicate elimination, and transliteration of Latin script entries to Persian-Arabic script. The resulting dataset was categorized into 11 entity types and organized for public distribution through GitHub under a non-commercial Creative Commons license.

## Key Results

## Limitations
- Limited evaluation of dataset effectiveness for actual NER tasks
- Potential bias toward formal entities from official sources, underrepresenting colloquial or informal named entities
- Lack of quantitative analysis of entity distribution across categories and geographical/temporal coverage
