# Is Attentional Channel Processing Design Required? Comprehensive Analysis Of Robustness Between Vision Transformers And Fully Attentional Networks

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.05495
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05495
- Reference count: 0
- Primary result: FAN-base-ViT demonstrates greater robustness against adversarial attacks compared to traditional ViTs

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a comprehensive study comparing the robustness of fully attentional networks (FAN) Vision Transformers (ViT) with traditional ViTs using various white-box and black-box attacks on the ImageNet dataset. The authors evaluated six FAN-ViT models and one traditional ViT model, employing attacks such as FGSM, MiM, PGD, and BIM. The results show that FAN-base-ViT outperforms other FAN models and demonstrates greater robustness against adversarial attacks compared to traditional ViTs. Additionally, the study highlights that FAN models exhibit lower transferability of adversarial examples to traditional ViTs, indicating better resistance to black-box attacks.

## Method Summary
The study evaluates baseline DeiT-S ViT and six FAN-ViT models (tiny/small/base, hybrid variants) against FGSM, MiM, PGD (1/2/5/10 steps), and BIM attacks. The evaluation uses a subset of the ImageNet validation set (1k images for attack evaluation, 100 images for black-box transferability analysis). Key metrics include attack success rate, L2 and L∞ distortion, and transferability rate. The study compares white-box attack effectiveness and black-box transferability between FAN and traditional ViT architectures.

## Key Results
- FAN-base-ViT outperforms other FAN models and traditional ViTs in adversarial robustness
- FAN models exhibit lower transferability of adversarial examples to traditional ViTs
- The attentional channel processing design in FAN-ViTs enhances robustness against adversarial perturbations

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: The attentional channel processing design in FAN-ViT models enhances their robustness against adversarial attacks.
- Mechanism: FAN models incorporate an attentional channel that selectively amplifies or suppresses feature representations based on learned importance weights, which makes the model less sensitive to small adversarial perturbations that target individual patches or tokens.
- Core assumption: The attentional channel's selective emphasis on robust feature patterns improves model invariance to noise.

- Break condition: If the attentional channel fails to learn meaningful importance weights, the robustness benefit diminishes.

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: FAN-base-ViT outperforms other FAN models and traditional ViTs in terms of robustness against adversarial attacks.
- Mechanism: FAN-base-ViT has a higher number of parameters and deeper architecture compared to FAN-tiny-ViT and FAN-small-ViT, allowing it to learn more complex and robust feature representations that are less susceptible to adversarial perturbations.
- Core assumption: Increased model capacity correlates with improved robustness against adversarial attacks.
- Evidence anchors:

- Break condition: If the increased model capacity leads to overfitting or memorization of adversarial patterns, the robustness benefit may be negated.

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: FAN models exhibit lower transferability of adversarial examples to traditional ViTs, indicating better resistance to black-box attacks.
- Mechanism: The attentional channel processing design in FAN models creates a feature representation space that is less aligned with the feature space of traditional ViTs, making it harder for adversarial examples crafted for traditional ViTs to transfer effectively to FAN models.
- Core assumption: The dissimilarity in feature representation spaces between FAN and traditional ViTs reduces the transferability of adversarial examples.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "the study highlights that FAN models exhibit lower transferability of adversarial examples to traditional ViTs, indicating better resistance to black-box attacks."

- Incomplete specification of FAN-ViT architectural details and training procedures
