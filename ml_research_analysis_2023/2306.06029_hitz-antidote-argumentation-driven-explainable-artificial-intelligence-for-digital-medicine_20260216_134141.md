# HiTZ@Antidote: Argumentation-driven Explainable Artificial Intelligence for Digital Medicine

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.06029
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06029
- Reference count: 28
- Primary result: ANTIDOTE combines deep learning with human argumentation to create explainable AI for medical diagnosis

## Executive Summary
The ANTIDOTE project addresses the critical challenge of explainable AI in medical diagnosis by integrating deep learning predictions with human argumentation schemes. The approach combines low-level neural network behavior with high-level argumentative structures to create explanations that are both technically grounded and medically meaningful. The project develops multitask learning models for diagnosis prediction while generating argumentative justifications, evaluated through medical student feedback on explanation quality.

## Method Summary
ANTIDOTE employs a cross-disciplinary approach combining deep learning, argumentation, and interactivity to create explainable AI for medical diagnosis. The method involves developing multitask learning models that predict diagnoses while simultaneously generating argumentative explanations. The project leverages cross-lingual knowledge transfer from English to Spanish medical argumentation datasets and implements an interactive dialogue system where medical students can ask follow-up questions to refine explanations. The Antidote CasiMedicos dataset provides clinical cases with differential diagnoses and explanations for both correct and incorrect answers.

## Key Results
- Development of the Antidote CasiMedicos dataset containing clinical cases with differential diagnoses and explanations
- Implementation of multitask learning models for diagnosis prediction and justification generation
- Exploration of crosslingual knowledge transfer for argumentation in the medical domain
