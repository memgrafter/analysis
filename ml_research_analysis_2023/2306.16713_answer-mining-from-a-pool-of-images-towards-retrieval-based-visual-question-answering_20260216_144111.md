# Answer Mining from a Pool of Images: Towards Retrieval-Based Visual Question Answering

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.16713
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16713
- Reference count: 18
- Key outcome: Introduces MI-BART framework and RETVQA dataset, achieving 76.5% accuracy and 79.3% fluency on RETVQA

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the problem of retrieval-based visual question answering (RETVQA) by proposing a unified Multi Image BART (MI-BART) framework. The approach combines a relevance encoder for retrieving relevant images from a large pool with a sequence-to-sequence transformer that jointly reasons over the retrieved multi-image context and the question to generate free-form natural language answers. The authors introduce the RETVQA dataset, the largest of its kind, containing 327K questions across various categories that require retrieval and reasoning over multiple images.

## Method Summary
The MI-BART framework leverages a two-stage approach: first, a relevance encoder retrieves images from a large pool based on the question; second, a sequence-to-sequence transformer jointly processes the retrieved images and the question to generate accurate, fluent answers. The method is trained end-to-end and achieves state-of-the-art performance on the newly introduced RETVQA dataset. The framework demonstrates superior performance on both the image segment of the WebQA dataset and the comprehensive RETVQA benchmark.

## Key Results
- Achieves 76.5% accuracy and 79.3% fluency on the RETVQA dataset
- Outperforms previous methods on the image segment of the WebQA dataset
- Introduces the largest RETVQA dataset with 327K questions across diverse categories

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The MI-BART framework succeeds by effectively combining image retrieval with multi-modal reasoning. The relevance encoder identifies contextually appropriate images from a large pool, while the sequence-to-sequence transformer leverages this retrieved context along with the question to generate comprehensive answers. This joint reasoning approach allows the model to synthesize information across multiple images and textual queries, enabling more accurate and contextually appropriate responses.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: Question -> Relevance Encoder -> Retrieved Images -> MI-BART Transformer -> Answer
- **Critical path**: The relevance encoder must accurately retrieve relevant images for the MI-BART transformer to generate correct answers
- **Design tradeoffs**: Balancing retrieval quality with generation accuracy; trade-off between computational efficiency and retrieval comprehensiveness
- **Failure signatures**: Poor retrieval quality leads to incorrect answers; insufficient context from retrieved images limits answer accuracy
- **First experiments**: 1) Ablation study removing relevance encoder to test standalone generation performance; 2) Retrieval quality analysis with precision@k metrics; 3) Human evaluation of answer fluency and accuracy
