# Are Chatbots Ready for Privacy-Sensitive Applications? An Investigation into Input Regurgitation and Prompt-Induced Sanitization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.15008
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15008
- Reference count: 12
- Primary result: ChatGPT retains PII verbatim in 57.4% of cases without sanitization, reduced to 15.2% with detailed sanitization prompts

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates privacy risks in large language models (LLMs) when used in sensitive applications like healthcare and hiring. The authors identify "input regurgitation" as a critical vulnerability where models reproduce sensitive Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and Protected Health Information (PHI) in their outputs. They propose a novel approach called "prompt-induced sanitization" that leverages explicit instructions to guide models toward compliance with privacy regulations such as HIPAA and GDPR. Through systematic experiments on synthetic datasets, they demonstrate that carefully crafted prompts can significantly reduce information leakage, though some risk remains.

## Method Summary
The authors created synthetic medical and hiring datasets containing controlled instances of PII and PHI, including names, addresses, medical conditions, and employment details. They developed a prompt engineering methodology that progressively enhanced sanitization instructions, starting with basic regulatory compliance statements and advancing to detailed step-by-step procedures for handling sensitive information. The experimental design compared ChatGPT's responses across different prompt formulations, measuring verbatim retention of sensitive information. The study also analyzed demographic subgroup patterns in information retention and open-sourced the datasets for reproducibility and further research.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT retained PII verbatim in 57.4% of cases without sanitization prompts
- Basic regulatory compliance prompts reduced verbatim retention to 30.5%
- Detailed step-by-step sanitization instructions achieved a 15.2% retention rate
- Non-binary individuals experienced lower PII copying rates compared to male or female counterparts

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism relies on the model's ability to follow explicit instructions when properly framed. By incorporating specific regulatory language and procedural guidance into prompts, the model appears to activate different response patterns that prioritize privacy compliance. The step-by-step instructions likely create a cognitive scaffold that helps the model systematically identify and redact sensitive information rather than simply responding to the immediate task. This suggests that LLMs can be steered toward safer behavior through careful prompt engineering, though the effect size indicates this is mitigation rather than elimination of the risk.

## Architecture Onboarding

Component map: Synthetic Dataset Creation -> Prompt Formulation -> Model Response Generation -> Privacy Risk Assessment -> Demographic Analysis

Critical path: The core experimental pipeline flows from dataset creation through prompt engineering to response evaluation, with the prompt formulation stage being the intervention point where privacy outcomes are most directly influenced.

Design tradeoffs: The authors chose synthetic datasets over real-world data to maintain control and avoid actual privacy violations, accepting the limitation of reduced external validity. They prioritized prompt simplicity and general applicability over highly specialized instructions that might only work in narrow contexts.

Failure signatures: High verbatim retention rates indicate prompt engineering failure, while demographic disparities suggest potential bias in the model's handling of different identity groups. Complete sanitization with loss of response utility would indicate over-constraining.

## Limitations
- Synthetic datasets may not capture the full complexity and variability of real-world privacy-sensitive interactions
- Experiments conducted with a single model version (ChatGPT), limiting generalizability to other LLMs or future updates
- Evaluation framework focuses primarily on verbatim PII retention, potentially missing more subtle forms of information leakage through paraphrasing or inference
