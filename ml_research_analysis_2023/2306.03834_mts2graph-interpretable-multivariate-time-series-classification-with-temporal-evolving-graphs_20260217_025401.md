# MTS2Graph: Interpretable Multivariate Time Series Classification with Temporal Evolving Graphs

## Quick Facts

## Executive Summary
MTS2Graph introduces an interpretable framework for multivariate time series (MTS) classification that bridges the gap between deep learning performance and model transparency. The method extracts highly activated temporal patterns from CNN layers, clusters them using K-shape to identify meaningful motifs, and constructs a temporal evolution graph that captures relationships between these patterns. By applying graph embedding techniques, MTS2Graph generates representations that maintain both high classification accuracy and interpretability, allowing practitioners to understand model decisions through the lens of temporal pattern evolution.

## Method Summary
MTS2Graph operates through a multi-stage process beginning with CNN-based feature extraction from multivariate time series data. The method identifies Multivariate Highly Activated Periods (MHAPs) from intermediate CNN layers, representing significant temporal patterns. These MHAPs undergo K-shape clustering to group similar temporal patterns while preserving shape information. A temporal evolution graph is then constructed where nodes represent clustered patterns and edges encode temporal relationships between consecutive patterns. Finally, graph embedding techniques transform this structured representation into feature vectors suitable for classification, maintaining the interpretability of the original temporal relationships.

## Key Results
- Achieved classification accuracy of 0.930 on UWave dataset
- Reached 0.990 accuracy on ArabicDigits dataset
- Demonstrated strong performance on both small and large dimensional MTS data, particularly excelling on ECG and Libras datasets

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The framework's effectiveness stems from its ability to extract semantically meaningful temporal patterns that CNNs naturally identify during feature learning, then explicitly model their temporal evolution relationships. By clustering similar patterns and preserving their temporal ordering through graph construction, MTS2Graph creates representations that capture both the local discriminative features and their global temporal context, leading to improved classification while maintaining interpretability through the graph structure.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component Map**: Raw MTS Data -> CNN Feature Extraction -> MHAP Identification -> K-shape Clustering -> Temporal Evolution Graph Construction -> Graph Embedding -> Classification
- **Critical Path**: CNN layers must successfully extract meaningful features, K-shape clustering must produce coherent pattern groups, and graph construction must accurately capture temporal relationships
- **Design Tradeoffs**: The method balances between computational efficiency (K-shape clustering) and pattern quality, and between graph complexity and interpretability
- **Failure Signatures**: Poor CNN feature extraction leads to meaningless MHAPs; inadequate clustering produces noisy graph nodes; incorrect temporal relationships create misleading interpretations
- **First 3 Experiments**: 1) Test CNN feature quality on synthetic MTS data with known patterns, 2) Evaluate K-shape clustering performance on benchmark time series, 3) Validate temporal graph construction on sequences with clear temporal dependencies

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
The paper acknowledges that interpretability assessment remains qualitative in nature, and the computational cost of K-shape clustering may become prohibitive for very large datasets. The framework's performance on datasets with irregular sampling patterns and extremely high-dimensional MTS data requires further investigation.

## Limitations
- May struggle with very high-dimensional MTS data (50+ dimensions) due to computational complexity
- Assumes consistent temporal structure, potentially limiting effectiveness on irregularly sampled data
- K-shape clustering computational cost could become prohibitive on large datasets
