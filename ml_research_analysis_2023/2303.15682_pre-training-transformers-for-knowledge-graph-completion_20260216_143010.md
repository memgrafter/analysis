# Pre-training Transformers for Knowledge Graph Completion

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2303.15682
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15682
- **Reference count**: 20
- **Primary result**: Achieved new state-of-the-art results on matched evaluations, with >25% relative improvement in mean reciprocal rank over previous SOTA models

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces iHT, a novel inductive KG representation model for knowledge graph completion that leverages large-scale pre-training on Wikidata5M. The model combines an entity encoder (e.g., BERT) with a neighbor-aware relational scoring function, both parameterized by Transformers. The key innovation is demonstrating that pre-trained KG representations can be effectively transferred to downstream KGs with entity and relational shifts, significantly improving performance on FB15K-237 and WN18RR while achieving new state-of-the-art results.

## Method Summary
The iHT model introduces a novel inductive approach to knowledge graph completion through large-scale pre-training. It combines two Transformer-based components: an entity encoder that processes entity descriptions and attributes, and a neighbor-aware relational scoring function that captures local graph structure. The model is pre-trained on the massive Wikidata5M dataset and then fine-tuned on downstream knowledge graphs. This approach enables the model to handle entity and relational shifts between pre-training and fine-tuning tasks, achieving significant improvements over previous state-of-the-art methods while maintaining strong inductive capabilities.

## Key Results
- Achieved new state-of-the-art results on matched evaluations with >25% relative improvement in mean reciprocal rank
- Demonstrated successful transfer of pre-trained representations to FB15K-237 and WN18RR with significant performance gains
- Showed effectiveness in handling both entity and relational shifts between pre-training and downstream tasks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The model's success stems from its dual-Transformer architecture that captures both entity semantics (through the entity encoder) and relational patterns in the graph structure (through the neighbor-aware scoring function). By pre-training on the large-scale Wikidata5M, the model learns rich, transferable representations that generalize well to smaller, different KGs. The neighbor-aware component allows the model to effectively leverage local graph structure during inference, while the pre-training process enables it to handle unseen entities and relations during transfer.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: Entity Encoder (BERT) -> Neighbor-aware Relational Scoring Function -> Triple Scoring
- **Critical path**: Entity descriptions → Entity Encoder → Entity embeddings → Neighbor aggregation → Relational scoring → Triple score prediction
- **Design tradeoffs**: The model trades computational efficiency for expressiveness by using attention mechanisms over entity neighbors, which increases memory usage but captures richer structural information.
- **Failure signatures**: Poor performance on entities with sparse neighborhoods, degradation when relational patterns differ significantly between pre-training and fine-tuning KGs, and computational bottlenecks with high-degree entities.
- **First experiments**: 1) Ablation study removing neighbor-aware component, 2) Testing on entities with varying neighborhood sizes, 3) Evaluating transfer performance with different pre-training dataset sizes

## Limitations
- High computational cost and memory requirements during both pre-training and inference, particularly with attention mechanisms over entity neighbors
- Limited evaluation on transferability across diverse knowledge graphs beyond FB15K-237 and WN18RR
- Lack of detailed ablation studies on architectural components and hyperparameter sensitivity
