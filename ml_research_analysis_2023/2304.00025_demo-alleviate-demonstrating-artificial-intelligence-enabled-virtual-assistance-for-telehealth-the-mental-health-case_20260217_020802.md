# Demo Alleviate: Demonstrating Artificial Intelligence Enabled Virtual Assistance for Telehealth: The Mental Health Case

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.00025
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.00025
- Reference count: 7
- Primary result: AI-powered mental health chatbot integrating knowledge graphs for personalized, safe patient care

## Executive Summary
Alleviate is an AI-powered mental health chatbot designed to assist both patients and clinicians through personalized care and safe interactions. The system integrates clinically validated mental health knowledge from diverse sources into personalized patient knowledge graphs, enabling medically informed decision-making. Its modular design supports explainable, feedback-based refinements via reinforcement learning, while providing safe medication reminders, adherence assessment, and emergency intervention detection using clinically established guidelines.

## Method Summary
Alleviate constructs personalized patient knowledge graphs by extracting information from provider notes and chatbot interactions using triple extraction techniques. These graphs integrate with validated mental health knowledge sources to enable context-aware decision-making. The system employs reinforcement learning with explainable decision-making for continuous refinement based on feedback, while using tree-based constraint models to detect emergency situations requiring human intervention. Dense representation similarities are computed against clinically established alarming behavior detection questionnaires represented as trees to identify emergency patterns in real-time conversations.

## Key Results
- Personalized patient knowledge graphs enable context-aware medication inquiries and troubleshooting
- Tree-based constraint models detect emergency situations requiring immediate human intervention
- Modular design with reinforcement learning supports continuous, explainable system refinement
