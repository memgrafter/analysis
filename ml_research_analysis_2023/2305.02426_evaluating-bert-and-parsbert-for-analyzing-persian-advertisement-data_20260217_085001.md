## Executive Summary
This study compares multilingual BERT (mBERT) and Persian-specific ParsBERT for binary classification of Persian advertisement data from the Divar marketplace. The authors preprocessed 540,362 Persian text samples using Hazm and fine-tuned both models for ad acceptance/rejection prediction. Results show ParsBERT achieving marginally higher accuracy and F1 scores than mBERT, demonstrating the potential value of language-specific models for Persian text classification tasks.

## Method Summary
The authors preprocessed the Divar dataset using the Hazm library for Persian text normalization and tokenization, then fine-tuned both mBERT and ParsBERT models for binary classification of accepted versus rejected advertisements. The models were evaluated on standard metrics including accuracy and F1 score, with ParsBERT showing slight improvements over the multilingual baseline on this Persian-language task.

## Key Results
- ParsBERT achieved 82.43% accuracy and 82.40% F1 score
- mBERT achieved 82.99% accuracy and 82.94% F1 score
- ParsBERT showed marginally better performance than mBERT on Persian text classification
