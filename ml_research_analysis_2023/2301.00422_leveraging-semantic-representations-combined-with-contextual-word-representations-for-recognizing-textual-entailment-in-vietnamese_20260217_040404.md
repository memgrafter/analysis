# Leveraging Semantic Representations Combined with Contextual Word Representations for Recognizing Textual Entailment in Vietnamese

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.00422
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00422
- Reference count: 19
- mBERT with semantic representation achieves 76.12% F1-score

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the Recognizing Textual Entailment (RTE) task in Vietnamese by combining semantic representations from Semantic Role Labeling (SRL) with contextual representations from transformer models. The proposed semantic-aware contextual representation model achieves approximately 1% higher performance than models without semantic representation. The research demonstrates that Vietnamese benefits more from semantic representation than English, highlighting the importance of SRL for understanding Vietnamese natural language. The best-performing model using XLM-R large achieves 88.89% F1-score.

## Method Summary
The proposed approach combines semantic representations learned through Semantic Role Labeling (SRL) with contextual representations from transformer models. The method leverages pre-trained multilingual models (mBERT, XLM-R base, and XLM-R large) and integrates SRL-based semantic information into the RTE pipeline. The semantic representations capture predicate-argument structures and semantic roles, which are then combined with the contextual embeddings from transformers to enhance the model's understanding of textual entailment relationships in Vietnamese.

## Key Results
- mBERT with semantic representation achieves 76.12% F1-score
- XLM-R base with semantic representation achieves 81.25% F1-score
- XLM-R large with semantic representation achieves 88.89% F1-score
- Approximately 1% improvement over models without semantic representation

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The combination of semantic and contextual representations works because SRL captures deeper semantic relationships between predicates and arguments in Vietnamese sentences, while transformer models provide rich contextual understanding. This dual representation allows the model to better capture both the syntactic structure and semantic meaning necessary for accurate entailment detection.

## Foundational Learning

- Transformer-based contextual embeddings: Required for capturing word context and relationships; quick check: verify model is pre-trained on multilingual corpus.
- RTE task formulation: Essential for understanding the binary classification of entailment relationships; quick check: confirm dataset labels follow standard entailment categories.
