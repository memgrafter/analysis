# Searching for Effective Neural Network Architectures for Heart Murmur Detection from Phonocardiogram

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.02988
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02988
- Reference count: 14
- Primary result: Multi-task CNN approach achieved weighted accuracy of 0.736 for murmur detection and cost score of 12944 for clinical outcome identification in PhysioNet Challenge 2022

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a multi-task learning approach for heart murmur detection from phonocardiograms (PCGs) using convolutional neural networks. The method employs hard parameter sharing across three tasks: heart sound segmentation, murmur detection, and clinical outcome classification. Several CNN architectures were evaluated, with modified versions of SE-ResNet and TResNetS showing promising results. The approach achieved competitive performance in the PhysioNet Challenge 2022, ranking 14th for murmur detection and 19th for clinical outcome identification among 40 teams.

## Method Summary
The approach uses multi-task learning with a shared CNN backbone trained on PCGs resampled to 1000 Hz and filtered with a Butterworth bandpass filter (25-400 Hz). Multiple CNN architectures were tested including SE-ResNet, TResNetS, MultiBranch, and a modified wav2vec2 model. The model outputs three predictions: heart sound segmentation, murmur detection, and clinical outcome classification. Hard parameter sharing was used to train all tasks simultaneously, leveraging shared representations to improve generalization across tasks.

## Key Results
- Weighted accuracy of 0.736 for murmur detection on hidden validation set
- Cost score of 12944 for clinical outcome identification
- Ranked 14th and 19th respectively among 40 participating teams
- Multi-task learning approach showed effectiveness compared to single-task baselines

## Limitations
- The paper lacks detailed ablation studies to isolate the contributions of different components to the final performance.
- No comparison to state-of-the-art transformer-based architectures for time-series analysis, which may be more effective than CNNs for this application.
- The specific benefits of adding the segmentation task to the multi-task learning setup are not conclusively demonstrated.

## Confidence

- CNN architectures are effective for PCG analysis: Medium
