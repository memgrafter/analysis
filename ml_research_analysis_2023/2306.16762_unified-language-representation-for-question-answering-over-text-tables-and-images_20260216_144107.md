# Unified Language Representation for Question Answering over Text, Tables, and Images

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.16762
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.16762
- Reference count: 20
- Primary result: Outperforms existing methods by 10.6-32.3 percentage points across ten metrics on two datasets

## Executive Summary
Solar introduces a unified language representation approach for multimodal question answering that transforms images and tables into natural language representations, converting the problem into a textual QA task. This approach operates entirely within a unified language space rather than the multimodal space, simplifying the overall architecture. The method employs three sequential steps - retrieval, ranking, and generation - to process multimodal inputs and generate answers. Solar demonstrates state-of-the-art performance on both MultimodalQA and MMCoQA datasets while achieving significant efficiency improvements through 99.7% reduction in feature storage.

## Method Summary
Solar operates by converting all input modalities (text, tables, and images) into unified natural language representations. The approach consists of three main stages: retrieval, where relevant information is extracted from the input; ranking, which determines the most pertinent content; and generation, which produces the final answer. By transforming images and tables into textual descriptions, Solar reduces the complex multimodal QA problem to a simpler textual QA problem. This unified representation enables the model to leverage existing textual QA architectures while maintaining effectiveness across diverse multimodal inputs. The approach is evaluated on two datasets (MultimodalQA and MMCoQA) and demonstrates competitive performance on the WebQA leaderboard.

## Key Results
- Outperforms existing methods by 10.6-32.3 percentage points across ten metrics on two datasets
- Achieves best performance on the WebQA leaderboard
- Reduces feature storage by 99.7% compared to traditional multimodal approaches

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Solar's effectiveness stems from its unified language representation approach that simplifies multimodal reasoning by converting all inputs to text. By transforming images and tables into natural language descriptions, the model can leverage powerful text-based language models without requiring complex multimodal architectures. This transformation reduces the problem complexity while maintaining the semantic richness needed for accurate QA. The three-stage pipeline (retrieval, ranking, generation) provides a structured approach to processing multimodal information that aligns well with how humans typically process complex information from multiple sources.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map:** Input -> Text Conversion -> Retrieval -> Ranking -> Generation -> Output

**Critical Path:** The most important sequence is Input -> Text Conversion -> Retrieval -> Generation, as ranking serves as a refinement step rather than a bottleneck.

**Design Tradeoffs:** The unified language approach trades off some potential multimodal interaction richness for simplicity and efficiency, choosing to represent all modalities in a single format rather than developing specialized multimodal architectures.

**Failure Signatures:** 
- Poor text conversion quality leads to information loss and incorrect answers
- Retrieval failures result in missing critical context for question answering
- Ranking errors cause focus on irrelevant information despite having correct content available
