# ProtoVAE: Prototypical Networks for Unsupervised Disentanglement

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.09092
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09092
- Reference count: 36
- State-of-the-art unsupervised disentanglement on benchmark datasets

## Executive Summary
ProtoVAE introduces a novel approach to unsupervised disentanglement by combining a Variational Autoencoder with a Prototypical Network. The key innovation lies in using self-supervised data generated through latent space interventions to train a Prototypical Network, which then constrains the VAE's latent space to achieve disentangled representations. This method eliminates the need for prior knowledge about the dataset's factors of variation, making it more flexible and applicable to real-world scenarios.

## Method Summary
ProtoVAE operates by first generating self-supervised data through interventions on the VAE's latent space. These interventions are designed to isolate individual factors of variation in the data. A Prototypical Network is then trained on this self-supervised data to learn representations that capture these factors. Finally, the VAE's latent space is constrained using the Prototypical Network, ensuring that changes in the representation space map to controlled changes in the data space. This approach allows ProtoVAE to learn disentangled representations without requiring explicit labels or knowledge of the number of factors.

## Key Results
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance on benchmark datasets (dSprites, 3DShapes, and MPI3D)
- Demonstrates strong performance on the real-world CelebA dataset
- Shows improved quantitative metrics and qualitative traversals in the latent space

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
ProtoVAE works by leveraging the strengths of both VAEs and Prototypical Networks. The VAE learns a compressed representation of the input data, while the Prototypical Network, trained on self-supervised data, learns to identify and separate the underlying factors of variation. By constraining the VAE's latent space with the Prototypical Network, ProtoVAE ensures that the learned representations are disentangled, meaning that each dimension of the latent space corresponds to a specific factor of variation in the data.

## Foundational Learning
- **Variational Autoencoders (VAEs)**: A generative model that learns to encode data into a latent space and decode it back, useful for learning compressed representations of data.
- **Prototypical Networks**: A metric learning approach that learns to classify data by comparing it to learned prototypes, useful for identifying and separating underlying factors of variation.
- **Self-supervised Learning**: A learning paradigm where the model generates its own labels from the data, useful for training without requiring explicit annotations.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component Map**: Input Data -> VAE Encoder -> Latent Space -> Prototypical Network -> Constrained Latent Space -> VAE Decoder -> Output Data
- **Critical Path**: The critical path involves generating self-supervised data through latent space interventions, training the Prototypical Network, and using it to constrain the VAE's latent space.
- **Design Tradeoffs**: The main tradeoff is between the computational overhead of training both the VAE and Prototypical Network versus the improved disentanglement performance.
- **Failure Signatures**: Poor disentanglement may occur if the self-supervised data generation process fails to adequately isolate factors of variation or if the Prototypical Network fails to learn meaningful prototypes.
- **First Experiments**: 1) Generate self-supervised data through latent space interventions. 2) Train a Prototypical Network on the self-supervised data. 3) Use the Prototypical Network to constrain the VAE's latent space and evaluate disentanglement.

## Limitations
- Reliance on self-supervised data generated through latent space interventions may limit performance on datasets with unknown or continuous factors of variation.
- Computational overhead of training both the VAE and Prototypical Network components may be a concern for practical applications.
- Performance on truly unconstrained real-world data beyond CelebA remains to be seen.

## Confidence
- State-of-the-art performance claims on benchmark datasets: High
- Effectiveness on real-world data (CelebA): Medium
- Scalability to high-dimensional, unconstrained datasets: Low
- Computational efficiency and practical applicability: Low
