# Deep Ranking Ensembles for Hyperparameter Optimization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.15212
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15212
- Reference count: 35
- New state-of-the-art results on HPO-B benchmark across 16 search spaces and 86 datasets/tasks

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces Deep Ranking Ensembles (DRE), a novel approach to training Bayesian Optimization (BO) surrogates using Learning to Rank (L2R) rather than standard regression. The method employs a meta-learned ensemble of neural networks optimized with list-wise ranking losses, trained across multiple datasets. DRE significantly outperforms 12 state-of-the-art HPO baselines on the HPO-B benchmark, establishing new state-of-the-art results. The authors demonstrate that their approach works effectively both with and without transfer learning, with weighted list-wise ranking losses proving superior to other loss types.

## Method Summary
DRE trains Bayesian Optimization surrogates through a Learning to Rank approach, using meta-learning to optimize an ensemble of neural networks with list-wise ranking losses. The method is trained on evaluations from multiple datasets to create a transferable model, which is then fine-tuned on target tasks using Expected Improvement acquisition. The approach leverages meta-features from datasets to enhance transfer learning capabilities and employs weighted list-wise ranking losses to optimize the surrogate model's ability to rank hyperparameter configurations by their expected performance.

## Key Results
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance across 16 search spaces and 86 datasets/tasks on HPO-B benchmark
- Outperforms 12 state-of-the-art HPO baselines including HEBO, FSBO, and DKLM
- Weighted list-wise ranking losses show superior performance compared to point-wise and pair-wise alternatives

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by reframing BO surrogate training as a ranking problem rather than a regression problem. By using list-wise ranking losses, the surrogate learns to correctly order multiple hyperparameter configurations simultaneously rather than predicting absolute performance values. This ranking-centric approach better captures the relative relationships between configurations that matter for BO acquisition functions. The meta-learning component allows the model to leverage knowledge across multiple datasets, improving sample efficiency and transfer capabilities.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component map**: Neural ensemble (multiple networks) -> List-wise ranking loss -> Meta-learning across datasets -> Fine-tuning on target task -> Expected Improvement acquisition
- **Critical path**: Meta-learning phase → Fine-tuning phase → Evaluation phase
- **Design tradeoffs**: List-wise vs. point-wise/pair-wise losses (better ranking vs. simpler optimization), ensemble vs. single model (robustness vs. efficiency)
- **Failure signatures**: Poor transfer learning indicates issues with meta-feature extraction or insufficient meta-training diversity; convergence issues suggest inappropriate learning rate or batch size settings
- **3 first experiments**: 1) Validate list-wise ranking loss implementation on synthetic ranking data, 2) Test meta-learning on a small set of datasets before scaling, 3) Compare single model vs. ensemble performance on a simple HPO task
