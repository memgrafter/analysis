# Faithfulness-Aware Decoding Strategies for Abstractive Summarization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.03278
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03278
- Reference count: 33
- Key outcome: This paper investigates how decoding strategies affect faithfulness in abstractive summarization.

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates how decoding strategies affect faithfulness in abstractive summarization. The authors find that beam search with large beam sizes produces the most faithful summaries, while nucleus sampling generates the least faithful ones. To improve faithfulness beyond current decoding techniques, they propose two methods: (1) ranking beam search candidates using automatic faithfulness metrics, and (2) incorporating lookahead heuristics that provide a faithfulness score for the future summary. Both methods significantly improve faithfulness across two datasets as evaluated by four automatic metrics and human evaluation. To reduce computational cost, the authors demonstrate a simple distillation approach that allows the model to generate faithful summaries with just greedy decoding.

## Method Summary
The authors analyze popular decoding strategies (greedy decoding, beam search, and nucleus sampling) and their impact on faithfulness in abstractive summarization. They propose two faithfulness-aware decoding methods: (1) ranking beam search candidates using composite faithfulness metrics (FactCC, DAE, QuestEval, BERTScore-Fact), and (2) lookahead heuristics that evaluate future summary faithfulness to guide token selection. They also demonstrate a distillation approach where a student model learns to generate faithful summaries directly through training with a teacher model's outputs. The methods are evaluated on two summarization datasets (XSum and CNN/DM) using both automatic faithfulness metrics and human evaluation.

## Key Results
- Beam search with larger beam sizes consistently produces more faithful summaries than nucleus sampling or greedy decoding
- Ranking beam search candidates using faithfulness metrics significantly improves faithfulness over baseline decoding strategies
- Lookahead heuristics with greedy decoding achieve faithfulness comparable to beam search while maintaining computational efficiency
- Distillation enables faithful summary generation with only greedy decoding, reducing computational cost

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Beam search with larger beam sizes explores more candidates and thus finds more faithful summaries.
- Mechanism: By expanding the search space with more beams, the model considers a wider range of possible summaries, increasing the likelihood of encountering faithful ones even if the model score itself isn't optimized for faithfulness.
- Core assumption: Larger search space leads to higher probability of including faithful summaries.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "We find a consistent trend where beam search with large beam sizes produces the most faithful summaries while nucleus sampling generates the least faithful ones."
  - [section] "As beam size increases, more faithful summaries exist in the list of candidates, but the faithfulness of the top beam improves only slightly."
- Break condition: If the faithfulness metric itself is flawed or not well-aligned with human judgment, the assumption breaks.

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Ranking candidates using faithfulness metrics selects more faithful summaries than relying on model scores.
- Mechanism: Re-ranking the beam search outputs based on faithfulness metrics (like FactCC, DAE, QuestEval) allows selection of summaries that are faithful even if they were not the top-scoring ones by the model.
- Core assumption: The faithfulness metrics are reliable indicators of faithfulness.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "ranking candidates generated by beam search using automatic faithfulness metrics"
  - [section] "Compared to the baseline decoding strategies we analyzed, the two proposed methods significantly improve faithfulness as evaluated by four automatic faithfulness metrics and further confirmed by human evaluation."
- Break condition: If the faithfulness metrics are biased or do not correlate well with human judgment, the ranking will fail.

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Lookahead heuristics guide token selection towards paths that lead to more faithful summaries.
- Mechanism: By simulating the full future summary starting from the current token and evaluating its faithfulness, the model can choose tokens that are less likely to lead to hallucinations, even if they are not the most probable next token.
- Core assumption: Evaluating future summaries provides an effective signal for guiding current token selection.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "incorporating lookahead heuristics that produce a faithfulness score on the future summary"
  - [section] "The added heuristic ensures that the selected tokens will lead to a more faithful path in the search space."
- Break condition: If the future summary evaluation is computationally expensive or does not provide reliable signals, the mechanism breaks.

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map:
  - Base decoder (BART/PEGASUS)
  - Decoding strategies (Greedy, Beam Search, Nucleus Sampling)
  - Faithfulness-aware methods (Ranking, Lookahead)
  - Distillation pipeline (Teacher model → Student model)

- Critical path:
  1. Generate summaries using base decoding strategy
  2. Apply faithfulness-aware method (Ranking or Lookahead)
  3. Evaluate using faithfulness metrics
  4. (Optional) Distill knowledge to student model for faster decoding

- Design tradeoffs:
  - Beam size vs. computational cost: Larger beam sizes improve faithfulness but increase computation.
  - Lookahead length vs. accuracy: Longer lookahead provides better guidance but is more expensive.
  - Ranking metric vs. robustness: Composite metrics are more robust but require tuning.

- Failure signatures:
  - Poor faithfulness despite large beam size: Faithfulness metrics may be unreliable.
  - No improvement with lookahead: Future summary evaluation may not be effective.
  - Distillation fails to improve speed: Student model may not learn the faithfulness patterns.

- First 3 experiments:
  1. Compare beam search with different beam sizes on faithfulness metrics.
  2. Apply ranking with a single faithfulness metric and observe improvements.
  3. Test lookahead with greedy decoding and evaluate faithfulness gains.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
- Question: How do different decoding strategies affect faithfulness across diverse domains and languages?
- Basis in paper: The paper investigates decoding strategies' effect on faithfulness in abstractive summarization, focusing on English news articles (XSum and CNN/DM datasets). It also briefly tests ranking robustness on WikiHow.
- Why unresolved: The study is limited to English news summarization datasets. The paper does not explore how decoding strategies perform across different domains (e.g., scientific articles, dialogue) or languages.
- What evidence would resolve it: Systematic experiments evaluating decoding strategies on diverse summarization datasets spanning different domains and languages, with comprehensive faithfulness and quality metrics.

### Open Question 2
- Question: What is the optimal balance between faithfulness and abstractiveness in abstractive summarization?
- Basis in paper: The paper discusses the tradeoff between faithfulness and abstractiveness, showing that more faithful models tend to be more extractive. It demonstrates lookahead heuristics can balance both aspects.
- Why unresolved: While the paper shows a tradeoff exists and demonstrates one method to balance them, it does not establish an optimal balance or provide guidelines for when to prioritize one over the other.
- What evidence would resolve it: Empirical studies establishing the relationship between faithfulness and abstractiveness across different summarization tasks and user preferences, potentially through user studies or task-specific evaluations.

### Open Question 3
- Question: How can computational overhead of faithfulness-aware decoding be minimized without sacrificing performance?
- Basis in paper: The paper acknowledges the heavy computational cost of lookahead heuristics and presents distillation as a solution to improve decoding speed.
- Why unresolved: While distillation shows promise, the paper does not explore other potential optimizations or hybrid approaches that could further reduce computational overhead while maintaining faithfulness gains.
- What evidence would resolve it: Development and evaluation of novel techniques to optimize faithfulness-aware decoding, such as parallel processing, caching strategies, or more efficient lookahead implementations, compared against current methods.

## Limitations
- Faithfulness metrics themselves have known limitations and may not fully capture semantic faithfulness or factual consistency
- The lookahead heuristics are computationally expensive and may not scale to longer summaries or larger models
- The distillation approach, while effective, requires access to both teacher and student model training infrastructure

## Confidence
- **High confidence**: Beam search with larger beam sizes produces more faithful summaries
- **Medium confidence**: Ranking candidates using faithfulness metrics significantly improves faithfulness
- **Medium confidence**: Lookahead heuristics improve faithfulness
