# A Fusion Model: Towards a Virtual, Physical and Cognitive Integration and its Principles

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.09992
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.09992
- Reference count: 17
- Key outcome: Proposes FU model for seamless integration of virtual, physical, and cognitive worlds using physical universe laws

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces the Fusion Universe (FU) model to address the challenge of integrating virtual, physical, and cognitive worlds by establishing principles derived from physical universe laws. The model tackles the fundamental problem of discrepancies between virtual and physical environments, where virtual objects often fail to follow real-world physics and interactions remain inconsistent. Through a VR shopping scenario, the paper demonstrates that seamless integration requires defining universal limits (size, speed, time, distance) and implementing probability distributions that enable smooth transitions across different domains. The FU model represents a comprehensive framework for creating immersive experiences that maintain consistency with physical reality while incorporating cognitive elements.

## Method Summary
The FU model is implemented through a systematic approach that begins with defining physical universe limits and probability principles, followed by practical application in a VR shopping scenario. The methodology involves creating unified distance calculations using spatial mapping with pixel sizes approaching quark-level precision, synchronizing time across domains through fragmentation, and generating unique digital identities through random mutation of body segments. Permutation Entropy is applied to analyze EEG signals for cognitive integration, while Bayesian theorem and the novel Butterfly Probability concept handle extreme cases and cognitive entropy. The approach emphasizes maintaining physical law consistency while enabling creative virtual experiences.

## Key Results
- Virtual objects following real-world physics principles reduce user-perceived discrepancies between virtual and physical worlds
- Unified spatial mapping with quark-level precision enables seamless object transitions across physical and virtual spaces
- Butterfly Probability and Permutation Entropy successfully handle extreme cognitive integration cases and EEG signal analysis
