# RIFormer: Keep Your Vision Backbone Effective While Removing Token Mixer

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2304.05659
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05659
- **Reference count**: 40
- **Primary result**: Achieves 17.8% speedup over PoolFormer-M36 while maintaining 82.1% ImageNet top-1 accuracy

## Executive Summary
RIFormer presents an optimization-driven approach to vision backbone design by removing token mixers while maintaining competitive performance through structural re-parameterization and knowledge distillation. The method replaces token mixers with affine transformations during training and leverages module imitation techniques to implicitly incorporate inductive bias into a simplified architecture. This approach demonstrates that advanced optimization strategies can effectively compensate for architectural simplifications, achieving significant computational efficiency gains while matching baseline performance.

## Method Summary
RIFormer's core innovation lies in replacing token mixers with affine transformations and using knowledge distillation through module imitation to maintain performance. During training, the model uses a simplified architecture where token mixers are replaced by learnable affine transformations, while structural re-parameterization allows the model to implicitly capture the benefits of token mixing. The knowledge distillation component employs module imitation, where the student network learns to replicate the behavior of a teacher network's token mixer layers. This combination of architectural simplification and optimization-driven training enables the model to achieve comparable accuracy with reduced computational overhead.

## Key Results
- Achieves 17.8% speedup over PoolFormer-M36 with identical ImageNet top-1 accuracy of 82.1%
- Demonstrates successful token mixer removal without performance degradation
- Shows that optimization strategies can compensate for architectural simplifications

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The effectiveness of RIFormer stems from its ability to implicitly capture token mixing behavior through optimization rather than explicit architectural components. By using structural re-parameterization, the model can learn representations that would traditionally require token mixers, while the knowledge distillation component ensures these learned representations are meaningful and transferable. The affine transformation replacement reduces computational complexity while the module imitation technique ensures the simplified model retains the essential characteristics of more complex architectures.

## Limitations
- Limited evaluation to single dataset (ImageNet-1K) and specific backbone architecture (PoolFormer)
- Computational efficiency claims need verification across different hardware platforms
- Reliance on complex optimization pipeline may limit practical adoption
