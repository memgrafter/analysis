# Vec2Vec: A Compact Neural Network Approach for Transforming Text Embeddings with High Fidelity

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.12689
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.12689
- Reference count: 15
- Primary result: Achieved 0.932 cosine similarity on transforming MPNet to text-ada-002 embeddings

## Executive Summary
Vec2Vec is a lightweight neural network model that transforms open-source MPNet embeddings into OpenAI's text-ada-002 embeddings. The model maps 768-dimensional MPNet embeddings to 1,536-dimensional text-ada-002 embeddings using a subset of 50,000 Amazon food reviews. Vec2Vec achieved an average cosine similarity of 0.932 on 10,000 unseen reviews, offering a cost-effective, offline alternative to proprietary embedding models. While it performs well on simple queries, the model shows performance degradation on complex queries compared to real text-ada-002 embeddings.

## Method Summary
The Vec2Vec model was trained on 50,000 Amazon food reviews to learn the transformation from MPNet embeddings to text-ada-002 embeddings. The architecture maps 768-dimensional MPNet embeddings to 1,536-dimensional text-ada-002 embeddings. The model was evaluated on 10,000 unseen reviews from the same dataset, achieving a cosine similarity of 0.932. The paper acknowledges limitations in performance on complex queries compared to real text-ada-002 embeddings.

## Key Results
- Achieved 0.932 cosine similarity on 10,000 unseen Amazon food reviews
- Successfully maps 768-dimensional MPNet embeddings to 1,536-dimensional text-ada-002 embeddings
- Demonstrated cost-effective, offline alternative to proprietary embedding models for simple queries

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Vec2Vec works by learning a transformation function that maps the semantic space of MPNet embeddings to that of text-ada-002 embeddings. The model leverages the fact that both embedding systems capture similar semantic relationships, but in different dimensional spaces and with different training objectives. By training on real examples of both embedding types from the same text corpus, Vec2Vec learns to approximate the complex transformation that OpenAI's proprietary training process produces.
