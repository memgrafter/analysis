# Diffusion-NAT: Self-Prompting Discrete Diffusion for Non-Autoregressive Text Generation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.04044
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.04044
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: Diffusion-NAT achieves +2.48 BLEU-2 improvement on PersonaChat compared to NAR baselines

## Executive Summary
Diffusion-NAT introduces a novel approach to non-autoregressive text generation by integrating discrete diffusion models with BART. The method combines BART's pre-trained knowledge with diffusion denoising to iteratively refine generated text. By removing time step embeddings and implementing self-prompting, the model achieves better coherence and diversity while maintaining computational efficiency. The approach demonstrates strong performance across 7 datasets, outperforming both NAR and semi-NAR baselines, and even surpassing autoregressive models in overall metrics.

## Method Summary
Diffusion-NAT unifies BART's non-autoregressive masked token recovery with discrete diffusion denoising. The model removes time step embeddings from the standard DDM setup and adds an iterative self-prompting mechanism that uses the model's own predictions as input for subsequent refinement steps. This allows the model to leverage BART's pre-trained knowledge while benefiting from the iterative refinement capabilities of diffusion models. The training process involves corrupting text with masks and noise, then learning to denoise through multiple steps, with the self-prompting mechanism enabling the model to generate more coherent outputs by feeding its own predictions back into the system.

## Key Results
- Outperforms NAR and semi-NAR baselines on 7 datasets
- Achieves +2.48 BLEU-2 improvement on PersonaChat
- Surpasses autoregressive models like BART in overall metrics
- Demonstrates improved coherence and diversity in generated text

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Diffusion-NAT works by combining the strengths of BART's pre-trained knowledge with discrete diffusion models' iterative refinement capabilities. The self-prompting mechanism allows the model to use its own predictions as input for subsequent refinement steps, creating a feedback loop that improves coherence. By removing time step embeddings, the model focuses on learning the denoising process without being constrained by specific diffusion steps. The iterative nature of diffusion models enables the model to refine its predictions multiple times, while BART's pre-trained knowledge provides a strong foundation for generating meaningful text.
