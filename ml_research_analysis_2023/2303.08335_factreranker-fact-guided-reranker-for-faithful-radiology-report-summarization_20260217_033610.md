# FactReranker: Fact-guided Reranker for Faithful Radiology Report Summarization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.08335
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.08335
- Reference count: 40
- Authors: Propose FactReranker to improve factual consistency in radiology report summarization by reranking candidates based on medical fact matching

## Executive Summary
FactReranker addresses a critical challenge in radiology report summarization: ensuring factual accuracy. The authors propose a two-stage approach that first extracts medical entities and relations from reports using RadGraph schema, then generates target summaries and scores candidates by matching extracted triplets. This explicit modeling of medical facts enables selection of factually accurate summaries rather than relying solely on likelihood-based generation. Experiments on MIMIC-CXR demonstrate significant improvements over state-of-the-art methods, with 4.84% gains in RadGraph score and 3.98% in ROUGE-1, confirming the superiority of fact-guided reranking for medical summarization tasks.

## Method Summary
FactReranker employs a two-stage approach for radiology report summarization. First, it uses the RadGraph schema to extract medical entities and their relationships from input reports. This schema-based extraction creates a structured representation of the medical facts present in each report. Second, the method generates multiple candidate summaries and scores them based on how well their extracted triplets match the target RadGraph summary. By explicitly modeling medical facts through this structured schema, FactReranker can evaluate and select summaries that are factually consistent with the source report, rather than simply choosing the most likely output from a language model.

## Key Results
- FactReranker achieves 4.84% improvement in RadGraph score over state-of-the-art methods
- The method shows 3.98% improvement in ROUGE-1 score on MIMIC-CXR dataset
- Fact-guided reranking demonstrates superior performance in maintaining factual consistency in medical summaries

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
FactReranker works by explicitly modeling the medical facts present in radiology reports through the RadGraph schema. Instead of relying solely on likelihood-based generation, which can produce fluent but factually incorrect summaries, the method extracts entities and relations from both the source report and candidate summaries. By scoring candidates based on triplet matching with the target RadGraph summary, the approach ensures that selected summaries maintain factual consistency with the source report. This structured representation of medical knowledge allows the reranker to identify and prioritize factually accurate summaries over those that may be more fluent but contain errors.

## Foundational Learning
- **RadGraph Schema**: Structured representation of medical entities and relations in radiology reports - needed for consistent fact extraction and comparison across different summaries
- **Triplet Matching**: Comparison of (subject, relation, object) tuples between source and candidate summaries - needed to quantify factual consistency
- **Two-stage Reranking**: Generating multiple candidates then selecting based on fact consistency - needed to balance fluency and accuracy
- **Medical Entity Recognition**: Identifying clinical terms, findings, and anatomical locations - needed for accurate fact extraction from reports
- **Relation Extraction**: Determining relationships between medical entities - needed to capture complete medical facts beyond individual terms

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map**: Radiology Report -> RadGraph Entity/Relation Extractor -> Fact Triplet Set -> Candidate Generator -> Multiple Summaries -> Triplet Extractor -> Fact Matcher -> Reranker -> Final Summary

**Critical Path**: The pipeline follows: source report → RadGraph extraction → candidate generation → fact-based scoring → final summary selection

**Design Tradeoffs**: The method trades additional computation (generating and scoring multiple candidates) for improved factual accuracy. It requires accurate RadGraph extraction but provides explicit control over factual consistency rather than relying on black-box generation.

**Failure Signatures**: Poor performance occurs when: RadGraph schema misses clinically relevant facts; entity/relation extraction introduces errors; candidate generation fails to produce factually correct alternatives; or fact matching metrics don't align with human judgment of correctness.

**3 First Experiments**: (1) Evaluate RadGraph extraction accuracy on a held-out test set; (2) Compare reranking performance with varying numbers of candidate summaries (2, 5, 10); (3) Analyze failure cases where FactReranker selects factually incorrect summaries despite high scores

## Limitations
- Potential over-reliance on RadGraph schema coverage—clinically relevant facts outside the schema cannot be captured
- Dependency on RadGraph model performance—extraction errors directly impact reranking quality
- Limited generalizability—experiments focus solely on MIMIC-CXR dataset

## Confidence
- High confidence in factual consistency advantage claim (clear experimental results and logical two-stage design)
- Medium confidence in general applicability (single dataset evaluation)
- Low confidence in clinical safety claims (no clinical expert review or deployment data)
