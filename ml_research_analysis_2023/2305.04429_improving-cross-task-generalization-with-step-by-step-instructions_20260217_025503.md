# Improving Cross-Task Generalization with Step-by-Step Instructions

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes a method to improve cross-task generalization of language models by generating detailed step-by-step instructions that supplement general task definitions. The approach uses ChatGPT to automatically generate these instructions, which are then used to fine-tune T5-LM models on the SUP-NATINST benchmark. Experiments demonstrate consistent improvements in cross-task generalization across different model sizes, with human evaluation confirming the quality and effectiveness of the generated instructions.

## Method Summary
The method involves using ChatGPT to generate step-by-step instructions for NLP tasks based on their general definitions and positive examples. These instructions are then refined through multiple interactions with ChatGPT to improve their quality. The original instructions are combined with the step-by-step instructions, and T5-LM models are fine-tuned using this combined instruction set. The approach is evaluated on the SUP-NATINST benchmark, measuring performance on unseen tasks using ROUGE-L score.

## Key Results
- Incorporating step-by-step instructions consistently improves cross-task generalization across different model sizes
- Human evaluation confirms the quality and effectiveness of the generated step-by-step instructions
- The order of steps in instructions is critical for correctness and effectiveness
- Automatic refinement through ChatGPT interactions improves instruction quality

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Step-by-step instructions improve cross-task generalization by providing detailed intermediate steps that help language models decompose complex tasks
- Mechanism: The step-by-step instructions act as a procedural guide that breaks down the target task into manageable subtasks, making it easier for language models to follow and complete the task correctly
- Core assumption: Language models benefit from explicit procedural guidance that shows the intermediate steps needed to complete a task
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract]: "the step-by-step instructions can provide the detailed and specific procedures for completing the target tasks"
  - [section 1]: "The step-by-step instructions can provide the task-level intermediate problem-solving steps without depending on any specific example"

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Refining step-by-step instructions through multiple interactions with ChatGPT improves their quality and effectiveness
- Mechanism: Progressive refinement allows ChatGPT to iteratively improve the instructions by addressing issues like iteration through datasets and incorporating example information
- Core assumption: Multiple interactions with ChatGPT can progressively improve the quality of generated instructions
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section 3.2]: "we propose to refine the step-by-step instruction through multiple interactions with ChatGPT"
  - [section 3.3]: "Both correctness and completeness of the step-by-step instructions of the test tasks increase after refining"

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: The order of steps in the step-by-step instructions is critical for their correctness and effectiveness
- Mechanism: Shuffling the order of steps can corrupt the correctness of the instructions, leading to decreased model performance
- Core assumption: Task completion procedures require steps to be followed in a specific order

## Limitations
- The approach relies heavily on ChatGPT for instruction generation, introducing potential quality variability
- The paper focuses on NLP tasks and may not generalize well to other domains or task types
- The exact prompts used for ChatGPT interactions are not fully specified, making it difficult to precisely reproduce the results

## Confidence
- **High confidence**: The experimental results showing consistent improvement in cross-task generalization across different model sizes when using step-by-step instructions
- **Medium confidence**: The claim that step-by-step instructions are more effective than chain-of-thought reasoning for task-level problem decomposition
- **Low confidence**: The assumption that automatic refinement through ChatGPT interactions is as effective as human refinement
