# Spanish Built Factual Freectianary (Spanish-BFF): the first AI-generated free dictionary

## Executive Summary
The Spanish-BFF project presents the first AI-generated Spanish dictionary, creating 66,353 lemma definitions using GPT-3. The authors developed prompts to generate definitions and compared them against the Diccionario de la Lengua Española (DLE) using BLEU, Levenshtein, Jaccard, and cosine similarity metrics. Results showed low scores on traditional metrics due to GPT-3 producing shorter definitions than DLE and not following all lexicographic rules. However, cosine similarity was higher for polysemous words, suggesting the model captured semantic relationships despite stylistic differences.

## Method Summary
The authors used GPT-3 to generate Spanish definitions through carefully crafted prompts, creating a dataset of 66,353 lemma definitions. They compared these AI-generated definitions against the authoritative Diccionario de la Lengua Española (DLE) using multiple evaluation metrics including BLEU, Levenshtein, Jaccard, and cosine similarity. The study identified several error patterns including circular definitions, tokenization issues, and language interference between Spanish and English.

## Key Results
- GPT-3 generated 66,353 Spanish lemma definitions using specialized prompts
- Traditional metrics (BLEU, Levenshtein, Jaccard) showed low similarity scores compared to DLE
- Cosine similarity was notably higher for polysemous words, suggesting semantic relationships were captured despite stylistic differences

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism works because GPT-3's transformer architecture can generate coherent definitions based on contextual understanding of words, even when those definitions differ stylistically from traditional dictionaries. The model's ability to handle polysemy through contextual embedding allows it to generate multiple senses for words, though the definitions may be more concise than traditional lexicographic entries.

## Limitations
- Traditional evaluation metrics may not appropriately capture AI-generated definition quality due to stylistic differences
- The study lacks systematic assessment of definition accuracy and completeness beyond automated metrics
- Claims about dictionary utility remain untested without user studies or downstream application validation
