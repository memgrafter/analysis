# Quantum Multi-Agent Actor-Critic Networks for Cooperative Mobile Access in Multi-UAV Systems

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.04445
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04445
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: QMACN algorithm improves UAV collaboration performance in MARL, achieving faster convergence and higher wireless service quality compared to classical methods.

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes a quantum multi-agent actor-critic network (QMACN) algorithm to address the challenge of building robust autonomous mobile access systems using multiple UAVs in dynamic, uncertain environments. The QMACN leverages quantum computing to improve the training and inference performance of UAV collaboration in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL). By incorporating noise injection and a centralized critic network, the algorithm mitigates scalability issues in quantum computing and enhances robustness to environmental uncertainties. The experimental results demonstrate that QMACN outperforms classical MARL methods, achieving faster convergence and higher wireless service quality in UAV-assisted mobile access scenarios.

## Method Summary
The QMACN algorithm is designed to optimize UAV collaboration in multi-agent reinforcement learning by leveraging quantum computing techniques. It uses a centralized critic network to train multiple actor networks iteratively, addressing scalability challenges in quantum computing. The algorithm incorporates noise injection (state and action noise) to handle environmental uncertainties and validate robustness. The QMACN is evaluated in a UAV-assisted mobile access scenario, where multiple UAVs provide wireless coverage to ground users in dynamic environments with wind, GPS noise, and interference. The algorithm's performance is compared against classical MARL methods, demonstrating superior convergence speed and wireless service quality.

## Key Results
- QMACN algorithm achieves faster convergence and higher wireless service quality compared to classical MARL methods in UAV-assisted mobile access scenarios.
- The algorithm demonstrates robustness to environmental uncertainties through the incorporation of noise injection during training.
- QMACN outperforms classical MARL methods in terms of support rate and QoS metrics, showcasing its potential for improving UAV collaboration in dynamic environments.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The QMACN algorithm leverages the computational power of quantum computing to enhance the training and inference performance of multi-agent reinforcement learning for UAV collaboration. By using a centralized critic network and incorporating noise injection, the algorithm addresses scalability challenges in quantum computing and improves robustness to environmental uncertainties. The quantum neural networks and parameterized quantum circuits (PQC) enable efficient exploration of the solution space, leading to faster convergence and improved wireless service quality in UAV-assisted mobile access scenarios.
