# Snowman: A Million-scale Chinese Commonsense Knowledge Graph Distilled from Foundation Model

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.10241
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10241
- Reference count: 11
- Introduces the first large-scale Chinese commonsense knowledge graph (CKG) distilled from ChatGPT

## Executive Summary
This paper presents Snowman, the first million-scale Chinese commonsense knowledge graph (CKG) generated directly from ChatGPT without translation. The authors use a few seed examples and targeted prompts to distill head items from ChatGPT, then generate tail items based on predefined relations such as xWant, xEffect, and HinderedBy. To improve quality, they apply a self-instruct filtering strategy using ChatGPT to automatically label and filter out invalid negative triples without human annotation. Snowman contains over 10 million high-quality Chinese triples, making it the largest Chinese CKG to date, with a reported human acceptance rate of 90.6%. Experiments demonstrate that commonsense knowledge generation models trained on Snowman significantly outperform those trained on prior CKGs, validating both the quality and usability of the constructed knowledge graph.

## Method Summary
The authors develop Snowman by first distilling head items (e.g., "PersonX learns to cook") from ChatGPT, then generating corresponding tail items with respect to predefined commonsense relations such as xWant, xEffect, and HinderedBy. To address quality issues with negative knowledge triples, they implement a self-instruct filtering strategy that uses ChatGPT to automatically label and filter out invalid negative triples without requiring human annotation. This approach enables direct Chinese generation rather than relying on translation, and scales to over 10 million triples.

## Key Results
- Snowman contains over 10 million high-quality Chinese commonsense triples
- Human acceptance rate of 90.6% for generated triples
- Commonsense knowledge generation models trained on Snowman significantly outperform those trained on prior CKGs
- Snowman is the largest Chinese commonsense knowledge graph to date

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Snowman leverages the strong commonsense reasoning capabilities of ChatGPT to directly generate Chinese commonsense knowledge triples. By using targeted prompts and predefined relations, the approach efficiently extracts relevant knowledge from the foundation model. The self-instruct filtering mechanism uses ChatGPT itself to identify and remove invalid negative triples, eliminating the need for human annotation and enabling scalable quality control. This direct generation from a foundation model bypasses the limitations of previous approaches that relied on English-centric sources or translation.

## Foundational Learning

**Few-shot prompting** - Why needed: Enables efficient guidance of the foundation model to generate relevant knowledge triples; Quick check: Test different numbers of seed examples to find optimal prompt efficiency.

**Self-instruct filtering** - Why needed: Automates quality control for negative triples without human annotation; Quick check: Compare filtering results across different prompt phrasings.

**Direct Chinese generation** - Why needed: Avoids quality loss and cultural bias from translation-based approaches; Quick check: Evaluate linguistic and cultural appropriateness of generated triples.

**Commonsense relation definitions** - Why needed: Provides structured framework for organizing and querying commonsense knowledge; Quick check: Verify coverage of key commonsense relations like xWant, xEffect, and HinderedBy.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component map:** ChatGPT -> Head item distillation -> Tail item generation -> Self-instruct filtering -> Snowman knowledge graph

**Critical path:** The most critical step is the self-instruct filtering, which determines the final quality and size of the knowledge graph by automatically removing invalid negative triples.

**Design tradeoffs:** The approach trades potential model bias and coverage limitations for scalability and direct Chinese generation capability. While the self-instruct filtering eliminates manual annotation, it may inherit biases from the foundation model.

**Failure signatures:** Low quality triples, missing commonsense relations, or demographic/cultural biases would indicate failures in the generation or filtering process. The human acceptance rate provides a key quality signal.

**First experiments:** 1) Test different seed example quantities to optimize prompt efficiency; 2) Evaluate filtering performance with various prompt phrasings; 3) Compare generated triples against human-annotated benchmarks for quality assessment.

## Limitations
- Quality and coverage fundamentally constrained by ChatGPT's internal knowledge and reasoning capabilities
- Potential demographic and cultural biases in generated commonsense triples not systematically evaluated
- Human evaluation protocol lacks transparency regarding annotator selection, inter-rater agreement, and error analysis
- Experimental validation limited to commonsense knowledge generation models, not tested in downstream NLP applications

## Confidence
- **High** confidence in novelty of scale and direct Chinese generation from ChatGPT
- **Medium** confidence in filtering methodology and reported performance improvements due to lack of transparency in evaluation protocols and potential model dependency

## Next Checks
