# SweCTRL-Mini: a data-transparent Transformer-based large language model for controllable text generation in Swedish

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.13994
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13994
- Reference count: 9
- SweCTRL-Mini matches or outperforms majority-vote baseline on 5 of 8 SuperLim tasks after minimal fine-tuning

## Executive Summary
This paper presents SweCTRL-Mini, a Swedish Transformer-based language model enabling both text generation and fine-tuning on a single consumer GPU. Built on the CTRL architecture, it allows controllable text generation via special genre tokens. The model is trained on a filtered subset of Swedish mC4 and Project Runeberg data, with detailed documentation of training sources and preprocessing for transparency. Automatic evaluation shows SweCTRL-Mini matches or outperforms a majority-vote baseline on 5 of 8 SuperLim tasks after minimal fine-tuning. Human evaluation reveals that GPT-3 generates higher-quality Swedish text with fewer errors, but SweCTRL-Mini with specific hyperparameters (r=1.0, p=0.9) performs comparably in some genres like ads. The model demonstrates practical controllability and efficiency, though text formatting and genre adherence need improvement.

## Method Summary
SweCTRL-Mini uses the CTRL architecture with halved hidden dimensions (d=640, f=4096) to reduce parameters to roughly one-third of the original model, enabling single-GPU operation. The model is trained on filtered Swedish mC4 and Project Runeberg data, categorized into 37 content categories with special opening and ending control codes for genre control. Training was performed on 8 A100 GPUs for approximately one month using AdamW optimizer with batch size 128. The model implements genre control through trainable token embeddings for opening control codes (OCC) and ending control codes (ECC) that guide generation style during inference.

## Key Results
- SweCTRL-Mini matches or outperforms majority-vote baseline on 5 of 8 SuperLim tasks after minimal fine-tuning
- GPT-3 generates higher-quality Swedish text with fewer errors than SweCTRL-Mini in human evaluation
- SweCTRL-Mini with hyperparameters r=1.0 and p=0.9 performs comparably to GPT-3 in specific genres like advertisements

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: SweCTRL-Mini can control text genre by inserting special tokens at generation time.
- Mechanism: The model uses opening control codes (OCC) and ending control codes (ECC) as trainable token embeddings that guide generation style.
- Core assumption: The OCC and ECC tokens learn genre-specific embeddings during training that influence the transformer's attention and output distribution.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "The model is based on the CTRL architecture by Keskar, McCann, Varshney, Xiong, and Socher (2019), which means that users of the SweCTRL-Mini model can control the genre of the generated text by inserting special tokens in the generation prompts."
  - [section] "The idea behind CTRL is to be able to steer the genre of the generated text by using special control codes as 1-token prompts."
- Break condition: If the OCC and ECC tokens do not learn distinct embeddings or if genre categories are too similar in training data, genre control will fail.

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: SweCTRL-Mini can be fine-tuned on a single consumer GPU.
- Mechanism: The model architecture is scaled down from the original CTRL model by halving hidden layer dimensions (d=640 instead of 768, f=4096 instead of 4096), reducing trainable parameters to roughly one-third of the original.
- Core assumption: Reducing model size proportionally reduces computational requirements while maintaining sufficient capacity for the Swedish language task.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "SweCTRL-Mini, a Transformer-based model for Swedish, allowing both inference and fine-tuning on a single consumer-grade GPU."
  - [section] "we have halved the hidden layer dimensions compared to the original CTRL implementation, more specifically: model dimension d = 640, and inner dimension f = 4096. These changes make the total number of trainable parameters in SweCTRL-Mini roughly a third of that of the original English CTRL."
- Break condition: If the reduced model size is insufficient for the complexity of the Swedish language or task requirements, performance will degrade significantly.

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: SweCTRL-Mini's training data overlap with evaluation datasets is minimal, ensuring fair evaluation.
- Mechanism: The authors calculated 7-gram and 13-gram overlap between training data and evaluation datasets, finding very low overlap percentages.
- Core assumption: Low n-gram overlap between training and evaluation data indicates minimal memorization and ensures the model is tested on unseen text.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section] "In order to get an insight into this issue, we attempted to estimate the overlap between the texts in each evaluation dataset D and texts from the training data T."
  - [table] "Dataset 7-grams 13-grams... N<7 O7 1 O7 10 O7 100 N<13 O13 1 O13 10 O13 100"
- Break condition: If evaluation datasets contain substantial Swedish text from the same sources as training data, overlap could be higher than reported, compromising evaluation fairness.

## Limitations
- Text quality and genre adherence are inferior to larger models like GPT-3, with issues in repetition, formatting, and genre-specific requirements
- The compact model size (one-third of original CTRL) may constrain ability to capture complex linguistic patterns across diverse genres
- Human evaluation relies on subjective judgments that may vary across evaluators

## Confidence

**High Confidence Claims:**
- The model architecture and training procedure are clearly documented and reproducible
- Data preprocessing steps and overlap calculations are methodologically sound
- Automatic evaluation results on SuperLim tasks are reliable and verifiable

**Medium Confidence Claims:**
- Human evaluation methodology, while transparent, relies on subjective judgments that may vary across evaluators
- The reported overlap calculations provide reasonable estimates but may not capture all forms of data contamination
- Genre control effectiveness is demonstrated but with room for improvement in consistency

**Low Confidence Claims:**
- Direct comparisons with GPT-3 may be influenced by model size differences rather than architecture or training quality
- Generalization claims across all Swedish text generation tasks require additional validation beyond the tested scenarios

## Next Checks
1. **Genre Control Consistency Test**: Generate 100 samples each from 5 different genres using SweCTRL-Mini with OCC tokens, then have independent human raters evaluate genre adherence without knowing the target genres. Measure inter-rater reliability and genre classification accuracy.

2. **Cross-Dataset Generalization**: Fine-tune SweCTRL-Mini on one SuperLim task and evaluate on all other tasks to measure zero-shot and few-shot generalization capabilities, comparing results to task-specific fine-tuning.

3. **Memory and Performance Profiling**: Document actual GPU memory usage during training and inference across different batch sizes and sequence lengths, measuring the trade-off between performance and hardware requirements.
