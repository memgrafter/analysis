# Unimodal Training-Multimodal Prediction: Cross-modal Federated Learning with Hierarchical Aggregation

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.15486
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15486
- Reference count: 38
- Key outcome: 15-20% improvement over state-of-the-art multimodal models with Corr = 0.625 on CMU-MOSEI and Corr = 0.55 on CMU-MOSI

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a Unimodal Training - Multimodal Prediction (UTMP) framework for federated learning, enabling multimodal models to be trained with unimodal data on edge devices. The authors propose a Hierarchical Aggregated Multimodal Federated Transformer (HA-Fedformer) that addresses data non-IID and sequence unalignment challenges. The framework employs Posterior-based Encoder Aggregation (PbEA) using MCMC sampling for uncertainty-aware encoder aggregation and Cross-modal Decoder Aggregation (CmDA) to align decoder parameters. Experiments on CMU-MOSI and CMU-MOSEI datasets demonstrate HA-Fedformer's effectiveness.

## Method Summary
The proposed UTMP framework enables multimodal model training with unimodal data in federated settings through a two-stage process. First, edge devices train unimodal transformers on their local data (text, audio, or video). Second, these unimodal models are aggregated into a multimodal model using hierarchical aggregation techniques. The core innovation is the HA-Fedformer architecture, which uses Posterior-based Encoder Aggregation with MCMC sampling to handle uncertainty in encoder aggregation and Cross-modal Decoder Aggregation to align decoder parameters across modalities. This approach specifically addresses the challenges of data non-IID distributions and sequence misalignment across different modalities in federated learning environments.

## Key Results
- HA-Fedformer achieves 15-20% improvement over state-of-the-art multimodal models under the UTMP framework
- Correlation coefficient of 0.625 on CMU-MOSEI dataset
- Correlation coefficient of 0.55 on CMU-MOSI dataset

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The framework works by leveraging the strengths of transformer models for both unimodal training and multimodal aggregation. The hierarchical aggregation approach allows for progressive fusion of unimodal information while maintaining the distinct characteristics of each modality. The MCMC-based uncertainty quantification ensures that the aggregation process accounts for the reliability of each participant's contribution, particularly important in non-IID settings. The cross-modal decoder aggregation aligns the decoding process across different modalities, enabling coherent multimodal predictions despite the disparate training data sources.

## Foundational Learning
- Federated Learning: Distributed model training without sharing raw data - needed to enable privacy-preserving training on edge devices with unimodal data
- Transformer Architecture: Self-attention mechanism for sequence modeling - needed to handle variable-length sequences across different modalities
- MCMC Sampling: Markov Chain Monte Carlo methods for posterior estimation - needed to quantify uncertainty in encoder aggregation
- Non-IID Data Distribution: Data heterogeneity across clients - needed to understand the challenge being addressed
- Sequence Unalignment: Temporal misalignment across modalities - needed to address synchronization issues in multimodal data
- Hierarchical Aggregation: Multi-level model parameter aggregation - needed to progressively combine unimodal models into a coherent multimodal model

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map**: Unimodal Transformers (Text/Audio/Video) -> PbEA (MCMC Sampling) -> Hierarchical Encoder Aggregation -> CmDA -> Multimodal Decoder -> Prediction

**Critical Path**: Local unimodal training → MCMC-based uncertainty quantification → Hierarchical encoder aggregation → Cross-modal decoder alignment → Multimodal prediction

**Design Tradeoffs**: The use of MCMC sampling provides robust uncertainty quantification but increases computational overhead; the hierarchical approach enables progressive aggregation but adds complexity to the training pipeline

**Failure Signatures**: Poor performance on datasets with high sequence unalignment, degraded results when data distribution is extremely non-IID, computational bottlenecks during MCMC sampling in large-scale deployments

**First Experiments**: 1) Verify unimodal transformer performance on each modality independently, 2) Test MCMC sampling convergence with varying numbers of clients, 3) Validate hierarchical aggregation with synthetic non-IID data distributions

## Limitations
- Experimental validation limited to only two benchmark datasets (CMU-MOSI and CMU-MOSEI)
- Lack of ablation studies to isolate contributions of individual components (PbEA, CmDA, hierarchical aggregation)
- No runtime comparisons or scalability analysis for MCMC sampling approach in practical federated settings

## Confidence
- Core claims about UTMP framework effectiveness: Medium
- Performance improvement metrics (15-20%): Medium
- Computational efficiency claims: Low
- Generalizability to other multimodal domains: Low
