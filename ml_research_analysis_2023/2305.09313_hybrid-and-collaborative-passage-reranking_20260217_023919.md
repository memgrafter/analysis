# Hybrid and Collaborative Passage Reranking

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.09313

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces HybRank, a novel passage reranking method that leverages collaboration between passages and hybrid sparse-dense features from upstream retrievers. Unlike existing reranking methods that treat passages in isolation, HybRank constructs hybrid and collaborative sequences using similarities between passages and a set of anchor texts derived from both sparse and dense retrievers. These sequences are then encoded into dense vectors through anchor-wise interaction and sequence aggregation, enabling fine-grained relevance estimation. Experiments on multiple datasets show that HybRank consistently improves the performance of various retrieval and reranking systems.

## Method Summary
HybRank constructs hybrid and collaborative sequences by combining passage similarities and anchor texts from both sparse and dense retrievers. These sequences are encoded into dense vectors using anchor-wise interaction and sequence aggregation, enabling fine-grained relevance estimation. The method is a flexible plug-in reranker capable of enhancing arbitrary passage lists, including previously reranked ones. HybRank is designed to capture both inter-passage relationships and hybrid feature fusion, distinguishing it from traditional reranking approaches.

## Limitations
- Assumes anchor texts from sparse and dense retrievers capture complementary signals, which may not hold uniformly across all datasets or domains.
- Computational overhead of constructing hybrid and collaborative sequences may limit scalability for very large corpora.
- Experiments focus on standard benchmark datasets, so generalization to real-world, noisy, or specialized domains remains uncertain.
