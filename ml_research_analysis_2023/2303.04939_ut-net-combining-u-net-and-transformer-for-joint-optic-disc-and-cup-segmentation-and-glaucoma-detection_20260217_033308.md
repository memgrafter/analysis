# UT-Net: Combining U-Net and Transformer for Joint Optic Disc and Cup Segmentation and Glaucoma Detection

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.04939
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04939
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: UT-Net achieves state-of-the-art performance on three datasets for optic disc and cup segmentation and glaucoma detection

## Executive Summary
This paper proposes UT-Net, a hybrid architecture combining U-Net and transformer encoders for joint optic disc (OD) and optic cup (OC) segmentation from retinal fundus images. The model integrates features from both encoders using a bilinear fusion block and incorporates multi-scale context extraction and multi-head contextual self-attention to capture long-range dependencies and local features. UT-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods on three publicly available datasets (DRISHTI-GS, RIM-ONE R3, and REFUGE) in terms of segmentation accuracy and glaucoma detection performance.

## Method Summary
UT-Net combines a U-Net-based encoder with a transformer encoder, using a bilinear fusion block to integrate features from both. The model employs multi-scale context extraction through modified receptive field blocks (RFB) and multi-head contextual self-attention (MCSA) to enhance feature representation and capture long-range dependencies. The architecture is trained using a combined loss function and evaluated on three datasets for OD and OC segmentation, as well as glaucoma detection based on Cup-to-Disc Ratio (CDR) measurement.

## Key Results
- UT-Net outperforms state-of-the-art methods on DRISHTI-GS, RIM-ONE R3, and REFUGE datasets for OD and OC segmentation
- The model achieves improved performance in glaucoma detection through accurate CDR measurement
- UT-Net demonstrates superior Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC), Intersection over Union (IoU), Precision, Sensitivity, and Accuracy compared to existing methods

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map:
  - Input: Retinal fundus images (H×W×C)
  - Encoder 1: U-Net-based encoder (5 downsampling steps with 3×3 conv, BN, ReLU, 2×2 maxpool)
  - Encoder 2: Transformer encoder (12 layers with Multi-Head Contextual Self-Attention)
  - Fusion: Bilinear fusion block (channel attention + spatial attention + Hadamard product)
  - Decoder: Standard U-Net decoder with modified RFB skip connections
  - Output: Segmentation maps for OD and OC

- Critical path: Image → U-Net encoder → Transformer encoder → Bilinear fusion → Decoder → Output
- Design tradeoffs:
  - Computational cost vs. accuracy: Using both U-Net and transformer increases parameter count but improves performance
  - Local vs. global information: U-Net captures local details while transformer captures global context
  - Skip connections: Retained to preserve high-level information from U-Net encoder

- Failure signatures:
  - Poor OD/OC boundary segmentation: May indicate issues with the bilinear fusion or attention mechanisms
  - Inconsistent results across datasets: Could suggest overfitting to specific dataset characteristics
  - High inference time: May indicate inefficient implementation of the transformer or fusion components
