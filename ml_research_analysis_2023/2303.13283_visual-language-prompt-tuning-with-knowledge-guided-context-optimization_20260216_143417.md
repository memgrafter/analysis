# Visual-Language Prompt Tuning with Knowledge-guided Context Optimization

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses catastrophic forgetting in prompt tuning for visual-language models, where learned prompts lose general textual knowledge and perform poorly on unseen classes. The proposed Knowledge-guided Context Optimization (KgCoOp) method minimizes the discrepancy between learned prompts and hand-crafted prompts to retain general knowledge while learning task-specific information. Experiments show KgCoOp achieves higher performance with less training time compared to existing methods, particularly improving generalization on unseen classes.

## Method Summary
KgCoOp combines standard contrastive learning with a knowledge-guided regularization term. The method learns prompts by minimizing both the cross-entropy loss for task discrimination and the Euclidean distance between learned prompt embeddings and hand-crafted prompt embeddings from CLIP. The final objective is L = Lce + λLkg, where λ balances the two losses. This approach retains general textual knowledge while adapting to specific tasks, addressing the forgetting problem that degrades performance on unseen classes.

## Key Results
- Improves harmonic mean from 76.16% to 77.0% in 16-shot setting
- Achieves higher performance with less training time compared to existing methods
- Demonstrates consistent improvements across 11 image classification datasets and four ImageNet variants
