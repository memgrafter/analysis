# On Efficient Training of Large-Scale Deep Learning Models: A Literature Review

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.03589
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03589
- Reference count: 40
- This survey comprehensively reviews techniques for efficient training of large-scale deep learning models across five perspectives.

## Executive Summary
This survey provides a systematic review of efficient training techniques for large-scale deep learning models, proposing a novel taxonomy that categorizes methods into five perspectives: data-centric, model-centric, optimization-centric, budgeted training, and system-centric approaches. The work covers fundamental components of deep learning training and presents various strategies to accelerate training while maintaining or improving model performance. By examining techniques ranging from data augmentation and model architecture optimization to advanced optimization strategies and system-level implementations, this survey serves as a comprehensive guide for researchers and practitioners seeking to improve training efficiency.

## Method Summary
The survey analyzes the fundamental gradient-based update formula and deconstructs it into distinct components, creating a taxonomy that encompasses five main perspectives for improving training efficiency. It systematically reviews techniques across data processing, model architecture optimization, learning rate and batch size tuning, model averaging, and system implementation strategies. The work examines both theoretical foundations and practical applications of various efficiency techniques, providing insights into their applicability across different deep learning architectures and tasks.

## Key Results
- Proposes a novel five-perspective taxonomy (data-centric, model-centric, optimization-centric, budgeted, system-centric) for categorizing efficient training techniques
- Comprehensive coverage of techniques from fundamental modules (linear layers, convolutions, attention) to advanced methods (quantization, sparsity, model averaging)
- Provides practical insights into achieving efficient training through appropriate learning rate selection, batch size optimization, and hardware-aware implementation

## Limitations
- The proposed five-perspective framework may oversimplify complex interdependencies between data, model, and system factors.
- Many claims rely on theoretical reasoning rather than extensive empirical validation.
- The relative importance of different efficiency techniques varies significantly based on model scale, dataset characteristics, and hardware constraints.

## Confidence

**High**: The survey accurately categorizes existing techniques into coherent groups (data-centric, model-centric, etc.)
**Medium**: The framework's utility for guiding practical implementation choices
**Low**: Specific performance gains claimed for individual techniques without extensive experimental validation
