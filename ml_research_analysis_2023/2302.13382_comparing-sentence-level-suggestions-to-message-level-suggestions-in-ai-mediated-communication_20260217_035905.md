# Comparing Sentence-Level Suggestions to Message-Level Suggestions in AI-Mediated Communication

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.13382
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13382
- Reference count: 40
- Participants receiving message-level suggestions completed tasks significantly faster (8.53 minutes) than control (16.40 minutes) or sentence-level (15.77 minutes) conditions

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates the trade-offs between sentence-level and message-level AI suggestions for assisting legislative staff in responding to constituent communications. Through an online experiment with 120 participants using a simulated platform called dispatch, the study found that message-level suggestions significantly improved task completion speed while also being perceived as more natural and useful. However, sentence-level suggestions maintained higher user agency and trust. The findings suggest that AI-mediated communication systems must balance efficiency gains with user control and trust considerations when designing assistance formats.

## Method Summary
The researchers developed a simulated platform called dispatch to replicate the scenario of legislative staff responding to constituent concerns. They recruited 120 participants through Mechanical Turk and randomly assigned them to three conditions: no assistance (control), sentence-level suggestions, or message-level suggestions. Participants were asked to respond to constituent messages about Social Security and Medicare policies, with the experimental conditions providing different levels of AI assistance in crafting responses. Task completion times, perceived naturalness, usefulness, satisfaction, and agency were measured and compared across conditions.

## Key Results
- Message-level suggestions reduced average task completion time to 8.53 minutes compared to 16.40 minutes (control) and 15.77 minutes (sentence-level)
- Message-level suggestions were rated significantly higher for naturalness and usefulness by participants
- Sentence-level suggestions maintained higher user agency ratings but required substantially more time to complete tasks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism behind message-level suggestions' effectiveness appears to stem from reducing cognitive load by providing complete, contextually appropriate responses rather than requiring users to integrate multiple sentence suggestions. This approach minimizes the mental effort needed to evaluate and combine individual suggestions, allowing for faster decision-making and response generation. However, this efficiency comes at the cost of user agency, as participants have less control over the final message composition.

## Foundational Learning
- AI-mediated communication effectiveness depends on balancing efficiency with user control
- Different suggestion formats (sentence-level vs. message-level) create distinct user experiences and performance outcomes
- User perception of naturalness and usefulness are critical factors in AI assistance adoption
- Agency and trust in AI systems are interconnected but distinct constructs that affect user satisfaction

## Architecture Onboarding
- Component map: User Interface -> AI Suggestion Engine -> Response Generation
- Critical path: Constituent message input -> AI analysis -> Suggestion generation -> User selection/approval -> Final response output
- Design tradeoffs: Speed/efficiency vs. user agency and trust
- Failure signatures: Overly generic suggestions reduce perceived naturalness; suggestions that don't match user intent reduce agency
- First experiments: 1) Test suggestion quality against different constituent message types, 2) Measure user satisfaction across multiple interaction sessions, 3) Compare suggestion acceptance rates between formats

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
None

## Limitations
- Sample consisted of Mechanical Turk workers rather than actual legislative staff, limiting external validity
- Simplified simulated task may not capture the full complexity of real legislative communication
- Agency measure conflated multiple constructs (ownership, trust, expertise) making interpretation difficult
- Lack of objective quality metrics for responses beyond completion time and agency ratings

## Confidence
- High confidence in relative speed differences between conditions
- Medium confidence in perceived naturalness and usefulness ratings
- Medium confidence in agency/trust findings (due to measurement concerns)
- Low confidence in generalizability to actual legislative communication contexts

## Next Checks
1. Replicate the study with actual legislative staff using real constituent correspondence to assess ecological validity
2. Implement objective quality metrics for constituent responses (e.g., expert ratings, constituent satisfaction proxies) beyond completion time and agency measures
3. Conduct a follow-up study testing the same conditions with message types varying in emotional intensity and complexity to identify boundary conditions for suggestion format effectiveness
