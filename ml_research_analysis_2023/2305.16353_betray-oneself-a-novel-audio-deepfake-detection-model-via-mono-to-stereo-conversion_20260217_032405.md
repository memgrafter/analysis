# Betray Oneself: A Novel Audio DeepFake Detection Model via Mono-to-Stereo Conversion

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.16353
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16353
- Reference count: 0
- Proposed a mono-to-stereo conversion based deepfake detection model achieving 1.34% EER on ASVspoof2019

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces M2S-ADD, a novel audio deepfake detection model that leverages stereo information to improve detection performance. Unlike traditional methods that use mono audio input, M2S-ADD first converts mono audio to stereo using a pretrained mono-to-stereo conversion model. The stereo audio is then processed through a dual-branch neural architecture that extracts features from both left and right channels. Experimental results on the ASVspoof2019 database demonstrate that M2S-ADD achieves state-of-the-art performance with an EER of 1.34%, outperforming baseline mono-based methods.

## Method Summary
M2S-ADD is an audio deepfake detection model that exploits stereo information for improved detection performance. The model consists of two main stages: mono-to-stereo conversion and deepfake detection. In the first stage, mono audio is converted to stereo using a pretrained mono-to-stereo conversion model. The stereo audio is then fed into a dual-branch neural architecture that processes the left and right channel signals separately. The extracted features from both channels are fused and fed into a classifier to determine the authenticity of the audio. The proposed approach is evaluated on the ASVspoof2019 database, where it achieves an EER of 1.34%, outperforming baseline mono-based methods.

## Key Results
- M2S-ADD achieves an EER of 1.34% on the ASVspoof2019 database, outperforming baseline mono-based methods.
- The proposed model leverages stereo information through mono-to-stereo conversion to improve deepfake detection performance.
- The dual-branch neural architecture processes left and right channel signals separately before fusing the extracted features for classification.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
M2S-ADD works by leveraging the additional information present in stereo audio to improve deepfake detection. Mono-to-stereo conversion introduces inter-channel dependencies that can be exploited by the dual-branch neural architecture. By processing the left and right channel signals separately, the model can capture channel-specific features and artifacts introduced during the conversion process. The fusion of these features allows the classifier to make more informed decisions about the authenticity of the audio.

## Limitations
- Performance evaluation is limited to the ASVspoof2019 database, which may not generalize to other datasets or real-world scenarios.
- Reliance on a pretrained mono-to-stereo conversion model introduces an additional dependency that could impact detection performance if the conversion quality varies.
- The dual-branch neural architecture may increase computational complexity compared to mono-based methods.
