# Methods and advancement of content-based fashion image retrieval: A Review

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2303.17371
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17371
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** Comprehensive review of content-based fashion image retrieval methods from 2017-2022, categorizing approaches into four main groups and analyzing networks, datasets, metrics, and loss functions.

## Executive Summary
This paper provides a systematic review of content-based fashion image retrieval (CBFIR) methods published between 2017 and 2022. The authors categorize CBFIR approaches into four main groups: image-guided, image & text-guided, sketch-guided, and video-guided methods. The review comprehensively analyzes various network architectures, datasets, evaluation metrics, and loss functions used in the field. The paper identifies key challenges including high-dimensional data handling, semantic gap bridging between visual signals and user intent, feature gaps between different media types, and domain-specific variations in fashion data.

The review highlights limitations of current methods and proposes future research directions, emphasizing the need for improved attribute labeling, style-aware retrieval systems, region-specific clothing attributes, and more comprehensive datasets for sketch and video-guided tasks. The paper serves as a valuable resource for researchers and practitioners in the fashion retrieval domain, providing insights into both established techniques and emerging approaches while identifying critical gaps that need to be addressed for advancing the field.

## Method Summary
The paper employs a systematic review methodology, categorizing CBFIR approaches based on input modalities and retrieval mechanisms. The authors analyze methods from 2017-2022, examining network architectures, loss functions, and evaluation metrics across different categories. The review methodology includes comprehensive coverage of recent developments while maintaining focus on practical implementation challenges and theoretical foundations of each approach category.

## Key Results
- CBFIR methods are systematically categorized into four main groups: image-guided, image & text-guided, sketch-guided, and video-guided approaches
- Major challenges identified include handling high-dimensional fashion data, bridging semantic gaps between visual signals and user intent, addressing feature gaps between different media types, and managing domain-specific variations
- Proposed future research directions include improving attribute labeling, developing style-aware retrieval systems, incorporating region-specific clothing attributes, and creating more comprehensive datasets for sketch and video-guided tasks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The systematic categorization of CBFIR methods into four distinct groups based on input modalities provides a clear framework for understanding the evolution and current state of fashion image retrieval. By analyzing methods from 2017-2022, the review captures the rapid advancements in deep learning techniques while identifying persistent challenges in bridging the gap between visual features and semantic understanding of fashion items.

The paper identifies several critical open questions for future research, including how to effectively incorporate region-specific clothing attributes into retrieval systems, methods for improving attribute labeling accuracy, approaches for developing style-aware retrieval systems that understand fashion semantics, and techniques for creating more comprehensive and diverse datasets for sketch and video-guided fashion retrieval tasks.
