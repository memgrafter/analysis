# ChartSumm: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Automatic Chart Summarization of Long and Short Summaries

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2304.13620
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13620
- **Reference count**: 14
- **Key outcome**: ChartSumm is a large-scale benchmark dataset for automatic chart summarization, consisting of 84,363 charts with metadata and descriptions. The dataset covers a wide range of topics and chart types, including line charts, bar charts, and pie charts. Extensive experiments with strong baseline models show that even though these models generate fluent and informative summaries, they often face issues like hallucination, missing out important data points, and incorrect explanation of complex trends. The dataset also shows potential for expansion to other languages using automated translation tools. ChartSumm is a challenging benchmark for future research in automatic chart summarization.

## Executive Summary
ChartSumm is a large-scale benchmark dataset for automatic chart summarization, containing 84,363 charts with metadata and human-written summaries. The dataset covers a wide range of topics and chart types, including line charts, bar charts, and pie charts. Extensive experiments with strong baseline models show that even though these models generate fluent and informative summaries, they often face issues like hallucination, missing out important data points, and incorrect explanation of complex trends. The dataset also shows potential for expansion to other languages using automated translation tools. ChartSumm is a challenging benchmark for future research in automatic chart summarization.

## Method Summary
ChartSumm contains 84,363 charts with metadata, data tables, titles, and human-written summaries compiled from Knoema and Statista. The charts cover various topics including Economy & Politics, Society & Science, Internet & Media, and others. The dataset includes line charts, bar charts, and pie charts. Models are fine-tuned on chart metadata and data tables using sequence-to-sequence training with standard cross-entropy loss. Evaluation uses BLEU, BLEURT, CIDER, Content Selection, and perplexity metrics, with separate test sets for short (Knoema) and long (Statista) summaries.

## Key Results
- ChartSumm contains 84,363 charts, nearly double the size of the previous largest dataset (Chart-To-Text with 44,085 examples)
- Models fine-tuned on ChartSumm achieve better performance than those fine-tuned on Chart-To-Text across both test-s and test-k sets
- Common failure modes include hallucination (generating irrelevant information), missing important data points, and incorrect trend explanations
- mT5 fine-tuned on machine-translated ChartSumm achieves promising results on Bengali summaries, suggesting cross-lingual potential

## Limitations
- The dataset relies on text-based chart representations rather than pixel-level understanding, limiting its applicability to chart extraction from images
- Limited diversity in chart types, focusing only on line charts, bar charts, and pie charts
- Potential bias in source data from Knoema and Statista, which may not represent all chart types and topics
