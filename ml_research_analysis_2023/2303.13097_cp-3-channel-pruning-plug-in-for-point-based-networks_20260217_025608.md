# CP$^3$: Channel Pruning Plug-in for Point-based Networks

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.13097
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13097
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: CP³ improves 3D point cloud network pruning by incorporating spatial coordinates and reusing discarded points, achieving 1.94% higher accuracy than baseline 2D pruning on ScanObjectNN at 57.8% pruning rate.

## Executive Summary
CP$^3$ (Channel Pruning Plug-in) addresses the challenge of applying 2D CNN pruning techniques to 3D point cloud networks by introducing two key innovations. The method recognizes that standard 2D pruning metrics fail to account for the spatial coordinates inherent in point cloud data, leading to suboptimal pruning decisions. CP$^3$ enhances traditional pruning approaches with a coordinate-aware metric and a knowledge recycling module that reuses information from pruned points, resulting in more robust and effective channel pruning for point-based networks.

## Method Summary
CP$^3$ extends 2D CNN pruning methods to 3D point cloud networks by addressing the fundamental mismatch between image-based and point-based data. The approach introduces a coordinate-enhanced metric that incorporates spatial information when evaluating channel importance, ensuring that pruning decisions consider both feature values and their 3D positions. Additionally, CP$^3$ implements a knowledge recycling mechanism that extracts and reuses useful information from channels marked for pruning, preventing loss of valuable spatial information. The plug-in is designed to work with various point-based backbone architectures without requiring architectural modifications.

## Key Results
- CP³ improves classification accuracy on ScanObjectNN by 1.94% at 57.8% pruning rate compared to baseline 2D pruning methods
- Method demonstrates consistent performance gains across multiple tasks: classification, segmentation, and detection
- Evaluated on diverse datasets including ModelNet40, ScanObjectNN, S3DIS, ScanNetV2, and SUN RGB-D

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
CP³ works by addressing two critical limitations when applying 2D pruning to 3D point clouds: the neglect of spatial coordinates and the wasteful discarding of potentially useful information. The coordinate-enhanced metric ensures that pruning decisions account for both the feature values and their spatial positions, which is crucial for maintaining geometric relationships in point clouds. The knowledge recycling module mitigates the information loss that occurs when pruning channels by intelligently extracting and reusing discarded information, leading to more robust pruning decisions.

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map:** Input point cloud → Point-based backbone → CP³ pruning module → Pruned network → Task-specific output
**Critical Path:** The pruning decision pipeline that incorporates coordinate information and knowledge recycling is the critical path, as it directly determines which channels are retained
**Design Tradeoffs:** CP³ trades additional computation during pruning for improved final model accuracy and efficiency; the coordinate metric adds overhead but prevents costly pruning mistakes
**Failure Signatures:** If pruning rate is too aggressive, the coordinate metric may fail to preserve sufficient geometric information; knowledge recycling may not compensate if too much information is discarded initially
