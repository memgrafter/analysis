# Enhanced Iterated local search for the technician routing and scheduling problem

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.13532
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.13532
- Reference count: 38
- Key outcome: eILS achieves three times lower overall gap than existing methods and improves 34/56 best-known solutions

## Executive Summary
This paper presents an enhanced iterated local search (eILS) approach for the technician routing and scheduling problem (TRSP), a complex variant of workforce scheduling that incorporates time windows, skill compatibility, and resource requirements. The proposed method combines intensification through elite solution sets with perturbation strategies using removal-repair heuristics and specialized local search operators. Experimental results on benchmark instances demonstrate significant performance improvements over existing methods, with eILS achieving the best overall gap and improving the best-known solutions for more than half of the tested instances while maintaining reasonable computational times.

## Method Summary
The eILS algorithm employs a two-phase approach consisting of intensification and perturbation phases. During intensification, the method explores neighborhoods of local optima using an elite set of high-quality solutions to enhance diversification. The perturbation phase uses removal-repair heuristics to modify solutions and escape local optima. Local search operators are specifically tailored for TRSP constraints including technician skills, time windows, and resource requirements. The algorithm iteratively applies these phases while maintaining and updating the elite solution set to guide search space exploration.

## Key Results
- eILS achieved the best overall gap, three times lower than existing methods
- Improved best-known solutions for 34 out of 56 benchmark instances
- Demonstrated reasonable computational times while maintaining solution quality

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The enhanced iterated local search works by combining intensification and perturbation strategies that balance exploitation of high-quality solutions with exploration of new regions in the search space. The elite solution set enables extensive neighborhood exploration around local optima while the perturbation phase prevents premature convergence. Tailored local search operators specifically address TRSP constraints like skill compatibility and resource requirements, while removal-repair heuristics maintain solution feasibility during modifications.
