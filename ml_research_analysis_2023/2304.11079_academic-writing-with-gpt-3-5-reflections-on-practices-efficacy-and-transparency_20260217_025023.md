# Academic Writing with GPT-3.5: Reflections on Practices, Efficacy and Transparency

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.11079
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.11079
- Reference count: 40
- This study explored the use of GPT-3.5 for academic writing through a personal experience experiment

## Executive Summary
This study investigated the application of GPT-3.5 for academic writing through a structured experiment where five distinct methods were identified and evaluated. The author documented their writing process transparently by comparing original prompts, GPT-3.5 outputs, and final edited text, revealing average differences of 70.27% from original to output and 29.13% from output to final edit. The research found that Chunk Stylist and Talk Textualizer methods were most effective for maintaining authorial voice while enhancing productivity. The study also examined ethical considerations including plagiarism risks, misinformation concerns, and ecological impacts of AI-assisted writing.

## Method Summary
The research employed a personal experience methodology where the author systematically applied five distinct methods of using GPT-3.5 for academic writing: Chunk Stylist, Bullet-to-Paragraph, Talk Textualizer, Research Buddy, and Polisher. Each method was evaluated for efficacy, transparency, and ethical implications. The writing process was documented through transparent comparisons between original prompts, GPT-3.5 outputs, and final edited text. This approach allowed for quantitative measurement of changes at each stage, with average differences calculated between text versions to assess the impact of AI assistance on the writing process.

## Key Results
- Five distinct methods for using GPT-3.5 in academic writing were identified and applied
- Chunk Stylist and Talk Textualizer methods were found most effective for retaining authorial voice while improving productivity
- Average differences of 70.27% from original to output and 29.13% from output to final edit were observed

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The study demonstrates that GPT-3.5 can effectively assist academic writing through structured approaches that balance automation with human oversight. The identified methods work by leveraging GPT-3.5's language generation capabilities while maintaining authorial control through specific prompting strategies. The transparency in documenting the writing process allows for clear attribution and understanding of how AI assistance shapes the final output. The effectiveness of different methods varies based on their ability to preserve the writer's unique voice while enhancing productivity and quality.

## Limitations
- The study relies on a single author's experience, limiting generalizability across different writing styles and academic disciplines
- Subjective evaluation of method efficacy introduces potential bias in effectiveness assessments
- The study lacks standardized benchmarks for what constitutes "effective" academic writing when using AI assistance

## Confidence
- High Confidence: Identification of five distinct methods and transparent documentation approach
- Medium Confidence: Efficacy rankings of different methods based on single-author experience
