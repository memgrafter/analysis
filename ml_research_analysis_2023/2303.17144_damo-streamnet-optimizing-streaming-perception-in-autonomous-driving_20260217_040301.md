# DAMO-StreamNet: Optimizing Streaming Perception in Autonomous Driving

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.17144
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17144
- Reference count: 15
- DAMO-StreamNet achieves state-of-the-art performance on Argoverse-HD with 37.8% sAP at normal resolution and 43.3% sAP at large resolution

## Executive Summary
DAMO-StreamNet introduces a novel framework for real-time perception in autonomous driving, specifically addressing challenges in object scale adaptation and long-term motion consistency. The method combines a robust neck structure with deformable convolution to enhance receptive field and feature alignment. A dual-branch architecture integrates short-path semantic and long-path temporal features for improved motion state prediction. Asymmetric Knowledge Distillation at the logits level efficiently transfers knowledge between teacher and student networks. The approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on the Argoverse-HD dataset without requiring additional data, setting a new benchmark for streaming perception in autonomous driving applications.

## Method Summary
DAMO-StreamNet employs a dual-branch architecture where short-path features capture immediate semantic information while long-path features maintain temporal consistency across video frames. The framework incorporates deformable convolution within its neck structure to adaptively adjust receptive fields based on object scale and position. Asymmetric Knowledge Distillation transfers knowledge from a teacher network to a student network at the logits level, improving efficiency without sacrificing accuracy. A real-time forecasting mechanism dynamically updates support frame features during inference, enabling continuous adaptation to changing driving conditions. The method addresses the unique challenges of streaming perception where objects must be tracked and classified across continuous video sequences rather than individual frames.

## Key Results
- Achieves 37.8% sAP at normal resolution on Argoverse-HD dataset
- Achieves 43.3% sAP at large resolution on Argoverse-HD dataset
- Surpasses existing methods without requiring additional training data

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
DAMO-StreamNet's effectiveness stems from its integrated approach to handling both spatial and temporal challenges in streaming perception. The deformable convolution allows the network to adaptively focus on relevant spatial regions while maintaining awareness of object scale variations. The dual-branch architecture enables simultaneous processing of immediate semantic information and historical temporal context, which is critical for tracking objects across frames. Asymmetric Knowledge Distillation provides an efficient way to transfer complex patterns from larger teacher models to more compact student models, reducing computational requirements while maintaining accuracy. The real-time forecasting mechanism ensures the system can adapt to new information as it becomes available, rather than relying on static pre-computed features.
