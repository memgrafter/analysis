# Extracting Psychological Indicators Using Question Answering

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.14891
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14891
- Reference count: 3
- Primary result: Fine-tuning RoBERTa on SQuAD 2.0 with 33% unanswerable examples yields F1 79.83 and EM 83.05 on Reddit comment trait extraction.

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a method for extracting trait-indicative sentences from Reddit comments using extractive question answering. The approach leverages a RoBERTa model fine-tuned on SQuAD 2.0 and further adapted to Reddit data. Three experiments with varying proportions of unanswerable questions (20%, 33%, and 66%) were conducted to assess their impact on model performance. The best F1 score of 87.32 and EM of 84.70 were achieved with 66% unanswerable examples, though due to dataset imbalance, the 33% configuration (F1 79.83, EM 83.05) is considered the baseline. These results align with SQuAD 2.0 benchmarks and provide a foundation for automated psychological traits prediction.

## Method Summary
The method fine-tunes a RoBERTa-base model first on SQuAD 2.0, then on Reddit comments formatted as extractive QA pairs. Answer spans are identified using cosine similarity (≥0.63) to handcrafted trait-indicative reference sentences (eTRSes). The model is trained with varying proportions of unanswerable questions (20%, 33%, 66%) to balance learning "no answer" decisions with span prediction. Performance is evaluated using F1 and exact match scores on both answerable and unanswerable subsets.

## Key Results
- Best F1 score of 87.32 and EM of 84.70 achieved with 66% unanswerable examples.
- Baseline F1 of 79.83 and EM of 83.05 obtained with 33% unanswerable examples.
- Performance degrades for answerable questions when unanswerable proportion is increased.
- Results are in line with SQuAD 2.0 benchmark and provide a good baseline for further research.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
Fine-tuning RoBERTa on SQuAD 2.0 with a balanced proportion of answerable and unanswerable questions enables it to learn when to abstain from predicting an answer span. The model is exposed to examples where the correct answer is the empty string, forcing it to learn a "no answer" decision boundary alongside extractive span prediction. If the unanswerable examples are not truly unanswerable or the model overfits to spurious cues, the decision boundary will fail, leading to false positives or negatives.

### Mechanism 2
Using sentence similarity (cosine similarity ≥0.63) to identify answer spans ensures that extracted text is semantically related to the BIG5 trait being queried. Sentences are matched against trait-indicative reference sentences (eTRSes), and those exceeding the threshold are labeled as correct answers for the corresponding trait question. If the eTRS examples are not sufficiently diverse or the similarity threshold is too low/high, the model will be trained on noisy or incomplete labels, harming generalization.

### Mechanism 3
Varying the percentage of unanswerable examples in training controls the model's sensitivity to unanswerable cases and balances performance across answerable and unanswerable subsets. By adjusting the proportion of negative examples, the model's loss function is weighted differently toward learning the "no answer" decision versus extractive span prediction. If the Reddit domain has different statistical properties than SQuAD 2.0, the fixed ratio may not generalize, leading to degraded performance.

  - **Why needed here**: The model must learn to output "no answer" when the trait is not present in the context, requiring negative examples during training.
  - **Quick check question**: What training signal does the model receive when the answer is an empty string, and how does this differ from a normal span prediction?

  - **Why needed here**: Sentences are matched to trait-indicative references using cosine similarity, requiring understanding of vector representations and similarity thresholds.

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map**: Reddit comment (context), BIG5 trait question -> RoBERTa-base fine-tuned on SQuAD 2.0 -> further fine-tuned on Reddit QA pairs -> Predicted answer span (text) or empty string (no answer) -> F1 score and exact match (EM) evaluation

- **Critical path**:
  1. Load pre-trained RoBERTa-base-squad2 model
  2. Prepare Reddit dataset with context, trait question, and answer spans (or empty)
  3. Fine-tune on Reddit data with chosen unanswerable ratio
  4. Evaluate on validation set using F1 and EM

- **Design tradeoffs**:
  - Using extractive QA preserves original text but limits flexibility compared to generative approaches.
  - Fixed unanswerable ratio simplifies training but may not adapt to domain-specific answer distributions.
  - Cosine similarity threshold (0.63) is heuristic and may require tuning for new datasets.

- **Failure signatures**:
  - Low F1 on answerable examples but high EM on unanswerable: model is biased toward "no answer."
  - High F1 on answerable but low EM on unanswerable: model fails to learn when to abstain.
  - Both metrics low: poor fine-tuning or noisy labels.

- **First 3 experiments**:
  1. Fine-tune with 20% unanswerable examples; expect lower performance on unanswerable subset.
  2. Fine-tune with 33% unanswerable examples; baseline aligned with SQuAD 2.0.
  3. Fine-tune with 66% unanswerable examples; expect higher performance on unanswerable subset but lower on answerable.

### Open Question 2

- **What evidence would resolve it**: Applying the method to datasets from other social media platforms and evaluating its performance would determine its applicability across different sources.

- **Question**: What is the impact of varying the similarity threshold for identifying trait-indicative sentences on the model's performance?

- **Why unresolved**: The choice of the threshold value is arbitrary, and its impact on the model's performance is not investigated.
- **What evidence would resolve it**: Experimenting with different similarity threshold values and analyzing the resulting changes in performance metrics would provide insights into the optimal threshold for this task.

## Limitations

- Evaluation is constrained by dataset imbalance, with only 3.9% of validation examples being answerable.
- The method relies on cosine similarity to predefined trait-indicative sentences (eTRSes), but the diversity and representativeness of these examples are not assessed.
- The fixed 0.63 similarity threshold is heuristic and may not generalize across different Reddit communities or languages.

## Confidence

- **High confidence**: The mechanism of fine-tuning RoBERTa on SQuAD 2.0 to learn unanswerable question handling is well-established and directly supported by the SQuAD 2.0 benchmark literature.
- **Medium confidence**: The claim that 33% unanswerable examples is optimal is supported by SQuAD 2.0 but may not hold for Reddit data with different statistical properties.
- **Low confidence**: The assertion that the method effectively extracts trait-indicative sentences is based on similarity thresholds and handcrafted labels, which are not independently validated.
