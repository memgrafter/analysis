# RNN-Guard: Certified Robustness Against Multi-frame Attacks for Recurrent Neural Networks

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.07980
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.07980
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: Introduces InterZono abstract domain for certified robustness against multi-frame attacks in RNNs, achieving 15.47 to 67.65 percentage points higher certified robust accuracy than other methods.

## Executive Summary
RNN-Guard is the first certified defense against multi-frame adversarial attacks for recurrent neural networks. The key innovation is the InterZono abstract domain, which provides tighter linear relaxations than Zonotope while maintaining the same time complexity. By adopting a perturb-all-frame strategy and introducing InterZono to address precision issues, RNN-Guard significantly improves certified robust accuracy against multi-frame attacks while also defending against one-frame attacks.

## Method Summary
RNN-Guard introduces the InterZono abstract domain to address precision issues in linear relaxations when using the perturb-all-frame strategy for multi-frame attack certification. InterZono combines a main domain (propagating variable relations) and a support domain (refining bounds) to compute tighter output bounds through their intersection. The method extends to certified training by computing a robustness loss based on the output InterZono and combining it with standard loss. Experiments demonstrate InterZono's superior precision over Zonotope and RNN-Guard's effectiveness across various datasets and model structures.

## Key Results
- InterZono is proven to be more precise than Zonotope while carrying the same time complexity
- Certified robust accuracy with InterZono is up to 2.18 times higher than with Zonotope
- RNN-Guard achieves 15.47 to 67.65 percentage points higher certified robust accuracy against multi-frame attacks compared to other methods
- The perturb-all-frame strategy captures all potential adversarial examples in multi-frame attacks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: InterZono provides tighter linear relaxations than Zonotope for non-linear RNN operations under perturb-all-frame strategy
- Mechanism: Combines main domain (variable relations) and support domain (bounds refinement) with intersection operation
- Core assumption: Intersection of two zonotopes yields tighter over-approximation than either domain alone
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "InterZono is more precise than Zonotope while carrying the same time complexity"
- Break condition: Numerical instability in intersection or overly conservative support domain in high dimensions

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Perturb-all-frame strategy captures all potential adversarial examples in multi-frame attacks
- Mechanism: Simultaneously perturbing all frames within bounded ℓ∞ norm creates perturbation space S2 that encompasses all multi-frame attacks
- Core assumption: Any multi-frame adversarial example must have each frame perturbed within the same ℓ∞ bound ε
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "We adopt the perturb-all-frame strategy to construct perturbation spaces consistent with those in multi-frame attacks"
- Break condition: Multi-frame attacks succeeding with different norms or computationally intractable perturbation space

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: RNN-Guard achieves high certified robust accuracy by combining perturb-all-frame with InterZono
- Mechanism: Uses output InterZono to compute robustness loss during training, encouraging correct classification across perturbation space
- Core assumption: Minimizing robustness loss leads to model maintaining high confidence across entire InterZono over-approximation
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "certified robust accuracy calculated by RNN-Guard with InterZono is up to 2.18 times higher than that with Zonotope"
- Break condition: Robustness loss optimization conflicting with standard loss, causing unacceptable accuracy degradation

## Foundational Learning

- Concept: Abstract interpretation and abstract domains in neural network verification
  - Why needed here: Used to over-approximate set of possible model outputs under input perturbations for certified robustness
  - Quick check question: What is the relationship between precision of an abstract domain and tightness of over-approximation it provides?

- Concept: Linear relaxations for non-linear activation functions
  - Why needed here: Designs abstract transformers using linear relaxations (parallelogram-style and box-style) to handle non-linear operations in RNN verification
  - Quick check question: How does choice of relaxation style (box vs. parallelogram) affect precision of output bounds for a given activation function?

- Concept: Certified training and the robustness-accuracy tradeoff
  - Why needed here: RNN-Guard extends to certified training by incorporating robustness loss into training objective, potentially decreasing clean accuracy while improving certified robustness
  - Quick check question: What is typical impact of certified training on clean accuracy, and why does this tradeoff exist?

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: Perturbation space → InterZono construction → Abstract transformer propagation → Output bounds computation → Robustness verification/training
- Critical path: Perturbation space → InterZono construction → Abstract transformer propagation → Output bounds computation → Robustness verification/training
- Design tradeoffs: InterZono vs. Zonotope precision vs. computational overhead; perturb-all-frame strategy vs. perturbation space size; certified training vs. clean accuracy
- Failure signatures: Numerical instability in InterZono intersection; overly conservative bounds leading to false non-robustness claims; training instability due to conflicting loss objectives
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Implement InterZono domain with main and support domains, verify intersection operation on simple 2D examples
  2. Implement abstract transformers for tanh and sigmoid operations, compare output bounds with Zonotope on benchmark RNN layers
  3. Implement certification module and verify robustness of small LSTM model on toy dataset (e.g., IMDB sentiment)

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

- Question: How does InterZono's precision compare to other abstract domains like Box or Parallelogram across different non-linear activation functions beyond tanh?
- Basis in paper: [explicit] Paper compares InterZono to Zonotope and mentions other abstract transformers but lacks comprehensive comparisons across activation functions
- Why unresolved: Focuses primarily on tanh demonstration, leaving relative performance with other common activation functions in RNNs unexplored
- What evidence would resolve it: Systematic experiments comparing InterZono to other abstract domains across various activation functions, measuring precision and efficiency trade-offs

## Limitations

- The empirical validation of InterZono's superiority over Zonotope relies on limited experimental evidence with only one figure showing bound tightness
- The certified training extension's effectiveness is primarily evaluated through certified robust accuracy metrics with limited discussion of clean accuracy trade-offs
- The method's scalability to larger transformer-based models is acknowledged as extremely challenging due to their enormous number of parameters

## Confidence

- High Confidence: Theoretical foundation of InterZono construction and perturb-all-frame strategy's ability to capture multi-frame adversarial examples
- Medium Confidence: Experimental results showing significant improvements in certified robust accuracy, though limited scope and implementation details reduce reproducibility confidence
- Low Confidence: Claims about InterZono's superior precision compared to Zonotope and certified training extension's effectiveness based on limited experimental evidence
