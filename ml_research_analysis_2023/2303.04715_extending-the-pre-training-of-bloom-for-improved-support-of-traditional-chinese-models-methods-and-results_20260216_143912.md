# Extending the Pre-Training of BLOOM for Improved Support of Traditional Chinese: Models, Methods and Results

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.04715
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04715
- Reference count: 21
- Key outcome: BLOOM-zh achieves lower perplexity and higher accuracy than predecessors on Traditional Chinese benchmarks while maintaining English performance

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces BLOOM-zh, a multilingual language model extending BLOOM's pre-training to enhance support for Traditional Chinese. The authors curated 7.4 billion tokens of Traditional Chinese and English data from diverse sources, then further pre-trained BLOOMZ checkpoint. BLOOM-zh achieves lower perplexity and higher accuracy than its predecessors on Traditional Chinese benchmarks like DRCD, FGC, and a newly created TTQA test, while maintaining English performance. Toxicity and bias evaluations show BLOOM-zh exhibits similar or reduced sensitivity to toxic prompts compared to BLOOM/BLOOMZ, with marginal gender bias observed in coreference tasks. The model weights and benchmarks are released publicly for research use.

## Method Summary
The authors curated 7.4 billion tokens of Traditional Chinese and English data from diverse sources including literature, government documents, and online forums. They further pre-trained the BLOOMZ checkpoint using this curated dataset, creating BLOOM-zh. The model was evaluated on Traditional Chinese benchmarks including DRCD, FGC, and a newly created TTQA test. Toxicity and bias assessments were conducted using 100 prompts across three evaluation tools. The study employed perplexity measurements, accuracy metrics, and bias detection tools to comprehensively evaluate model performance.

## Key Results
- BLOOM-zh achieves lower perplexity on Traditional Chinese text compared to BLOOM and BLOOMZ
- Model shows higher accuracy on Traditional Chinese benchmarks (DRCD, FGC, TTQA) while maintaining English performance
- Toxicity evaluations indicate BLOOM-zh exhibits similar or reduced sensitivity to toxic prompts compared to predecessors

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The extended pre-training on 7.4 billion tokens of Traditional Chinese data allows BLOOM-zh to better capture linguistic patterns and nuances specific to Traditional Chinese. The diverse data sources, including literature, government documents, and online forums, provide comprehensive coverage of the language's usage contexts. By continuing training from the BLOOMZ checkpoint rather than starting from scratch, the model leverages existing multilingual capabilities while specializing in Traditional Chinese.

- Dataset curation depends on publicly available sources that may not represent complete linguistic landscape
