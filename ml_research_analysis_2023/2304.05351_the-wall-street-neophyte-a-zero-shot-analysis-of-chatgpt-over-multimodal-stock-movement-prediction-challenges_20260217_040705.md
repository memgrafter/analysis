# The Wall Street Neophyte: A Zero-Shot Analysis of ChatGPT Over MultiModal Stock Movement Prediction Challenges

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.05351
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05351
- Reference count: 6
- Key outcome: ChatGPT underperforms compared to state-of-the-art and traditional methods in multimodal stock prediction, with accuracies ranging from 48.16% to 55.43%.

## Executive Summary
This paper evaluates ChatGPT's zero-shot capabilities for predicting stock price movements using multimodal data (historical prices and tweets). The study finds that ChatGPT underperforms not only advanced methods but also basic approaches like linear regression across three benchmark datasets. While including tweets provides some benefit, the overall performance remains weak with Matthews Correlation Coefficients near zero. Chain-of-Thought prompting shows limited improvement in explainability but does not substantially enhance prediction accuracy. The results indicate ChatGPT lacks the robustness needed for reliable financial predictions without specialized training.

## Method Summary
The study conducts a zero-shot analysis of ChatGPT (gpt-3.5-turbo) for binary stock movement prediction using three benchmark datasets containing historical price data and tweets. Researchers employ both vanilla and Chain-of-Thought prompting strategies, with and without tweets. Predictions are evaluated using accuracy and Matthews Correlation Coefficient. The approach tests ChatGPT's ability to fuse numerical price features with textual sentiment from tweets without any task-specific training or fine-tuning.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT achieves accuracies between 48.16% and 55.43% across three datasets, underperforming both state-of-the-art and traditional methods.
- Including tweets improves performance across all datasets, though gains remain modest.
- Chain-of-Thought prompting enhances explainability but provides minimal accuracy improvements.
- MCC scores remain near zero, indicating predictions are close to random guessing.
- ChatGPT struggles with complex multimodal relationships, showing limitations in fusing numerical and textual features.

- Datasets focus on high-trade-volume US stocks, limiting generalizability to other markets or stock types.
