# Semi-supervised counterfactual explanations
## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.12634
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.12634
- Reference count: 10
- Primary result: Joint training of autoencoder and classifier produces sparser, more interpretable counterfactuals

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a semi-supervised learning framework for generating counterfactual explanations that are more faithful to both data distributions and class boundaries. By jointly training an autoencoder with a classifier, the latent space becomes aware of class tag information, enabling more robust prototype-guided counterfactual searches. The approach demonstrates improved interpretability metrics across multiple datasets while requiring fewer labeled examples than traditional methods.

## Method Summary
The method trains an autoencoder jointly with a classifier in a semi-supervised manner, allowing the latent space to incorporate class information during training. This joint optimization aligns the latent representation with class boundaries, which improves the quality of counterfactual explanations generated through prototype-guided searches. The framework can operate effectively even with limited labeled data, making it suitable for post-hoc explanation generation scenarios where labeling resources are constrained.

## Key Results
- Generated counterfactuals are sparser than those produced by separately trained autoencoders
- Improved proximity metrics indicate better alignment with target class distributions
- Enhanced interpretability metrics across multiple benchmark datasets
- Effective performance maintained with limited labeled data

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The joint training architecture allows the latent space to simultaneously optimize for reconstruction accuracy and classification performance. This creates a representation that naturally separates classes while preserving reconstruction capabilities, enabling more meaningful counterfactual perturbations that respect both data manifold structure and class boundaries.

## Foundational Learning
- **Autoencoder fundamentals**: Learn compressed data representations while preserving reconstruction ability; needed to create interpretable latent spaces; quick check: can the model reconstruct input samples accurately
- **Counterfactual explanation basics**: Generate minimally modified inputs that change classifier predictions; needed to understand the explanation task; quick check: can the method produce valid counterfactuals that flip predictions
- **Semi-supervised learning principles**: Leverage both labeled and unlabeled data for training; needed to understand the efficiency gains; quick check: does performance improve with additional unlabeled data
- **Prototype-guided search**: Use representative samples to guide counterfactual generation; needed for efficient exploration of latent space; quick check: are generated counterfactuals close to actual class prototypes
- **Latent space geometry**: Understanding how class boundaries manifest in compressed representations; needed to interpret the quality of counterfactuals; quick check: do class clusters separate clearly in latent space
- **Faithfulness metrics**: Measures of how well explanations reflect model behavior; needed to evaluate counterfactual quality; quick check: do improved metrics correlate with human interpretability

## Architecture Onboarding
Component map: Data → Autoencoder+Classifier → Latent Space → Counterfactual Generator → Explanations

Critical path: The joint training loop where autoencoder reconstruction loss and classifier accuracy gradients flow through shared latent representations. This is where the semi-supervised benefits materialize through mutual regularization between reconstruction and classification objectives.

Design tradeoffs: Joint vs. separate training (this work favors joint for better latent space alignment), reconstruction accuracy vs. classification performance (balanced through weighted loss terms), prototype selection strategy (affects counterfactual diversity and quality).

Failure signatures: Poor counterfactuals when latent space fails to capture class boundaries clearly, reconstruction artifacts that propagate to explanations, sensitivity to initialization affecting joint optimization convergence, limited diversity in generated counterfactuals.

First experiments: 1) Verify reconstruction quality on held-out data to ensure autoencoder learning, 2) Test classification accuracy to confirm classifier effectiveness, 3) Generate baseline counterfactuals with separately trained autoencoder to establish performance floor.

## Limitations
- Empirical validation limited to benchmark datasets without real-world deployment testing
- Interpretability improvements measured through synthetic metrics rather than human studies
- Unclear definition of what constitutes "limited" labeled data for the claimed efficiency benefits

## Confidence
- Core methodological claims: Medium (sound theoretical foundation but limited empirical scope)
- Limited labeled data effectiveness: Low (insufficient baseline comparisons and definition of "limited")
- Interpretability improvements: Medium (consistent metric improvements but lack of external validation)
