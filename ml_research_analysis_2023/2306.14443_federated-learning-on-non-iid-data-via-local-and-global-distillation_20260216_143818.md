## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of federated learning on non-iid data, where existing methods based on FedAvg suffer from poor performance and high communication overhead. The authors propose FedND, a federated learning algorithm that leverages knowledge distillation for optimization. In the client, they introduce a self-distillation method to train the local model, while in the server, they generate noisy samples for each client and use them to distill other clients. The global model is then obtained by aggregating the local models. Experimental results demonstrate that FedND achieves the best performance and is more communication-efficient compared to state-of-the-art methods. The paper provides insights into the effectiveness of the proposed approach and its ability to handle non-iid data scenarios in federated learning.

## Method Summary
The paper introduces FedND, a federated learning algorithm that combines local self-distillation and global distillation to handle non-iid data. In the client, a self-distillation method is used to train the local model by generating pseudo-labels from the model's predictions on unlabeled data. In the server, noisy samples are generated for each client, and these samples are used to distill other clients' models. The global model is then obtained by aggregating the local models. This approach aims to improve performance and communication efficiency compared to existing methods based on FedAvg.

## Key Results

## Foundational Learning
- Federated Learning: A distributed learning paradigm where multiple clients collaborate to train a global model without sharing their local data. Needed to understand the context and challenges of training models on decentralized data.
- Knowledge Distillation: A technique where a smaller model (student) is trained to mimic the behavior of a larger model (teacher) by minimizing the difference in their output distributions. Quick check: Verify that the student model's performance approaches that of the teacher model on a held-out validation set.
- Non-IID Data: Data that is not identically and independently distributed across different clients in federated learning. Needed to understand the challenges of training models on heterogeneous data distributions.
