# Probabilistic Bilevel Coreset Selection

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.09880
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.09880
- Reference count: 40
- This paper presents a novel approach for coreset selection in deep learning, formulated as a continuous probabilistic bilevel optimization problem.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a probabilistic bilevel optimization framework for coreset selection in deep learning. The method learns a probabilistic weight for each training sample, enabling efficient coreset selection through sampling-based inner loops and outer loop updates based on model performance. The approach demonstrates superior performance across multiple tasks including data summarization, continual learning, streaming scenarios, and feature selection, particularly excelling in challenging conditions like label noise and class imbalance.

## Method Summary
The proposed method formulates coreset selection as a bilevel optimization problem where the outer loop updates sample probabilities based on model performance while the inner loop samples coresets for training. The approach uses unbiased policy gradient for efficient optimization, avoiding the computational complexity of implicit differentiation. The method learns a continuous probability distribution over training samples, enabling differentiable and efficient coreset selection that can be integrated into existing deep learning pipelines.

## Key Results
- Demonstrates superior performance in data summarization, continual learning, streaming, and feature selection tasks
- Shows notable improvements in challenging scenarios with label noise and class imbalance
- Outperforms existing methods particularly in settings where traditional approaches fail
- Provides computational efficiency and convergence properties

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by learning a probabilistic weighting scheme for training samples through bilevel optimization. The inner loop samples coresets based on current probability distributions, while the outer loop updates these probabilities based on model performance metrics. This creates a feedback loop where samples that contribute more to model performance receive higher selection probabilities over time.
