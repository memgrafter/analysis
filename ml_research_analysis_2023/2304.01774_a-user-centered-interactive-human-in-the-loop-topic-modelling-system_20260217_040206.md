# A User-Centered, Interactive, Human-in-the-Loop Topic Modelling System

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.01774
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.01774
- Reference count: 11
- Primary result: Interactive system enables users to refine topic models through six refinement operations, improving topic coherence from 0.445 to 0.482

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a user-centered interactive system for topic modeling that enables human-in-the-loop refinement of topic models. The system supports six refinement operations (add/remove words, swap word order, remove documents, merge/split topics) and introduces a novel query-driven topic modeling approach with targeted-analysis capabilities. The system was evaluated through user studies with 20 participants and two real-world use cases, demonstrating improved topic quality and user satisfaction compared to traditional LDA methods.

## Method Summary
The system uses a query-driven topic modeling algorithm as its foundation, supporting both full-analysis (traditional topic modeling) and targeted-analysis (focused on specific aspects) capabilities. Users can iteratively refine topic models through six operations: adding or removing words from topics, swapping word order within topics, removing documents from topics, merging two topics, or splitting topics. A novel topic words suggestion feature helps users identify relevant words to add based on semantic similarity. The system maintains a model history panel allowing users to track and revert changes throughout the refinement process.

## Key Results
- Topic coherence scores improved from 0.445 to 0.482 in one dataset
- Users reported high satisfaction with new features: model history panel (4.35/5 agreement) and words suggestion feature (4.1/5 agreement)
- System enabled researchers to identify more relevant topics in real-world applications compared to traditional approaches
- User studies with 20 participants and two use cases demonstrated better quality topics than traditional LDA

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The system works by providing users direct control over topic refinement through intuitive operations while maintaining model coherence. The query-driven approach allows targeted analysis on specific aspects of the corpus, making the process more efficient than traditional full-analysis methods. The words suggestion feature leverages semantic similarity to help users identify relevant terms they might otherwise miss, reducing the expertise barrier for effective topic modeling.

## Foundational Learning
- Interactive refinement operations: Why needed - Enables domain experts to improve model quality based on their knowledge; Quick check - Users can successfully perform all six refinement operations
- Query-driven topic modeling: Why needed - Provides targeted analysis capability beyond traditional LDA; Quick check - System can focus on specific aspects of corpus as requested by user
- Semantic similarity for word suggestions: Why needed - Helps users identify relevant terms they might miss; Quick check - Suggested words are semantically related to topic content
- Model history tracking: Why needed - Allows users to track changes and revert when needed; Quick check - Users can successfully navigate and revert to previous model states
- Human-in-the-loop validation: Why needed - Ensures model quality through expert knowledge; Quick check - Users can effectively validate and improve generated topics
- Iterative refinement process: Why needed - Enables gradual improvement of model quality; Quick check - Multiple refinement cycles lead to improved topic coherence

## Architecture Onboarding

Component Map: User Interface -> Query Engine -> Topic Model -> Refinement Operations -> Display

Critical Path: User selects refinement operation -> System updates topic model -> Display shows updated topics

Design Tradeoffs: Interactive refinement provides better quality but requires more user effort vs. fully automated approaches; query-driven approach offers efficiency but may miss unexpected topics

Failure Signatures: Poor topic coherence despite refinement suggests inappropriate operations or insufficient corpus; user confusion with operations indicates need for better guidance

First Experiments:
1. Test basic refinement operations (add/remove words) on simple dataset
2. Verify query-driven targeting works as expected on specific corpus aspects
3. Validate semantic similarity suggestions produce relevant words

## Limitations
- Evaluation relies on limited user studies with only 20 participants and two use cases
- Reported topic quality improvements are modest (0.445 to 0.482 coherence score)
- User satisfaction ratings may be influenced by novelty effects
- Results may not generalize beyond tested domains
- Long-term effectiveness of iterative refinement not fully established

## Confidence
- System functionality and novel features: High
- User satisfaction metrics: Medium
- Topic quality improvements: Medium
- Generalizability of results: Low

## Next Checks
1. Conduct larger-scale user studies (n > 100) across diverse domains and user expertise levels
2. Perform longitudinal studies to assess sustained effectiveness of iterative refinement over multiple sessions
3. Compare query-driven topic modeling performance against state-of-the-art alternatives using standardized benchmarks and datasets
