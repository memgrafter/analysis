# Negotiated Reasoning: On Provably Addressing Relative Over-Generalization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.05353
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05353
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Novel framework provably addresses relative over-generalization in MARL through negotiation policies

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses relative over-generalization (RO) in multi-agent reinforcement learning, where agents become overly cautious by overfitting to past experiences and fail to achieve optimal cooperation. The authors establish theoretical conditions under which RO can be avoided through consistent reasoning requirements. They introduce negotiated reasoning (NR), a novel framework enabling explicit reasoning through negotiation policies during training. The proposed Stein variational negotiated reasoning (SVNR) algorithm implements this framework using Stein variational gradient descent to derive negotiation policies that provably avoid RO under maximum entropy policy iteration. The method is parameterized with neural networks for efficient amortized learning and demonstrates superior performance compared to state-of-the-art approaches in two challenging environments.

## Method Summary
The authors develop negotiated reasoning (NR) as a framework for provably addressing relative over-generalization in MARL. They first prove that RO can be avoided when MARL methods satisfy consistent reasoning requirements under specific conditions. The SVNR algorithm implements NR using Stein variational gradient descent to derive negotiation policies that enable agents to reason explicitly during training. The method is further parameterized with neural networks to enable efficient amortized learning. By integrating these components, SVNR achieves provably RO avoidance under maximum entropy policy iteration while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Key Results
- SVNR algorithm provably avoids relative over-generalization under maximum entropy policy iteration
- Theoretical foundation establishes conditions for RO avoidance through consistent reasoning
- Numerical experiments demonstrate superiority over state-of-the-art methods in two challenging environments
- Neural network parameterization enables efficient amortized learning of negotiation policies

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism works by introducing explicit reasoning through negotiation policies that allow agents to coordinate their decision-making processes. Through Stein variational gradient descent, the algorithm derives negotiation policies that satisfy consistent reasoning requirements, preventing agents from overfitting to past experiences. The maximum entropy policy iteration framework provides the theoretical foundation for proving RO avoidance, while neural network parameterization ensures practical computational efficiency.
