# Towards Adversarially Robust Continual Learning

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.17764
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17764
- Reference count: 0
- The paper proposes TABA (Task-Aware Boundary Augmentation) to improve adversarial robustness in continual learning, achieving best or second-best performance under PGD and AutoAttack attacks while maintaining high standard accuracy.

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the critical challenge of maintaining adversarial robustness in continual learning scenarios where models must learn new tasks without forgetting previously acquired robustness. The authors identify that catastrophic forgetting of adversarial robustness occurs because decision boundaries established during adversarial training are not properly maintained when learning new tasks. They propose Task-Aware Boundary Augmentation (TABA), a novel data augmentation method that synthesizes boundary data from both old and new classes to preserve decision boundaries. Through extensive experiments on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100, TABA demonstrates superior performance in maintaining both standard and robust accuracy compared to existing methods.

## Method Summary
The paper proposes Task-Aware Boundary Augmentation (TABA) to improve adversarial robustness in continual learning. TABA works by collecting boundary data from misclassified adversarial samples of both old and new classes, then synthesizing new samples through interpolation with restricted λ range [0.45, 0.55]. This approach addresses the class imbalance between old and new tasks while preserving decision boundaries established during adversarial training. The method is evaluated on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100 using ResNet18, with performance measured under PGD-7 attacks and AutoAttack, showing superior robust accuracy compared to baseline methods.

## Key Results
- TABA achieves the best or second-best robustness under PGD attacks and AutoAttacks on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100
- The method maintains high standard accuracy while significantly improving robust accuracy
- TABA outperforms Mixup and boundary exemplar baselines in both standard and robust accuracy metrics
- The restricted interpolation range [0.45, 0.55] proves crucial for maintaining local invariance properties

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Boundary data synthesis helps preserve decision boundaries across tasks, mitigating catastrophic forgetting of adversarial robustness in continual learning
- Mechanism: By selecting misclassified adversarial samples (boundary data) from both old and new classes and interpolating them with restricted λ range [0.45, 0.55], the method generates augmented samples that are closer to decision boundaries while avoiding excessive linearity
- Core assumption: Samples near decision boundaries are more informative for adversarial robustness than randomly selected samples
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "preventing catastrophic forgetting of adversarial robustness in continual learning is equivalent to maintaining the decision boundary learned by adversarial training"
  - [section] "not all data points contribute equally during adversarial training and samples that are close to the decision boundaries should be emphasised"
  - [corpus] Weak - no direct supporting evidence found in neighbor papers
- Break condition: If boundary data selection fails to capture true decision boundary regions, or if interpolation creates samples that cross class boundaries in non-beneficial ways

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Task-aware sample selection from both old and new boundary data addresses class imbalance in continual learning
- Mechanism: By separately collecting boundary data from old classes (Bo) and new classes (Bn), then interpolating between them, the method maintains balanced representation of both task types in the augmented dataset
- Core assumption: Class imbalance between old and new tasks is a primary driver of forgetting in continual learning
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section] "data from previously learned tasks in continual learning are partially accessible, causing the imbalance between previous tasks and new tasks"
  - [section] "the significant imbalance between old classes and new classes can be more severe"
  - [corpus] Weak - no direct supporting evidence found in neighbor papers
- Break condition: If the imbalance is not the primary cause of forgetting, or if the interpolation ratio doesn't adequately address the imbalance

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Restricted interpolation range [0.45, 0.55] preserves adversarial robustness better than Mixup's [0, 1] range
- Mechanism: The narrower interpolation range creates augmented samples that stay closer to decision boundaries without introducing excessive linearity that breaks local invariance properties of adversarially trained models
- Core assumption: Mixup's wider interpolation range breaks the local invariance properties that adversarial training establishes
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section] "Mixup, however, is not specially designed for adversarial training or continual learning. It breaks the local invariance of adversarially trained models by linear interpolation"
  - [section] "we restrict the interpolation weight λ to a interval of [0.45, 0.55] rather than [0, 1] in Mixup to avoid the linearity"
  - [corpus] Weak - no direct supporting evidence found in neighbor papers
- Break condition: If the restricted range doesn't provide sufficient augmentation diversity, or if the linearity concern is overstated
