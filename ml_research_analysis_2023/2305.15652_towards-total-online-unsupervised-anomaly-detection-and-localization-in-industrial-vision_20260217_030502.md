# Towards Total Online Unsupervised Anomaly Detection and Localization in Industrial Vision

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.15652
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15652
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: LeMO achieves I-AUROC of 0.967 on MVTec AD and 0.986 on VisA datasets in online settings

## Executive Summary
This paper presents LeMO, a fully online learning image anomaly detection method designed to overcome the limitations of offline learning paradigms in industrial vision applications. The method introduces learnable memory initialized with orthogonal random noise, eliminating the need for extensive pre-collected data. By employing a contrastive learning-based loss function, LeMO enables online joint optimization of memory and image features, achieving superior performance in both online and offline settings while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Method Summary
LeMO addresses the challenge of unsupervised anomaly detection by implementing a novel online learning approach. The method uses a learnable memory bank initialized with orthogonal random noise to store representative features without requiring pre-collected training data. A contrastive learning framework is employed to optimize both the memory and image features simultaneously, enabling continuous adaptation to new data. The system achieves anomaly detection through reconstruction error analysis and localization through feature deviation mapping, making it suitable for real-time industrial quality control applications.

## Key Results
- Achieves I-AUROC of 0.967 on MVTec AD dataset in online settings
- Achieves I-AUROC of 0.986 on VisA dataset in online settings
- Demonstrates excellent performance in offline settings and few-shot scenarios

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The effectiveness of LeMO stems from its online learning capability that eliminates the need for pre-collected training data while maintaining high performance. The learnable memory initialized with orthogonal random noise provides a dynamic representation space that can adapt to new data distributions without catastrophic forgetting. The contrastive learning-based loss function enables effective joint optimization of memory and image features, allowing the system to continuously update its understanding of normal patterns while detecting anomalies in real-time. This approach combines the benefits of online learning with the performance of offline methods.

## Foundational Learning
1. Contrastive Learning

   - Quick check: Confirm memory updates maintain orthogonality and prevent catastrophic forgetting

3. Online Joint Optimization
   - Why needed: Allows continuous model adaptation while maintaining stability and performance
   - Quick check: Monitor training stability and convergence speed during online updates
