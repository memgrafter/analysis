# Arithmetic Intensity Balancing Convolution for Hardware-aware Efficient Block Design

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.04016
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04016
- Reference count: 21
- Primary result: ABConv reduces latency by up to 28% on Arm Ethos-U65 NPU while maintaining accuracy on CIFAR100

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses a critical hardware bottleneck in edge devices where convolution operations with small spatial sizes suffer from low arithmetic intensity. The authors propose ABConv, a method that balances arithmetic intensity between weight and activation data through strategic feature map reshaping and grouped convolutions. The approach achieves significant latency improvements (up to 28%) on tested hardware platforms while maintaining or slightly improving accuracy. The core insight is that by increasing weight arithmetic intensity, the method can overcome performance limits imposed by hardware constraints on small spatial convolutions.

## Method Summary
The proposed ABConv method addresses hardware inefficiencies in small spatial convolutions by reshaping feature maps and applying grouped convolutions to increase overall arithmetic intensity. The approach works by converting the standard convolution operation into a grouped convolution with a carefully chosen group number, effectively increasing the weight arithmetic intensity while maintaining the same total number of MAC operations. This transformation is particularly effective for hardware like NPUs where performance is often limited by activation arithmetic intensity rather than weight arithmetic intensity. The method maintains the same number of output channels while improving hardware utilization through better arithmetic intensity balancing.

## Key Results
- Achieves up to 28% lower latency on Arm Ethos-U65 NPU compared to standard convolutions
- Maintains or slightly improves accuracy on CIFAR100 benchmark
- Demonstrates similar improvements on NVIDIA Jetson Nano platform
- Shows effectiveness across different hardware architectures with similar bottleneck characteristics

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by addressing the fundamental mismatch between computational requirements and hardware limitations in small spatial convolutions. When spatial sizes are small, activation arithmetic intensity becomes the bottleneck on many hardware platforms. By converting standard convolutions to grouped convolutions with optimized group numbers, ABConv increases the weight arithmetic intensity, which better utilizes the hardware's computational capabilities. This balance between weight and activation arithmetic intensities allows the hardware to process the operations more efficiently, reducing latency without compromising model accuracy.

  - Why needed: Key mechanism for increasing weight arithmetic intensity
