# Towards Explainable AI Writing Assistants for Non-native English Speakers

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID**: 2304.02625
- **Source URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.02625
- **Reference count**: 40
- **Key outcome**: Study identifies challenges NNESs face evaluating AI paraphrases due to lack of explanations, proposing four design implications for explainable AI writing assistants.

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates the challenges non-native English speakers (NNESs) encounter when using AI writing assistants for paraphrasing tasks. Through interviews with 15 NNESs, the study reveals that participants struggle to evaluate AI-generated paraphrases due to the absence of explanatory features. Key issues include difficulties in conveying intended meaning, controlling tone, and assessing the appropriateness of suggestions. The research proposes four design implications to enhance AI writing assistants with explainable features, aiming to improve the user experience and effectiveness for NNESs.

## Method Summary
The study conducted semi-structured interviews with 15 non-native English speakers who use AI writing assistants for paraphrasing tasks. Participants were asked about their experiences, challenges, and needs when interacting with AI-generated paraphrases. The interviews explored issues related to meaning conveyance, tone control, and the evaluation of appropriateness. The qualitative data collected informed the identification of challenges and the proposal of design implications for explainable AI writing assistants.

## Key Results
- NNESs struggle to evaluate AI-generated paraphrases due to lack of explanations
- Participants reported difficulties in conveying intended meaning and controlling tone
- Proposed design implications include confidence scores, example sentences, usage trends, and textual explanations

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The study identifies that NNESs face challenges in evaluating AI-generated paraphrases because current AI writing assistants lack explanatory features. Without explanations, users cannot understand why certain suggestions are made or how they align with their intended meaning and tone. By incorporating explainable features such as confidence scores, example sentences, usage trends, and textual explanations, AI writing assistants can provide NNESs with the necessary context to make informed decisions about the suggestions, thereby improving their writing experience and outcomes.

- **Component map**: AI writing assistant -> Confidence scores -> Example sentences -> Usage trends -> Textual explanations
- **Critical path**: User input -> AI processing -> Suggestion generation -> Explanation generation -> User evaluation
- **Design tradeoffs**: Balancing explanation complexity with user comprehension; ensuring explanations are accurate and relevant
- **Failure signatures**: Inaccurate explanations leading to user confusion; explanations that are too technical or too simplistic

## Limitations
- Small sample size of 15 participants limits generalizability
- Focus on paraphrasing tasks may not represent all AI writing assistance scenarios
- Varied English proficiency levels and AI tool familiarity among participants may influence results

## Confidence
- **High confidence**: Identified challenges NNESs face when using AI writing assistants (difficulty evaluating suggestions, conveying intended meaning, controlling tone)
- **Medium confidence**: Proposed design implications for explainable AI writing assistants, as these are based on a small sample and require empirical validation
- **Low confidence**: Generalizability of findings to other AI writing tools beyond ChatGPT and other writing tasks beyond paraphrasing

## Next Checks
1. Conduct a larger-scale quantitative study with diverse NNES participants to validate the identified challenges and design implications across different AI writing tools
2. Implement and test prototypes of the proposed explainable features (confidence scores, example sentences, usage trends, textual explanations) with NNESs to evaluate their effectiveness
3. Compare the performance and user experience of NNESs using explainable AI writing assistants versus traditional AI tools without explanations across various writing tasks
