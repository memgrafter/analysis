# From Plate to Prevention: A Dietary Nutrient-aided Platform for Health Promotion in Singapore

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.03829
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.03829
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: FoodSG-SCL achieves 78.87% top-1 and 93.13% top-5 accuracy on FoodSG-233 dataset

## Executive Summary
This paper presents FoodSG, a cloud-based dietary nutrition platform designed for health applications in Singapore. The core contribution is FoodSG-233, a localized Singaporean food dataset with 209,861 high-quality images across 233 categories, each containing at least 400 images. The authors introduce FoodSG-SCL, a supervised contrastive learning approach that significantly improves food recognition accuracy by mining hard positive and negative samples, achieving up to 8.7 percentage points improvement over baseline models.

## Method Summary
The researchers developed a comprehensive pipeline for food image recognition focused on the Singaporean culinary context. They curated FoodSG-233 from 226,809 collected images, filtering to 209,861 high-quality images with rigorous category requirements. The FoodSG-SCL model integrates supervised contrastive learning to address the challenge of distinguishing visually similar dishes. This approach systematically identifies and leverages hard positive and negative samples during training, enhancing the model's discriminative capabilities for food recognition tasks.

## Key Results
- FoodSG-SCL achieves 78.87% top-1 accuracy and 93.13% top-5 accuracy on FoodSG-233
- SCL improves top-1 accuracy by up to 8.7 percentage points over baseline models
- FoodSG-SCL outperforms five state-of-the-art image recognition models on the dataset

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The success stems from combining localized data with contrastive learning. By focusing on Singaporean cuisine, the dataset captures region-specific visual characteristics that generic food datasets miss. Supervised contrastive learning enhances the model's ability to distinguish between similar-looking dishes by explicitly training on hard examples, creating tighter clusters for each food category while maintaining separation between visually similar classes.

## Foundational Learning
- **Food image recognition**: Essential for translating visual food data into nutritional information for healthcare applications
- **Supervised contrastive learning**: Key technique for improving model discrimination between similar classes
- **Dataset curation for deep learning**: Critical for ensuring model quality through sufficient intra-class variation and inter-class diversity
- **Perceptual distance metrics**: Used to validate dataset quality by measuring visual similarity between categories
- **Cloud-based healthcare platforms**: Architecture pattern for scalable deployment of AI models in clinical settings
- **Localization of AI datasets**: Important for capturing cultural and regional variations in food appearance

## Architecture Onboarding
**Component Map**: Image Collection -> Preprocessing -> FoodSG-233 Dataset -> FoodSG-SCL Model -> Healthcare Applications

**Critical Path**: High-quality image collection and curation → SCL-based model training → Healthcare application integration

**Design Tradeoffs**: 
- Local Singaporean focus provides cultural relevance but limits generalizability
- SCL improves top-1 accuracy but slightly reduces top-5 performance
- Large dataset size enables better model performance but increases computational requirements

**Failure Signatures**: 
- Poor performance on non-Singaporean dishes
- Confusion between visually similar local dishes
- Degradation in accuracy with low-quality or non-standard food presentations

1. Evaluate baseline model performance without SCL on FoodSG-233
