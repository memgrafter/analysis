# NeuralMind-UNICAMP at 2022 TREC NeuCLIR: Large Boring Rerankers for Cross-lingual Retrieval

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.16145
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16145
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: mT5-XXL reranker achieves top performance in cross-lingual retrieval tasks across Russian, Chinese, and Persian, even with suboptimal first-stage retrieval.

## Executive Summary
This study evaluates the mT5-XXL model as a reranker for cross-lingual information retrieval (CLIR) tasks using the NeuCLIR dataset. The approach involves fine-tuning mT5-XXL on a multilingual corpus and applying it to rerank documents retrieved by BM25, SPLADE, or RRF first-stage systems. The results demonstrate outstanding performance across all languages, with the reranker excelling even when initial retrieval recall is low. The study underscores the effectiveness of large pre-trained models in CLIR scenarios and highlights the importance of combining strong first-stage retrieval with advanced reranking.

## Method Summary
The method involves fine-tuning the mT5-XXL model on the mMARCO dataset with hyperparameters including a batch size of 128, max sequence length of 512, 100k iterations, learning rate of 0.001, and dropout of 0.1. The reranker is then applied to first-stage retrieval runs (BM25, SPLADE, RRF) from the NLE and h2loo teams, using optimal query translation configurations per language. Evaluation is conducted using metrics such as nDCG@20, MAP, RBP, R@100, and R@1000. The study emphasizes the synergy between effective first-stage retrieval and the reranker’s ability to improve ranking quality.

## Key Results
- mT5-XXL achieves top rankings in CLIR tasks across Russian, Chinese, and Persian.
- The reranker performs well even with suboptimal first-stage retrieval, particularly for Persian and Chinese.
- Optimal configurations involve using SPLADE for Russian and Bing-translated queries, and BM25 RRF for Chinese with Youdao-translated queries.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mT5-XXL model leverages its large-scale pre-training on multilingual data to effectively rerank documents in cross-lingual settings. By fine-tuning on mMARCO, the model adapts to retrieval-specific tasks, improving relevance judgments. The combination of strong first-stage retrieval (e.g., SPLADE, BM25 RRF) with the reranker ensures high recall and precision, even when initial retrieval is suboptimal. The model’s ability to handle long sequences (512 tokens) and its robustness to translation noise contribute to its effectiveness.

- The exact first-stage retrieval runs (BM25, SPLADE, RRF) from the NLE and h2loo teams are not publicly available, limiting reproducibility.
