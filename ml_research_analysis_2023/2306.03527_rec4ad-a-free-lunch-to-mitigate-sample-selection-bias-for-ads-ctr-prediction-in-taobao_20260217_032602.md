# Rec4Ad: A Free Lunch to Mitigate Sample Selection Bias for Ads CTR Prediction in Taobao

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.03527
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03527
- Reference count: 37
- Key result: Up to +6.6% CTR and +2.9% RPM lift in Taobao's display advertising system

## Executive Summary
Rec4Ad addresses sample selection bias (SSB) in ads CTR prediction by leveraging recommendation samples as a "free lunch" to mitigate the bias without costly uniform data collection. The method retrieves related recommendation samples and maps them to pseudo ad samples, then jointly trains on both types using a disentangled representation learning framework. This framework separates system-specific confounders from system-invariant user-item interest through alignment and decorrelation modules. Deployed in Taobao's display advertising system, Rec4Ad achieves significant online improvements and strong offline validation.

## Method Summary
Rec4Ad mitigates SSB by using recommendation samples from a different selection system that shares user decision rationales with ad samples but has uncorrelated confounders. The method retrieves related recommendation samples and maps them to pseudo ad samples via random recent-K mapping, then jointly trains on both sample types. A disentangled representation learning framework with alignment (via adversarial discriminator) and decorrelation modules separates system-specific confounders from system-invariant user-item interest. Source-aware batch normalization stabilizes training by preventing distribution mismatch between ad and pseudo ad samples.

## Key Results
- Up to +6.6% CTR and +2.9% RPM lift in Taobao's display advertising system
- Offline AUC improvement of +0.0027 over strong baselines
- Offline ECE reduction of -0.0005, indicating better calibration
- Alignment module discriminator AUC drops to 0.5, indicating indistinguishability between sources

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: Rec4Ad reduces sample selection bias by disentangling system-specific confounders from system-invariant user-item interest using recommendation samples.
- Mechanism: Recommendation samples from a different selection system share user decision rationales with ad samples but have uncorrelated confounders. By jointly training on both, Rec4Ad aligns representations across systems (alignment module) and decorrelates them to separate confounders (decorrelation module), yielding improved generalization.
- Core assumption: The user's click decision depends on user-item interest, not on whether the item is sponsored or organic. Confounders specific to the ad and recommendation systems are largely non-overlapping.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [abstract] "impressions of ads and recommendations are selected by different systems but share the same user decision rationales."
  - [section 2.2.3] "We find that though some groups of ads have few impressions in 𝐷𝑎𝑑, their corresponding items contribute an important portion of impressions in 𝐷𝑟𝑒𝑐."
  - [corpus] Weak match. Corpus neighbors do not discuss this disentanglement mechanism; the closest is general SSB mitigation.
- Break condition: If user behavior changes systematically between ad and recommendation contexts, the assumption of shared decision rationales fails and the alignment module cannot properly extract invariant features.

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: The decorrelation module ensures that the system-specific confounders are captured separately from the invariant user-item interest representation.
- Mechanism: After projecting raw features into invariant (x_inv) and confounder (x_con) subspaces, Pearson correlation between every pair of dimensions across x_inv and x_con is penalized. This forces x_con to encode only system-specific factors independent from x_inv.
- Core assumption: Confounders and invariant interest factors can be linearly separated in the learned representation space, and penalizing cross-correlation is sufficient to disentangle them.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section 3.2.3] "By optimizing 𝐿𝐷, x𝑐𝑜𝑛 are encouraged to capture residual information independent from x𝑖𝑛𝑣, i.e., system-specific confounders ∆ ad/∆ rec."
  - [section 4.2.4] "we observe that ad samples and rec samples are mostly separated in different areas" in the x_con space.
  - [corpus] Weak match. No corpus neighbor discusses decorrelation-based disentanglement.
- Break condition: If confounders and user interest are highly entangled or nonlinearly related, simple decorrelation penalties may be insufficient, leading to residual bias.

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Source-aware batch normalization (SABN) stabilizes training by preventing distribution mismatch between ad and pseudo ad samples.
- Mechanism: Instead of using global BN statistics across both sample types, SABN computes and applies normalization parameters separately for ad and recommendation sources. This avoids distorted scaling of network activations during online serving.
- Core assumption: Ad and pseudo ad samples have different feature distributions, and normalizing them together during training would misalign the statistics used at inference time.
- Evidence anchors:
  - [section 3.2.1] "we design source-aware BN (SABN), which adaptively normalize samples according to their sources."
  - [section 4.2.3] "After removing SABN, model calibration experiences an obvious degeneration."
  - [corpus] Weak match. No corpus neighbor mentions source-aware BN.
- Break condition: If the difference in feature distributions between sources is minimal, SABN offers no benefit and adds unnecessary complexity.

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: Data Augmentation -> Embedding -> SABN -> MLP -> Projections -> Alignment/Decorrelation -> Prediction
- Critical path: Data augmentation → embedding → SABN → MLP → projections → alignment/decorrelation → prediction
- Design tradeoffs:
  - Using recommendation samples avoids costly uniform sampling but requires careful mapping to maintain ad format.
  - Alignment via adversarial discriminator is cheaper than explicit distribution distance metrics.
  - Decorrelation regularization adds computation but is necessary to isolate confounders.
- Failure signatures:
  - Degradation in ECE after removing SABN indicates distribution mismatch.
  - Sudden AUC drop when alignment module is removed signals loss of invariant feature extraction.
  - Poor calibration after decorrelation removal suggests confounders still leaking into predictions.
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Verify data augmentation produces valid pseudo ad samples by checking that mapped items exist in the ad index and that the random mapping preserves label consistency.
  2. Test the alignment module by measuring adversary AUC over training steps; it should decrease toward 0.5, indicating indistinguishability.
  3. Validate decorrelation effectiveness by computing average cross-correlation between x_inv and x_con dimensions; it should be near zero after training.

## Limitations
- The assumption that user decision rationales are shared between ad and recommendation contexts may not hold if user behavior systematically differs across contexts.
- The decorrelation module's effectiveness depends on the linearity assumption about confounders and invariant factors; nonlinear relationships may not be adequately captured.
- The necessity of source-aware batch normalization indicates that ad and recommendation samples have fundamentally different distributions, which may limit the "free lunch" aspect.

## Confidence
- Confidence: High for offline improvements, Medium for the core SSB mitigation claim due to assumption sensitivity, and Medium for the decorrelation mechanism's robustness.
