# EaSyGuide : ESG Issue Identification Framework leveraging Abilities of Generative Large Language Models

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.06662
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06662
- Reference count: 5
- Primary result: First place in English text subtask (F1-score 0.69) and second place in French text subtask (F1-score 0.78) for ESG issue identification

## Executive Summary
The paper introduces EaSyGuide, a framework for identifying ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) issues in news articles using generative large language models. The approach employs techniques like GPT3Mix augmentation, zero-shot classification, and translation to improve the performance of encoder models such as RoBERTa, DeBERTa, and FinBERT. The framework achieves first place in the English text subtask and second place in the French text subtask of the FinNLP-2023 shared task, demonstrating its effectiveness in identifying 35 ESG key issues across different languages.

## Method Summary
EaSyGuide utilizes a multi-stage approach to identify ESG issues in news articles. The framework starts by collecting and preprocessing news articles in English and French, using web scraping and zero-shot classification to expand the training data. GPT3Mix augmentation is then applied to generate synthetic text samples, which are used to train and fine-tune encoder models like RoBERTa, DeBERTa, and FinBERT. The models undergo knowledge distillation and additional training to improve their performance. Finally, ensemble methods are employed to combine the predictions from multiple models, resulting in the final ESG issue classification.

## Key Results
