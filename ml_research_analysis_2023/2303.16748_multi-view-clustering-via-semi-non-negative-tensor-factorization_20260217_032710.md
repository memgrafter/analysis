# Multi-View Clustering via Semi-non-negative Tensor Factorization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.16748
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16748
- Reference count: 39
- Key outcome: Achieves clustering accuracy of up to 99% on Mnist4 dataset and 66% on NUS dataset

## Executive Summary
This paper presents Semi-NTF, a novel multi-view clustering method that extends non-negative matrix factorization to tensor factorization with one-side orthogonal constraints. The approach directly processes multi-view data in tensor form to preserve spatial structure and capture between-view relationships. By using tensor Schatten p-norm regularization, the method exploits complementary information across views while maintaining computational efficiency through anchor graph construction. Experimental results on benchmark datasets demonstrate superior performance compared to state-of-the-art multi-view clustering methods.

## Method Summary
The Semi-NTF method constructs a 3rd-order tensor from anchor graphs of each view and performs factorization directly on this tensor. The model uses tensor Schatten p-norm regularization to characterize cluster structure and exploit complementary information across views. Anchor graphs are employed as input to dramatically reduce computational complexity while preserving essential clustering structure. The algorithm employs alternating optimization with augmented Lagrangian multipliers to update factor matrices while maintaining non-negativity and orthogonality constraints.

## Key Results
- Achieves clustering accuracy of up to 99% on Mnist4 dataset
- Achieves clustering accuracy of 66% on NUS dataset
- Outperforms state-of-the-art multi-view clustering methods including AMGL, MVGL, CSMSC, GMC, LMVSC, SMSC, SFMC, and FMCNOF

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- **Claim**: Semi-NTF directly processes multi-view data in tensor form, preserving within-view spatial structure and capturing between-view relationships.
- **Mechanism**: The model constructs a 3rd-order tensor from anchor graphs of each view and performs factorization on this tensor directly, rather than decomposing each view separately.
- **Core assumption**: The spatial structure of multi-view data is better preserved when processed as a tensor rather than as separate matrices.
- **Evidence anchors**:
  - [abstract]: "Our model directly performs Semi-NTF on the 3rd-order tensor which is composed of anchor graphs of views. Thus, our model directly considers the between-view relationship."
  - [section]: "To fully utilize the spatial information of multi-view data, we extend non-negative matrix factorization to the 3rd-order tensor to make it more suitable for processing multi-view data."
  - [corpus]: Weak evidence - no direct citations to tensor-based multi-view clustering methods.
- **Break condition**: If anchor graph construction fails to preserve meaningful relationships between data points, tensor factorization would not capture useful structure.

### Mechanism 2
- **Claim**: Tensor Schatten p-norm regularization exploits complementary information across views while approximating rank structure.
- **Mechanism**: The regularization term minimizes divergence between cluster indicator matrices from different views, promoting consensus while preserving view-specific information.
- **Core assumption**: Minimizing tensor Schatten p-norm effectively characterizes cluster structure and encourages views to share complementary information.
- **Evidence anchors**:
  - [abstract]: "we use the tensor Schatten p-norm regularization as a rank approximation of the 3rd-order tensor which characterizes the cluster structure of multi-view data and exploits the between-view complementary information."
  - [section]: "Inspired by the outstanding performance of the tensor Schatten p-norm [19], we minimize the tensor Schatten p-norm of H aiming to diminish the divergence of the cluster indicator matrices from different views."
  - [corpus]: Weak evidence - no direct citations showing tensor Schatten p-norm has been used in multi-view clustering before.
- **Break condition**: If p is poorly chosen (outside 0<p≤1) or λ is mis-tuned, the regularization may either over-constrain or under-constrain the clustering solution.

### Mechanism 3
- **Claim**: Using anchor graphs instead of original data dramatically reduces computational complexity while maintaining clustering quality.
- **Mechanism**: Anchor graph construction reduces n×m dimensionality (where m << n is the number of anchors), making tensor operations computationally feasible.
- **Core assumption**: Anchor graphs preserve essential clustering structure while being much smaller than original data matrices.
- **Evidence anchors**:
  - [abstract]: "we regard the anchor graph obtained from the original data as the input of the non-negative matrix factorization, which reduces the complexity of our proposed algorithm considerably."
  - [section]: "Considering that previous NMF-based multi-view clustering methods are performed directly on the original data, Yang et al. [18] introduced an anchor graph as the input of G-orthogonal NMF. Since the number of anchors is much smaller than that of the original data, the matrix factorization of the anchor graph can indeed be more efficient."
  - [corpus]: Weak evidence - while anchor graphs are mentioned in related work, no direct evidence that tensor-based anchor graph methods have been validated.
- **Break condition**: If too few anchors are chosen, important local structure may be lost; if too many, computational benefits diminish.

## Limitations

- The anchor graph construction method may compromise local structure preservation, potentially losing important clustering information
- Hyperparameter selection (p, λ, µ, ρ) lacks systematic guidance and may require extensive tuning for different datasets
- The tensor Schatten p-norm regularization has no established precedent in multi-view clustering literature, raising questions about its theoretical justification

## Confidence

- **High Confidence**: The overall framework of using tensor factorization for multi-view clustering is sound and well-motivated
- **Medium Confidence**: The specific choice of tensor Schatten p-norm regularization and its implementation details
- **Low Confidence**: The anchor graph construction method's ability to preserve all relevant structural information for clustering
