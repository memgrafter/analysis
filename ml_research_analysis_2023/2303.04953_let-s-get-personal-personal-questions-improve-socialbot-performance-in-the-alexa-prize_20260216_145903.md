# Let's Get Personal: Personal Questions Improve SocialBot Performance in the Alexa Prize

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.04953
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.04953
- Reference count: 40
- Personal questions significantly improve user ratings and conversation length in open-domain chatbots

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a novel approach to enhancing conversational AI engagement through strategic use of personal questions. The authors demonstrate that by incorporating Would You Rather and Hypothetical questions early in conversations, they can create more engaging and satisfying interactions with users. Their research addresses fundamental challenges in open-domain conversational systems, including maintaining topic coverage, driving engagement without explicit goals, and building social rapport.

The study's significance lies in its large-scale A/B testing approach using real Alexa Prize users, providing empirical evidence for the effectiveness of personalization strategies in conversational AI. The results show measurable improvements in both user ratings and conversation duration, suggesting that personal questions can serve as a powerful tool for creating more engaging conversational experiences.

## Method Summary
The authors developed a comprehensive system for incorporating personal questions into conversational AI interactions. Their approach involved three main components: building user models through initial probing questions, deploying personal opinion questions (Would You Rather and Hypothetical) to increase topical depth, and implementing a feedback analysis system to continuously improve the conversation quality. The system was tested through large-scale A/B testing with real users during the Alexa Prize competition, comparing conversations with and without personal questions.

## Key Results
- Personal opinion questions significantly increased user ratings compared to control groups
- Conversations incorporating personal questions showed measurable increases in duration
- The system successfully addressed multiple core challenges in open-domain conversation, including topic coverage and user engagement

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
Personal questions work by creating a sense of individual attention and investment in the conversation. When users are asked about their preferences and hypothetical scenarios, they become more engaged and willing to share more information. This increased engagement leads to longer, more satisfying conversations. The personal nature of the questions also helps build rapport and trust between the user and the AI system, making the interaction feel more natural and less mechanical.

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out
The paper does not explicitly call out open questions, but several areas remain unexplored based on the limitations section.

## Limitations
- Evaluation relies heavily on subjective user ratings and conversation length metrics
- No analysis of cultural or demographic variations in user responses to personal questions
- Lacks investigation of long-term effects on user trust and sustained engagement

## Confidence

High confidence: The finding that personal opinion questions improve engagement metrics (ratings and conversation length) is well-supported by the A/B testing data

Medium confidence: The claim that personalization addresses core challenges in open-domain conversation is reasonable but requires more direct evidence

Low confidence: The broader implications for social rapport and long-term user engagement remain speculative

## Next Checks
1. Conduct cross-cultural validation studies to assess how different populations respond to personal questions in conversational AI
2. Implement longitudinal studies tracking user engagement over multiple conversations to evaluate sustained effectiveness
3. Design controlled experiments comparing personal questions against alternative engagement strategies (e.g., topical questions, humor, or task-oriented dialogue) to isolate the specific benefits of personalization
