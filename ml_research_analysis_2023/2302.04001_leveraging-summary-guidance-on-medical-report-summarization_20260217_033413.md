# Leveraging Summary Guidance on Medical Report Summarization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.04001
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04001
- Reference count: 40
- Presents three deidentified medical text datasets with 50K, 16K, and 378K report-summary pairs

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces three large-scale deidentified medical text datasets (DISCHARGE, ECHO, RADIOLOGY) and implements abstractive summarization baselines using encoder-decoder models including BERT2BERT, T5-large, and BART. The key innovation involves leveraging sampled summaries from training data as prior knowledge guidance to enhance BART's representation learning. This guidance approach improves both ROUGE and BERTScore metrics, outperforming even the larger T5-large model on the medical summarization tasks.

## Method Summary
The authors implement abstractive summarization baselines using pre-trained encoder-decoder models (BERT2BERT, T5-large, BART) on three newly released medical text datasets. Their novel approach involves sampling summaries from the training set and using them as prior knowledge guidance to improve the BART model's representation learning. This guidance mechanism is integrated into the model training process to provide additional context and improve summarization performance.

## Key Results
- Three deidentified medical datasets released: DISCHARGE (50K), ECHO (16K), and RADIOLOGY (378K pairs)
- Proposed guidance sampling approach improves ROUGE and BERTScore metrics
- BART model with guidance sampling outperforms larger T5-large model
- Guidance sampling enhances representation learning in abstractive summarization

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The paper doesn't explicitly detail the mechanism behind why guidance sampling improves performance. The approach appears to work by providing the model with additional contextual information through sampled summaries, which helps the model learn better representations for medical text summarization. The guidance likely helps the model capture domain-specific patterns and relationships in medical reports more effectively than training without such guidance.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map**: Training data (medical reports/summaries) -> Encoder-decoder model (BART) -> Guidance sampler -> Enhanced representation learning -> Summarized output

**Critical Path**: The core workflow involves feeding medical reports into the encoder-decoder model, sampling guidance summaries, integrating this guidance into the representation learning process, and generating abstractive summaries that outperform baseline models.

**Design Tradeoffs**: The paper prioritizes performance improvement through guidance sampling over model architecture changes, choosing to enhance an existing effective model (BART) rather than developing entirely new architectures. This approach balances computational efficiency with performance gains.

**Failure Signatures**: Potential failure modes include over-reliance on sampled guidance leading to generic summaries, guidance samples that don't match the input report's characteristics, or the guidance mechanism introducing noise rather than useful context.
