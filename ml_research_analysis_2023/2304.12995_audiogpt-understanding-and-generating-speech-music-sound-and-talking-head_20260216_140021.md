# AudioGPT: Understanding and Generating Speech, Music, Sound, and Talking Head

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.12995
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.12995
- Reference count: 10
- Primary result: AudioGPT extends ChatGPT to process complex audio information and conduct spoken dialogue through integration with specialized audio foundation models

## Executive Summary
AudioGPT is a multi-modal AI system that extends ChatGPT's capabilities to handle speech, music, sound, and talking head synthesis tasks. It integrates ChatGPT with specialized audio foundation models and interfaces for speech recognition, synthesis, and translation. The system uses ChatGPT as a general-purpose interface to interpret user queries, select appropriate audio models, and generate responses. AudioGPT demonstrates the ability to handle diverse audio tasks in multi-round conversations through a four-stage process: modality transformation, task analysis, model assignment, and response generation.

## Method Summary
AudioGPT extends ChatGPT by integrating it with specialized audio foundation models through a four-stage process. First, ASR converts spoken queries to text while TTS converts responses back to speech. Second, the system analyzes tasks and generates structured arguments through a prompt manager. Third, appropriate audio models are selected based on input/output modalities. Finally, responses are generated using ChatGPT. The system evaluates consistency, capability, and robustness through human evaluation and benchmark datasets.

## Key Results
- AudioGPT can handle diverse audio tasks including speech synthesis, music generation, sound editing, and talking head synthesis
- The system successfully conducts multi-round conversations with consistent performance
- Experiments demonstrate capability across speech, music, sound, and talking head tasks with various evaluation metrics
