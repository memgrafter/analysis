# Distilling Script Knowledge from Large Language Models for Constrained Language Planning

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.05252
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.05252
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Distilled CoScript dataset (55k scripts) enables smaller models to match LLM performance on constrained language planning

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the under-explored problem of constrained language planning, where specific goals must be achieved while satisfying multiple constraints (e.g., "make a cake for diabetics"). The authors propose an over-generate-then-filter approach using InstructGPT to create high-quality constrained scripts, which are distilled into the CoScript dataset. Experiments demonstrate that this approach significantly improves constraint faithfulness compared to baselines, and training smaller models on CoScript achieves comparable performance to large language models while maintaining constraint satisfaction.

## Method Summary
The authors employ an over-generate-then-filter approach where InstructGPT first generates specific goals from abstract goals using in-context learning with constraint examples, then generates multiple candidate scripts for each specific goal. A filter model selects the most constraint-faithful script based on semantic similarity (using InstructGPT embeddings) and keyword matching. This process creates the CoScript dataset of 55,000 constrained scripts. Smaller models (T5, GPT-2) are then trained on CoScript and evaluated on their ability to generate constraint-faithful scripts, with retrieval augmentation used to improve semantic completeness.

## Key Results
- InstructGPT with in-context learning achieves 98% accuracy in generating high-quality specific goals from abstract goals
- Over-generate-then-filter approach improves constraint faithfulness by 45% compared to InstructGPT without filtering
- T5 (3B) trained on CoScript with retrieval augmentation matches or exceeds most LLM performance on constrained language planning tasks

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
- Claim: InstructGPT with in-context learning can generate high-quality specific goals from abstract goals when provided with constraint examples.
- Mechanism: InstructGPT leverages the provided examples to understand the mapping between abstract goals and constrained specific goals, enabling it to generalize and produce new specific goals with appropriate constraints.
- Core assumption: InstructGPT's few-shot learning capability is sufficient to generalize the goal-to-constraint mapping pattern.
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "InstructGPT achieves 98.00% accuracy, indicating that LLMs can derive specific goals of rather high quality."

### Mechanism 2
- Claim: Over-generate-then-filter approach improves script generation faithfulness to constraints.
- Mechanism: InstructGPT generates multiple candidate scripts, and a filter model selects the most constraint-faithful script based on semantic similarity and keyword matching.
- Core assumption: At least one of the over-generated scripts will be faithful to the constraint, and the filter can reliably identify it.
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "Our method greatly improves the planning quality both in semantic completeness and faithfulness."

### Mechanism 3
- Claim: Training smaller models on CoScript dataset enables them to achieve comparable performance to LLMs in constrained language planning.
- Mechanism: CoScript provides high-quality, constraint-specific training data that teaches smaller models the mapping from constrained goals to faithful scripts.
- Core assumption: The distilled knowledge in CoScript is sufficient for smaller models to learn the constrained planning task without requiring massive model sizes.
- Evidence anchors: [abstract] "Training smaller models on CoScript achieves comparable results to LLMs."

## Foundational Learning

- Concept: In-context learning
  - Why needed here: Enables LLMs to perform constrained language planning without gradient updates or fine-tuning.
  - Quick check question: How does in-context learning differ from traditional fine-tuning in terms of data efficiency and task adaptation?

- Concept: Semantic similarity measurement
  - Why needed here: Used in the filter model to assess constraint faithfulness of generated scripts.
  - Quick check question: What are the trade-offs between using InstructGPT embeddings versus other pre-trained models like SimCSE for semantic similarity?

- Concept: Knowledge distillation
  - Why needed here: Allows transferring the constrained planning capability from LLMs to smaller, more deployable models via dataset generation.
  - Quick check question: What are the potential biases introduced when distilling knowledge from LLMs to smaller models?

## Architecture Onboarding

- Component map: Abstract Goal → InstructGPT (specific goal generation) → InstructGPT (script generation) → Filter model → Faithful script
- Critical path: InstructGPT generates specific goals → multiple scripts generated → filter selects most faithful script → dataset created
- Design tradeoffs:
  - Larger K for over-generation increases chance of faithful script but also increases computation cost
  - Using InstructGPT embeddings for similarity vs. other models (better performance vs. potential bias)
  - Including retrieved examples for augmentation (improves completeness but may hurt constraint faithfulness)
- Failure signatures:
  - Low constraint faithfulness in generated scripts
  - High variance in script quality across different goals
  - Smaller models failing to generalize from CoScript
- First 3 experiments:
  1. Evaluate InstructGPT's ability to generate specific goals from abstract goals with different numbers of examples
  2. Test over-generation with varying K values to find optimal balance between quality and cost
  3. Compare smaller models trained on CoScript vs. wikiHow to quantify the benefit of constrained data

## Limitations

- Evaluation relies heavily on automatic metrics rather than comprehensive human evaluation, raising questions about nuanced constraint satisfaction
- CoScript generation process uses specific prompt templates that are not fully disclosed, making it difficult to assess potential biases
- Comparison between LLMs and smaller models is limited - effectiveness for much smaller models or edge deployments is unclear

## Confidence

- **High Confidence**: The core methodology of over-generate-then-filter is sound and the pipeline for generating CoScript is reproducible
- **Medium Confidence**: The claim that this approach significantly improves constraint faithfulness relies on metrics that may not fully capture constraint satisfaction complexity
- **Low Confidence**: The generalizability of CoScript to other domains beyond the wikiHow corpus and long-term effectiveness in diverse real-world applications remain uncertain

## Next Checks

1. Conduct comprehensive human evaluation on a stratified sample of generated scripts to validate automatic metric results, particularly focusing on nuanced constraint satisfaction and semantic completeness trade-offs

2. Test the approach with much smaller models (e.g., 100M-500M parameters) to evaluate the lower bounds of effective knowledge distillation and identify the minimum viable model size for practical deployment

3. Generate CoScript using alternative LLM architectures (e.g., Claude, Gemini) with the same prompt templates to assess sensitivity to model-specific generation patterns and potential bias in the distilled knowledge
