# Towards Personalized Review Summarization by Modeling Historical Reviews from Customer and Product Separately

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces HHRRS, a novel personalized review summarization model that separately models historical reviews from customers and products using graph reasoning. The model incorporates rating information as high-level abstraction of customer preference and achieves superior performance on both review summarization and sentiment classification tasks. Experiments on four benchmark datasets demonstrate significant improvements over state-of-the-art baselines, particularly in capturing personalized aspects of reviews.

## Method Summary
HHRRS employs a graph reasoning module to separately model historical reviews from customers and products, capturing their distinct preferences and characteristics. The model integrates rating information as a high-level abstraction of customer preference, enhancing the personalization of generated summaries. By leveraging this dual-graph approach, HHRRS can effectively learn from the historical review patterns of both customers and products, leading to more accurate and personalized summarization.

## Key Results
- HHRRS achieves state-of-the-art performance on review summarization and sentiment classification tasks
- Significant improvements in Rouge-1 F1 scores: 10.53%, 17.69%, and 10.34% on Home dataset; 11.16%, 17.09%, and 10.90% on Toys dataset compared to BART+Senti
- Demonstrates effectiveness of separately modeling customer and product historical reviews for personalized summarization
