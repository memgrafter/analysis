# DVFO: Learning-Based DVFS for Energy-Efficient Edge-Cloud Collaborative Inference

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.01811
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01811
- Reference count: 28
- Key outcome: Reduces energy consumption by 33% and end-to-end latency by up to 54% compared to state-of-the-art approaches, while maintaining DNN accuracy within 1% loss on average.

## Executive Summary
DVFO is a learning-based edge-cloud collaborative inference framework that co-optimizes dynamic voltage and frequency scaling (DVFS) and feature map offloading using deep reinforcement learning (DRL). It automatically tunes CPU, GPU, and memory frequencies of edge devices and selectively offloads feature maps to cloud servers, guided by a spatial-channel attention mechanism. A thinking-while-moving concurrent mechanism accelerates DRL learning, and quantization reduces transmission overhead. Evaluations across two datasets and six DNN models show significant improvements in energy efficiency and latency over state-of-the-art approaches.

## Method Summary
DVFO combines DVFS and feature map offloading for energy-efficient DNN inference on edge devices. It uses a deep reinforcement learning framework with a thinking-while-moving concurrent mechanism to optimize DVFS settings and feature map offloading decisions. The framework leverages a spatial-channel attention mechanism to evaluate feature map importance and guide offloading. DVFO is trained on two datasets (CIFAR-100 and ImageNet-2012) using six DNN models (ResNet-18, MobileNet-v2, EfficientNet-B0, and ViT-B16). The goal is to minimize a cost metric that combines energy consumption and end-to-end latency, while maintaining DNN accuracy within 1% loss on average.

## Key Results
- Reduces energy consumption by 33% compared to state-of-the-art approaches
- Reduces end-to-end latency by up to 54% compared to state-of-the-art approaches
- Maintains DNN accuracy within 1% loss on average

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
DVFO leverages deep reinforcement learning to jointly optimize DVFS and feature map offloading decisions based on the runtime state of the edge device and DNN model. The thinking-while-moving concurrent mechanism accelerates DRL learning by allowing the agent to explore multiple actions concurrently. The spatial-channel attention mechanism evaluates the importance of different feature maps and guides the offloading decisions, ensuring that only the most critical information is transmitted to the cloud. Quantization is used to reduce transmission overhead and further improve energy efficiency.

## Foundational Learning
- **Deep Reinforcement Learning**: A machine learning paradigm where an agent learns to make decisions by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties. Needed to optimize DVFS and feature map offloading decisions based on runtime conditions. Quick check: Verify that the DRL agent can learn to balance energy consumption and latency effectively.
- **Spatial-Channel Attention Mechanism**: A technique used in computer vision to evaluate the importance of different spatial locations and channels in feature maps. Needed to guide feature map offloading decisions and ensure that only the most critical information is transmitted to the cloud. Quick check: Verify that the attention mechanism can accurately identify important feature maps for offloading.
- **Dynamic Voltage and Frequency Scaling (DVFS)**: A power management technique that adjusts the voltage and frequency of a processor based on the workload. Needed to optimize the energy consumption of edge devices during DNN inference. Quick check: Verify that the DVFS settings can be effectively tuned to balance energy consumption and performance.

## Architecture Onboarding
- **Component Map**: Edge Device (CPU, GPU, Memory) -> Feature Extractor -> Spatial-Channel Attention -> DRL-Based DVFO Optimizer -> Offload Decision -> Cloud Server
- **Critical Path**: DNN inference on edge device -> Feature extraction -> Attention-based feature map evaluation -> DRL-based DVFS and offloading decision -> Offload to cloud (if applicable) -> Cloud-based inference -> Result aggregation
- **Design Tradeoffs**: Balancing energy consumption and latency by adjusting DVFS settings and feature map offloading decisions. Quantization level vs. transmission overhead and inference accuracy. Exploration vs. exploitation in DRL training.
