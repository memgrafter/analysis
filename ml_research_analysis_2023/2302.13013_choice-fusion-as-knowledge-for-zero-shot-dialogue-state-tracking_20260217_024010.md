# Choice Fusion as Knowledge for Zero-Shot Dialogue State Tracking

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.13013
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.13013
- Reference count: 0

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the challenge of zero-shot dialogue state tracking (DST) for task-oriented dialogue systems, which traditionally require large amounts of in-domain training data. The authors propose a novel approach, Choice-Fusion Dialogue State Tracking (CoFunDST), that leverages large-scale question-answering (QA) datasets to enable zero-shot DST across multiple domains. CoFunDST formulates DST as a generative machine reading comprehension problem and introduces two key innovations: appreciative choice selection to identify highly relevant candidate slot-values, and context-choice fusion to incorporate these choices as knowledge for the decoder. The model is trained on domain-agnostic QA data and uses candidate slot-values as knowledge to constrain the generation of dialogue states.

## Method Summary
The CoFunDST approach formulates dialogue state tracking as a generative machine reading comprehension problem. It leverages large-scale question-answering datasets to enable zero-shot DST across multiple domains. The method consists of two key innovations: appreciative choice selection, which identifies highly relevant candidate slot-values from the dialogue context, and context-choice fusion, which incorporates these choices as knowledge for the decoder. The model is trained on domain-agnostic QA data and uses candidate slot-values as knowledge to constrain the generation of dialogue states, eliminating the need for in-domain DST data.

## Key Results
- CoFunDST achieves superior joint goal accuracy compared to existing zero-shot DST approaches on MultiWOZ 2.1
- Demonstrates particularly strong performance in Restaurant, Train, and Taxi domains
- Shows effectiveness of choice-fusion mechanism in improving domain-agnostic training for generalization to new domains

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The approach works by transforming the DST task into a generative reading comprehension problem where the model learns to extract and generate dialogue states from context using knowledge derived from candidate slot-values. The appreciative choice selection mechanism identifies relevant slot-value pairs from the dialogue context, which are then fused with the context as additional knowledge for the decoder. This allows the model to leverage patterns learned from large-scale QA data while constraining generation using domain-relevant choices, enabling effective zero-shot generalization.

## Architecture Onboarding

Component Map:
Dialogue Context -> Appreciative Choice Selection -> Context-Choice Fusion -> Generative Decoder

Critical Path:
The critical path flows from dialogue context through appreciative choice selection to identify relevant slot-values, then through context-choice fusion to incorporate these as knowledge, finally reaching the generative decoder that produces the dialogue states. Each component builds on the previous, with choice selection quality directly impacting fusion effectiveness and final generation quality.

Design Tradeoffs:
The approach trades computational overhead from choice selection and fusion mechanisms for improved zero-shot generalization. Using large-scale QA data instead of domain-specific DST data reduces annotation costs but may introduce domain mismatch. The generative approach allows flexible output but requires careful constraint through choice fusion to maintain accuracy.

Failure Signatures:
Performance degradation may occur when candidate slot-values are not representative of the target domain, when dialogue contexts are too complex for effective choice selection, or when the fusion mechanism fails to properly incorporate knowledge. The model may also struggle with rare slot-value combinations not well-represented in the training QA data.
