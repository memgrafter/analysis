# Effective Neural Topic Modeling with Embedding Clustering Regularization

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.04217
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.04217
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Novel regularization technique prevents topic collapsing by forcing each topic embedding to be the center of a separately aggregated word embedding cluster

## Executive Summary
This paper addresses the persistent problem of topic collapsing in neural topic models, where discovered topics become semantically similar and repetitive. The authors propose Embedding Clustering Regularization Topic Model (ECRTM), which introduces a novel regularization technique that forces each topic embedding to be the center of a separately aggregated word embedding cluster. This approach prevents topic embeddings from collapsing together and ensures each topic contains distinct word semantics. The model jointly optimizes topic modeling and embedding clustering objectives, producing diverse and coherent topics with high-quality document-topic distributions.

## Method Summary
The proposed method introduces embedding clustering regularization into neural topic models to address topic collapsing. ECRTM forces each topic embedding to be the center of a separately aggregated word embedding cluster, preventing topic embeddings from collapsing together and ensuring each topic contains distinct word semantics. The model jointly optimizes both the topic modeling objective and the embedding clustering regularization, producing diverse and coherent topics while maintaining high-quality document-topic distributions.

## Key Results
- ECRTM effectively addresses topic collapsing and produces more diverse topics compared to state-of-the-art baselines
- The model demonstrates consistent improvement in topic quality, document clustering, and downstream classification tasks
- Experimental results on benchmark datasets validate the effectiveness of the proposed embedding clustering regularization approach

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The mechanism works by introducing a regularization term that explicitly enforces separation between topic embeddings through word embedding clustering. By forcing each topic embedding to be the center of its own word embedding cluster, the model creates a natural repulsion between topics that prevents them from collapsing into similar semantic spaces. This geometric constraint in the embedding space translates directly to more diverse and distinct topics in the final model output.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map**: Word Embeddings -> Topic Embeddings -> Clustering Regularization -> Topic Modeling Objective -> Document-Topic Distributions

**Critical Path**: The critical path involves generating topic embeddings, applying clustering regularization to enforce separation, and jointly optimizing with the topic modeling objective to produce final document-topic distributions.

**Design Tradeoffs**: The model trades computational complexity for improved topic diversity. The clustering regularization adds overhead but prevents the model from collapsing into redundant topics. The regularization strength parameter must be carefully tuned to balance diversity with coherence.

**Failure Signatures**: Without sufficient regularization, topics collapse into similar semantic spaces. With excessive regularization, topics may become artificially fragmented, losing semantic coherence. The model may also struggle with domain-specific vocabulary where natural topic boundaries are less clear.

## Limitations

- Evaluation scope is limited to standard benchmark datasets, lacking comprehensive testing on specialized domains or low-resource languages
- Computational overhead from clustering regularization is not fully characterized, with no detailed complexity analysis or runtime comparisons
- The assumption that forcing topic embeddings to be cluster centers inherently produces semantically diverse topics lacks rigorous validation
