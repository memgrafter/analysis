# Heterogeneous Tri-stream Clustering Network

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.04451
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.04451
- Reference count: 40
- Primary result: Outperforms state-of-the-art deep clustering approaches on four image datasets in terms of NMI, ACC, and ARI.

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a novel deep clustering method called Heterogeneous Tri-stream Clustering Network (HTCN). The approach leverages both instance-level and cluster-level contrastive learning within a tri-stream architecture. The network comprises two weight-sharing online networks and a target network, where the target network's parameters are updated as an exponential moving average of the online networks. HTCN is designed to predict instance representations and enforce consistency between cluster representations, leading to improved clustering performance.

## Method Summary
HTCN is a deep clustering method that integrates dual-instance and cluster-level contrastive learning. It uses two online networks sharing weights and a target network whose parameters are updated as an exponential moving average of the online networks. The online networks are trained to predict the instance representations of the target network and ensure consistency between their cluster representations and those of the target network. This dual-stream architecture facilitates effective clustering by leveraging both instance and cluster-level information.

## Key Results
- HTCN achieves superior performance compared to state-of-the-art deep clustering methods on four image datasets.
- The method demonstrates improvements in NMI, ACC, and ARI metrics.
- HTCN's effectiveness is validated through extensive experimental evaluations.

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
HTCN's success is attributed to its tri-stream architecture, which allows for simultaneous instance-level and cluster-level contrastive learning. The use of a target network with parameters updated as an exponential moving average of the online networks ensures stability and consistency in training. By enforcing consistency between the cluster representations of the online and target networks, HTCN effectively captures both fine-grained and coarse-grained cluster structures.
