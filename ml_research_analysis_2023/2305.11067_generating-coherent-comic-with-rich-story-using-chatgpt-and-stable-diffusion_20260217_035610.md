# Generating coherent comic with rich story using ChatGPT and Stable Diffusion

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.11067
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.11067
- Reference count: 3
- The paper presents a method to generate coherent comics with rich storylines by combining ChatGPT and Stable Diffusion

## Executive Summary
This paper introduces a novel approach for generating coherent comics with rich storylines by combining ChatGPT and Stable Diffusion. The method uses ChatGPT to generate storylines and dialogue, then fine-tunes Stable Diffusion with techniques like LoRA and ControlNet to maintain character fidelity and art style. A novel story score metric is introduced to evaluate AI-generated stories by comparing them to the original manga and other popular manga. The approach outperforms Midjourney V5 in character fidelity and art style metrics (SSIM and FID scores).

## Method Summary
The method combines ChatGPT for story and dialogue generation with fine-tuned Stable Diffusion for image generation. The process involves using ChatGPT to create comic storylines, then fine-tuning Stable Diffusion using LoRA for character-specific adaptation and ControlNet with Canny edge detection for pose control. The fine-tuning sequence includes applying LoRA trained on One Piece characters, adjusting parameters, changing Clip-Skip, using waifu-diffusion's VAE, adding extra prompts, and applying ControlNet conditioning. The generated stories are evaluated using a novel story score metric that combines BERT-based similarity to source material with similarity to popular manga plots.

## Key Results
- The method outperforms Midjourney V5 in character fidelity and art style metrics (SSIM and FID scores)
- Achieves state-of-the-art performance on character fidelity and art style through fine-tuning
- Successfully generates coherent comics with rich storylines combining ChatGPT and Stable Diffusion

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
LoRA fine-tuning enables targeted adaptation of Stable Diffusion to generate specific characters while preserving the base model's general capabilities. LoRA introduces low-rank adaptations that modify the model's behavior for specific concepts without altering the original weights, allowing the model to learn character-specific features while maintaining its ability to generate general scenes and styles.

### Mechanism 2
ControlNet with Canny edge detection provides precise pose control for generated characters, ensuring consistency across panels. ControlNet extracts structural information from reference images and conditions the diffusion process to maintain these structures in generated outputs, allowing the model to place characters in specific poses as dictated by the story requirements.

### Mechanism 3
The story score metric effectively evaluates AI-generated narratives by balancing similarity to source material with general plot quality. The story score combines cosine similarity of BERT embeddings with average similarity to popular manga, weighted by an originality factor, providing a quantitative measure of narrative quality.

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map**: ChatGPT → LoRA fine-tuned Stable Diffusion → ControlNet conditioning → Image generation → SSIM/FID evaluation
- **Critical path**: ChatGPT generates storylines and dialogue → LoRA fine-tuning adapts Stable Diffusion to characters → ControlNet provides pose control → Image generation produces comic panels → SSIM/FID evaluate image quality
- **Design tradeoffs**: LoRA vs full fine-tuning (smaller file size vs potentially better fidelity), story score components (similarity to source vs general quality), ControlNet conditioning (precise pose vs natural expressions)
- **Failure signatures**: Character inconsistency across panels (ControlNet issues), poor character fidelity (insufficient LoRA training), weak narrative coherence (inadequate story score or prompts)
- **First 3 experiments**:
  1. Test LoRA fine-tuning on a single character with different rank values to find optimal balance between file size and fidelity
  2. Evaluate ControlNet conditioning with different reference images to assess pose preservation consistency
  3. Validate story score metric by comparing its rankings with human evaluations on a small set of generated stories

## Open Questions the Paper Calls Out

### Open Question 1
How can the story score metric be validated and proven to accurately evaluate AI-generated stories? The paper states "Our story score metric needs more testing and proof" as a limitation. Comparative studies showing the story score's correlation with human evaluations of story quality, and tests against other existing story evaluation metrics would resolve this.

### Open Question 2
Are there better metrics than SSIM and FID for evaluating AI-generated comics? The paper mentions "SSIM and FID score is not the perfect way to evaluate the AI-generated comic, we need to invent better metrics" as a limitation. Development and validation of new metrics that specifically measure aspects like character consistency, scene coherence, and narrative flow would resolve this.

### Open Question 3
How does the performance of the fine-tuned Stable Diffusion model scale with larger datasets and more diverse characters? The paper mentions "Our experiment could be better if we have more time and resources, we could test more characters and larger random batches" as a limitation. Experiments using larger datasets with more varied characters and styles would resolve this.

## Limitations
- Evaluation relies heavily on quantitative metrics without extensive human evaluation of narrative coherence or artistic quality
- Approach tested exclusively on One Piece characters and style, limiting generalizability
- Several critical implementation details are missing, including exact LoRA model specifications

## Confidence

**High Confidence**: The technical feasibility of using LoRA for character-specific fine-tuning and ControlNet for pose consistency is well-established in the literature.

**Medium Confidence**: The quantitative improvements against Midjourney V5 are reported, but without full methodological details or statistical significance testing.

**Low Confidence**: The story score metric's ability to genuinely measure narrative quality and coherence is questionable without human validation studies.

## Next Checks

1. **Human Evaluation Study**: Conduct a blind study where human manga readers evaluate the narrative coherence and artistic quality of comics generated using this method versus baseline approaches.

2. **Cross-Style Generalization Test**: Apply the exact same pipeline to a different manga/comic style (e.g., Naruto, Attack on Titan, or Western comics like Calvin and Hobbes) and measure whether the same level of character fidelity and narrative quality is maintained.

3. **Ablation Study on Story Score Components**: Systematically vary the weights (γ parameter) in the story score formula and test how sensitive the final rankings are to these changes. Additionally, compare the story score rankings with human evaluations to determine which components actually correlate with perceived narrative quality.
