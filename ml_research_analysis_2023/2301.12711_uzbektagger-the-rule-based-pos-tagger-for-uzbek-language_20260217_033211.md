# UzbekTagger: The rule-based POS tagger for Uzbek language

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2301.12711
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.12711
- Reference count: 8
- First publicly available POS-tagged dataset and rule-based tagger for Uzbek language

## Executive Summary
The paper introduces UzbekTagger, a rule-based part-of-speech tagger specifically designed for the Uzbek language. The authors created this system to address the lack of computational linguistic resources for Uzbek, leveraging the language's agglutinative nature through a stemming approach with affix/suffix stripping. The work includes the development of a new annotated dataset containing 1,581 sentences and 23,482 words across 23 POS categories, manually annotated by linguistic experts. The tagger demonstrates strong performance with overall accuracy of 89.78%, ranging from 79.5% to 97% across different POS categories, representing a significant contribution to Uzbek natural language processing.

## Method Summary
The authors developed UzbekTagger using a rule-based approach that exploits Uzbek's agglutinative morphology. The system employs a stemming methodology with affix and suffix stripping, utilizing a comprehensive database of Uzbek word stems. This approach is particularly well-suited for Uzbek, which features extensive morphological inflection through suffixes that modify words to express grammatical relationships. The tagger processes text by first normalizing it, then applying morphological analysis rules to identify and strip affixes, and finally matching the remaining stem to entries in the stem database to determine the base word and its POS category. The methodology was evaluated against a newly created annotated dataset of 1,581 sentences containing 23,482 words across 23 POS categories, manually annotated by Uzbek linguistic experts.

## Key Results
- Achieved overall accuracy of 89.78% on the newly created Uzbek POS dataset
- Individual POS category accuracy ranges from 79.5% to 97%
- Successfully created the first publicly available POS-tagged dataset for Uzbek language
- Demonstrated effectiveness of rule-based approach for agglutinative language morphology

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The rule-based approach works effectively for Uzbek because the language's agglutinative structure follows relatively consistent morphological patterns. By systematically stripping affixes and matching stems to a comprehensive database, the tagger can accurately identify base words and their grammatical functions. This method leverages the predictable nature of Uzbek morphology, where grammatical relationships are primarily expressed through suffixes rather than through word order or auxiliary words.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component Map:** Text Normalization -> Morphological Analysis -> Affix/Suffix Stripping -> Stem Database Lookup -> POS Assignment

**Critical Path:** The system processes text through normalization, then applies morphological rules to identify and strip affixes, followed by stem lookup to determine the base word and POS category. The stem database is the critical component, as it must contain comprehensive coverage of Uzbek vocabulary.

**Design Tradeoffs:** Rule-based approach prioritizes determinism and explainability over potential accuracy gains from statistical methods. This choice is justified by the lack of large training datasets for Uzbek and the language's predictable morphological patterns, but may struggle with morphological ambiguities that statistical approaches could resolve.

**Failure Signatures:** The system may fail when encountering words with irregular morphology, loanwords that don't follow standard Uzbek patterns, or ambiguous affix sequences. Words with multiple valid morphological interpretations or those not present in the stem database will also cause errors.
