# Can ChatGPT Write a Good Boolean Query for Systematic Review Literature Search?

## Quick Facts
- **arXiv ID:** 2302.03495
- **Source URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03495
- **Reference count:** 40
- **Primary result:** ChatGPT generates Boolean queries with higher precision but lower recall compared to state-of-the-art methods

## Executive Summary
This study evaluates the effectiveness of ChatGPT for generating Boolean queries in systematic review literature searches. The researchers found that ChatGPT-generated queries achieve higher precision but lower recall compared to existing state-of-the-art methods. Guided prompts significantly improve query effectiveness, though the non-deterministic nature of ChatGPT leads to high variability across multiple runs. The presence of incorrect MeSH terms in generated queries poses additional challenges for reproducibility and reliability in systematic review searches.

## Method Summary
The study compares ChatGPT-generated Boolean queries against established systematic review query methods across multiple medical review topics. Researchers tested different prompt variations to assess their impact on query effectiveness, measuring precision and recall metrics. Multiple runs were conducted with identical prompts to evaluate consistency and variability in generated queries. The evaluation included analysis of MeSH term accuracy and overall query structure compared to gold standard queries.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT queries achieved higher precision but significantly lower recall than state-of-the-art methods
- Guided prompts led to improved query effectiveness compared to unguided approaches
- High variability in query performance across multiple runs raised reproducibility concerns

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
ChatGPT's language understanding capabilities allow it to generate syntactically correct Boolean queries with logical operators and structured search terms. The model's training on diverse text sources enables it to identify relevant concepts and relationships for medical literature searches. However, its probabilistic nature and lack of domain-specific grounding in medical indexing systems like MeSH lead to inconsistencies and errors in term selection.

## Limitations
- High variability in query performance across multiple runs raises concerns about reproducibility
- Incorrect MeSH terms in generated queries may compromise search comprehensiveness
- Small sample size of review topics limits generalizability of findings

## Confidence
- **Medium confidence** in the core finding that ChatGPT achieves higher precision but lower recall compared to state-of-the-art methods
- Main limitations include high variability across runs, incorrect MeSH terms, and small sample size
