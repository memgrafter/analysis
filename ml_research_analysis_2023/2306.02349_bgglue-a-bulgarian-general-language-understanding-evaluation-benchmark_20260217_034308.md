# bgGLUE: A Bulgarian General Language Understanding Evaluation Benchmark

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.02349
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.02349
- Reference count: 35
- Introduces first comprehensive Bulgarian NLU benchmark with nine diverse tasks spanning token classification, regression, and text classification

## Executive Summary
bgGLUE presents the first comprehensive Bulgarian General Language Understanding Evaluation benchmark, consisting of nine diverse NLU tasks. The benchmark includes curated datasets with cleaned data leaks and new topic-based or temporal splits to ensure robust evaluation. The tasks span multiple formats including token classification, regression, and text classification. Baseline experiments using six multilingual transformer models demonstrate strong performance on simpler tasks like POS tagging and NER, while showing limited success on more complex reasoning tasks such as question answering and fact-checking.

## Method Summary
The benchmark was constructed through careful dataset curation, including removal of data leaks and creation of topic-based or temporal splits to prevent model leakage. Nine distinct NLU tasks were selected to cover the full spectrum of language understanding challenges. The evaluation framework tests model performance across token classification, regression, and text classification tasks. Baseline experiments were conducted using six multilingual transformer models, with fine-tuned models released alongside the benchmark. A public leaderboard was established to facilitate ongoing community evaluation and model comparison.

## Key Results
- Strong performance on POS tagging and NER tasks across multilingual models
- Limited success on complex reasoning tasks like question answering and fact-checking
- Larger and more robustly pre-trained models outperform smaller ones, while knowledge-distilled models remain competitive despite fewer parameters

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The benchmark's effectiveness stems from its comprehensive task coverage and rigorous dataset preparation. By removing data leaks and implementing temporal and topic-based splits, the evaluation ensures models are tested on genuinely novel data rather than memorized patterns. The inclusion of nine diverse tasks spanning different complexity levels provides a holistic assessment of language understanding capabilities. The release of cleaned datasets, training code, and fine-tuned models creates a complete ecosystem for reproducible research and development.

## Limitations
- Benchmark effectiveness depends heavily on dataset quality and representativeness
- Temporal and topic-based splits may introduce domain-specific biases
- Evaluation limited to multilingual transformer models, excluding potential monolingual Bulgarian-specific approaches

## Confidence

- Medium confidence in baseline results due to limited model diversity
- Medium confidence in task difficulty assessments due to potential data quantity/quality issues
