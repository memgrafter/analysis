# Diffusion models with location-scale noise

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2304.05907
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.05907
- Reference count: 2
- Non-Gaussian noise distributions do not outperform Gaussian noise in diffusion models

## Executive Summary
This paper investigates whether non-Gaussian noise distributions can improve diffusion model performance for image generation. The authors propose GDDIM, a generalized framework extending DDPM to handle location-scale family distributions including Gaussian, Laplace, Uniform, Student-t, and Generalized-Gaussian. Using the Method of Moments to estimate noise parameters without explicitly modeling intermediate steps, they test various distributions on CIFAR-10 with 100 reverse steps.

## Method Summary
The authors extend DDPM to create GDDIM, a framework supporting location-scale family noise distributions. Rather than modeling each diffusion step explicitly, they employ the Method of Moments to estimate noise distribution parameters directly. This approach allows sampling from non-Gaussian distributions while maintaining computational efficiency. The framework theoretically supports any location-scale distribution but requires careful parameter estimation to ensure stable reverse process sampling.

## Key Results
- GDDIM performs slightly worse than standard DDIM on CIFAR-10
- Gaussian noise consistently outperforms all other tested distributions
- Among non-Gaussian options, Laplace distribution performs best, with heavier-tailed distributions showing better performance than lighter-tailed ones
