# Explaining Legal Concepts with Augmented Large Language Models (GPT-4)

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2306.09525
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.09525
- Reference count: 38
- GPT-4 augmented with case law significantly reduces hallucinations in legal explanations

## Executive Summary
This study evaluates GPT-4's ability to generate factually accurate explanations of legal terms from statutory provisions, comparing a baseline approach (direct GPT-4) against an augmented approach that provides GPT-4 with relevant case law sentences. Two legal experts annotated 168 explanations across five categories: factuality, clarity, relevance, information richness, and on-pointedness. The augmented approach significantly outperformed the baseline in factuality (eliminating hallucinations) and relevance, while both approaches performed similarly in other categories. This demonstrates that combining GPT-4 with legal information retrieval effectively addresses the model's hallucination problem and produces higher quality legal explanations.

## Method Summary
The study compared two approaches for generating legal explanations: a baseline approach using direct GPT-4 prompts, and an augmented approach that first retrieved relevant case law sentences using legal information retrieval systems before prompting GPT-4. Two legal experts independently annotated 168 explanations across five evaluation categories: factuality, clarity, relevance, information richness, and on-pointedness. The annotations were then analyzed to determine the effectiveness of the augmented approach in improving explanation quality compared to the baseline.

## Key Results
- Augmented approach eliminated hallucinations completely, achieving perfect factuality scores
- Significant improvement in relevance scores compared to baseline
- Both approaches performed similarly in clarity, information richness, and on-pointedness
- The study focused on 168 explanations from Italian statutory provisions

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The augmentation works by providing GPT-4 with concrete legal precedents that ground its explanations in actual case law, preventing the model from generating plausible-sounding but factually incorrect information. By constraining the model's output to information supported by retrieved case law, the approach leverages GPT-4's language capabilities while ensuring factual accuracy through external validation sources.

## Limitations
- Evaluation focused on 168 explanations from Italian statutory provisions, limiting generalizability
- Five-category annotation scheme may not capture all dimensions of explanation quality
- Results may not apply to other legal systems or jurisdictions
- Computational costs of augmented approach not examined

## Confidence
- **High confidence**: Elimination of hallucinations through augmentation is well-supported
- **Medium confidence**: Performance differences in relevance and information richness may vary by domain
- **Medium confidence**: Similar performance in clarity suggests augmentation primarily addresses factual accuracy
