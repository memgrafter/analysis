# Can ChatGPT Understand Too? A Comparative Study on ChatGPT and Fine-tuned BERT

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2302.10198
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10198
- Reference count: 5
- Primary result: ChatGPT achieves comparable average performance to BERT-base (78.7% vs 79.2%) on GLUE benchmark but underperforms powerful BERT-style models like RoBERTa-large

## Executive Summary
This paper conducts a systematic evaluation of ChatGPT's understanding ability on the GLUE benchmark, comparing its zero-shot performance against fine-tuned BERT-style models. The results show that ChatGPT achieves comparable average performance to BERT-base (78.7% vs 79.2%) despite not being fine-tuned on task-specific data. However, it still underperforms powerful BERT-style models like RoBERTa-large (87.8%). The study reveals that ChatGPT excels at inference tasks while struggling with paraphrase and similarity tasks, particularly for negative instances.

## Method Summary
The study evaluates ChatGPT's performance on GLUE benchmark tasks using zero-shot inference with task-specific prompts, comparing results against fine-tuned BERT-base, BERT-large, RoBERTa-base, and RoBERTa-large models. The evaluation uses randomly sampled subsets from GLUE dev sets (25 instances per class, 50 for STS-B) and appropriate metrics including accuracy, correlation coefficients, Matthew correlation, and F1 scores. Task-specific prompts are designed based on task descriptions, though exact formulations are not fully specified in the paper.

## Key Results
- ChatGPT achieves comparable average performance to BERT-base (78.7% vs 79.2%) on GLUE benchmark
- ChatGPT underperforms RoBERTa-large by a clear margin (87.8% vs 78.7%)
- ChatGPT outperforms all BERT models on inference tasks, demonstrating strong reasoning ability
- ChatGPT performs poorly on paraphrase and similarity tasks, especially for negative instances

## Why This Works (Mechanism)

### Mechanism 1
ChatGPT achieves comparable average performance to BERT-base (78.7% vs 79.2%) on GLUE benchmark despite being a general-purpose LLM rather than task-specific fine-tuned model. Zero-shot inference capabilities allow ChatGPT to generalize across multiple NLU tasks without task-specific training, leveraging its broad pretraining knowledge. The core assumption is that ChatGPT's pretraining on diverse web-scale data provides sufficient semantic understanding to handle multiple NLU tasks without fine-tuning.

### Mechanism 2
ChatGPT excels at inference/reasoning tasks compared to BERT models due to its emergent reasoning abilities. The model's architecture enables chain-of-thought reasoning and logical inference without explicit fine-tuning on inference tasks. The core assumption is that emergent reasoning capabilities arise from scale and pretraining diversity rather than task-specific optimization.

### Mechanism 3
ChatGPT's limitations on paraphrase and similarity tasks stem from lack of fine-grained semantic training. Without task-specific fine-tuning, ChatGPT relies on coarse-grained semantic patterns that miss subtle distinctions between similar but non-equivalent sentences. The core assumption is that human feedback during training focused on generation quality rather than semantic precision for understanding tasks.

## Architecture Onboarding

- **Component map**: ChatGPT operates as a frozen pretrained LLM using prompt engineering for task adaptation, while BERT-style models use task-specific fine-tuning layers on top of pretrained encoders
- **Critical path**: Input → Prompt engineering → Zero-shot inference → Output generation (ChatGPT) vs Input → Fine-tuning → Task-specific layers → Output (BERT-style)
- **Design tradeoffs**: ChatGPT trades task-specific optimization for flexibility and zero-shot capability, while BERT-style models optimize for specific tasks but lose generality
- **Failure signatures**: Poor performance on tasks requiring fine-grained semantic distinctions, confused logic in inference tasks, inability to handle domain-specific terminology

## Limitations
- The exact prompt templates used for each GLUE task are not specified, which could significantly impact ChatGPT's performance
- The study relies on randomly sampled subsets rather than full dev sets, which may not capture the complete distribution of task difficulty
- The non-deterministic nature of ChatGPT introduces variance that isn't fully quantified in the results

## Confidence

- **High Confidence**: The finding that ChatGPT achieves comparable average performance to BERT-base (78.7% vs 79.2%) is well-supported by the reported results
- **Medium Confidence**: The claim about ChatGPT's superiority on inference tasks relative to BERT models is supported but could benefit from more rigorous statistical validation
- **Low Confidence**: The explanation for ChatGPT's limitations on paraphrase and similarity tasks (lack of human feedback on semantic precision) remains speculative without direct evidence
