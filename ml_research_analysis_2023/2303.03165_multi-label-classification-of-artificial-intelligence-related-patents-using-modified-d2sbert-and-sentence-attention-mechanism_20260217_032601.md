# Multi label classification of Artificial Intelligence related patents using Modified D2SBERT and Sentence Attention mechanism

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2303.03165
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.03165
- Reference count: 39
- Primary result: F1-macro of 0.7234 and F1-micro of 0.7341 on USPTO AI patent classification

## Executive Summary
This paper presents a modified D2SBERT architecture with Sentence Attention mechanism for multi-label classification of AI-related patents into International Patent Classification (IPC) codes. The model addresses BERT's sequence length limitations by processing patents at the sentence level rather than treating the entire document as a single sequence. Using USPTO patent data from 2019-2020, the approach achieves superior performance compared to traditional deep learning methods including CNN, LSTM, Bi-LSTM, and BERT variants, with F1-macro of 0.7234 and F1-micro of 0.7341.

## Method Summary
The proposed method modifies D2SBERT to process patent documents at the sentence level using Spacy tokenization, then applies a Sentence Attention mechanism to capture temporal order and relationships between sentences. The model uses pre-trained BERT as a sentence encoder, followed by attention-weighted sentence representations for classification. The approach addresses BERT's maximum sequence length limitation (512 tokens) by avoiding document-level truncation and instead focusing on sentence-level representations that preserve important patent information while maintaining computational efficiency.

## Key Results
- Achieved F1-macro of 0.7234 and F1-micro of 0.7341 on USPTO AI patent classification
- Outperformed CNN, LSTM, Bi-LSTM, and BERT variants in multi-label classification
- Demonstrated effectiveness of sentence-level processing for long patent documents

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The Sentence Attention mechanism effectively captures important sentences and their relationships within patent documents by preserving temporal order at the sentence level rather than treating documents as flat sequences. This approach maintains crucial contextual information while avoiding BERT's sequence length limitations, allowing the model to process the full content of patent descriptions without truncation. The attention mechanism weights sentence representations based on their relevance to specific IPC codes, enabling accurate multi-label classification of complex AI patents.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component map**: USPTO patent data -> Spacy sentence tokenization -> Modified D2SBERT encoding -> Sentence Attention mechanism -> Multi-label classification

**Critical path**: The sequence from sentence tokenization through Sentence Attention to final classification is critical. Each component must function correctly - errors in tokenization propagate through encoding, and attention weights directly impact classification accuracy.

**Design tradeoffs**: Sentence-level processing trades some document-level context for computational efficiency and complete information retention. The Sentence Attention mechanism balances between capturing sentence relationships and maintaining manageable sequence lengths for BERT processing.

**Failure signatures**: Poor performance indicates issues in any component - tokenization errors cause encoding problems, incorrect attention weights lead to wrong classifications, and sequence length violations cause BERT truncation losses.

**First experiments**: 1) Test sentence tokenization on sample patents to verify proper sentence boundaries, 2) Validate BERT encoding produces consistent sentence representations, 3) Check attention weight distributions to ensure they capture meaningful sentence relationships.
