# Boosting Distress Support Dialogue Responses with Motivational Interviewing Strategy

## Quick Facts
- arXiv ID: 2305.10195
- Source URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10195
- Reference count: 40
- Key outcome: Improves distress-support dialogue responses using Motivational Interviewing strategies by detecting non-MI-adherent "Advise without permission" responses and rephrasing them into MI-adherent "Advise with permission" form, achieving high style transfer strength and good semantic similarity.

## Executive Summary
This work presents a method for improving distress support dialogue responses using Motivational Interviewing (MI) strategies. The approach focuses on detecting non-MI-adherent "Advise without permission" responses and rephrasing them into MI-adherent "Advise with permission" form, framed as a text style transfer problem. By automatically constructing pseudo-parallel corpora from Reddit and peer support data using template replacement and retrieval methods, the authors avoid costly human annotation while achieving significant improvements in style transfer strength and semantic similarity.

## Method Summary
The method involves training an MI classifier on an annotated dataset of distress-support responses, then creating pseudo-parallel corpora containing pairs of non-MI-adherent and MI-adherent responses. These corpora are used to fine-tune Blender and GPT-3 models with prompting techniques. The approach combines template-based replacement and similarity-based retrieval to construct training data, then evaluates both automatically and through human raters to assess style transfer strength and semantic preservation.

## Key Results
- GPT-3-based rephrasers achieved higher style transfer strength (73.53-79.41% classifier accuracy) compared to Blender-based ones
- Semantic similarity scores averaged around 3 on 0-4 Likert scales for both automatic and human evaluations
- Data augmentation and template-based methods yielded best results with larger datasets
- The approach enables safer, more effective chatbot responses in mental health support

## Why This Works (Mechanism)
The method works by leveraging the structure of Motivational Interviewing to identify specific non-adherent response patterns ("Advise without permission") and systematically transform them into adherent ones ("Advise with permission"). By using template replacement and retrieval methods to construct training data, the approach captures the semantic essence of advice while changing its delivery style. The prompting techniques help guide the model when training data is limited, and the combination of automatic and human evaluation provides robust assessment of both style transfer and semantic preservation.

## Architecture Onboarding

**Component map**: MI dataset -> MI classifier -> Pseudo-parallel corpora (template + retrieval) -> Fine-tuned Blender/GPT-3 models -> Evaluation

**Critical path**: The core workflow flows from annotated MI dataset through classifier training to pseudo-parallel corpus construction, then to model fine-tuning and evaluation. Each stage depends on the previous one, with the quality of the pseudo-parallel corpora being particularly critical for downstream performance.

**Design tradeoffs**: The approach trades human annotation costs for potential noise in automatically constructed corpora. Template-based methods offer more control but may be limited in coverage, while retrieval-based methods can capture more natural variations but risk semantic drift.

**Failure signatures**: Poor style transfer strength may indicate inadequate classifier training or corpus construction issues. Low semantic similarity suggests template replacement or retrieval methods are introducing meaning changes. Discrepancies between automatic and human evaluations may reveal limitations in metric design.

**First experiments**: 1) Train MI classifier and validate on held-out data, 2) Inspect sample pseudo-parallel pairs for quality, 3) Fine-tune small model on limited corpus to verify methodology before scaling up.

## Limitations
- Automatic corpus construction may introduce noise or bias into training data, with quality not thoroughly validated
- Evaluation relies on automatic metrics that may not fully capture MI adherence nuances, with unclear correlation to human judgment
- Generalizability to other domains or languages is not addressed, with uncertainty about effectiveness beyond English distress-support dialogues
